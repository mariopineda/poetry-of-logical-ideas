A five-person planning committee is chosen from 10 students and 8 staff members. If exactly 3 members must be students, how many different committees are possible?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The 3 students can be selected in $\binom{10}{3}=120$ ways and the 2 staff in $\binom82=28$ ways. The choices are independent, giving $120\cdot28=\boxed{3360}$ committees.
