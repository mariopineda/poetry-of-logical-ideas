A wildlife exhibition creates three-part display headings. The first word comes from a list of seven descriptive words, **three of which begin with R**. The second word comes from a list of four habitat words, and the final word comes from a list of six photographic techniques.

A heading must use exactly one word from each list, in that order. How many different headings can begin with the letter R? Justify your calculation.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> There are $3$ eligible first words, $4$ possible second words and $6$ possible final words. The choices are independent, and their order by category is fixed.
>
> By the fundamental counting principle,
>
> $$3\times4\times6=\boxed{72}$$
>
> headings can begin with R.
