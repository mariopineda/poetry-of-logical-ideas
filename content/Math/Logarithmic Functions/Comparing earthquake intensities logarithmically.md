Two seismic stations report magnitude measurements of $8.1$ and $6.7$ for different earthquakes. For this model, the ratio of shaking intensities is $10^{M_1-M_2}$, where $M_1$ is the larger magnitude.

Calculate how many times as intense the stronger earthquake is, **to the nearest whole number**. Show the exponent used.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Subtract the magnitudes before evaluating the power of 10:
>
> $$M_1-M_2=8.1-6.7=1.4.$$
>
> The intensity ratio is
>
> $$\frac{I_1}{I_2}=10^{1.4}\approx25.118864.$$
>
> Thus the stronger earthquake is approximately $\boxed{25\text{ times as intense}}$. The magnitude difference itself is not the intensity ratio.
