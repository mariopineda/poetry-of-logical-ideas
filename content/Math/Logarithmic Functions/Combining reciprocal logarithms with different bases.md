Suppose $\log_a 27=3$ and $\log_9 b=\frac32$. Determine $\log_a b+\log_b a$ as an exact value. Show how the values of $a$ and $b$ are found.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Because $a>0$ and $a^3=27$, $\boxed{a=3}$. Also $b=9^{3/2}=(\sqrt9)^3=27$.
>
> Therefore
>
> $$\log_a b=\log_3 27=3,\qquad \log_b a=\log_{27}3=\frac13.$$
>
> Their sum is
>
> $$\boxed{3+\frac13=\frac{10}{3}}.$$
>
> A reciprocal logarithm is valid because $\log_{27}3=1/\log_3 27$.
