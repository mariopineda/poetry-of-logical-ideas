The logarithmic relationship

$$4\log_p q=r$$

holds for $p>1$ and $q>0$. Rewrite the relationship **in exponential form** using only $p$, $q$, and $r$; justify the transformation.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Apply the power law:
>
> $$4\log_p q=\log_p(q^4)=r.$$
>
> Using $\log_p M=r\iff p^r=M$ yields
>
> $$\boxed{p^r=q^4}.$$
>
> The restrictions $p>1$ and $q>0$ make the logarithm well-defined.
