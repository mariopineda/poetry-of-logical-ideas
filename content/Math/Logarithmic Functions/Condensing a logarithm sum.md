Condense $\log_4(x+3)+\log_4(x-5)$ into a single logarithm. State the domain of the original expression explicitly.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Product rule yields $\boxed{\log_4((x+3)(x-5))}=\log_4(x^2-2x-15)$. Both arguments must be positive: $x+3>0$ and $x-5>0$, so $\boxed{x>5}$.
