Express $7=2^{3x-5}$ as an equivalent logarithmic equation. Then isolate $x$ exactly.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> By the definition of a logarithm, $\boxed{\log_2 7=3x-5}$. Hence $\boxed{x=\frac{5+\log_2 7}{3}}$.
