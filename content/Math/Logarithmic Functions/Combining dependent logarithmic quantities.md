The quantities $u$ and $v$ are defined by $\log_2 7=2u$ and $\log_2 3=5v$. A positive quantity $m$ satisfies $\log_2 m=4$. Express

$$\log_2(441m^3)$$

as an expression involving only $u$ and $v$. Show the logarithm laws used.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Since $441=7^2\cdot3^2$,
>
> $$\log_2(441m^3)=2\log_2 7+2\log_2 3+3\log_2m=\boxed{4u+10v+12}.$$
