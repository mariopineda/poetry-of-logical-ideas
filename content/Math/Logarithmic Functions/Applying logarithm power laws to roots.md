If $\log_a b=1.47$, with $a>0$, $a\ne1$, and $b>0$, determine $\log_a(\sqrt[3]{b})$ to the nearest hundredth. Explain.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The cube root equals the power $b^{1/3}$. Applying the logarithm power law (valid for $b>0$ and $a>0$, $a\ne1$) gives
>
> $$\log_a\sqrt[3]{b}=\log_a(b^{1/3})=\frac13\log_a b=\frac{1.47}{3}=\boxed{0.49}.$$
>
> This is exact to two decimal places.
