Let $p,q,r>1$. Express the following as a single logarithm and simplify the argument:

$$\log_p q+3\log_p(pr)-3.$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> By the power and product rules,
>
> $$\log_p[q(pr)^3]-\log_p p^3=\log_p\left(\frac{qp^3r^3}{p^3}\right)=\boxed{\log_p(qr^3)}.$$
