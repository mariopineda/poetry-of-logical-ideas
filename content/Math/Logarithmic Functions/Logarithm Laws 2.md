Write each expression as a single logarithm and then evaluate the expression

1.

$$
4 \log_{2} 2 - \frac{1}{3} \log_{2} 64
$$
2.

$$
4 \log_{5} 2 - \log_{5} \left( \frac{16}{5} \right)
$$
3.

$$
8 \log_{8} 2 + 2 \log_{8} 0.5
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> $$
> 4\log_2 2
> -
> \frac13\log_2 64
> $$
>
> Use the Power Rule:
>
> $$
> =
> \log_2(2^4)
> -
> \log_2(64^{1/3}).
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> =
> \log_2\left(\frac{16}{4}\right)
> =
> \log_2 4
> =
> \boxed{2}.
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> $$
> 4\log_5 2
> -
> \log_5\left(\frac{16}{5}\right).
> $$
>
> Use the Power Rule:
>
> $$
> =
> \log_5 16
> -
> \log_5\left(\frac{16}{5}\right).
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> =
> \log_5\left(
> \frac{16}{16/5}
> \right)
> =
> \log_5 5
> =
> \boxed{1}.
> $$
>
> ### 3.
>
> $$
> 8\log_8 2
> +
> 2\log_8 0.5.
> $$
>
> Use the Power Rule:
>
> $$
> =
> \log_8(2^8)
> +
> \log_8\left[\left(\frac12\right)^2\right].
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> =
> \log_8\left(2^8\cdot2^{-2}\right)
> =
> \log_8(2^6)
> =
> \log_8 64
> =
> \boxed{2}.
> $$
