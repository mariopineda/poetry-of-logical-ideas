Evaluate by converting to exponential form.

1.

$$
4\log_{10} 0.001
$$
2.

$$
6^{\log_{6} 36}
$$
3.

$$
3 \log_{\frac{1}{10}} 10 - 2 \log_{\frac{1}{10}} \left( \frac{1}{100} \right)
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> $$
> 4\log_{10}(0.001)
> $$
>
> Since
>
> $$
> 0.001=10^{-3},
> $$
>
> we have
>
> $$
> \log_{10}(0.001)=-3.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{-12}.
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> $$
> 6^{\log_6 36}
> $$
>
> Using
>
> $$
> b^{\log_b x}=x,
> $$
>
> we get
>
> $$
> \boxed{36}.
> $$
>
> ### 3.
>
> $$
> 3\log_{\frac1{10}}10
> -
> 2\log_{\frac1{10}}\left(\frac1{100}\right).
> $$
>
> Since
>
> $$
> \left(\frac1{10}\right)^{-1}=10,
> $$
>
> $$
> \log_{\frac1{10}}10=-1.
> $$
>
> Also,
>
> $$
> \left(\frac1{10}\right)^2=\frac1{100},
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> \log_{\frac1{10}}\left(\frac1{100}\right)=2.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> 3(-1)-2(2)
> =
> \boxed{-7}.
> $$
