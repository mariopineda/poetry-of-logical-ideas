For any base $a>1$, consider the four logarithmic expressions:

$$P=\log_a(a^3),\quad Q=\log_a\sqrt a,\quad R=\log_a\left(\frac1a\right),\quad S=\log_a a.$$

Evaluate all four and place them in order from **least to greatest**, with justification.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Use $\log_a(a^k)=k$ for $a>0$, $a\ne1$:
>
> $$P=3,\qquad Q=\tfrac12,\qquad R=-1,\qquad S=1.$$
>
> Thus $\boxed{R<Q<S<P}$, or $\log_a(1/a)<\log_a\sqrt a<\log_a a<\log_a(a^3)$.
