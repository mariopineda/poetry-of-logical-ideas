Write $\dfrac25\log_3x-\log_3 7$ as one logarithm, given $x>0$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> First use the power law, then the quotient law: $\frac25\log_3x-\log_3 7=\log_3(x^{2/5})-\log_3 7=\boxed{\log_3\left(\frac{x^{2/5}}{7}\right)}$.
