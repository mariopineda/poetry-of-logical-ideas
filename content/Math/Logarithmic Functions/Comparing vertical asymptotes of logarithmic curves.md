Consider the five functions below, all with base $4$. Determine which have the vertical asymptote $x=0$ and justify your classification by examining where each logarithm's argument approaches zero: $f_1(x)=\log_4 x$, $f_2(x)=3\log_4 x$, $f_3(x)=\log_4(5x)$, $f_4(x)=\log_4(x-2)$, and $f_5(x)=\log_4 x+6$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> A logarithmic function $\log_4 A(x)$ has a vertical boundary where the positive argument tends to zero. For $f_1$, $f_2$, and $f_5$, $A(x)=x$, so the boundary is $x=0$. For $f_3$, $A(x)=5x$, also zero at $x=0$. In contrast, $f_4$ has $A(x)=x-2$, giving the boundary $x=2$. A multiplier on the whole logarithm or a vertical translation does not move the asymptote.
>
> Therefore the functions with vertical asymptote $x=0$ are $\boxed{f_1,f_2,f_3,f_5}$, **four in total**.
