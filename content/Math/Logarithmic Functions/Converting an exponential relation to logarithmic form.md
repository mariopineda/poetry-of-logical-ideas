Express $7^{x-1}=49$ in logarithmic form, then solve for $x$. Explain what the logarithm represents.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The exponential equation $7^{x-1}=49$ is equivalent to
>
> $$\boxed{\log_7 49=x-1}.$$
>
> This says that $x-1$ is the exponent to which the base $7$ must be raised to produce $49$. Since $49=7^2$, we have $x-1=2$ and
>
> $$\boxed{x=3}.$$
>
> The logarithm is defined because the base $7>0$ and is not $1$, and the argument $49>0$.
