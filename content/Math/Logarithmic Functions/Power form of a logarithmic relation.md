For $a>0$, $a\ne1$, suppose $\log_a u=5$. Express $u^2$ as a single power of $a$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The logarithmic statement $\log_a u=5$ is equivalent to $u=a^5$ when $a>0$ and $a\ne1$. Squaring both sides gives $u^2=(a^5)^2=\boxed{a^{10}}$. No extra branch appears because $u=a^5>0$, consistent with the logarithm's argument restriction.
