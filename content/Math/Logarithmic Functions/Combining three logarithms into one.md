For $b>1$, combine $\log_b48+\log_b27-\log_b12$ into a single logarithm $\log_b M$ and determine $M$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Apply the product and quotient laws: $\log_b\left(\frac{48\cdot27}{12}\right)=\log_b108$. Therefore $\boxed{M=108}$.
