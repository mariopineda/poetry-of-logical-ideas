A greenhouse technician records the average height of seedlings over their first six weeks.

| Age $t$ (weeks) | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Average height $h$ (cm) | 4.5 | 7.8 | 9.7 | 11.4 | 12.5 | 13.5 |

Use logarithmic regression of the form $h=a+b\ln t$ to estimate the average height when $t=10$ weeks, **to the nearest centimetre**. State the regression equation and keep unrounded calculator coefficients until the final step.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Enter the six data pairs in the calculator and perform logarithmic regression with $\ln t$ as the predictor. Least-squares regression gives
>
> $$a\approx4.386538,\qquad b\approx5.028045.$$
>
> The model is therefore
>
> $$h(t)\approx4.386538+5.028045\ln t.$$
>
> At $t=10$,
>
> $$h(10)\approx4.386538+5.028045\ln(10)\approx15.9640.$$
>
> The predicted height is $\boxed{16\text{ cm}}$ to the nearest centimetre. This is an extrapolation beyond the six observed weeks, so the model's reliability is not guaranteed.
