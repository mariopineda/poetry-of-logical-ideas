For a base $a>1$, a quantity $y$ satisfies $y=\dfrac{a^x}{a^{3x-8}}$ and $\log_a y=x$. Determine the value of $x$ without assuming a particular base.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The exponent laws give $y=a^{x-(3x-8)}=a^{8-2x}$. Since $\log_a y=x$, also $y=a^x$. Because $a>1$, the exponents must match: $8-2x=x$. Thus $\boxed{x=\tfrac83}$.
