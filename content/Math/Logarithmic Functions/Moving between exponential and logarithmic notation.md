A science-lab simulation models the population at time $t$ by the equation $5^t=N$.

Express the equation in **logarithmic notation**, isolating $t$. Explain how the base, exponent and argument correspond, and give the restriction on $N$ for real solutions. Verify the notation using $t=3$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> By the definition of logarithms,
>
> $$\boxed{t=\log_5 N}.$$
>
> The base $5$ is unchanged, $N$ is the logarithm's argument, and $t$ is the exponent. The real logarithm requires $\boxed{N>0}$.
>
> Checking $t=3$: $5^3=125$ is equivalent to $\boxed{\log_5 125=3}$.
