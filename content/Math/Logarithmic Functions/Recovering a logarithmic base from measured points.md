A calibration curve has the form $y=\log_b(x+c)$, where $b>1$. It passes through $(0,0)$ and $(5,1.5)$, as shown in the accompanying graph. Determine $b$ to the nearest hundredth and $c$ exactly.

![[diploma-2019-original-log-base.svg]]

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Using $(0,0)$ gives $0=\log_b c$, hence $c=1$. Using $(5,1.5)$ gives $1.5=\log_b6$, so $b^{3/2}=6$ and $b=6^{2/3}\approx3.3019$. Therefore $\boxed{c=1,\ b\approx3.30}$.
