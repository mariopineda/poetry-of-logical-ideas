Rewrite the expression below as **one logarithm** with base 5. State the resulting argument, and name the law you use.

$$\log_5 4+\log_5 25.$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Both logarithms have the same positive base, so the **product law** applies:
>
> $$\log_5a+\log_5b=\log_5(ab),\qquad a,b>0.$$
>
> Therefore
>
> $$\log_5 4+\log_5 25=\log_5(4\cdot25)=\boxed{\log_5 100}.$$
>
> The requested argument is $\boxed{100}$; no assumption about an integer logarithm value is necessary.
