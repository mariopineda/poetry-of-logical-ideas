Simplify

$$\log_4(80)-2\log_4(2)$$

into a **single logarithm** of the form $\log_4 a$. Determine the value of $a$ and explicitly show how the logarithm power law and quotient law are used.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> First, use the power law to move the coefficient into the argument:
>
> $$2\log_4(2)=\log_4(2^2)=\log_4 4.$$
>
> Then use the quotient law:
>
> $$\log_4 80-\log_4 4=\log_4\left(\frac{80}{4}\right)=\boxed{\log_4 20}.$$
>
> Hence $\boxed{a=20}$; we are not being asked to evaluate $\log_4 20$ numerically.
