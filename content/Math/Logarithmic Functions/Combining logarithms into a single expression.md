For $b>1$, use logarithm laws to rewrite

$$\log_b 37-\log_b 8$$

as one logarithm $\log_b k$. Determine $k$ **to the nearest hundredth**. Show the quotient used.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> By the quotient law,
>
> $$\log_b37-\log_b8=\log_b\!\left(\frac{37}{8}\right)=\log_b(4.625).$$
>
> Thus $\boxed{k\approx4.63}$ to the nearest hundredth. The exact expression remains $\log_b(37/8)$; rounding its argument produces an approximate equality.
