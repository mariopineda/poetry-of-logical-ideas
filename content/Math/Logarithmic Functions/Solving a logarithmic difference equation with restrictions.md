Solve the equation below, giving the answer to the nearest hundredth. State the restrictions that must be satisfied before applying logarithm laws.

$$\log_4(x+1)-\log_4(x-3)=2.$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The domain requires $x+1>0$ and $x-3>0$, so $x>3$. Combine logs: $\log_4\!\left(\frac{x+1}{x-3}\right)=2$. Thus $(x+1)/(x-3)=16$, hence $x+1=16x-48$, $49=15x$, and $\boxed{x=49/15\approx3.27}$. This is permitted since $49/15>3$.
