Suppose $a=\log_3 5$ and $b=\log_3 8$. Express $\log_3 200$ in terms of $a$ and $b$, showing the relevant product and power rules.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Factor the target argument **in terms of the known arguments**:
>
> $$200=5^2\cdot8.$$
>
> Then the product and power laws, valid for positive arguments, give
>
> $$\log_3 200=\log_3(5^2\cdot8)=2\log_3 5+\log_3 8=\boxed{2a+b}.$$
>
> No approximation of either logarithm is needed.
