For positive $a$ and $b$, write $2\log a-2\log b$ as one logarithm. Show both logarithm laws that you use.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> For $a>0$ and $b>0$, the power law gives $2\log a=\log(a^2)$ and $2\log b=\log(b^2)$. The quotient law then gives
>
> $$2\log a-2\log b=\log(a^2)-\log(b^2)=\boxed{\log\left(\frac{a^2}{b^2}\right)}.$$
>
> The argument is positive because $a,b>0$, so the combined logarithm is defined.
