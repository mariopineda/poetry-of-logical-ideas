For a calibration system, positive quantities $c$ and $d$ satisfy $7c^4=d$, with $c>0$ and $c\ne1$. Rewrite the equation in logarithmic form with base $c$. State the required domain restriction on the logarithm argument.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> First isolate the power, because $7$ is a coefficient rather than a logarithm base:
>
> $$7c^4=d\quad\Longrightarrow\quad c^4=\frac d7.$$
>
> The inverse relationship $a^p=q\iff\log_a q=p$ holds for $a>0$, $a\neq1$, $q>0$. Thus
>
> $$\boxed{\log_c\!\left(\frac d7\right)=4}.$$
>
> Here $d=7c^4>0$, so $d/7>0$, and the specified restrictions $c>0$ and $c\neq1$ make this a valid logarithm.
