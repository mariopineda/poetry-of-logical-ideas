The Venn diagram shows a community centre's participants. $W$ means using the workshop, $M$ means using the music room, and $F$ means using the fitness space. The numbers count people in each **mutually exclusive** region.

![[diploma-batch07-2013-mc10-venn-regions.svg]]

How many people use the workshop **or** the music room (or both)? Determine $n(W\cup M)$ and explain which diagram regions belong in the union.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The union $W\cup M$ contains everyone inside **either** the workshop circle or the music circle, including their overlap and the regions that also overlap fitness. Add each included region once:
>
> $$n(W\cup M)=8+9+5+4+3+2=\boxed{31}.$$
>
> The fitness-only region ($6$) and neither region ($7$) are excluded. As a separate check, $n(U)=44$ and $44-6-7=31$.
