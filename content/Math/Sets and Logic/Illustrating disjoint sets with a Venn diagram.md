The universal set is $U=\{1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8\}$. Choose two **nonempty disjoint subsets** $A$ and $B$ and draw a labelled Venn diagram of your choice in the rectangle below. Place every member of $U$ in its appropriate region.

![[diploma-batch07-2013-mc13-blank-universe.svg]]

Write $A\cap B$ and justify how your drawing represents the intersection.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> One possible answer is $A=\{1,3,5\}$ and $B=\{2,4,6\}$. Place these in two **non-overlapping** circles inside the rectangle representing $U$, and put $7$ and $8$ outside both circles.
>
> There is no element common to both sets:
>
> $$\boxed{A\cap B=\varnothing}.$$
>
> Other answers are valid provided both circles represent nonempty subsets of $U$, do not overlap, and all eight elements are assigned to their correct regions.
