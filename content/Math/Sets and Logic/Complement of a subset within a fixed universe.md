In the universe $U=\{\text{apple, pear, peach, plum, kiwi, mango, grape}\}$, let $R=\{\text{apple, peach, mango}\}$. Find the complement $R^c$ relative to $U$, showing your set notation.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The complement of $R$ consists of exactly the elements in $U$ that are **not** in $R$:
>
> $$R^c=U\setminus R.$$
>
> Removing apple, peach, and mango from the seven named fruits leaves
>
> $$\boxed{R^c=\{\text{pear, plum, kiwi, grape}\}}.$$
>
> Every listed element belongs to $U$ and none belongs to $R$, so this is the full complement relative to the stated universe.
