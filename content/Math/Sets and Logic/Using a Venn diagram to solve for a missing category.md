A community makerspace has 41 members. Six attend neither the metalwork workshop nor the photography workshop, and seven attend both. The number attending **metalwork only** is four greater than the number attending **photography only**.

How many members attend photography only? Use a four-region Venn partition or equivalent equation, and verify that all 41 members are counted.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Let $m$ denote the number attending photography only. Then metalwork-only is $m+4$, both is $7$, and neither is $6$. These four Venn regions partition the $41$ learners, so
>
> $$m+(m+4)+7+6=41.$$
>
> Simplify:
>
> $$2m+17=41\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 2m=24\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\boxed{m=12}.$$
>
> Check: photography only $12$, metalwork only $16$, both $7$, neither $6$, and $12+16+7+6=41$.
