(Topic 2-2: Graphs of Polynomial Functions in a nutshell)

### Goals
By the end of this lesson, I should be able to:
- Define and identify the **zeros** of a polynomial function.  
- Explain **multiplicity of a zero** and how it affects the graph.  
- Distinguish between:  
  - Zero with multiplicity 1.  
  - Zero with an even multiplicity.  
  - Zero with an odd multiplicity greater than 1.  
- Recognize a **point of inflection** in polynomial graphs.  
- Describe how **transformations** affect polynomial functions.
