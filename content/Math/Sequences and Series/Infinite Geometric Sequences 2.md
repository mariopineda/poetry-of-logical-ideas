Evaluate $\sum_{n=2}^{\infty} 8\left( -\frac{1}{3} \right)^n$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The series is
>
> $$
> \sum_{n=2}^{\infty}
> 8\left(-\frac13\right)^n.
> $$
>
> The first term is
>
> $$
> a
> =
> 8\left(-\frac13\right)^2
> =
> \frac89.
> $$
>
> The common ratio is
>
> $$
> r=-\frac13.
> $$
>
> Since
>
> $$
> |r|<1,
> $$
>
> the series converges.
>
> Using
>
> $$
> S_\infty=\frac{a}{1-r},
> $$
>
> we get
>
> $$
> S_\infty
> =
> \frac{\frac89}
> {1+\frac13}
> =
> \frac{\frac89}{\frac43}
> =
> \boxed{\frac23}.
> $$
