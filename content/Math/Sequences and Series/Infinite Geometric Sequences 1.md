Evaluate $\sum_{n=3}^{9} 8$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The sum is
>
> $$
> \sum_{n=3}^{9}8.
> $$
>
> There are
>
> $$
> 9-3+1=7
> $$
>
> terms.
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{7(8)=56}.
> $$
