Evaluate $\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{3n^2-n}{1+2n^2}$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Evaluate
>
> $$
> \lim_{n\to\infty}
> \frac{3n^2-n}{1+2n^2}.
> $$
>
> Divide numerator and denominator by $n^2$:
>
> $$
> \lim_{n\to\infty}
> \frac{3-\frac1n}
> {\frac1{n^2}+2}.
> $$
>
> As
>
> $$
> n\to\infty,
> $$
>
> the fractions involving $n$ approach zero.
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac32}.
> $$
