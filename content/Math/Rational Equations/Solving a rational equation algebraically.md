Solve $\dfrac{x+2}{3x}=\dfrac54$ exactly. State the excluded value and verify the solution.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The denominator requires $x\ne0$. Cross multiply: $4(x+2)=15x$, so $4x+8=15x$, $11x=8$ and $\boxed{x=\frac8{11}}$. Substitution gives $\frac{8/11+2}{24/11}=\frac{30}{24}=\frac54$, confirming the answer.
