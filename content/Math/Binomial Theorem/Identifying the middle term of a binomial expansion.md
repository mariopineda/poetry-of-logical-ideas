Determine the complete **middle term** in the expansion of $(a^2+a^5)^8$, written in ascending powers of $a$. Justify its position and calculate its coefficient.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> A binomial raised to the eighth power has $8+1=9$ terms. Since the exponents of $a$ increase strictly by three from term to term, there are no like terms to combine; the **fifth** term is the unique middle term. Using $k=4$ in the binomial theorem,
>
> $$T_5=\binom84(a^2)^{8-4}(a^5)^4=70a^{8}a^{20}=\boxed{70a^{28}}.$$
>
> This is indeed the fifth term in ascending powers of $a$.
