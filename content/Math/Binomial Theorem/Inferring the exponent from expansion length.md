An expansion of $(x+3)^n$, where $n$ is a nonnegative integer, has exactly seven terms. Determine $n$ and the constant term.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> A binomial raised to nonnegative integer power $n$ has $n+1$ nonzero terms. With seven terms, $n+1=7$, hence $\boxed{n=6}$.
>
> The constant term of $(x+3)^6$ arises when no factor of $x$ is selected: $\binom60 x^0 3^6=\boxed{729}$. No other term is constant.
