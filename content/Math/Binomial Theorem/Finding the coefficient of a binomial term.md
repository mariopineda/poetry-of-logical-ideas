When $(x+\sqrt2)^8$ is expanded in descending powers of $x$, determine the numerical coefficient of its **fifth term**. Show the binomial-theorem expression.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The fifth term has four selections of the second term $\sqrt2$; in the binomial theorem it is the term with $k=4$:
>
> $$T_5=\binom84x^{8-4}(\sqrt2)^4.$$
>
> Its numerical coefficient is
>
> $$\binom84(\sqrt2)^4=70\cdot 4=\boxed{280}.$$
>
> The exponent on $x$ is $4$; the coefficient is not $70$ because the radical factor contributes an additional $4$.
