The daylight duration at a research station is approximated by

$$D(t)=11+4\sin\left(\frac{2\pi}{365}(t-80)\right),$$

where $t$ is the day of the year and $D$ is measured in hours. Evaluate $D(172)$ to the nearest hundredth of an hour. Show the substitution and state the calculator angle mode.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Use **radian mode** because the sine argument is expressed using $\pi$:
>
> $$D(172)=11+4\sin\left(\frac{2\pi}{365}(172-80)\right)$$
> $$=11+4\sin\left(\frac{184\pi}{365}\right)\approx14.9996666.$$
>
> To the nearest hundredth of an hour, $\boxed{D(172)=15.00\text{ h}}$.
