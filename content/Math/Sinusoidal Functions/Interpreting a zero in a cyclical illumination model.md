A rotating light installation is modelled by the following graph of the percentage of its panel that is illuminated over a 24-hour cycle.

![[batch09-illumination-graph.svg]]

Identify **which graphical feature** determines when the panel is completely dark. Estimate the first such time from the graph and explain what the corresponding point means in context. Verify your estimate using $I(t)=50+50\cos(\pi t/12)$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Complete darkness means $I(t)=0$, so the relevant graph feature is an **$x$-intercept**.
>
> The graph touches the horizontal axis at about $t=12$ hours. Algebraically,
>
> $$0=50+50\cos\left(\frac{\pi t}{12}\right)\implies\cos\left(\frac{\pi t}{12}\right)=-1.$$
>
> On $0\le t\le24$, this first occurs when $\pi t/12=\pi$, so $\boxed{t=12\text{ h}}$.
>
> The point $(12,0)$ means that **0% of the panel is illuminated** 12 hours after the cycle begins; it is not the maximum or the midline.
