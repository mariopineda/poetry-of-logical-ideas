A seasonal wildlife survey is modelled by $N(t)=a\cos[b(t-c)]+d$. A maximum of $175$ animals occurs in month $3$, and the next minimum of $95$ occurs in month $12$. Assuming $a>0$ and $b>0$, determine the amplitude $a$, period, and angular frequency $b$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The peak and next trough are $175$ and $95$. The amplitude is half the vertical spread:
>
> $$\boxed{a=\frac{175-95}{2}=40}.$$
>
> The next trough occurs half a cycle after the peak, so $T/2=12-3=9$ months and $\boxed{T=18\text{ months}}$. For the standard positive angular frequency,
>
> $$b=\frac{2\pi}{T}=\boxed{\frac\pi9\text{ radians/month}}.$$
>
> As a consistency check the midline is $d=(175+95)/2=135$, so $135\pm40$ gives the supplied extremes.
