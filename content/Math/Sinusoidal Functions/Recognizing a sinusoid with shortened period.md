Two signals are modelled by $f(x)=\sin(x-\pi/3)+1$ and $g(x)=\sin(3(x-\pi/3))+1$. Use the accompanying graph to identify which parameter changes from $f$ to $g$. Determine both periods and state which properties remain unchanged.

![[diploma-2017-new-sinusoid-frequency.svg]]

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Read the two formulas and confirm from the figure: both oscillate about $y=1$ with maximum $2$ and minimum $0$, so amplitude $1$ and midline $1$ are unchanged. The parent $f(x)=\sin(x-\pi/3)+1$ has $b=1$ and period $2\pi$. The transformed graph $g(x)=\sin(3(x-\pi/3))+1$ has $b=3$, so
>
> $$\boxed{T_f=2\pi,\qquad T_g=\frac{2\pi}{3}}.$$
>
> Only the frequency parameter changes, $\boxed{b:1\longrightarrow3}$. The phase-shift parameter remains $c=\pi/3$. The figure exhibits three cycles of $g$ for every one cycle of $f$.
