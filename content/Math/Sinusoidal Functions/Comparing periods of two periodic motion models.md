Two experimental pendulums begin $10^\circ$ to the right of vertical. Their angular displacements were measured over time and plotted below.

![[batch09-pendulum-periods.svg]]

Estimate the period of each pendulum **from the graphs**, and explain which pendulum swings more slowly. Describe how the equal heights and differing horizontal spacing of peaks relate to amplitude and period.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> A complete cycle runs from one positive peak to the next positive peak. From the graphs, Pendulum A repeats after approximately $\boxed{1.2\text{ s}}$; Pendulum B repeats after approximately $\boxed{1.6\text{ s}}$.
>
> Both have amplitude $\boxed{10^\circ}$, since the curves reach $+10^\circ$ and $-10^\circ$ from the equilibrium at $0^\circ$.
>
> **Pendulum B swings more slowly**, because each of its cycles takes longer. Equal amplitude does **not** imply equal period.
