## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Use the slope and one or two convenient points for each sketch.
>
> 1.
>
> $$
> y=-\frac27x+5
> $$
>
> has slope $-\frac27$ and $y$-intercept $(0,5)$. Another convenient point is $(7,3)$.
>
> 2.
>
> $$
> x=6
> $$
>
> is a vertical line through $x=6$.
>
> 3.
>
> $$
> y=-3
> $$
>
> is a horizontal line through $y=-3$.
>
> 4.
>
> $$
> y=4x-9
> $$
>
> has slope $4$ and $y$-intercept $(0,-9)$. Another convenient point is $(3,3)$.
