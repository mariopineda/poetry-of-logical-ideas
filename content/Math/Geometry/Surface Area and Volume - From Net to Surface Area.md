The diagram below is the **net of a 3D object**.

![[Surface Area and Volume - From Net to Surface Area.svg]]

1. Use a ruler to **measure the net to the nearest $0.1\text{ cm}$**.
2. **Label the dimensions on the net.**
3. Draw the **3D object** represented by the net and **label its dimensions**.
4. Calculate the **surface area** of the 3D object. Show your work and include units.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The net represents a **rectangular prism**.
>
> ![[Surface Area and Volume - From Net to Surface Area - Solution.svg]]
>
> The dimensions are:
>
> $$
> l=5.1\text{ cm},\qquad w=3.0\text{ cm},\qquad h=4.3\text{ cm}
> $$
>
> The surface area of a rectangular prism is:
>
> $$
> SA=2lw+2lh+2wh
> $$
>
> Substitute the measured dimensions:
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> SA
> &=2(5.1)(3.0)+2(5.1)(4.3)+2(3.0)(4.3)\\
> &=30.6+43.86+25.8\\
> &=100.26\text{ cm}^2
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> To the nearest tenth:
>
> $$
> \boxed{SA=100.3\text{ cm}^2}
> $$
