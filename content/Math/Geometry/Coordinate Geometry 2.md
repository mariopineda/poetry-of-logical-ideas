Determine the equation of the straight line that passes through the points $C(-6,5)$ and $D(2,-7)$. Answer in general form.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The slope is
>
> $$
> m
> =
> \frac{-7-5}{2-(-6)}
> =
> \frac{-12}{8}
> =
> -\frac32.
> $$
>
> Using point-slope form with $C(-6,5)$,
>
> $$
> y-5=-\frac32(x+6).
> $$
>
> Multiply by $2$:
>
> $$
> 2y-10=-3x-18.
> $$
>
> Rearranging to general form,
>
> $$
> \boxed{3x+2y+8=0}.
> $$
