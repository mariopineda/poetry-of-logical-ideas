A sensor model is given by

$$f(x)=\frac{(x-2)(x+5)}{(x-2)(x-4)}.$$

Identify every non-permissible value and determine which produces a **hole** and which produces a **vertical asymptote**. Give the equation of the asymptote and the coordinates of the hole.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The original denominator is zero at $x=2$ and $x=4$. Cancelling $x-2$ leaves $f(x)=\frac{x+5}{x-4}$ for $x\ne2,4$. The cancelled factor creates a hole at $x=2$ with height $\frac{2+5}{2-4}=-\frac72$; the remaining denominator creates the vertical asymptote $\boxed{x=4}$. Thus the hole is $\boxed{(2,-7/2)}$.
