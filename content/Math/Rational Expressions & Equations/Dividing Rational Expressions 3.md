## Question

Simplify each quotient and state all non-permissible values as restrictions on the variable.

### A.

$$
\frac{14m^3}{9}\div\frac{21m}{18}
$$

### B.

$$
\frac{m+7}{m-2}\div\frac{4m+28}{m+5}
$$

### C.

$$
\frac{m^2-49}{m+1}\div\frac{m-7}{m-3}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> The divisor cannot equal zero:
>
> $$
> \frac{21m}{18}\ne0,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> m\ne0.
> $$
>
> Multiply by the reciprocal:
>
> $$
> \frac{14m^3}{9}\div\frac{21m}{18}
> =
> \frac{14m^3}{9}\cdot\frac{18}{21m}
> =
> \frac{4m^2}{3}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{4m^2}{3}},\qquad \boxed{m\ne0}.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> The original denominators give
>
> $$
> m\ne2,\qquad m\ne-5.
> $$
>
> The divisor must also be nonzero:
>
> $$
> 4m+28=4(m+7)\ne0,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> m\ne-7.
> $$
>
> Factor and multiply by the reciprocal:
>
> $$
> \frac{m+7}{m-2}\div\frac{4(m+7)}{m+5}
> =
> \frac{m+7}{m-2}\cdot\frac{m+5}{4(m+7)}
> =
> \frac{m+5}{4(m-2)}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{m+5}{4(m-2)}},
> \qquad
> \boxed{m\ne-7,-5,2}.
> $$
>
> ### C.
>
> The original denominators give
>
> $$
> m\ne-1,\qquad m\ne3.
> $$
>
> The divisor must also be nonzero:
>
> $$
> m-7\ne0,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> m\ne7.
> $$
>
> Factor the difference of squares and multiply by the reciprocal:
>
> $$
> \frac{(m-7)(m+7)}{m+1}
> \div
> \frac{m-7}{m-3}
> =
> \frac{(m-7)(m+7)}{m+1}
> \cdot
> \frac{m-3}{m-7}
> =
> \frac{(m+7)(m-3)}{m+1}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{(m+7)(m-3)}{m+1}},
> \qquad
> \boxed{m\ne-1,3,7}.
> $$
