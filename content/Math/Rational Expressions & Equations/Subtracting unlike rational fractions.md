Subtract the two rational expressions and write the answer as a single simplified fraction:

$$\frac{2}{x+3}-\frac{1}{x-1}.$$

State all non-permissible values.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Using the common denominator $(x+3)(x-1)$ gives
>
> $$\frac{2(x-1)-(x+3)}{(x+3)(x-1)}=\boxed{\frac{x-5}{(x+3)(x-1)}}.$$
>
> The restrictions are $\boxed{x\ne-3,1}$.
