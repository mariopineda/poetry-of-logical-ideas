## Question

A farmer is planning to put a fence around a rectangular field. The area of the field, in square metres, can be represented by the expression $18a^2-2$, and the width of the field is $3a+1$ metres.

1. Write and simplify an expression for the length of the field. State any restriction on $a$ that comes from the original rational expression.
2. If the farmer requires $88$ m of fencing, determine the value of $a$ and the length and width of the field.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1. Determine the length
>
> Since
>
> $$
> \text{Area}=(\text{length})(\text{width}),
> $$
>
> the length is
>
> $$
> L=\frac{18a^2-2}{3a+1}.
> $$
>
> The original denominator cannot equal zero:
>
> $$
> 3a+1\ne0,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> a\ne-\frac13.
> $$
>
> Factor the numerator:
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> 18a^2-2
> &=2(9a^2-1)\\
> &=2(3a-1)(3a+1).
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> L
> &=\frac{2(3a-1)(3a+1)}{3a+1}\\
> &=2(3a-1)\\
> &=6a-2.
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \boxed{L=6a-2},\qquad
> \boxed{a\ne-\frac13}.
> $$
>
> In the physical context, both dimensions must also be positive.
>
> ### 2. Use the perimeter
>
> The width is
>
> $$
> W=3a+1
> $$
>
> and the length is
>
> $$
> L=6a-2.
> $$
>
> A rectangle with perimeter $88$ m satisfies
>
> $$
> 2L+2W=88.
> $$
>
> Substitute:
>
> $$
> 2(6a-2)+2(3a+1)=88.
> $$
>
> Simplify:
>
> $$
> 12a-4+6a+2=88
> $$
>
> $$
> 18a-2=88
> $$
>
> $$
> 18a=90
> $$
>
> $$
> a=5.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> W=3(5)+1=16\text{ m}
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> L=6(5)-2=28\text{ m}.
> $$
>
> Check:
>
> $$
> 2(28)+2(16)=88.
> $$
>
> Hence,
>
> $$
> \boxed{a=5,\qquad L=28\text{ m},\qquad W=16\text{ m}}.
> $$
