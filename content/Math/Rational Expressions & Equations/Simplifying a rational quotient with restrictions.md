Simplify the rational expression

$$\frac{x^2-9}{x^2+2x-15}$$

and state **every restriction inherited from the original expression**, even if a factor cancels.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Factor first:
>
> $$\frac{(x-3)(x+3)}{(x+5)(x-3)}.$$
>
> The original denominator vanishes at $x=3$ and $x=-5$. For permissible $x$, cancel $x-3$:
>
> $$\boxed{\frac{x+3}{x+5}},\qquad \boxed{x\ne 3,-5}.$$
>
> Cancellation cannot restore the original value at $x=3$.
