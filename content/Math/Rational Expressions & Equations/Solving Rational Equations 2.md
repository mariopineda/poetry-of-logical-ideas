Solve the following equations.

#### A.

$$
x^2-10x+24=0
$$

#### B.

$$
x^2-10x-24=0
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> $$
> x^2-10x+24=0
> $$
>
> factors as
>
> $$
> (x-4)(x-6)=0.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=4,\ 6}.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> $$
> x^2-10x-24=0
> $$
>
> factors as
>
> $$
> (x-12)(x+2)=0.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=-2,\ 12}.
> $$
