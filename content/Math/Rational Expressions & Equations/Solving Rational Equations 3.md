Solve the following equations.

#### A.

$$
3x^2-13x-10=0
$$

#### B.

$$
6x^2+17x=3
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A. $3x^2-13x-10=0$
>
> **Decomposition ($a\ne1$).**
>
> Identify the numerical coefficients $a=3$, $b=-13$, and $c=-10$. Thus $ac=-30$; we need two numbers with product $-30$ and sum $-13$. Since $ac$ is negative, the numbers have opposite signs.
>
> - **Sequential division:** Test divisors $1,2,3,\ldots$ of $|ac|=30$ until a factor pair has the required difference: $30\div1=30$ (difference $29$); $30\div2=15$ (difference $13$, **match**).
> - **Square-root-guided search:** $\sqrt{30}\approx 5.48$, so the possible smaller divisors end at $5$. Start at $5$ and work downward: $30\div5=6$ (difference $1$); $30\div4$ is not a whole number; $30\div3=10$ (difference $7$); $30\div2=15$ (difference $13$, **match**).
>
> The required numbers are $m=-15$ and $n=2$: $-15+(2)=-13$ and $(-15)(2)=-30$.
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> 3x^2-13x-10&=3x^2-15x+2x-10\\
> &=3x(x-5)+2(x-5)\\
> &=(3x+2)(x-5)=0\\
> \implies\quad &\boxed{x=-\tfrac23,\;5}.
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> ### B. $6x^2+17x=3$
>
> First rewrite in standard form: $6x^2+17x-3=0$.
>
> **Decomposition ($a\ne1$).**
>
> Identify the numerical coefficients $a=6$, $b=17$, and $c=-3$. Thus $ac=-18$; we need two numbers with product $-18$ and sum $17$. Since $ac$ is negative, the numbers have opposite signs.
>
> - **Sequential division:** Test divisors $1,2,3,\ldots$ of $|ac|=18$ until a factor pair has the required difference: $18\div1=18$ (difference $17$, **match**).
> - **Square-root-guided search:** $\sqrt{18}\approx 4.24$, so the possible smaller divisors end at $4$. Start at $4$ and work downward: $18\div4$ is not a whole number; $18\div3=6$ (difference $3$); $18\div2=9$ (difference $7$); $18\div1=18$ (difference $17$, **match**).
>
> The required numbers are $m=18$ and $n=-1$: $18+(-1)=17$ and $(18)(-1)=-18$.
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> 6x^2+17x-3&=6x^2+18x-x-3\\
> &=6x(x+3)-1(x+3)\\
> &=(6x-1)(x+3)=0\\
> \implies\quad &\boxed{x=\tfrac16,\;-3}.
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
