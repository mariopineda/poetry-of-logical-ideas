Solve the following equations.

#### A.

$$
3x^2-13x-10=0
$$

#### B.

$$
6x^2+17x=3
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> $$
> 3x^2-13x-10=0
> $$
>
> factors as
>
> $$
> (3x+2)(x-5)=0.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=-\frac23,\ 5}.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> Move all terms to one side:
>
> $$
> 6x^2+17x-3=0.
> $$
>
> Factor:
>
> $$
> (6x-1)(x+3)=0.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=\frac16,\ -3}.
> $$
