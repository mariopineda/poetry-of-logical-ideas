A designer wants the product

$$\frac{A}{B}\cdot\frac{4p^3}{12q^2}$$

to simplify to $\dfrac{p}{q}$, where $p\ne0$ and $q\ne0$. Possible choices are:

| Expression for $A$ | Expression for $B$ |
|:---:|:---:|
| $3q$ | $p^2$ |
| $6q^2$ | $2p^2q$ |
| $2q$ | $2p^2$ |
| $9q$ | $3p^2$ |

Any option from the left column may be paired with any option from the right column. **List all valid pairs $(A,B)$**, and justify algebraically why they work. Explain why the other choices fail.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Since $\frac{4p^3}{12q^2}=\frac{p^3}{3q^2}$, the required ratio is
>
> $$\frac{A}{B}=\frac{p/q}{p^3/(3q^2)}=\frac{3q}{p^2}.$$
>
> The **three valid pairs** are
>
> $$\boxed{(3q,\ p^2),\quad(6q^2,\ 2p^2q),\quad(9q,\ 3p^2)}.$$
>
> Their ratios all equal $3q/p^2$. The fourth available numerator, $2q$, divided by any listed denominator cannot give that ratio for all nonzero $p,q$. No other cross-pair has the required constant coefficient and power of $q$.
