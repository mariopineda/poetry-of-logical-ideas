## Question

In each case, write a rational expression with the given variable and non-permissible value.

1. variable is $x$, non-permissible value is $0$
2. variable is $b$, non-permissible value is $1$
3. variable is $a$, non-permissible value is $-8$
4. variable is $m$, non-permissible value is $\frac{3}{4}$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> A non-permissible value is a value that makes the denominator equal to zero. Many answers are possible; one simple choice in each case is:
>
> ### 1. Non-permissible value \(x=0\)
>
> Choose a denominator that is zero when \(x=0\):
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{1}{x}}
> $$
>
> Since the denominator is zero at \(x=0\),
>
> $$
> \boxed{x\ne0}.
> $$
>
> ### 2. Non-permissible value \(b=1\)
>
> Choose
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{1}{b-1}}.
> $$
>
> Since
>
> $$
> b-1=0
> $$
>
> when \(b=1\),
>
> $$
> \boxed{b\ne1}.
> $$
>
> ### 3. Non-permissible value \(a=-8\)
>
> Choose
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{1}{a+8}}.
> $$
>
> Since
>
> $$
> a+8=0
> $$
>
> when \(a=-8\),
>
> $$
> \boxed{a\ne-8}.
> $$
>
> ### 4. Non-permissible value \(m=\frac34\)
>
> Choose
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{1}{4m-3}}.
> $$
>
> The denominator is zero when
>
> $$
> 4m-3=0
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> m=\frac34.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{m\ne\frac34}.
> $$
>
