## Question

Simplify and state the non-permissible value as restrictions on the variable

#### A.

$$\frac{3b+9}{b} \div \frac{b+3}{b}$$

#### B.

$$\frac{6a-1}{3a} \div \frac{24a-4}{9a^3}$$

#### C.

$$\frac{r+8}{8r} \div (64-r^2)$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> The original denominator requires
>
> $$
> b\ne0.
> $$
>
> Because the second rational expression is the divisor, it also cannot equal zero:
>
> $$
> b+3\ne0
> \quad\Rightarrow\quad
> b\ne-3.
> $$
>
> Multiply by the reciprocal:
>
> $$
> \frac{3b+9}{b}\div\frac{b+3}{b}
> =
> \frac{3(b+3)}{b}\times\frac{b}{b+3}
> =
> \boxed{3}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{3},
> \qquad
> \boxed{b\ne0,-3}.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> The original denominators require
>
> $$
> a\ne0.
> $$
>
> The divisor cannot equal zero, so
>
> $$
> 24a-4\ne0
> \quad\Rightarrow\quad
> a\ne\frac16.
> $$
>
> Factor \(24a-4=4(6a-1)\), then multiply by the reciprocal:
>
> $$
> \frac{6a-1}{3a}
> \times
> \frac{9a^3}{4(6a-1)}
> =
> \boxed{\frac{3a^2}{4}}.
> $$
>
> Hence,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{3a^2}{4}},
> \qquad
> \boxed{a\ne0,\frac16}.
> $$
>
> ### C.
>
> The first denominator requires
>
> $$
> r\ne0.
> $$
>
> The divisor cannot equal zero:
>
> $$
> 64-r^2\ne0
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> r\ne8,\qquad r\ne-8.
> $$
>
> Factor the difference of squares:
>
> $$
> 64-r^2=(8-r)(8+r).
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> \frac{r+8}{8r}\div(64-r^2)
> =
> \frac{r+8}{8r(8-r)(8+r)}
> =
> \boxed{\frac{1}{8r(8-r)}}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{1}{8r(8-r)}},
> \qquad
> \boxed{r\ne-8,0,8}.
> $$
