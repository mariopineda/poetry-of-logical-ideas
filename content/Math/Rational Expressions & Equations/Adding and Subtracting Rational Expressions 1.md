## Question

Simplify. Express answers in lowest terms and indicate non-permissible values as restriction on the variable.

1.

$$
\frac{2}{y}+\frac{3}{y}+\frac{4}{y}
$$
2.

$$
\frac{1}{2x}+\frac{3}{2x}-\frac{5}{2x}
$$
3.

$$
\frac{7y}{3y+8}+\frac{4y}{3y+8}
$$
4.

$$
\frac{a+2}{a^2}-\frac{2-a}{a^2}
$$
5.

$$
\frac{4b+1}{b+3}-\frac{2b-5}{3+b}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> The denominators are already the same:
>
> $$
> \frac{2}{y}+\frac{3}{y}+\frac{4}{y}
> =
> \frac{2+3+4}{y}
> =
> \boxed{\frac{9}{y}},
> \qquad
> \boxed{y\ne0}.
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> $$
> \frac{1}{2x}+\frac{3}{2x}-\frac{5}{2x}
> =
> \frac{1+3-5}{2x}
> =
> \boxed{-\frac{1}{2x}},
> \qquad
> \boxed{x\ne0}.
> $$
>
> ### 3.
>
> The common denominator is \(3y+8\):
>
> $$
> \frac{7y}{3y+8}+\frac{4y}{3y+8}
> =
> \boxed{\frac{11y}{3y+8}}.
> $$
>
> The denominator cannot equal zero:
>
> $$
> 3y+8\ne0
> \quad\Rightarrow\quad
> \boxed{y\ne-\frac83}.
> $$
>
> ### 4.
>
> $$
> \frac{a+2}{a^2}-\frac{2-a}{a^2}
> =
> \frac{a+2-(2-a)}{a^2}
> =
> \frac{2a}{a^2}
> =
> \boxed{\frac{2}{a}},
> \qquad
> \boxed{a\ne0}.
> $$
>
> ### 5.
>
> Since \(3+b=b+3\),
>
> $$
> \frac{4b+1}{b+3}-\frac{2b-5}{3+b}
> =
> \frac{4b+1-(2b-5)}{b+3}.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \frac{2b+6}{b+3}
> =
> \frac{2(b+3)}{b+3}
> =
> \boxed{2},
> \qquad
> \boxed{b\ne-3}.
> $$
>
> The restriction remains even though the factor \(b+3\) cancels.
>
