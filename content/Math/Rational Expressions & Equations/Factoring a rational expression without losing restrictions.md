The rational expression

$$\frac{4x-5}{x+4},\qquad x\ne -4,0,$$

is rewritten by multiplying the numerator and denominator by the **same monomial** in this form:

$$\frac{Ax^B-10x}{Cx^2+Dx}.$$

The coefficients $A,B,C,D$ are single-digit whole numbers. **Determine all four coefficients**, show the equivalent-expression step, and state the restrictions inherited from the first expression.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The term $-5$ must become $-10x$, so multiply numerator and denominator by $2x$:
>
> $$\frac{4x-5}{x+4}\cdot\frac{2x}{2x}=\frac{8x^2-10x}{2x^2+8x}.$$
>
> Hence $\boxed{A=8,\ B=2,\ C=2,\ D=8}$. The original restriction is $x\ne-4$; the expanded expression also has $x\ne0$. Both are excluded, as stated: $\boxed{x\ne-4,0}$.
