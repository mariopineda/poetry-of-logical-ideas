An underwater robotics trial uses the model

$$t=\frac{1800}{d-20},\qquad d>20,$$

where $d$ is the maximum depth in metres and $t$ is the maximum safe deployment time in minutes. Determine the depth corresponding to a $60$-minute deployment. Show the algebra.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Substitute $t=60$:
>
> $$60=\frac{1800}{d-20}.$$
>
> Multiplying by $d-20$ and dividing by $60$ gives $d-20=30$, so $\boxed{d=50\text{ m}}$. This satisfies $d>20$.
