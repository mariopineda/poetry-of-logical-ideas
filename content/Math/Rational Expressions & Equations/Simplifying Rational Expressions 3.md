## Question

1. $\displaystyle\frac{4a^2}{16a}$
2. $\displaystyle\frac{25y^4}{10y^9}$
3. $\displaystyle\frac{(a+3)(a-8)}{(a+1)(a-8)}$
4. $\displaystyle\frac{(x+7)(x-2)}{x(x-2)(x+14)}$
