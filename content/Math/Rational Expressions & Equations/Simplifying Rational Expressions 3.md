Express the following rational expressions in simplest form, stating the non-permissible values as restrictions.


1.

$$
\frac{4a^2}{16a}
$$
2.

$$
\frac{25y^4}{10y^9}
$$
3.

$$
\frac{(a+3)(a-8)}{(a+1)(a-8)}
$$
4.

$$
\frac{(x+7)(x-2)}{x(x-2)(x+14)}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> The denominator cannot equal zero, so
>
> $$
> a\ne0.
> $$
>
> Simplify:
>
> $$
> \frac{4a^2}{16a}
> =\frac{a}{4}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{a}{4}},\qquad \boxed{a\ne0}.
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> The denominator cannot equal zero, so
>
> $$
> y\ne0.
> $$
>
> Simplify the numerical coefficients and subtract exponents:
>
> $$
> \frac{25y^4}{10y^9}
> =\frac{5}{2}y^{-5}
> =\frac{5}{2y^5}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{5}{2y^5}},\qquad \boxed{y\ne0}.
> $$
>
> ### 3.
>
> From the original denominator,
>
> $$
> (a+1)(a-8)\ne0,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> a\ne-1,\qquad a\ne8.
> $$
>
> Cancel the common factor:
>
> $$
> \frac{(a+3)(a-8)}{(a+1)(a-8)}
> =\frac{a+3}{a+1}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{a+3}{a+1}},\qquad \boxed{a\ne-1,8}.
> $$
>
> ### 4.
>
> From the original denominator,
>
> $$
> x(x-2)(x+14)\ne0,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> x\ne0,\qquad x\ne2,\qquad x\ne-14.
> $$
>
> Cancel the common factor:
>
> $$
> \frac{(x+7)(x-2)}{x(x-2)(x+14)}
> =\frac{x+7}{x(x+14)}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{x+7}{x(x+14)}},\qquad
> \boxed{x\ne-14,0,2}.
> $$
