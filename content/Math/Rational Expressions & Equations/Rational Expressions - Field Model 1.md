A rectangular field has area

$$
A(x)=12x^2-27
$$

square metres and width

$$
W(x)=2x+3
$$

metres.

1. Write the length as a rational expression, simplify it, and state the restriction from the original expression.
2. Determine the **contextual domain** for $x$ if both dimensions must be positive.
3. The farmer wants the length to be $\frac95$ times the width. Determine $x$ and the dimensions of the field.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The length is
>
> $$
> L(x)=\frac{12x^2-27}{2x+3}.
> $$
>
> The original denominator gives
>
> $$
> x\ne-\frac32.
> $$
>
> Factor the numerator:
>
> $$
> 12x^2-27=3(4x^2-9)=3(2x-3)(2x+3).
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> L(x)=3(2x-3)=6x-9,
> $$
>
> with
>
> $$
> \boxed{x\ne-\frac32}.
> $$
>
> For the physical field,
>
> $$
> 2x+3>0
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> 6x-9>0.
> $$
>
> These give
>
> $$
> x>-\frac32
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> x>\frac32.
> $$
>
> Hence the contextual domain is
>
> $$
> \boxed{x>\frac32}.
> $$
>
> The condition on the dimensions is
>
> $$
> L=\frac95W.
> $$
>
> Substitute:
>
> $$
> 6x-9=\frac95(2x+3).
> $$
>
> Multiply by $5$:
>
> $$
> 30x-45=18x+27.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> 12x=72
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=6}.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> W=2(6)+3=15\text{ m}
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> L=6(6)-9=27\text{ m}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{W=15\text{ m},\qquad L=27\text{ m}}.
> $$
