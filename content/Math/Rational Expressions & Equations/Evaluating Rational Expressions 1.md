## Question

Evaluate without a calculator and express in lowest terms.

1. $$(-12)(-2) \times [-4+(-3)(-1)]$$
2. $$\frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{2}{3}-\frac{5}{4} \right)$$
3. $$15 \div [(-4)(-4)+(-11)]$$
4. $$ 2+\frac{5}{8} \div \frac{1}{4}-\left( -\frac{1}{3} \right)$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> 1. $$(-12)(-2) \times [-4+(-3)(-1)] = 24 \times [-4+3] = 24 \times (-1) = -24$$
> 2. $$\frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{2}{3}-\frac{5}{4} \right) = \frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{8}{12}-\frac{15}{12} \right) = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{-7}{12} = -\frac{7}{24}$$
> 3. $$15 \div [(-4)(-4)+(-11)] = 15 \div [16-11] = 15 \div 5 = 3$$
> 4. $$ 2+\frac{5}{8} \div \frac{1}{4}-\left( -\frac{1}{3} \right)$$
>    $$ = 2+\frac{5}{8} \div \frac{1}{4}+\frac{1}{3}$$
>    $$ = 2+\frac{5}{8} \times \frac{4}{1}+\frac{1}{3}$$
>    $$ = 2+\frac{5}{2} \times \frac{1}{1}+\frac{1}{3}$$
>    $$ = 2+\frac{5}{2} + \frac{1}{3}$$
>    $$ = \frac{12}{6} + \frac{15}{6} + \frac{2}{6} = \frac{29}{6}$$
>
