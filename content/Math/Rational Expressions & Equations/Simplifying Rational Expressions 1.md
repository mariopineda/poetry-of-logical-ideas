## Question

Express each rational expression in simplest form. State all non-permissible values as restrictions on the variable.

1.

$$
\displaystyle\frac{18a^4}{24a^2}
$$
2.

$$
\displaystyle\frac{(x+5)(x-2)}{(x+1)(x-2)}
$$
3.

$$
\displaystyle\frac{3m+6}{6m}
$$
4.

$$
\displaystyle\frac{y^2-16}{(y-4)(y+2)}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> The original denominator cannot equal zero:
>
> $$
> 24a^2\ne0
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> a\ne0.
> $$
>
> Simplify:
>
> $$
> \frac{18a^4}{24a^2}
> =\frac{3a^2}{4}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{3a^2}{4}},\qquad \boxed{a\ne0}.
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> From the original denominator,
>
> $$
> (x+1)(x-2)\ne0,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> x\ne-1,\qquad x\ne2.
> $$
>
> Cancel the common factor:
>
> $$
> \frac{(x+5)(x-2)}{(x+1)(x-2)}
> =\frac{x+5}{x+1}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{x+5}{x+1}},\qquad \boxed{x\ne-1,2}.
> $$
>
> ### 3.
>
> The original denominator gives
>
> $$
> m\ne0.
> $$
>
> Factor the numerator and denominator:
>
> $$
> \frac{3m+6}{6m}
> =\frac{3(m+2)}{6m}
> =\frac{m+2}{2m}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{m+2}{2m}},\qquad \boxed{m\ne0}.
> $$
>
> ### 4.
>
> From the original denominator,
>
> $$
> (y-4)(y+2)\ne0,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> y\ne4,\qquad y\ne-2.
> $$
>
> Factor the numerator as a difference of squares:
>
> $$
> y^2-16=(y-4)(y+4).
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> \frac{(y-4)(y+4)}{(y-4)(y+2)}
> =\frac{y+4}{y+2}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{y+4}{y+2}},\qquad \boxed{y\ne-2,4}.
> $$
