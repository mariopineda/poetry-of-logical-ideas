## Question

1. $$\frac{6x}{(x-4)^2} - \frac{2}{x-4}$$
2. $$\frac{3}{x-7} - \frac{4}{x^2-49}$$
3. $$\frac{4}{x-3} - \frac{2}{3-x}$$
4. $$\frac{7x(x-3)}{(x+7)(x+2)} \div \frac{21x(x-3)}{(x+7)^2}$$
5. $$\left( 6-\frac{2}{x} \right) \div \left( 9 - \frac{1}{x^2} \right)$$
