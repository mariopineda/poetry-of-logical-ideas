Simplify each sum or difference. State all non-permissible values as restrictions on the variable.

### A.

$$
\frac{2}{a}+\frac{5}{a-6}
$$

### B.

$$
\frac{5a+1}{a-3}-\frac{2a-4}{a-3}
$$

### C.

$$
\frac{6}{p-5}-\frac{4}{p+2}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> $$
> a\ne0,\qquad a\ne6
> $$
>
> $$
> \frac{2}{a}+\frac{5}{a-6}
> =
> \frac{2(a-6)+5a}{a(a-6)}
> =
> \frac{7a-12}{a(a-6)}.
> $$
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{7a-12}{a(a-6)}},
> \qquad
> \boxed{a\ne0,6}.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> $$
> a\ne3
> $$
>
> $$
> \frac{5a+1}{a-3}-\frac{2a-4}{a-3}
> =
> \frac{(5a+1)-(2a-4)}{a-3}
> =
> \frac{3a+5}{a-3}.
> $$
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{3a+5}{a-3}},
> \qquad
> \boxed{a\ne3}.
> $$
>
> ### C.
>
> $$
> p\ne5,\qquad p\ne-2
> $$
>
> $$
> \frac{6}{p-5}-\frac{4}{p+2}
> =
> \frac{6(p+2)-4(p-5)}{(p-5)(p+2)}
> =
> \frac{2(p+16)}{(p-5)(p+2)}.
> $$
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{2(p+16)}{(p-5)(p+2)}},
> \qquad
> \boxed{p\ne-2,5}.
> $$
