Simplify each sum or difference. State all non-permissible values as restrictions on the variable.

### A.

$$
\frac{5}{y}+\frac{2}{y-3}
$$

### B.

$$
\frac{4y-1}{y+2}-\frac{y-3}{y+2}
$$

### C.

$$
\frac{7}{m-4}-\frac{2}{m+1}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> $$
> y\ne0,\qquad y\ne3
> $$
>
> $$
> \frac{5}{y}+\frac{2}{y-3}
> =
> \frac{5(y-3)+2y}{y(y-3)}
> =
> \frac{7y-15}{y(y-3)}.
> $$
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{7y-15}{y(y-3)}},
> \qquad
> \boxed{y\ne0,3}.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> $$
> y\ne-2
> $$
>
> $$
> \frac{4y-1}{y+2}-\frac{y-3}{y+2}
> =
> \frac{(4y-1)-(y-3)}{y+2}
> =
> \frac{3y+2}{y+2}.
> $$
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{3y+2}{y+2}},
> \qquad
> \boxed{y\ne-2}.
> $$
>
> ### C.
>
> $$
> m\ne4,\qquad m\ne-1
> $$
>
> $$
> \frac{7}{m-4}-\frac{2}{m+1}
> =
> \frac{7(m+1)-2(m-4)}{(m-4)(m+1)}
> =
> \frac{5(m+3)}{(m-4)(m+1)}.
> $$
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{5(m+3)}{(m-4)(m+1)}},
> \qquad
> \boxed{m\ne-1,4}.
> $$
