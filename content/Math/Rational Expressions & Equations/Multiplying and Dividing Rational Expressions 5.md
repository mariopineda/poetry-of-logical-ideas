## Question

Simplify and state the non-permissible value as restrictions on the variable

#### A.

$$\frac{p^3(p+2)}{p} \div \frac{49(p+2)}{7(p-5)}$$

#### B.

$$\left( \frac{10x+4}{5x-1} \right) \div \left( \frac{35x+14}{x-1} \right)$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> The first denominator requires
>
> $$
> p\ne0.
> $$
>
> The second denominator requires
>
> $$
> p\ne5.
> $$
>
> Because the second rational expression is the divisor, its numerator cannot equal zero:
>
> $$
> 49(p+2)\ne0
> \quad\Rightarrow\quad
> p\ne-2.
> $$
>
> Now divide by multiplying by the reciprocal:
>
> $$
> \frac{p^3(p+2)}{p}
> \div
> \frac{49(p+2)}{7(p-5)}
> =
> p^2(p+2)
> \times
> \frac{7(p-5)}{49(p+2)}.
> $$
>
> Cancel \(p+2\) and simplify:
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{p^2(p-5)}{7}},
> \qquad
> \boxed{p\ne-2,0,5}.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> The original denominators require
>
> $$
> 5x-1\ne0
> \quad\Rightarrow\quad
> x\ne\frac15,
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> x-1\ne0
> \quad\Rightarrow\quad
> x\ne1.
> $$
>
> The divisor must also be non-zero:
>
> $$
> 35x+14=7(5x+2)\ne0
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> x\ne-\frac25.
> $$
>
> Factor and multiply by the reciprocal:
>
> $$
> \frac{2(5x+2)}{5x-1}
> \times
> \frac{x-1}{7(5x+2)}
> =
> \boxed{\frac{2(x-1)}{7(5x-1)}}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{2(x-1)}{7(5x-1)}},
> \qquad
> \boxed{x\ne-\frac25,\frac15,1}.
> $$
