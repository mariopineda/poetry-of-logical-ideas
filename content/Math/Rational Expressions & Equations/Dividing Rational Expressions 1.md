## Question

Simplify each quotient and state all non-permissible values as restrictions on the variable.

### A.

$$
\frac{6x^3}{5}\div\frac{9x}{10}
$$

### B.

$$
\frac{x+4}{x-3}\div\frac{2x+8}{x+1}
$$

### C.

$$
\frac{x^2-25}{x+2}\div\frac{x-5}{x-4}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> The divisor cannot equal zero:
>
> $$
> \frac{9x}{10}\ne0,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> x\ne0.
> $$
>
> Multiply by the reciprocal:
>
> $$
> \frac{6x^3}{5}\div\frac{9x}{10}
> =
> \frac{6x^3}{5}\cdot\frac{10}{9x}
> =
> \frac{4x^2}{3}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{4x^2}{3}},\qquad \boxed{x\ne0}.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> The original denominators give
>
> $$
> x\ne3,\qquad x\ne-1.
> $$
>
> The divisor must also be nonzero:
>
> $$
> 2x+8=2(x+4)\ne0,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> x\ne-4.
> $$
>
> Factor and multiply by the reciprocal:
>
> $$
> \frac{x+4}{x-3}\div\frac{2(x+4)}{x+1}
> =
> \frac{x+4}{x-3}\cdot\frac{x+1}{2(x+4)}
> =
> \frac{x+1}{2(x-3)}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{x+1}{2(x-3)}},
> \qquad
> \boxed{x\ne-4,-1,3}.
> $$
>
> ### C.
>
> The original denominators give
>
> $$
> x\ne-2,\qquad x\ne4.
> $$
>
> The divisor must also be nonzero:
>
> $$
> x-5\ne0,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> x\ne5.
> $$
>
> Factor the difference of squares and multiply by the reciprocal:
>
> $$
> \frac{(x-5)(x+5)}{x+2}
> \div
> \frac{x-5}{x-4}
> =
> \frac{(x-5)(x+5)}{x+2}
> \cdot
> \frac{x-4}{x-5}
> =
> \frac{(x+5)(x-4)}{x+2}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{(x+5)(x-4)}{x+2}},
> \qquad
> \boxed{x\ne-2,4,5}.
> $$
