## Question

Simplify each sum or difference. State all non-permissible values as restrictions on the variable.

### A.

$$
\frac{4}{x}+\frac{3}{x-5}
$$

### B.

$$
\frac{3x+2}{x-4}-\frac{x-5}{x-4}
$$

### C.

$$
\frac{5}{n-2}-\frac{3}{n+4}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> $$
> x\ne0,\qquad x\ne5
> $$
>
> $$
> \frac{4}{x}+\frac{3}{x-5}
> =
> \frac{4(x-5)+3x}{x(x-5)}
> =
> \frac{7x-20}{x(x-5)}.
> $$
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{7x-20}{x(x-5)}},
> \qquad
> \boxed{x\ne0,5}.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> $$
> x\ne4
> $$
>
> $$
> \frac{3x+2}{x-4}-\frac{x-5}{x-4}
> =
> \frac{(3x+2)-(x-5)}{x-4}
> =
> \frac{2x+7}{x-4}.
> $$
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{2x+7}{x-4}},
> \qquad
> \boxed{x\ne4}.
> $$
>
> ### C.
>
> $$
> n\ne2,\qquad n\ne-4
> $$
>
> $$
> \frac{5}{n-2}-\frac{3}{n+4}
> =
> \frac{5(n+4)-3(n-2)}{(n-2)(n+4)}
> =
> \frac{2(n+13)}{(n-2)(n+4)}.
> $$
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{2(n+13)}{(n-2)(n+4)}},
> \qquad
> \boxed{n\ne-4,2}.
> $$
