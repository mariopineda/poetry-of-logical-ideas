Evaluate and simplify without a calculator.

A.

$$
\frac{\displaystyle 2x}{\displaystyle 7} + \frac{\displaystyle 2x}{\displaystyle 4} - \frac{\displaystyle x}{\displaystyle 2}
$$
B.

$$
\frac{\displaystyle 5x}{\displaystyle 6} - \frac{\displaystyle 2x}{\displaystyle 12x}
$$
C.

$$
\frac{\displaystyle 5x^2}{\displaystyle 6} \times \frac{\displaystyle 3}{\displaystyle 10x}
$$
D.

$$
\frac{\displaystyle 2x^2}{\displaystyle 9x} \div \frac{\displaystyle x}{\displaystyle 3x^3}
$$
E.

$$
\frac{\displaystyle 4x}{\displaystyle 9x}-\frac{\displaystyle 2x^2}{\displaystyle 6x^2}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> $$
> \frac{2x}{7}+\frac{2x}{4}-\frac{x}{2}
> =
> \frac{2x}{7}+\frac{x}{2}-\frac{x}{2}
> =
> \boxed{\frac{2x}{7}}.
> $$
>
> There are no variable restrictions because all denominators are non-zero constants.
>
> ### 2.
>
> From the original denominator,
>
> $$
> 12x\ne0,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> x\ne0.
> $$
>
> For \(x\ne0\),
>
> $$
> \frac{2x}{12x}=\frac16.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \frac{5x}{6}-\frac{2x}{12x}
> =
> \frac{5x}{6}-\frac16
> =
> \boxed{\frac{5x-1}{6}},
> \qquad
> \boxed{x\ne0}.
> $$
>
> ### 3.
>
> The denominator \(10x\) gives
>
> $$
> x\ne0.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> \frac{5x^2}{6}\times\frac{3}{10x}
> =
> \frac{15x^2}{60x}
> =
> \boxed{\frac{x}{4}},
> \qquad
> \boxed{x\ne0}.
> $$
>
> ### 4.
>
> The original denominators require
>
> $$
> x\ne0.
> $$
>
> Divide by multiplying by the reciprocal:
>
> $$
> \frac{2x^2}{9x}\div\frac{x}{3x^3}
> =
> \frac{2x^2}{9x}\times\frac{3x^3}{x}.
> $$
>
> Simplifying,
>
> $$
> \frac{6x^5}{9x^2}
> =
> \boxed{\frac{2x^3}{3}},
> \qquad
> \boxed{x\ne0}.
> $$
>
> ### 5.
>
> The original denominators require
>
> $$
> x\ne0.
> $$
>
> Simplify each quotient:
>
> $$
> \frac{4x}{9x}-\frac{2x^2}{6x^2}
> =
> \frac49-\frac13
> =
> \frac49-\frac39
> =
> \boxed{\frac19},
> \qquad
> \boxed{x\ne0}.
> $$
