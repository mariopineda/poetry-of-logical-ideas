## Question

Simplify and state the non-permissible value as restrictions on the variable

#### A.

$$
\frac{x^2-9}{6x+24} \times \frac{10x+40}{x(x+3)}
$$

#### B.

$$
\left( \frac{x+3}{3x} \right) \left( \frac{6x}{x^2+9} \right)
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> Factor:
>
> $$
> x^2-9=(x-3)(x+3),
> $$
>
> $$
> 6x+24=6(x+4),
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> 10x+40=10(x+4).
> $$
>
> From the original denominators,
>
> $$
> x\ne-4,\qquad x\ne0,\qquad x\ne-3.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> \frac{(x-3)(x+3)}{6(x+4)}
> \times
> \frac{10(x+4)}{x(x+3)}
> =
> \boxed{\frac{5(x-3)}{3x}}.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{5(x-3)}{3x}},
> \qquad
> \boxed{x\ne-4,-3,0}.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> The original denominator \(3x\) requires
>
> $$
> x\ne0.
> $$
>
> Over the real numbers,
>
> $$
> x^2+9>0,
> $$
>
> so it creates no additional restriction.
>
> Multiply and cancel:
>
> $$
> \frac{x+3}{3x}\times\frac{6x}{x^2+9}
> =
> \boxed{\frac{2(x+3)}{x^2+9}},
> \qquad
> \boxed{x\ne0}.
> $$
>
