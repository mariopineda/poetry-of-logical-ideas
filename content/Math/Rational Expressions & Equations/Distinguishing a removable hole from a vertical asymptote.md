A signal model is $R(x)=\dfrac{(x-5)(x+2)}{(x-5)(2x+3)}$. Find the vertical asymptote and the removable discontinuity. State the original domain restrictions, and explain why the two discontinuities differ.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The original denominator vanishes at $x=5$ and $x=-\tfrac32$. For $x\ne5$, $R(x)=\dfrac{x+2}{2x+3}$. The factor $x-5$ cancels, so $x=5$ is a hole; its height would be $(5+2)/(10+3)=7/13$. The uncancelled denominator gives the vertical asymptote $\boxed{x=-\tfrac32}$. Original restrictions are $\boxed{x\ne5,-\tfrac32}$.
