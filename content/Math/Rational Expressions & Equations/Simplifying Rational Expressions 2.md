## Question

Express each rational expression in simplest form. State all non-permissible values as restrictions on the variable.

1. $$\displaystyle\frac{(a+1)(a-6)}{(a+7)(a+1)}$$
2. $$\displaystyle\frac{8y+2}{8y}$$
3. $$\displaystyle\frac{3t^2-75}{(t+3)(t-5)}$$
4. $$\displaystyle\frac{c-4}{4-c}$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> From the original denominator,
>
> $$
> (a+7)(a+1)\ne0,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> a\ne-7,\qquad a\ne-1.
> $$
>
> Cancel the common factor:
>
> $$
> \frac{(a+1)(a-6)}{(a+7)(a+1)}
> =\frac{a-6}{a+7}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{a-6}{a+7}},\qquad \boxed{a\ne-7,-1}.
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> The original denominator gives
>
> $$
> y\ne0.
> $$
>
> Factor the numerator:
>
> $$
> \frac{8y+2}{8y}
> =\frac{2(4y+1)}{8y}
> =\frac{4y+1}{4y}.
> $$
>
> No variable factor cancels across the sum in the numerator.
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{4y+1}{4y}},\qquad \boxed{y\ne0}.
> $$
>
> ### 3.
>
> From the original denominator,
>
> $$
> (t+3)(t-5)\ne0,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> t\ne-3,\qquad t\ne5.
> $$
>
> Factor the numerator:
>
> $$
> 3t^2-75
> =3(t^2-25)
> =3(t-5)(t+5).
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> \frac{3(t-5)(t+5)}{(t+3)(t-5)}
> =\frac{3(t+5)}{t+3}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{3(t+5)}{t+3}},\qquad \boxed{t\ne-3,5}.
> $$
>
> ### 4.
>
> The original denominator cannot equal zero:
>
> $$
> 4-c\ne0,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> c\ne4.
> $$
>
> Since
>
> $$
> 4-c=-(c-4),
> $$
>
> we have
>
> $$
> \frac{c-4}{4-c}
> =\frac{c-4}{-(c-4)}
> =-1.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{-1},\qquad \boxed{c\ne4}.
> $$
