## Question

Express each rational expression in simplest form. State all non-permissible values as restrictions on the variable.

1. $$\frac{8x+16}{2x}$$
2. $$\frac{5x}{10x+40}$$
3. $$\frac{6y+9}{3y-12}$$
4. $$\frac{8-6x}{4+12x}$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> The original denominator gives
>
> $$
> x\ne0.
> $$
>
> Factor:
>
> $$
> \frac{8x+16}{2x}
> =\frac{8(x+2)}{2x}
> =\frac{4(x+2)}{x}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{4(x+2)}{x}},\qquad \boxed{x\ne0}.
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> The original denominator cannot equal zero:
>
> $$
> 10x+40=10(x+4),
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> x\ne-4.
> $$
>
> Simplify:
>
> $$
> \frac{5x}{10(x+4)}
> =\frac{x}{2(x+4)}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{x}{2(x+4)}},\qquad \boxed{x\ne-4}.
> $$
>
> ### 3.
>
> The denominator factors as
>
> $$
> 3y-12=3(y-4),
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> y\ne4.
> $$
>
> Factor and simplify:
>
> $$
> \frac{6y+9}{3y-12}
> =\frac{3(2y+3)}{3(y-4)}
> =\frac{2y+3}{y-4}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{2y+3}{y-4}},\qquad \boxed{y\ne4}.
> $$
>
> ### 4.
>
> The denominator factors as
>
> $$
> 4+12x=4(1+3x),
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> 1+3x\ne0
> $$
>
> and therefore
>
> $$
> x\ne-\frac13.
> $$
>
> Factor and simplify:
>
> $$
> \frac{8-6x}{4+12x}
> =\frac{2(4-3x)}{4(1+3x)}
> =\frac{4-3x}{2(1+3x)}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{4-3x}{2(1+3x)}},\qquad
> \boxed{x\ne-\frac13}.
> $$
