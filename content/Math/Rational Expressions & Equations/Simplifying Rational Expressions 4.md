## Question

Express the following rational expressions in simplest form, stating the non-permissible values as restrictions.

1. $$\displaystyle\frac{6b^2}{18b}$$
2. $$\displaystyle\frac{21m^4}{14m^9}$$
3. $$\displaystyle\frac{(p+4)(p-7)}{(p-2)(p-7)}$$
4. $$\displaystyle\frac{(t+5)(t-3)}{t(t-3)(t+10)}$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> The denominator cannot equal zero, so
>
> $$
> b\ne0.
> $$
>
> Simplify:
>
> $$
> \frac{6b^2}{18b}
> =\frac{b}{3}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{b}{3}},\qquad \boxed{b\ne0}.
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> The denominator cannot equal zero, so
>
> $$
> m\ne0.
> $$
>
> Simplify the numerical coefficients and subtract exponents:
>
> $$
> \frac{21m^4}{14m^9}
> =\frac{3}{2}m^{-5}
> =\frac{3}{2m^5}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{3}{2m^5}},\qquad \boxed{m\ne0}.
> $$
>
> ### 3.
>
> From the original denominator,
>
> $$
> (p-2)(p-7)\ne0,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> p\ne2,\qquad p\ne7.
> $$
>
> Cancel the common factor:
>
> $$
> \frac{(p+4)(p-7)}{(p-2)(p-7)}
> =\frac{p+4}{p-2}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{p+4}{p-2}},\qquad \boxed{p\ne2,7}.
> $$
>
> ### 4.
>
> From the original denominator,
>
> $$
> t(t-3)(t+10)\ne0,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> t\ne0,\qquad t\ne3,\qquad t\ne-10.
> $$
>
> Cancel the common factor:
>
> $$
> \frac{(t+5)(t-3)}{t(t-3)(t+10)}
> =\frac{t+5}{t(t+10)}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{t+5}{t(t+10)}},\qquad
> \boxed{t\ne-10,0,3}.
> $$
