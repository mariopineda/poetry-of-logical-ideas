## Question

Write each of the following as a simplified rational expression.

#### A.

$$\frac{c}{2} - \frac{c+2}{6}$$

#### B.

$$\frac{a+2}{3} + \frac{a-3}{5}$$

#### C.

$$\frac{t-2}{4} - \frac{t-3}{5}$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> Use a common denominator of \(6\):
>
> $$
> \frac{c}{2}-\frac{c+2}{6}
> =
> \frac{3c-(c+2)}{6}
> =
> \frac{2c-2}{6}
> =
> \boxed{\frac{c-1}{3}}.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> Use a common denominator of \(15\):
>
> $$
> \frac{a+2}{3}+\frac{a-3}{5}
> =
> \frac{5(a+2)+3(a-3)}{15}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \frac{5a+10+3a-9}{15}
> =
> \boxed{\frac{8a+1}{15}}.
> $$
>
> ### C.
>
> Use a common denominator of \(20\):
>
> $$
> \frac{t-2}{4}-\frac{t-3}{5}
> =
> \frac{5(t-2)-4(t-3)}{20}.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \frac{5t-10-4t+12}{20}
> =
> \boxed{\frac{t+2}{20}}.
> $$
>
> There are no variable restrictions in these three expressions because all original denominators are non-zero constants.
