Solve the following equations.

#### A.

$$
\frac{2x+1}{x-3} - \frac{4x-1}{2x-3} = 0
$$

#### B.

$$
\frac{6y-2}{3y-2} - \frac{2y+6}{y+6} = 0
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> Restrictions:
>
> $$
> x\neq3,\ \frac32.
> $$
>
> Set
>
> $$
> \frac{2x+1}{x-3}
> =
> \frac{4x-1}{2x-3}.
> $$
>
> Cross-multiply:
>
> $$
> (2x+1)(2x-3)
> =
> (4x-1)(x-3).
> $$
>
> Expand:
>
> $$
> 4x^2-4x-3
> =
> 4x^2-13x+3.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> 9x=6
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=\frac23}.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> Restrictions:
>
> $$
> y\neq\frac23,-6.
> $$
>
> Set
>
> $$
> \frac{6y-2}{3y-2}
> =
> \frac{2y+6}{y+6}.
> $$
>
> Cross-multiply:
>
> $$
> (6y-2)(y+6)
> =
> (2y+6)(3y-2).
> $$
>
> Expand:
>
> $$
> 6y^2+34y-12
> =
> 6y^2+14y-12.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> 20y=0
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> \boxed{y=0}.
> $$
