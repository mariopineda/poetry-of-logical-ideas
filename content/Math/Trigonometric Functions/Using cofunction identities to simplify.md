Simplify $\sin\left(\frac\pi2-\theta\right)-\cos\left(\frac\pi2+\theta\right)$ to an expression involving only $\sin\theta$ and $\cos\theta$. Justify each identity.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Apply the angle-subtraction and angle-addition formulas (or the cofunction identities):
>
> $$\sin\left(\frac\pi2-\theta\right)=\cos\theta,\qquad\cos\left(\frac\pi2+\theta\right)=-\sin\theta.$$
>
> Subtracting the latter gives
>
> $$\boxed{\sin\left(\frac\pi2-\theta\right)-\cos\left(\frac\pi2+\theta\right)=\cos\theta+\sin\theta}.$$
>
> Both identities are valid for every real $\theta$.
