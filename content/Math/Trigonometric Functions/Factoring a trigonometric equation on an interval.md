Solve $2\sin^2x=\sin x$ on the half-open interval $[-\pi,\pi)$. Show the algebraic factorization and list **all** solutions in the interval.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Move all terms to the same side and factor:
>
> $$2\sin^2x-\sin x=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\sin x(2\sin x-1)=0.$$
>
> Therefore $\sin x=0$ or $\sin x=1/2$. On $[-\pi,\pi)$, the zero-sine values are $-\pi$ and $0$ (but not $\pi$), and the positive half-sine values are $\pi/6$ and $5\pi/6$. All four satisfy the original equation:
>
> $$\boxed{x\in\left\{-\pi,\,0,\,\frac\pi6,\,\frac{5\pi}{6}\right\}}.$$
>
> The half-open endpoint convention is essential.
