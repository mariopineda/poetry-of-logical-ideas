An inspection robot travels at constant speed around a circular track of radius $120$ m. The lowest point of the track is $15$ m above a **horizontal reference line**. A sensor records the robot's **perpendicular distance above that line**. The robot completes five laps in $30$ minutes.

![[diploma-batch06-2016-mc21-robot-track.svg]]

The sensor measurement is modelled by $s(t)=a\cos[b(t-c)]+d$. Determine the positive angular frequency $b$ and the midline height $d$. Justify both values using the geometry and time information.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> One complete cycle takes $T=30/5=6$ minutes. The angular frequency is therefore
>
> $$b=\frac{2\pi}{T}=\boxed{\frac{\pi}{3}\text{ rad/min}}.$$
>
> The lowest sensor distance is $15$ m and the highest is $15+2(120)=255$ m. Thus
>
> $$d=\frac{15+255}{2}=\boxed{135\text{ m}}.$$
>
> The amplitude is $120$ m, agreeing with the track radius. A valid model when the robot starts at its lowest point is $s(t)=135-120\cos(\pi t/3)$. This has minimum $15$ m and maximum $255$ m.
