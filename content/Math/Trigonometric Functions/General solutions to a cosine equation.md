Solve $4\cos\theta+2=0$ for **all real** $\theta$. Give the complete general solution in radians.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> First isolate cosine:
>
> $$4\cos\theta+2=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\cos\theta=-\frac12.$$
>
> The two angles on $[0,2\pi)$ with cosine $-1/2$ are $2\pi/3$ and $4\pi/3$. Periodicity of cosine yields the complete real solution:
>
> $$\boxed{\theta=\frac{2\pi}{3}+2\pi n\quad\text{or}\quad\theta=\frac{4\pi}{3}+2\pi n,\quad n\in\mathbb Z}.$$
>
> No other solutions occur because these are the only points on one full unit-circle revolution with the specified x-coordinate.
