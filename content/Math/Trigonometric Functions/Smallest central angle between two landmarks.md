Two directions are represented by points $A=(\sqrt2/2,\sqrt2/2)$ and $B=(-\sqrt3/2,1/2)$ on the unit circle. Determine the **smaller central angle** $\angle AOB$, in degrees, where $O=(0,0)$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> On the unit circle, $A=(\sqrt2/2,\sqrt2/2)$ lies at $45^\circ$. Point $B=(-\sqrt3/2,1/2)$ lies in Quadrant II at $150^\circ$. Their counterclockwise angular separation is
>
> $$150^\circ-45^\circ=105^\circ.$$
>
> Since $105^\circ<180^\circ$, this is already the smaller of the two central angles. Therefore $\boxed{\angle AOB=105^\circ}$.
