A small lamp is attached to the rim of a rotating kinetic sculpture. Its vertical height $h$ above the gallery floor, in metres, is modelled by

$$h(t)=18\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{20}t+\frac{\pi}{2}\right)+23,$$

where $t$ is the number of seconds after the sculpture's motion is first recorded. Explain the physical meaning of the vertical intercept of the graph and determine its value.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The vertical intercept of a height-versus-time graph is the value at $t=0$; in context, it is the lamp's height when timing begins.
>
> $$h(0)=18\sin\left(\frac\pi2\right)+23=18(1)+23=\boxed{41\text{ m}}.$$
>
> It is **not** the time to complete a revolution. In this particular model the initial height happens to equal the maximum height, but the general meaning of the vertical intercept is the initial height.
