A rotating metal art disk is mounted vertically on a fixed stand, with its centre **25 cm above the floor**. A reflective dot is attached **7 cm inward from the outer rim** of the disk. The disk has radius **25 cm** and rotates at constant angular speed, so the dot's height above the floor varies sinusoidally.

Determine the sinusoid's **amplitude** and **midline height**. Explain how the dot's radial distance differs from the height of the disk centre.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The centre of the mounted disk remains $25$ cm above the floor, so the vertical midpoint of the dot's motion is $\boxed{25\text{ cm}}$.
>
> The dot is mounted $7$ cm inward from the disk rim, so its distance from the centre is $25-7=\boxed{18\text{ cm}}$. This is the maximum vertical distance it can move above or below the disk centre, i.e. the **amplitude**.
>
> The dot's lowest and highest heights are $25-18=7$ cm and $25+18=43$ cm, whose midpoint is $25$ cm and half-difference $18$ cm. Constant angular velocity makes the dot height sinusoidal as a function of time.
