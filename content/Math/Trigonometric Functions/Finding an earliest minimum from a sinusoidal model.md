A greenhouse's air temperature on a particular day is modelled by

$$T(t)=3\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{6}t+\frac{\pi}{2}\right)+8,$$

where $t$ is the number of hours after midnight and $T$ is measured in degrees Celsius. At what earliest time after midnight does the greenhouse reach its minimum modelled temperature? Give the time in 24-hour clock notation, and show your reasoning.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The amplitude is $3$, so the temperature reaches its minimum when sine is $-1$, at $8-3=5^\circ\mathrm{C}$. The first phase angle greater than or equal to the starting phase $\pi/2$ for which $\sin\theta=-1$ is $3\pi/2$.
>
> $$\frac\pi6t+\frac\pi2=\frac{3\pi}2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac\pi6t=\pi\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\boxed{t=6\text{ h}}.$$
>
> Therefore the earliest minimum after midnight is **06:00**. Later temperature minima occur every $P=2\pi/(\pi/6)=12$ hours.
