# Properties of Functions 14

Given $f(x)=3x^2-4$, evaluate the following. Show substitution.

(a) $f(0)$  
(b) $f(-2)$  
(c) $f(3)$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> (a) $f(0)=3(0)^2-4=\boxed{-4}$
>
> (b) $f(-2)=3(-2)^2-4=12-4=\boxed{8}$
>
> (c) $f(3)=3(3)^2-4=27-4=\boxed{23}$
