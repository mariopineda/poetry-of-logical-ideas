A water-taxi travels $60$ km downstream and $60$ km upstream. Its speed in still water is $v$ km/h, and the river current flows at $4$ km/h. The total travel time is $5$ hours.

Build a rational equation for the trip and solve for $v$ to the nearest tenth. State the physical restriction, show how you deal with the extraneous solution, and check the approximate trip time.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Downstream speed is $v+4$, upstream speed is $v-4$, and a boat must travel against the current, so $v>4$.
>
> $$\frac{60}{v+4}+\frac{60}{v-4}=5.$$
>
> Multiplying by $(v+4)(v-4)$ gives
>
> $$60(v-4)+60(v+4)=5(v^2-16)$$
> $$120v=5v^2-80\quad\Longrightarrow\quad v^2-24v-16=0.$$
>
> The quadratic formula gives $v=12\pm4\sqrt{10}$. The negative root is physically invalid. Hence
>
> $$\boxed{v=12+4\sqrt{10}\approx24.6\text{ km/h}}.$$
>
> The calculated times are approximately $60/28.649=2.094$ h and $60/20.649=2.906$ h, totalling about $5.000$ h.
