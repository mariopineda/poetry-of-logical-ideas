A student tries to simplify

$$\frac{x+2}{x^2-4}\div\frac{x+2}{x-2}.$$

They change the division sign to multiplication **without inverting the divisor**. Identify the error, complete the correct simplification, and state every excluded value from the original expression, including values making the divisor zero.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Dividing by a nonzero rational expression means multiplying by its reciprocal, **not** the expression itself:
>
> $$\frac{x+2}{(x-2)(x+2)}\times\frac{x-2}{x+2}=\boxed{\frac1{x+2}}.$$
>
> The first denominator excludes $x=-2$ and $x=2$. The divisor $\frac{x+2}{x-2}$ is undefined at $x=2$ and equals zero at $x=-2$.
>
> Thus, despite cancellation, the original expression requires $\boxed{x\ne -2,2}$.
