For nonzero real $m$, $n$, and $p$, simplify the **product** below to lowest terms. Use only positive exponents in the final expression, and state the net exponent of each variable before removing negative exponents.

$$\frac{8m^5n^2}{3p^3}\times\frac{9p^5}{12m^2n^4}.$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Multiply numerators and denominators and collect like bases:
>
> $$\frac{8m^5n^2}{3p^3}\cdot\frac{9p^5}{12m^2n^4}=\frac{72}{36}\,m^{5-2}n^{2-4}p^{5-3}.$$
>
> The coefficient is $2$, and the net exponents are $m^3$, $n^{-2}$, and $p^2$. Rewrite $n^{-2}=1/n^2$:
>
> $$\boxed{\frac{2m^3p^2}{n^2}}.$$
>
> Because $m$, $n$, and $p$ are nonzero, all original denominators and the final expression are defined. Substituting any permissible values in the original product and the simplified expression yields equal values.
