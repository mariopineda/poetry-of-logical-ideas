Simplify $$\frac{2x+6}{8x+24}-\frac{1}{4x+12}$$ to one rational expression in lowest terms. Give its denominator and the non-permissible value.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> First factor the denominators, retaining the original restriction $x\ne-3$:
>
> $$\frac{2x+6}{8x+24}-\frac1{4x+12}=\frac{2(x+3)}{8(x+3)}-\frac1{4(x+3)}.$$
>
> For permissible $x$, the first term is $1/4$. Put the two fractions over the same denominator:
>
> $$\frac14-\frac1{4(x+3)}=\frac{x+3-1}{4(x+3)}=\boxed{\frac{x+2}{4(x+3)}},\qquad\boxed{x\ne-3}.$$
>
> The result's denominator is $\boxed{4(x+3)}$. The cancellation does not restore the excluded input.
