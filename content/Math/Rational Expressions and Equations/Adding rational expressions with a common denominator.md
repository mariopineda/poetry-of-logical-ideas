Write the sum below as a **single simplified rational expression**:

$$\frac{x+3}{4x}+\frac14.$$

Express your result as $\frac{Ax+B}{Cx}$, where $A$, $B$, and $C$ are **single-digit positive integers** in lowest terms. Determine the three digits and state every non-permissible value.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Use the common denominator $4x$:
>
> $$\frac{x+3}{4x}+\frac14=\frac{x+3}{4x}+\frac{x}{4x}=\frac{2x+3}{4x}.$$
>
> The numerator and denominator share no common factor. Hence
>
> $$\boxed{A=2,\quad B=3,\quad C=4},\qquad\boxed{x\ne0}.$$
>
> The restriction is inherited from the original denominator $4x$.
