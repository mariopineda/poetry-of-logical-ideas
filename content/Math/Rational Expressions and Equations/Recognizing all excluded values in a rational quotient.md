Determine every non-permissible value of $x$ in $$\frac{x-4}{x(x+5)}\div\frac{x-4}{x-2}.$$ Explain why the divisor creates an additional restriction.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The first rational expression has denominator $x(x+5)$, requiring $x\ne0,-5$. The divisor has denominator $x-2$, so $x\ne2$.
>
> A divisor must also be **nonzero**. Its numerator $x-4$ is zero at $x=4$, which must be excluded.
>
> Therefore all non-permissible values are
>
> $$\boxed{x\in\{-5,0,2,4\}}.$$
>
> Multiplying by the reciprocal later cannot remove any of these original restrictions.
