A ferry and an express train each cover a $540$ km route. The express train's average speed is **four times** the ferry's speed. The ferry requires **nine hours longer** than the express train.

Determine the ferry's average speed in kilometres per hour by forming and solving a rational equation. State any restrictions and check the result.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Let $v>0$ be the ferry's average speed in km/h. The express train moves at $4v$ km/h. As time equals distance divided by speed, their trip times are $540/v$ and $540/(4v)$ hours.
>
> The ferry takes nine hours longer, so
>
> $$\frac{540}{v}-\frac{540}{4v}=9.$$
>
> With common denominator $4v$, $\frac{2160-540}{4v}=\frac{405}{v}=9$. Multiply by $v$:
>
> $$405=9v\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\boxed{v=45\text{ km/h}}.$$
>
> Check: ferry time $540/45=12$ h; express train time $540/180=3$ h; difference $12-3=9$ h. The result respects the restriction $v>0$.
