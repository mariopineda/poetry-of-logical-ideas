Solve each. Answer in interval notation.

1.

$$
x^2<-25
$$
2.

$$
x^2>-49
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> $$
> x^2<-25
> $$
>
> has no real solutions because
>
> $$
> x^2\geq0
> $$
>
> for every real $x$.
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\varnothing}.
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> $$
> x^2>-49
> $$
>
> is true for every real number because
>
> $$
> x^2\geq0>-49.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{(-\infty,\infty)}.
> $$
>
