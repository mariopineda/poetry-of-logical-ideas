Solve $5x+4 \leq 2x-11<8x+13$. Answer in interval notation.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The compound inequality
>
> $$
> 5x+4\leq2x-11<8x+13
> $$
>
> requires both inequalities to be true.
>
> From
>
> $$
> 5x+4\leq2x-11,
> $$
>
> we get
>
> $$
> 3x\leq-15
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> x\leq-5.
> $$
>
> From
>
> $$
> 2x-11<8x+13,
> $$
>
> we get
>
> $$
> -24<6x
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> x>-4.
> $$
>
> No real number satisfies both
>
> $$
> x\leq-5
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> x>-4.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\varnothing}.
> $$
