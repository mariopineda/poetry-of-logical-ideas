Solve $|x^2+6x-8| = 8$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> For
>
> $$
> |x^2+6x-8|=8,
> $$
>
> solve the two cases.
>
> ### Case 1
>
> $$
> x^2+6x-8=8
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> x^2+6x-16=0
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> (x+8)(x-2)=0.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> x=-8,\ 2.
> $$
>
> ### Case 2
>
> $$
> x^2+6x-8=-8
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> x^2+6x=0
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> x(x+6)=0.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> x=0,\ -6.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{x\in\{-8,-6,0,2\}}.
> $$
