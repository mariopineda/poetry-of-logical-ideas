## Question

Determine the polynomial that would have to be added to $6x^2-3x+6$ so that the sum of the polynomials is $x^2+4x+2$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> \[
> \begin{array}{cl}
> & (x^2+4x+2)-(6x^2-3x+6) \\
> = & x^2+4x+2-6x^2+3x-6 \\
> = & -5x^2+7x-4
> \end{array}
> \]
