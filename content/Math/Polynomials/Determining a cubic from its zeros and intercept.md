A cubic function has three distinct zeros at $-2$, $1$, and $4$. Its $y$-intercept is $16$. Determine its fully factored equation and evaluate $P(3)$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Because the zeros are distinct, a cubic with zeros $-2$, $1$ and $4$ must be
>
> $$P(x)=a(x+2)(x-1)(x-4).$$
>
> The $y$-intercept gives $P(0)=a(2)(-1)(-4)=8a=16$, so $a=2$. Consequently
>
> $$\boxed{P(x)=2(x+2)(x-1)(x-4)}.$$
>
> At $x=3$, $P(3)=2(5)(2)(-1)=\boxed{-20}$.
