### 1. Exponent Laws

Simplify using exponent laws. Express your answer with positive exponents.

$$
\frac{(5p^2q^3)^2}{25pq^2}
$$

### 2. Polynomial Operations

Simplify. Express your answer in descending order.

$$
2(4x^2-x+3)-(3x^2+6x-2)
$$

### 3. Factoring

Factor fully.

$$
32x^4-200x^2
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1. Exponent Laws
>
> Apply the power of a product and power of a power rules:
>
> $$
> (5p^2q^3)^2=25p^4q^6.
> $$
>
> Then divide and subtract exponents on like bases:
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> \frac{25p^4q^6}{25pq^2}
> &=p^{4-1}q^{6-2}\\
> &=p^3q^4.
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> **Answer:** $\boxed{p^3q^4}$
>
> ### 2. Polynomial Operations
>
> Distribute:
>
> $$
> 8x^2-2x+6-3x^2-6x+2.
> $$
>
> Combine like terms:
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> (8x^2-3x^2)+(-2x-6x)+(6+2)
> &=5x^2-8x+8.
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> **Answer:** $\boxed{5x^2-8x+8}$
>
> ### 3. Factoring
>
> First factor out the greatest common factor:
>
> $$
> 32x^4-200x^2=8x^2(4x^2-25).
> $$
>
> The remaining binomial is a difference of squares:
>
> $$
> 4x^2-25=(2x)^2-5^2=(2x-5)(2x+5).
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> 32x^4-200x^2=8x^2(2x-5)(2x+5).
> $$
>
> **Answer:** $\boxed{8x^2(2x-5)(2x+5)}$
