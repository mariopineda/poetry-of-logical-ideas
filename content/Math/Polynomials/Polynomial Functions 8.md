Without using technology, determine the following characteristics of the given functions,

- Number of x-intercepts
- Value of the y-intercept
- Range
- Nature of turning point
- End behaviour
- The slope for linear functions

1.

$$
f(x)=4
$$
2.

$$
f(x)=-2x+6
$$
3.

$$
f(x)=2x^2+1
$$
4.

$$
f(x)=-x^2+9
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
>
> ### 1.
>
> $$
> f(x)=4
> $$
>
> - Number of $x$-intercepts: $\boxed{0}$
> - $y$-intercept: $\boxed{4}$
> - Range:
>
> $$
> \boxed{\{4\}}
> $$
>
> - Turning point: none
> - End behaviour:
>
> $$
> x\to\pm\infty
> \quad\Rightarrow\quad
> f(x)\to4
> $$
>
> - Slope:
>
> $$
> \boxed{0}
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> $$
> f(x)=-2x+6
> $$
>
> Set
>
> $$
> -2x+6=0
> $$
>
> to obtain
>
> $$
> x=3.
> $$
>
> Therefore:
>
> - Number of $x$-intercepts: $\boxed{1}$
> - $x$-intercept: $\boxed{(3,0)}$
> - $y$-intercept: $\boxed{6}$
> - Range:
>
> $$
> \boxed{\mathbb R}
> $$
>
> - Turning point: none
> - End behaviour:
>
> $$
> x\to-\infty\Rightarrow f(x)\to\infty
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> x\to\infty\Rightarrow f(x)\to-\infty
> $$
>
> - Slope:
>
> $$
> \boxed{-2}
> $$
>
> ### 3.
>
> $$
> f(x)=2x^2+1
> $$
>
> Since
>
> $$
> 2x^2+1\geq1,
> $$
>
> there are no real $x$-intercepts.
>
> Therefore:
>
> - Number of $x$-intercepts: $\boxed{0}$
> - $y$-intercept: $\boxed{1}$
> - Range:
>
> $$
> \boxed{[1,\infty)}
> $$
>
> - Turning point: minimum at
>
> $$
> \boxed{(0,1)}
> $$
>
> - End behaviour:
>
> $$
> x\to\pm\infty
> \quad\Rightarrow\quad
> f(x)\to\infty
> $$
>
> ### 4.
>
> $$
> f(x)=-x^2+9
> $$
>
> Set
>
> $$
> -x^2+9=0.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> x^2=9
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> x=\pm3.
> $$
>
> Therefore:
>
> - Number of $x$-intercepts: $\boxed{2}$
> - $x$-intercepts:
>
> $$
> \boxed{(-3,0),\ (3,0)}
> $$
>
> - $y$-intercept: $\boxed{9}$
> - Range:
>
> $$
> \boxed{(-\infty,9]}
> $$
>
> - Turning point: maximum at
>
> $$
> \boxed{(0,9)}
> $$
>
> - End behaviour:
>
> $$
> x\to\pm\infty
> \quad\Rightarrow\quad
> f(x)\to-\infty
> $$
