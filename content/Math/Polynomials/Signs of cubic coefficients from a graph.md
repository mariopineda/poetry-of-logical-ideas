The supplied graph represents a cubic function of the form

$$f(x)=ax^3+bx^2+cx+d.$$

![[batch12-cubic-coefficient-signs.svg]]

Using **only the graph**, determine whether $a$ is positive or negative and whether $d$ is positive or negative. Justify each conclusion using the end behaviour and the vertical intercept.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> On the far left the curve falls and on the far right it rises. For a cubic, that end behaviour requires a **positive leading coefficient**, so $\boxed{a>0}$.
>
> At $x=0$, the graph crosses the vertical axis **below** the horizontal axis. Since $f(0)=d$, we have $\boxed{d<0}$. The conclusion depends on reading the graph, not on the particular numerical coefficients used to draw it.
