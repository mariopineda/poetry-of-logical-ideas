## Question

The graph below shows the height of a ball during its flight.

![[Polynomial Functions 16 Graph.png]]

1. What is the **maximum height** of the ball, and when does it occur?
2. During what time interval is the ball **at least 12 m above the ground**?
3. How long is the ball in the air altogether?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> The maximum value occurs at the **vertex** of the parabola.
>
> From the graph, the vertex is $(4,16)$.
>
> So the ball reaches a **maximum height of 16 m after 4 s**.
>
> ### 2.
>
> The dashed line represents $h=12$.
>
> From the graph, the parabola intersects this line at $t=2$ and $t=6$.
>
> Therefore, the ball is **at least 12 m above the ground** for
>
> $$
> 2\le t\le 6
> $$
>
> ### 3.
>
> The ball is in the air from the time it is thrown until it returns to the ground.
>
> From the graph, the x-intercepts are $(0,0)$ and $(8,0)$.
>
> Therefore, the ball is in the air for **8 s** altogether.
