## Question

### 1. Exponent Laws

Simplify using exponent laws. Express your answer with positive exponents.

$$
\frac{20m^8n^6}{5m^4n^3}
$$

### 2. Polynomial Operations

Simplify. Express your answer in descending order.

$$
(4x^2-3x+2)-(2x^2+3x-5)
$$

### 3. Factoring

Factor fully.

$$
27x^3-48x
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1. Exponent Laws
>
> Divide the coefficients and subtract exponents on like bases:
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> \frac{20m^8n^6}{5m^4n^3}
> &=4m^{8-4}n^{6-3}\\
> &=4m^4n^3.
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> **Answer:** $\boxed{4m^4n^3}$
>
> ### 2. Polynomial Operations
>
> Distribute the subtraction through the second polynomial:
>
> $$
> 4x^2-3x+2-2x^2-3x+5.
> $$
>
> Combine like terms:
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> (4x^2-2x^2)+(-3x-3x)+(2+5)
> &=2x^2-6x+7.
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> **Answer:** $\boxed{2x^2-6x+7}$
>
> ### 3. Factoring
>
> First factor out the greatest common factor:
>
> $$
> 27x^3-48x=3x(9x^2-16).
> $$
>
> The remaining binomial is a difference of squares:
>
> $$
> 9x^2-16=(3x)^2-4^2=(3x-4)(3x+4).
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> 27x^3-48x=3x(3x-4)(3x+4).
> $$
>
> **Answer:** $\boxed{3x(3x-4)(3x+4)}$
