## Question

The elevation of a hiking trail is modelled by

$$
E(d)=0.5d^3-6d^2+18d+5,\qquad 0\le d\le8
$$

where $d$ is the distance from the trailhead, in kilometres, and $E$ is the elevation relative to a reference level, in metres.

1. **Sketch the graph for $0\le d\le8$.** Your sketch must include:
   - labelled axes with variables and units
   - an appropriate scale
   - the endpoints and turning points clearly labelled
2. Describe how the elevation changes as the hiker travels along the trail.
3. Determine the coordinates of any **local maximum and local minimum**.
4. State the **domain and range in context**.

Use your graphing calculator as needed.

> ### 2. Description
>
> From the trailhead, the elevation **increases** until approximately $d=2$ km.
>
> It then **decreases** until approximately $d=6$ km.
>
> From there, it **increases again** until the end of the model at $d=8$ km.
>

>

> $$
>
> ### 4. Domain and range
>
> The trail model is defined from $0$ km to $8$ km:
>
> $$
> 0\le d\le8
> $$
>
> Over this interval, the elevation ranges from $5$ m to $21$ m:
>
> $$
> 5\le E\le21
