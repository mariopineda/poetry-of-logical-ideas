A weather instrument is released **from rest** at a height of $78.4$ m. Ignoring air resistance, its height above the ground is modelled by

$$
h(t)=78.4-4.9t^2,
$$

where $t$ is measured in seconds.

Determine when it reaches the ground, to the nearest hundredth of a second, and state the **contextual domain** of the model from release to impact.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The instrument reaches the ground when $h(t)=0$:
>
> $$
> 78.4-4.9t^2=0
> \quad\Longrightarrow\quad t^2=\frac{78.4}{4.9}=16.
> $$
>
> The algebraic roots are $t=\pm4$, but negative elapsed time does not apply after release. Therefore the instrument hits the ground at
>
> $$
> \boxed{t=4.00\text{ s}}.
> $$
>
> The model is being used from release until impact, so the contextual domain is
>
> $$
> \boxed{0\le t\le4}.
> $$
