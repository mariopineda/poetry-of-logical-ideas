Without using graphing technology, determine the **end behaviour** and the sign of the $y$-intercept of the cubic function

$$f(x)=-2x^3+4x+5.$$

Name the quadrants containing the far-left and far-right ends of the graph.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The leading term $-2x^3$ dominates for large $|x|$.
>
> As $x\to-\infty$, $-2x^3\to+\infty$, so the graph extends into **Quadrant II**. As $x\to+\infty$, $-2x^3\to-\infty$, so the graph extends into **Quadrant IV**.
>
> At $x=0$, $f(0)=5$, hence the $y$-intercept is $\boxed{(0,5)}$, which is positive.
