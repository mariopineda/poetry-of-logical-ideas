## Question

### 1. Exponent Laws

Simplify using exponent laws. Express your answer with positive exponents.

$$
\frac{28p^9q^5}{7p^5q^2}
$$

### 2. Polynomial Operations

Simplify. Express your answer in descending order.

$$
(5x^2-2x+4)-(3x^2+4x-3)
$$

### 3. Factoring

Factor fully.

$$
50x^3-98x
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1. Exponent Laws
>
> Divide the coefficients and subtract exponents on like bases:
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> \frac{28p^9q^5}{7p^5q^2}
> &=4p^{9-5}q^{5-2}\\
> &=4p^4q^3.
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> **Answer:** $\boxed{4p^4q^3}$
>
> ### 2. Polynomial Operations
>
> Distribute the subtraction through the second polynomial:
>
> $$
> 5x^2-2x+4-3x^2-4x+3.
> $$
>
> Combine like terms:
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> (5x^2-3x^2)+(-2x-4x)+(4+3)
> &=2x^2-6x+7.
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> **Answer:** $\boxed{2x^2-6x+7}$
>
> ### 3. Factoring
>
> First factor out the greatest common factor:
>
> $$
> 50x^3-98x=2x(25x^2-49).
> $$
>
> The remaining binomial is a difference of squares:
>
> $$
> 25x^2-49=(5x)^2-7^2=(5x-7)(5x+7).
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> 50x^3-98x=2x(5x-7)(5x+7).
> $$
>
> **Answer:** $\boxed{2x(5x-7)(5x+7)}$
