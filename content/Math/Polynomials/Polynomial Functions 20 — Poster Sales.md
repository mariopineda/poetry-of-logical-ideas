## Question

The monthly profit from selling posters is modelled by

$$
P(n)=-0.5(n-8)^2+18,\qquad 0\le n\le16
$$

where $n$ represents the number of **hundreds of posters sold** and $P$ represents the monthly profit, in **thousands of dollars**.

1. **Sketch the graph over the given domain.** Your sketch must include:
   - labelled axes with variables and units
   - an appropriate scale
   - the vertex and intercepts clearly labelled
2. How many posters should be sold to obtain the **maximum profit**?
3. Determine the **maximum monthly profit**, in dollars.
4. Determine how many posters must be sold for the business to **break even**.
5. State the **domain and range in context**, including units.

Use your graphing calculator as needed.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1. Sketch
>
> ![[Polynomial Functions 20 Solution Graph.png]]
>
> Important points include
>
> $$
> (0,-14),\quad(2,0),\quad(8,18),\quad(14,0),\quad(16,-14)
> $$
>
> ### 2. Number of posters for maximum profit
>
> The vertex is
>
> $$
> (8,18)
> $$
>
> Since $n$ represents hundreds of posters,
>
> $$
> 8(100)=800
> $$
>
> The business should sell **800 posters**.
>
> ### 3. Maximum profit
>
> The maximum value is
>
> $$
> P=18
> $$
>
> Since profit is measured in thousands of dollars, the maximum monthly profit is
>
> $$
> \$18\,000
> $$
>
> ### 4. Break-even points
>
> Break-even occurs when $P(n)=0$:
>
> $$
> -0.5(n-8)^2+18=0
> $$
>
> $$
> (n-8)^2=36
> $$
>
> $$
> n=2\quad\text{or}\quad n=14
> $$
>
> Therefore, the business breaks even at **200 posters** and **1400 posters**.
>
> ### 5. Domain and range
>
> The given contextual domain is
>
> $$
> 0\le n\le16
> $$
>
> which represents **0 to 1600 posters**.
>
> Over this domain, the range is
>
> $$
> -14\le P\le18
> $$
>
> which represents a monthly result from a **\$14,000 loss** to an **\$18,000 profit**.
