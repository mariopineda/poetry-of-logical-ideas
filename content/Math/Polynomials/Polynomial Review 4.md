## Question

1. A triangle has side lengths of $9a+4$, $2a+2$ and $a-4$.

1. Determine a simplifed expression for the perimeter of this triangle.
2. If the perimeter of this triangle is 38 cm, determine the value of $a$.

2. A triangle has side lengths of $2b+1$, $3b-1$ and a perimeter of $13b^2+3b+2$. Determine the expression for the length of the third side.
3. In $\triangle ABC$, $AB = AC$, $BC=b+1$ and $AB+AC+BC=3b+5$. Determine the expression for $AB$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1A.
>
> \[
> \begin{array}{cl}
> & (9a+4)+(2a+2)+(a-4) \\
> = & 9a+4+2a+2+a-4 \\
> = & 12a + 2
> \end{array}
> \]
>
> ### 1B.
>
> \[
> \begin{array}{rcl}
> 12a+2 & = & 38 \\
> 12a & = & 36 \\
> a & = & 36 \div 12 \\
> a & = & 3
> \end{array}
> \]
>
> ### 2.
>
> The length of the third side is the perimeter minus the length of the two other sides of the triangle.
> \[
> \begin{array}{cl}
> & 13b^2+3b+2-(2b+1)-(3b-1) \\
> = & 13b^2+3b+2-2b-1-3b+1 \\
> = & 13b^2-2b+2
> \end{array}
> \]
>
> ### 3.
>
> If $x = AB = AC$, then $x+x+b+1=3b+5$. Solve for $x$.
> \[
> \begin{array}{rcl}
> x+x+b+1 & = & 3b+5 \\
> 2x+b+1 & = & 3b+5 \\
> 2x & = & 3b+5-b-1 \\
> 2x & = & 2b+4 \\
> x & = & \frac{2b+4}{2} \\
> x & = & b+2
> \end{array}
> \]
> Hence $AB = b+2$.
>
