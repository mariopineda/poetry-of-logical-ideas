## Question

The graph below shows the monthly profit of a small business.

![[Polynomial Functions 15 Graph.png]]

Here, $x$ is the number of **hundreds of products sold** and $P(x)$ is the profit in **thousands of dollars**.

1. How many products must be sold for the business to **break even**?
2. For what numbers of products sold is the profit **at least $12\,000$**?
3. What is the **maximum monthly profit**, and how many products must be sold to achieve it?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> The business breaks even when the profit is $0$.
>
> From the graph, the x-intercepts are $(1,0)$ and $(9,0)$.
>
> Since $x$ is measured in **hundreds of products**, the business breaks even at **100 products** or **900 products**.
>
> ### 2.
>
> The dashed line represents $P(x)=12$, which means a profit of **$12\,000$**.
>
> From the graph, the parabola intersects this line at $x=3$ and $x=7$.
>
> Therefore, the profit is **at least $12\,000$** when
>
> $$
> 3\le x\le 7
> $$
>
> Since $x$ is measured in hundreds of products, the business must sell **between 300 and 700 products, inclusive**.
>
> ### 3.
>
> The maximum profit occurs at the **vertex** of the parabola.
>
> From the graph, the vertex is $(5,16)$.
>
> Since $x=5$ means **500 products**, the business should sell **500 products**.
>
> Since $P(x)=16$ means **$16\,000$**, the maximum monthly profit is **$16\,000$**.
