Write the following expressions in fully factored form.

1.

$$
12x-15
$$
2.

$$
2x^2+x
$$
3.

$$
x^2-16
$$
4.

$$
25-4a^2
$$
5.

$$
x^3+9x
$$
6.

$$
x^3-9x
$$
7.

$$
16x-4x^2
$$
8.

$$
16x-4x^3
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> $$
> 12x-15
> =
> \boxed{3(4x-5)}.
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> $$
> 2x^2+x
> =
> \boxed{x(2x+1)}.
> $$
>
> ### 3.
>
> $$
> x^2-16
> =
> \boxed{(x-4)(x+4)}.
> $$
>
> ### 4.
>
> $$
> 25-4a^2
> =
> \boxed{(5-2a)(5+2a)}.
> $$
>
> ### 5.
>
> $$
> x^3+9x
> =
> \boxed{x(x^2+9)}.
> $$
>
> ### 6.
>
> $$
> x^3-9x
> =
> x(x^2-9)
> =
> \boxed{x(x-3)(x+3)}.
> $$
>
> ### 7.
>
> $$
> 16x-4x^2
> =
> \boxed{4x(4-x)}.
> $$
>
> ### 8.
>
> $$
> 16x-4x^3
> =
> 4x(4-x^2)
> =
> \boxed{4x(2-x)(2+x)}.
> $$
