## Question

The graph below shows the monthly profit of a small business.

![[Polynomial Functions 14 Graph.png]]

Here, $x$ is the number of **hundreds of products sold** and $P(x)$ is the profit in **thousands of dollars**.

1. How many products must be sold for the business to **break even**?
2. How many products should be sold to **maximize profit**?
3. What is the **maximum monthly profit**, in dollars?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> The business breaks even when the profit is $0$.
>
> From the graph, the x-intercepts are $(1,0)$ and $(7,0)$.
>
> Since $x$ is measured in **hundreds of products**, the business breaks even at **100 products** or **700 products**.
>
> ### 2.
>
> The maximum profit occurs at the **vertex** of the parabola.
>
> From the graph, the vertex is $(4,9)$.
>
> Since $x=4$ means **400 products**, the business should sell **400 products** to maximize profit.
>
> ### 3.
>
> The maximum value of the function is $9$.
>
> Since profit is measured in **thousands of dollars**, the maximum monthly profit is **$9,000**.
