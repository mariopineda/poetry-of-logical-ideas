### 1. Exponent Laws

Simplify using exponent laws. Express your answer with positive exponents.

$$
\frac{(4m^2n^3)^2}{16mn^2}
$$

### 2. Polynomial Operations

Simplify. Express your answer in descending order.

$$
2(3x^2-2x+5)-(2x^2+4x-3)
$$

### 3. Factoring

Factor fully.

$$
18x^5-98x^3
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1. Exponent Laws
>
> Apply the power of a product and power of a power rules:
>
> $$
> (4m^2n^3)^2=16m^4n^6.
> $$
>
> Then divide and subtract exponents on like bases:
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> \frac{16m^4n^6}{16mn^2}
> &=m^{4-1}n^{6-2}\\
> &=m^3n^4.
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> **Answer:** $\boxed{m^3n^4}$
>
> ### 2. Polynomial Operations
>
> Distribute:
>
> $$
> 6x^2-4x+10-2x^2-4x+3.
> $$
>
> Combine like terms:
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> (6x^2-2x^2)+(-4x-4x)+(10+3)
> &=4x^2-8x+13.
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> **Answer:** $\boxed{4x^2-8x+13}$
>
> ### 3. Factoring
>
> First factor out the greatest common factor:
>
> $$
> 18x^5-98x^3=2x^3(9x^2-49).
> $$
>
> The remaining binomial is a difference of squares:
>
> $$
> 9x^2-49=(3x)^2-7^2=(3x-7)(3x+7).
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> 18x^5-98x^3=2x^3(3x-7)(3x+7).
> $$
>
> **Answer:** $\boxed{2x^3(3x-7)(3x+7)}$
