## Question

The graph below shows the volume of water in a tank over time.

![[Polynomial Functions 12 Graph.png]]

1. How much water is in the tank after **5 minutes**?
2. After how many minutes is the tank empty?
3. State an appropriate **domain and range** for this situation.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> From the graph, the line passes through $(0,600)$ and $(15,0)$.
>
> ### 1.
>
> At $t=5$, the graph has a value of $V=400$.
>
> **400 L** of water remains after 5 minutes.
>
> ### 2.
>
> The tank is empty at the **x-intercept**.
>
> The x-intercept is $(15,0)$, so the tank is empty after **15 minutes**.
>
> ### 3.
>
> Since the situation starts at $t=0$ and ends when the tank is empty,
>
> $$
> 0\le t\le 15
> $$
>
> The volume starts at $600$ L and decreases to $0$ L, so
>
> $$
> 0\le V\le 600
> $$
