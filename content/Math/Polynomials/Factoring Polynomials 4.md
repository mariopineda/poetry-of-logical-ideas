1.

x^2+10x+24
$$
2.

$$
2x^2+7x+6
$$

$$
9a^3-39a^2+42a
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A. $x^2+10x+24$
>
> **Inspection ($a=1$).**
>
> Identify the numerical coefficients $a=1$, $b=10$, and $c=24$. Thus $ac=24$; we need two numbers with product $24$ and sum $10$. Since $ac$ is positive, the numbers have same signs.
>
> - **Sequential division:** Test divisors $1,2,3,\ldots$ of $|ac|=24$ until a factor pair has the required sum: $24\div1=24$ (sum $25$); $24\div2=12$ (sum $14$); $24\div3=8$ (sum $11$); $24\div4=6$ (sum $10$, **match**).
> - **Square-root-guided search:** $\sqrt{24}\approx 4.90$, so the possible smaller divisors end at $4$. Start at $4$ and work downward: $24\div4=6$ (sum $10$, **match**).
>
> The required numbers are $m=6$ and $n=4$: $6+(4)=10$ and $(6)(4)=24$.
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> x^2+10x+24 &= (x+4)(x+6)\\
> &=\boxed{(x+4)(x+6)}.
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> ### B. $2x^2+7x+6$
>
> **Decomposition ($a\ne1$).**
>
> Identify the numerical coefficients $a=2$, $b=7$, and $c=6$. Thus $ac=12$; we need two numbers with product $12$ and sum $7$. Since $ac$ is positive, the numbers have same signs.
>
> - **Sequential division:** Test divisors $1,2,3,\ldots$ of $|ac|=12$ until a factor pair has the required sum: $12\div1=12$ (sum $13$); $12\div2=6$ (sum $8$); $12\div3=4$ (sum $7$, **match**).
> - **Square-root-guided search:** $\sqrt{12}\approx 3.46$, so the possible smaller divisors end at $3$. Start at $3$ and work downward: $12\div3=4$ (sum $7$, **match**).
>
> The required numbers are $m=4$ and $n=3$: $4+(3)=7$ and $(4)(3)=12$.
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> 2x^2+7x+6 &=2x^2+4x+3x+6\\
> &=2x(x+2)+3(x+2)\\
> &=\boxed{(2x+3)(x+2)}.
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> ### C. $9a^3-39a^2+42a$
>
> First remove the greatest common factor:
>
> $$
> 9a^3-39a^2+42a=3a(3a^2-13a+14).
> $$
>
> **Decomposition ($a\ne1$).**
>
> Identify the numerical coefficients $a=3$, $b=-13$, and $c=14$. Thus $ac=42$; we need two numbers with product $42$ and sum $-13$. Since $ac$ is positive, the numbers have same signs.
>
> - **Sequential division:** Test divisors $1,2,3,\ldots$ of $|ac|=42$ until a factor pair has the required sum: $42\div1=42$ (sum $43$); $42\div2=21$ (sum $23$); $42\div3=14$ (sum $17$); $42\div4$ is not a whole number; $42\div5$ is not a whole number; $42\div6=7$ (sum $13$, **match**).
> - **Square-root-guided search:** $\sqrt{42}\approx 6.48$, so the possible smaller divisors end at $6$. Start at $6$ and work downward: $42\div6=7$ (sum $13$, **match**).
>
> The required numbers are $m=-7$ and $n=-6$: $-7+(-6)=-13$ and $(-7)(-6)=42$.
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> 9a^3-39a^2+42a &=3a(3a^2-6a-7a+14)\\
> &=3a[3a(a-2)-7(a-2)]\\
> &=\boxed{3a(3a-7)(a-2)}.
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
