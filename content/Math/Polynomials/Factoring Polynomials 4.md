## Question

Factor:

1. $$x^2+10x+24$$
2. $$2x^2+7x+6$$
3. $$9a^3-39a^2+42a$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> $a=1 \Rightarrow$ Factor by Method of Inspection. $b=10$ and $c=24 \Rightarrow 6+4=b=10$ and $6 \times 4=c=24$, hence $x^2+10x+24 = \boxed{(x+6)(x+4)}$
>
> ### B.
>
> $a \neq 1 \Rightarrow$ Factor by Method of Decomposition. $b=7$ and $a \times c = 2 \times 6=12 \Rightarrow 3+4=b=7$ and $4 \times 4 = a \times c=12 \Rightarrow$ decompose x-term
> $$
> \begin{align}
> &= 2x^2+4x+3x+6 \\
> &= 2x(x+2)+3(x+2) \\
> &= \boxed{(2x+3)(x+2)}
> \end{align}
> $$
>
> ### C.
>
> GCF=$3a \Rightarrow 3a(3a^2-13a+14)$. $a \neq 1 \Rightarrow$ Factor by Method of Decomposition. $b=-13$ and $a \times c=3 \times 14=42 \Rightarrow -6+(-7)=b=-13$ and $(-6)(-7)=a \times c=42 \Rightarrow$ decompose x-term.
> $$
> \begin{align}
> &= 3a(3a^2-6a-74+14) \\
> &= 3a[3a(a-2)-7(a-2)] \\
> &= \boxed{3a[(3a-7)(a-2)]}
> \end{align}
> $$
>
