## Question

The height of a soccer ball after it is kicked is modelled by

$$
h(t)=-4(t-2)^2+16
$$

where $h$ is the height of the ball, in metres, and $t$ is the time after the ball is kicked, in seconds.

1. **Sketch the graph of the ball's flight.** Your sketch must include:
   - labelled axes with variables and units
   - an appropriate scale
   - the vertex and intercepts clearly labelled
   - only the portion of the graph that is meaningful in this situation
2. Determine the **maximum height** of the ball and when it occurs.
3. Determine when the ball **hits the ground**.
4. State an appropriate **domain and range** for the ball's flight.

Use your graphing calculator as needed.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1. Sketch
>
> ![[Polynomial Functions 19 Solution Graph.png]]
>
> The important points are
>
> $$
> (0,0),\qquad (2,16),\qquad (4,0)
> $$
>
> ### 2. Maximum height
>
> The function is in vertex form:
>
> $$
> h(t)=-4(t-2)^2+16
> $$
>
> so the vertex is
>
> $$
> (2,16)
> $$
>
> The ball reaches a **maximum height of 16 m after 2 s**.
>
> ### 3. When the ball hits the ground
>
> The ball is on the ground when $h(t)=0$:
>
> $$
> -4(t-2)^2+16=0
> $$
>
> $$
> (t-2)^2=4
> $$
>
> $$
> t=0\quad\text{or}\quad t=4
> $$
>
> The ball starts on the ground at $t=0$ and returns to the ground after **4 s**.
>
> ### 4. Domain and range
>
> $$
> 0\le t\le4
> $$
>
> $$
> 0\le h\le16
> $$
