A vibration sensor recorded the following experimental results. A **quadratic regression model** $y=ax^2+bx+c$ is appropriate.

| Time $x$ (s) | 0 | 10 | 20 | 30 | 40 | 50 |
|:--|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| Reading $y$ (units) | 1.1 | 3.2 | 7.4 | 14.8 | 23.3 | 34.9 |

Use regression technology to determine $a,b,c$ to at least four decimal places. Then **predict the reading at $x=15$ seconds to the nearest tenth**. Use the unrounded coefficients for the prediction.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Quadratic least-squares regression gives approximately
>
> $$y=0.0115536x^2+0.0986071x+1.0607143.$$
>
> At $x=15$ seconds, using **unrounded** coefficients,
>
> $$y(15)\approx 5.13938\approx\boxed{5.1\text{ units}}.$$
>
> The readings are not exactly quadratic; a regression is required rather than simply taking second differences.
