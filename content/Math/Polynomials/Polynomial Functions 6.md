## Question

For each of the following functions determine the,

- degree
- leading coefficient
- numerical constant
- x-intercept(s)
- y-intercept
- domain
- range
- end bahaviour
- coordinates of turning point

Round decimals to the nearest hundreth.

1. $g(x) = -6x^2+4x-2$
2. $h(x) = -x^3+x+4$
3. $f(x) = (x+1)(x+2)$
4. $k(x) = 2x^2+6x+4$
5. $p(x) = x^3-2x^2+6x+4$

## Solution

> - leading coefficient: $-6$
> - numerical constant: $-2$
