A shipping crate is a rectangular prism. Its **length is twice its width**, and its **height is five times its width**. The volume is $17\,280\text{ cm}^3$.

Determine the crate's width, length, and height to the nearest tenth of a centimetre. Show the polynomial equation used, and explain why only one solution is physically meaningful.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Let the width be $x$ centimetres, so the length is $2x$ and the height is $5x$.
>
> The volume equation is
>
> $$
> 17\,280=x(2x)(5x)=10x^3.
> $$
>
> Thus
>
> $$
> x^3=1728\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x=\sqrt[3]{1728}=12.
> $$
>
> Because the dimensions must be positive, the physical solution has $x>0$. The dimensions, to the nearest tenth, are
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{Width }12.0\text{ cm},\quad\text{length }24.0\text{ cm},\quad\text{height }60.0\text{ cm}.}
> $$
>
> Check: $12(24)(60)=17\,280\text{ cm}^3$.
