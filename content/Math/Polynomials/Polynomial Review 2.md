## Question

Multiply.

1. $$4(x+3)$$
2. $$3(3x-2)$$
3. $$2(x^2-3x+5)$$
4. $$2x(3x-4)$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> 1. $$4(x+3) = 4x+12$$
> 2. $$3(3x-2) = 9x-6$$
> 3. $$2(x^2-3x+5) = 2x^2-6x+10$$
> 4. $$2x(3x-4) = 6x^2-8x$$
>
