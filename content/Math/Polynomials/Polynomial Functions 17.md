## Question

A farmer is building a rectangular pen using **40 m** of fencing. The graph below shows the area of the pen, $A(x)$, as a function of its width, $x$.

![[Polynomial Functions 17 Graph.png]]

1. What width gives the **maximum area**, and what is that maximum area?
2. For what widths is the area **at least $75\text{ m}^2$**?
3. If the width is chosen to give the maximum area, what is the **length** of the pen?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> The maximum area occurs at the **vertex** of the parabola.
>
> From the graph, the vertex is $(10,100)$.
>
> Therefore, the pen has a **maximum area of $100\text{ m}^2$** when the width is **10 m**.
>
> ### 2.
>
> The dashed line represents $A(x)=75$, or an area of **$75\text{ m}^2$**.
>
> From the graph, the parabola intersects this line at $x=5$ and $x=15$.
>
> Therefore, the area is **at least $75\text{ m}^2$** when
>
> $$
> 5\le x\le 15
> $$
>
> So the width must be **between 5 m and 15 m, inclusive**.
>
> ### 3.
>
> With 40 m of fencing, the length and width satisfy
>
> $$
> 2L+2W=40
> $$
>
> which simplifies to
>
> $$
> L+W=20
> $$
>
> When the width is $10$ m,
>
> $$
> L=20-10=10
> $$
>
> So the **length is 10 m**.
