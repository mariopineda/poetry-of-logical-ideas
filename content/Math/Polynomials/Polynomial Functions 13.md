## Question

The graph below shows the height of a ball during its flight.

![[Polynomial Functions 13 Graph.png]]

1. What is the **maximum height** of the ball, and when does it occur?
2. After it is thrown, when does the ball **return to the ground**?
3. State an appropriate **domain and range** for the ball's flight.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> The maximum value occurs at the **vertex** of the parabola.
>
> From the graph, the vertex is $(3,9)$.
>
> So the ball reaches a **maximum height of 9 m after 3 s**.
>
> ### 2.
>
> The ball is on the ground when the height is $0$.
>
> From the graph, the parabola crosses the t-axis at $t=0$ and $t=6$.
>
> Since the question asks when it **returns** to the ground, the answer is **6 s**.
>
> ### 3.
>
> The flight begins at $t=0$ and ends at $t=6$:
>
> $$
> 0\le t\le 6
> $$
>
> The height ranges from $0$ m to $9$ m:
>
> $$
> 0\le h\le 9
> $$
