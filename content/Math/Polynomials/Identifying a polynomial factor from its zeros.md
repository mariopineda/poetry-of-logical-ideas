Factor $f(x)=x^3-5x^2+2x+8$ completely. Demonstrate a factor using substitution.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Test the suggested root $x=-1$:
>
> $$f(-1)=(-1)^3-5(-1)^2+2(-1)+8=-1-5-2+8=0.$$
>
> Thus $(x+1)$ is a factor by the factor theorem. Polynomial division yields
>
> $$\frac{x^3-5x^2+2x+8}{x+1}=x^2-6x+8=(x-2)(x-4).$$
>
> Therefore the complete factorization is
>
> $$\boxed{f(x)=(x+1)(x-2)(x-4)}.$$
>
> Expanding verifies the original coefficients: $x^3-5x^2+2x+8$.
