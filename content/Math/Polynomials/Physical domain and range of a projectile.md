A foam ball is **launched upward** from a platform $5$ m above ground level. Its height after $t$ seconds is modelled by

$$h(t)=-5t^2+20t+5.$$

Determine the physically meaningful **domain and range** from launch until the ball first reaches the ground. Give exact bounds and approximate the flight duration to the nearest hundredth of a second.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The vertex occurs at $t=-b/(2a)=-20/(2(-5))=2$ s. Its height is $h(2)=-20+40+5=25$ m.
>
> To find landing time, solve $-5t^2+20t+5=0$, or $t^2-4t-1=0$:
>
> $$t=2\pm\sqrt5.$$
>
> Only $t=2+\sqrt5\approx4.24$ is after launch. The ball's path therefore has
>
> $$\boxed{0\le t\le2+\sqrt5\text{ s}},\qquad \boxed{0\le h\le25\text{ m}}.$$
>
> Negative time and the trajectory below ground are not physically meaningful here.
