## Question

### 1. Exponent Laws

Simplify using exponent laws. Express your answer with positive exponents.

$$
\frac{18a^7b^5}{6a^3b^2}
$$

### 2. Polynomial Operations

Simplify. Express your answer in descending order.

$$
(3x^2-4x+5)-(x^2+2x-3)
$$

### 3. Factoring

Factor fully.

$$
12x^3-75x
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1. Exponent Laws
>
> Divide the coefficients and subtract exponents on like bases:
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> \frac{18a^7b^5}{6a^3b^2}
> &=3a^{7-3}b^{5-2}\\
> &=3a^4b^3.
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> **Answer:** $\boxed{3a^4b^3}$
>
> ### 2. Polynomial Operations
>
> Distribute the subtraction through the second polynomial:
>
> $$
> 3x^2-4x+5-x^2-2x+3
> $$
>
> Combine like terms:
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> (3x^2-x^2)+(-4x-2x)+(5+3)
> &=2x^2-6x+8.
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> **Answer:** $\boxed{2x^2-6x+8}$
>
> ### 3. Factoring
>
> First factor out the greatest common factor:
>
> $$
> 12x^3-75x=3x(4x^2-25).
> $$
>
> The remaining binomial is a difference of squares:
>
> $$
> 4x^2-25=(2x)^2-5^2=(2x-5)(2x+5).
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> 12x^3-75x=3x(2x-5)(2x+5).
> $$
>
> **Answer:** $\boxed{3x(2x-5)(2x+5)}$
