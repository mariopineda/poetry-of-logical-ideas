An artisan cooperative pays a helper **3 dollars** on the first of seven consecutive workdays. Each day after the first, the helper is paid **twice** the previous day's amount.

Calculate the **total earnings over all seven days**, not just the payment on Day 7. Show the payments or derive a geometric-series formula.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The seven daily payments are $3,6,12,24,48,96,192$. They form a geometric sequence with first term $a=3$ and common ratio $r=2$.
>
> $$S_7=a\frac{r^7-1}{r-1}=3\frac{2^7-1}{2-1}=3(127)=\boxed{\$381}.$$
>
> Checking by direct addition gives the same total.
