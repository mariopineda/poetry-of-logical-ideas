Analyze the rational function

$$
r(x)=\frac{(4x+3)(x-2)}{(x+5)(x-2)}.
$$

Determine its **vertical asymptote**, **horizontal asymptote**, and **removable discontinuity**, including the hole's coordinates. Explain each result.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The original domain excludes $x=-5$ and $x=2$. For other $x$,
>
> $$
> r(x)=\frac{4x+3}{x+5}.
> $$
>
> The remaining denominator zero gives the vertical asymptote $\boxed{x=-5}$. Since numerator and denominator have the same degree, the ratio of leading coefficients gives the horizontal asymptote $\boxed{y=4}$.
>
> At $x=2$ the common factor was cancelled, so the original function has a hole at
>
> $$
> \left(2,\frac{4(2)+3}{2+5}\right)=\boxed{\left(2,\frac{11}{7}\right)}.
> $$
