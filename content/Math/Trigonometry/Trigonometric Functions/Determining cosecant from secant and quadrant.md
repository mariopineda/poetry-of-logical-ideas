For an angle $\theta$ in quadrant IV, $\sec\theta=17/15$. Determine the exact value of $\csc\theta$, showing the appropriate sign.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> From $\sec\theta=17/15$, $\cos\theta=15/17$. The $8$-$15$-$17$ triangle gives $|\sin\theta|=8/17$. Since the angle is in quadrant IV, $\sin\theta=-8/17$ and $\boxed{\csc\theta=-17/8}$.
