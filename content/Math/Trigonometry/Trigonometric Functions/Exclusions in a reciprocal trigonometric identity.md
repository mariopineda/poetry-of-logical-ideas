Consider the identity $\csc\theta\tan\theta=\sec\theta$. Show why the identity holds wherever the expressions are defined. List **all** real angles that must be excluded from its domain.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Using identities, $\csc\theta\tan\theta=\frac1{\sin\theta}\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}=\frac1{\cos\theta}=\sec\theta$. We must require both $\sin\theta\ne0$ and $\cos\theta\ne0$, so exclude $\boxed{\theta=\frac{n\pi}{2},\ n\in\mathbb Z}$.
