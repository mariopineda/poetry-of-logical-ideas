A lighthouse beam makes an angle $\theta$ in standard position, where $90^\circ\leq\theta\leq180^\circ$. Measurements show that

$$\log_3 k=\frac12,\qquad \csc\theta=k.$$

Determine $\theta$ to the nearest degree, showing how the logarithm and quadrant restriction are used.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> From the logarithm $k=\sqrt3$, so $\sin\theta=1/\sqrt3$. The reference angle is $\alpha=\sin^{-1}(1/\sqrt3)\approx35.264^\circ$. Quadrant II requires $\theta=180^\circ-\alpha\approx\boxed{145^\circ}$.
