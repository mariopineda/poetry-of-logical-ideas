The point $(-4,3)$ lies on the terminal arm of $\theta$ in standard position.

1. Find $\theta$, to the nearest tenth of a degree.
2. Find the exact values of the six trigonometric ratios.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> $$
> r=\sqrt{(-4)^2+3^2}=5.
> $$
>
> The point lies in Quadrant II, so
>
> $$
> \theta=180^\circ-\tan^{-1}\left(\frac34\right)
> \approx\boxed{143.1^\circ}.
> $$
>
> The six ratios are
>
> $$
> \boxed{\sin\theta=\frac35},
> \quad
> \boxed{\cos\theta=-\frac45},
> \quad
> \boxed{\tan\theta=-\frac34},
> $$
>
> $$
> \boxed{\csc\theta=\frac53},
> \quad
> \boxed{\sec\theta=-\frac54},
> \quad
> \boxed{\cot\theta=-\frac43}.
> $$
