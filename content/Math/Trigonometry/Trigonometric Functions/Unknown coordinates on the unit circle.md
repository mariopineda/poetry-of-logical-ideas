A point $P=(a,-3a)$ lies on the unit circle, with $a>0$. Determine the exact value of $a$ and the coordinates of $P$. State the quadrant.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> For a unit-circle point, $a^2+(-3a)^2=1$, so $10a^2=1$. Since $a>0$, $\boxed{a=1/\sqrt{10}}$. Then $\boxed{P=(1/\sqrt{10},-3/\sqrt{10})}$, in quadrant IV.
