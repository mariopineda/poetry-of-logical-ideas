An angle $\theta$ lies in quadrant IV and $\sec\theta=\frac{17}{15}$. Find the exact value of $\csc\theta$. Explain how the quadrant determines its sign.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Since $\cos\theta=15/17$, a $8$-$15$-$17$ triangle gives $|\sin\theta|=8/17$. In quadrant IV, sine is negative, so $\sin\theta=-8/17$ and $\boxed{\csc\theta=-17/8}$.
