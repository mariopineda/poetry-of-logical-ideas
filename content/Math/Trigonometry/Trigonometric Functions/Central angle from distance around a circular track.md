A small maintenance vehicle travels along the circular edge of a landscaped plaza of radius $18\,\mathrm{m}$. It travels $14\,\mathrm{m}$ along the arc, as shown in the schematic.

![[diploma-batch02-central-angle.svg]]

Determine the **central angle** swept out (a) exactly in radians and (b) to the nearest degree. Explain the relationship used.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Arc length and central angle satisfy $s=r\theta$ when $\theta$ is in radians. Therefore
>
> $$\theta=\frac{s}{r}=\frac{14}{18}=\boxed{\frac79\text{ rad}}.$$
>
> In degrees,
>
> $$\theta=\frac79\cdot\frac{180^\circ}{\pi}\approx44.56^\circ,$$
>
> so the answer to the nearest degree is $\boxed{45^\circ}$.
