The terminal arm of an angle $\theta$ passes through the unit-circle point

$$P\left(\frac12,\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\right).$$

Determine the exact point reached by the angle $\theta-\frac\pi2$ and explain your method using a clockwise rotation.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> A clockwise $90^\circ$ rotation maps $(x,y)$ to $(y,-x)$. Consequently $\boxed{P'\left(\frac{\sqrt3}{2},-\frac12\right)}$.
