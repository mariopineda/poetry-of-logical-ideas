A rotating laboratory arm sweeps an annular sector through an angle of $\frac{\pi}{3}$ radians. The inner edge is $8$ cm from the pivot and the outer edge is $18$ cm from it. The diagram shows the region swept, including its straight radial boundaries.

![[diploma-batch04-2014-mc7-annulus.svg]]

Calculate the **entire perimeter** of the swept region, including both curved edges, to the nearest centimetre. Explain each term of your formula.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> A swept annular sector has **two circular arcs** and **two straight radial boundaries**. The inner arc has length $r\theta=8(\pi/3)=8\pi/3$. The outer arc has length $R\theta=18\pi/3=6\pi$. Each radial boundary has length $R-r=18-8=10$ cm. Their sum is
>
> $$P=\frac{8\pi}{3}+6\pi+2(10)=\frac{26\pi}{3}+20\approx47.227\text{ cm}.$$
>
> Hence the total perimeter, to the nearest centimetre, is $\boxed{47\text{ cm}}$. The two straight segments must not be omitted.
