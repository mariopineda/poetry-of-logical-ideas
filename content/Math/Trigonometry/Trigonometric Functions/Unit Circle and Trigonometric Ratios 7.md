Using the unit circle, determine the exact values of

$$
\cos\pi,\qquad
\tan\frac{\pi}{2},\qquad
\sin\frac{5\pi}{4},\qquad
\sec\frac{7\pi}{4}.
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> $$
> \boxed{\cos\pi=-1}.
> $$
>
> Since $\cos\frac{\pi}{2}=0$,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\tan\frac{\pi}{2}\text{ is undefined}}.
> $$
>
> $$
> \boxed{\sin\frac{5\pi}{4}=-\frac{\sqrt2}{2}}.
> $$
>
> $$
> \sec\frac{7\pi}{4}
> =
> \frac{1}{\sqrt2/2}
> =
> \boxed{\sqrt2}.
> $$
