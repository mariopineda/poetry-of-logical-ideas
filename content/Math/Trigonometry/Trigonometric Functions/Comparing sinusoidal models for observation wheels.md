Two observation wheels begin with their passenger pods at the lowest point, $2\,\mathrm{m}$ above the ground. The wheels have diameters $16\,\mathrm{m}$ and $30\,\mathrm{m}$ and each takes $50$ seconds to complete one revolution.

![[diploma-batch02-observation-wheels.svg]]

Model each passenger's height with $h(t)=a\cos[b(t-c)]+d$, where $t$ is measured in seconds. Identify **which two parameters must differ** between the models and **which two may match**, explaining each choice.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The radii are $8$ and $15$ m, respectively. Each wheel starts at its lowest height of $2$ m, so the centre heights are $d_1=2+8=10$ m and $d_2=2+15=17$ m.
>
> Because each completes a revolution in $50$ s, both have angular frequency $b=2\pi/50=\pi/25$ rad/s. Taking $c=0$, a negative cosine amplitude makes the starting position the minimum. Valid models are
>
> $$h_1(t)=-8\cos\left(\frac\pi{25}t\right)+10,$$
> $$h_2(t)=-15\cos\left(\frac\pi{25}t\right)+17.$$
>
> Therefore the **amplitude parameter $a$** and **vertical shift $d$** must differ in magnitude/value, while $\boxed{b=\pi/25}$ and $\boxed{c=0}$ may be the same. In these models, $\boxed{a_1=-8,\ a_2=-15;\ d_1=10,\ d_2=17}$.
