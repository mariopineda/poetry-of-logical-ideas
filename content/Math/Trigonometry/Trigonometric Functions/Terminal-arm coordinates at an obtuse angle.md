Find the point where the terminal arm of $155^\circ$ meets the unit circle. Round both coordinates to two decimal places and justify the signs.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> A terminal arm at $155^\circ$ has unit-circle coordinates $(\cos155^\circ,\sin155^\circ)$. Because the angle is in Quadrant II, cosine is negative and sine is positive. Numerically,
>
> $$\cos155^\circ\approx-0.9063078,\qquad \sin155^\circ\approx0.4226183.$$
>
> Therefore the point, to two decimal places, is $\boxed{(-0.91,\ 0.42)}$.
