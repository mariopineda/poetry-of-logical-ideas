For an angle $\theta$ in standard position,

$$
\cos\theta=-\frac37,
\qquad
180^\circ<\theta<270^\circ.
$$

1. Find $\theta$ to the nearest tenth of a degree.
2. Find the exact value of $\csc\theta$. Rationalize the denominator.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The angle lies in Quadrant III.
>
> The reference angle is
>
> $$
> \alpha=\cos^{-1}\left(\frac37\right)\approx64.623^\circ.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\theta\approx244.6^\circ}.
> $$
>
> For the exact ratio, take $x=-3$ and $r=7$. Then
>
> $$
> y=-\sqrt{49-9}=-2\sqrt{10}.
> $$
>
> Hence,
>
> $$
> \sin\theta=-\frac{2\sqrt{10}}{7}
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> \csc\theta
> =
> -\frac{7}{2\sqrt{10}}
> =
> \boxed{-\frac{7\sqrt{10}}{20}}.
> $$
