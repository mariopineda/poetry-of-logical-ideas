Use a $30^\circ$-$60^\circ$-$90^\circ$ triangle to find the exact values of

$$
\sin30^\circ,\qquad \cos30^\circ,\qquad \tan30^\circ.
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> $$
> \boxed{\sin30^\circ=\frac12},
> $$
>
> $$
> \boxed{\cos30^\circ=\frac{\sqrt3}{2}},
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> \tan30^\circ
> =
> \frac{1}{\sqrt3}
> =
> \boxed{\frac{\sqrt3}{3}}.
> $$
