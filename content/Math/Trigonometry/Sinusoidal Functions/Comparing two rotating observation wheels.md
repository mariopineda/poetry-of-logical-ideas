Two observation wheels begin at their lowest points, $2$ m above the ground, and each completes one revolution in $36$ s. The first wheel has radius $9$ m and the second has radius $15$ m. For each wheel, write a cosine model for a passenger's height $h(t)$, with $t=0$ at the lowest point. Identify **which parameters are the same** and **which differ**, and explain why.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> A convenient lowest-point model is $h(t)=D-A\cos(\frac{2\pi}{T}t)$. Both wheels have period $T=36$, so the angular frequency is $\frac{\pi}{18}$ radians/s and the phase can be the same. For the first wheel, $D=2+9=11$, giving $\boxed{h_1(t)=11-9\cos(\frac{\pi t}{18})}$. For the second, $D=2+15=17$, giving $\boxed{h_2(t)=17-15\cos(\frac{\pi t}{18})}$. Amplitude and midline differ; period, phase and direction of travel can be the same.
