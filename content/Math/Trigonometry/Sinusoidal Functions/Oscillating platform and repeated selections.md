A laboratory platform moves up and down periodically. Its marker begins at its lowest height, **54 cm**, reaches a maximum of **82 cm** after 2 seconds, and returns to the minimum after 4 seconds. The pattern repeats. The platform is observed for 8 seconds.

**(a)** On the blank axes below, **sketch and label** a smooth sinusoidal height graph from $t=0$ to $t=8$, including every maximum and minimum.

![[batch12-blank-periodic-grid.svg]]

**(b)** State the amplitude, period and median (midline), including units.

**(c)** The entire platform is raised 6 cm while its motion remains otherwise unchanged. Explain what happens to its amplitude, period and median.

**(d)** Twelve distinct tickets are available, five of them green and seven blue. Two different tickets are chosen in succession without replacement. Determine the probability that both tickets are green, with reasoning.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> **(a)** The curve starts at $(0,54)$, reaches maxima $(2,82)$ and $(6,82)$, and returns to minima $(4,54)$ and $(8,54)$. Draw a smooth sinusoid through these points on the correctly scaled axes. One possible model is
>
> $$h(t)=68-14\cos\!\left(\frac{\pi t}{2}\right),\qquad 0\le t\le8.$$
>
> ![[batch12-periodic-solution.svg]]
>
> **(b)** The amplitude is half the vertical range: $\frac{82-54}{2}=\boxed{14\text{ cm}}$. The period is $\boxed{4\text{ s}}$ and the median is $\frac{82+54}{2}=\boxed{68\text{ cm}}$.
>
> **(c)** Raising the whole mechanism adds 6 to every height. The amplitude stays 14 cm, the period stays 4 s, and the median rises to $\boxed{74\text{ cm}}$.
>
> **(d)** There are five green tickets initially and four left after the first green is selected. Thus
>
> $$P(\text{both green})=\frac5{12}\cdot\frac4{11}=\boxed{\frac5{33}\approx0.1515}.$$
