## Question

The graph below represents a sinusoidal function.

![[Sinusoidal Functions - Characteristics from a Graph 2 Graph.png]]

Use the graph to determine:

1. the **maximum value** and **minimum value**;
2. the equation of the **midline**;
3. the **amplitude**;
4. the **period**;
5. the **range**.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> 1. **Maximum and minimum**
>
>    Maximum: $\boxed{5}$
>
>    Minimum: $\boxed{-3}$
>
> 2. **Midline**
>
>    $$
>    y=\frac{5+(-3)}{2}=1
>    $$
>
>    Therefore, the equation of the midline is
>
>    $$
>    \boxed{y=1}.
>    $$
>
> 3. **Amplitude**
>
>    $$
>    \text{amplitude}=\frac{5-(-3)}{2}=4
>    $$
>
>    Therefore,
>
>    $$
>    \boxed{4}.
>    $$
>
> 4. **Period**
>
>    The horizontal distance between corresponding points on consecutive cycles is
>
>    $$
>    \boxed{180^\circ}.
>    $$
>
> 5. **Range**
>
>    $$
>    \boxed{\{y\mid -3\le y\le 5,\ y\in\mathbb{R}\}}.
>    $$
