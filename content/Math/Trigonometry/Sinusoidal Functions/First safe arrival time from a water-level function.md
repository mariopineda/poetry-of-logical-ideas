The water depth at a small harbour is modelled by

$$d(t)=12.7+2\sin(0.42t+4.25),$$

where $d$ is in metres, $t$ is hours after midnight, and all angles are in **radians**. A boat needs a water depth of at least $13.1$ m.

Determine the **earliest time after midnight** when the boat may enter. Give your answer to the nearest hundredth of an hour, and explain how you selected the first permissible solution.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> At the threshold,
>
> $$12.7+2\sin(0.42t+4.25)=13.1\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\sin(0.42t+4.25)=0.2.$$
>
> At midnight, $d(0)=12.7+2\sin(4.25)\approx10.91<13.1$ m. The angle $4.25$ lies after $\pi$ and before $3\pi/2$, so the depth first decreases to a minimum and then rises.
>
> The first subsequent *rising* solution for the required depth is
>
> $$0.42t+4.25=2\pi+\arcsin(0.2).$$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$t=\frac{2\pi+\arcsin(0.2)-4.25}{0.42}\approx\boxed{5.32\text{ h after midnight}}.$$
>
> This is about $05{:}19$ local time (the decimal-hour result is primary).
