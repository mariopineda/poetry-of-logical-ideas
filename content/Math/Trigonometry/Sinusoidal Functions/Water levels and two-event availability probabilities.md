A floating sensor in a pumped-storage reservoir follows a controlled water-level cycle. Its height, $h(t)$ metres, above a reference level $t$ hours after monitoring begins, is modelled by
$$h(t)=7+3\sin\left(\frac{\pi t}{6}\right).$$

**(a)** Determine the minimum and maximum height.

**(b)** Determine the shortest interval between a low point and the next high point. Explain how the period determines the result.

Two local conservation volunteers, **A** and **B**, separately request approval to join an overnight survey. Their approval outcomes are recorded in a partially completed Venn diagram. The rectangle represents all possible outcomes.

![[diploma-2021-venn-partial-original.svg]]

**(c)** Complete the missing overlap probability in the diagram. Determine the **odds in favour** of volunteer B being approved. Show how you used the Venn regions.

**(d)** On the blank Venn diagram, shade the event $A\cup B$. Explain in words what this union means in the survey context and determine its probability.

![[diploma-2021-venn-blank-original.svg]]

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> **(a)** The midline is $7$ m and the amplitude is $3$ m. Therefore the minimum is $7-3=\boxed{4\text{ m}}$ and the maximum is $7+3=\boxed{10\text{ m}}$.
>
> **(b)** The angular frequency is $\pi/6$, so one complete period is
> $$T=\frac{2\pi}{\pi/6}=12\text{ h}.$$
> A minimum and the next maximum are half a period apart. The shortest waiting time is $\boxed{6\text{ h}}$.
>
> **(c)** The probabilities of the four disjoint Venn regions must add to $1$. Write $p$ for the missing intersection:
> $$0.28+p+0.18+0.12=1\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\boxed{p=0.42}.$$
> Thus $P(B)=0.42+0.18=0.60$ and $P(B^c)=0.40$. The **odds in favour** of B's approval are
> $$\boxed{P(B):P(B^c)=0.60:0.40=3:2}.$$
>
> ![[diploma-2021-venn-completed-original.svg]]
>
> **(d)** Shade every point inside circle A **or** circle B, including their intersection; leave the region outside both circles unshaded.
>
> ![[diploma-2021-venn-union-solution.svg]]
>
> The event $A\cup B$ means **at least one of the two volunteers is approved** (possibly both). Its probability is
> $$P(A\cup B)=0.28+0.42+0.18=\boxed{0.88}=1-0.12.$$
