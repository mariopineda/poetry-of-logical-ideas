## Question

The graph below represents a sinusoidal function.

![[Sinusoidal Functions - Radian Graph Characteristics 2 Graph.png]]

Use the graph to answer the following:

1. What is the **period** of the graph?
2. Find the **maximum** and **minimum** values.
3. Using the maximum and minimum values only, determine:
   - the **range**
   - the equation of the **midline**
   - the **amplitude**

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1. Period
>
> A maximum and the next minimum are **half a period apart**.
>
> From the graph, the maximum occurs at $x=0$ and the minimum occurs at $x=\frac{\pi}{2}$.
>
> So half the period is
>
> $$
> \frac{\pi}{2}-0=\frac{\pi}{2}
> $$
>
> Therefore, the period is
>
> $$
> 2\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right)=\pi
> $$
>
> So the period is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\pi}
> $$
>
> ### 2. Maximum and minimum values
>
> Maximum:
>
> $$
> \boxed{3}
> $$
>
> Minimum:
>
> $$
> \boxed{-5}
> $$
>
> ### 3. Range, midline, and amplitude
>
> The range is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\{y\mid -5\le y\le 3,\ y\in\mathbb{R}\}}
> $$
>
> The midline is
>
> $$
> y=\frac{3+(-5)}{2}=-1
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> \boxed{y=-1}
> $$
>
> The amplitude is
>
> $$
> \text{amplitude}=\frac{3-(-5)}{2}=4
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> \boxed{4}
> $$
