A sensor attached to a rotating circular frame records its **vertical height above the floor**, in centimetres. The frame is illustrated below, and the height is modelled by

$$p(t)=12\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{4}t\right)+28,$$

where $t$ is time in seconds.

![[batch10-rotating-sensor.svg]]

Determine the **amplitude**, the **period**, the **minimum and maximum heights**, and the physical meaning of the midline.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The amplitude is $|12|=\boxed{12\text{ cm}}$, which is the rotation radius.
>
> The period is
>
> $$T=\frac{2\pi}{\pi/4}=\boxed{8\text{ s}}.$$
>
> The midline is $p=\boxed{28\text{ cm}}$, the centre height. Therefore the minimum is $28-12=\boxed{16\text{ cm}}$ and the maximum is $28+12=\boxed{40\text{ cm}}$.
