## Question

Consider the sinusoidal function

$$
y=2\sin(4x)+3
$$

for

$$
0\le x\le 2\pi.
$$

1. State the values of $a$, $b$, $c$, and $d$ when the function is written in the form

$$
y=a\sin(bx+c)+d.
$$

2. Determine the:
   - **amplitude**;
   - equation of the **midline**;
   - **period**.

3. Determine the **maximum value**, **minimum value**, and **range**.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1. Parameters
>
> Comparing
>
> $$
> y=2\sin(4x)+3
> $$
>
> with
>
> $$
> y=a\sin(bx+c)+d,
> $$
>
> gives
>
> $$
> \boxed{a=2,\quad b=4,\quad c=0,\quad d=3}.
> $$
>
> ### 2. Amplitude, midline, and period
>
> The amplitude is
>
> $$
> \boxed{2}.
> $$
>
> The equation of the midline is
>
> $$
> \boxed{y=3}.
> $$
>
> The period is
>
> $$
> \frac{2\pi}{b}
> =\frac{2\pi}{4}
> =\frac{\pi}{2}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{period}=\frac{\pi}{2}}.
> $$
>
> ### 3. Maximum, minimum, and range
>
> The maximum value is
>
> $$
> d+a=3+2=5.
> $$
>
> The minimum value is
>
> $$
> d-a=3-2=1.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{maximum}=5}
> $$
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{minimum}=1}
> $$
>
> and the range is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\{y\mid 1\le y\le 5,\ y\in\mathbb{R}\}}.
> $$
