## Question

The graph below represents a sinusoidal function.

![[Sinusoidal Functions - Radian Graph Characteristics 3 Graph.png]]

Use the graph to answer the following:

1. What is the **period** of the graph?
2. Find the **maximum** and **minimum** values.
3. Using the maximum and minimum values only, determine:
   - the **range**
   - the equation of the **midline**
   - the **amplitude**

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1. Period
>
> A maximum and the next minimum are **half a period apart**.
>
> From the graph, the maximum occurs at $x=\pi$ and the minimum occurs at $x=3\pi$.
>
> So half the period is
>
> $$
> 3\pi-\pi=2\pi
> $$
>
> Therefore, the period is
>
> $$
> 2\left(2\pi\right)=4\pi
> $$
>
> So the period is
>
> $$
> \boxed{4\pi}
> $$
>
> ### 2. Maximum and minimum values
>
> Maximum:
>
> $$
> \boxed{4}
> $$
>
> Minimum:
>
> $$
> \boxed{0}
> $$
>
> ### 3. Range, midline, and amplitude
>
> The range is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\{y\mid 0\le y\le 4,\ y\in\mathbb{R}\}}
> $$
>
> The midline is
>
> $$
> y=\frac{4+(0)}{2}=2
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> \boxed{y=2}
> $$
>
> The amplitude is
>
> $$
> \text{amplitude}=\frac{4-(0)}{2}=2
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> \boxed{2}
> $$
