## Question

Create a sinusoidal function of the form

$$
y=a\sin(bx)+d
$$

that has the following characteristics:

- range:

$$
\{y\mid 1\le y\le 7,\ y\in\mathbb{R}\}
$$

- period: **8**

Show how you determined the values of $a$, $b$, and $d$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The amplitude is half the difference between the maximum and minimum values:
>
> $$
> a=\frac{7-(1)}{2}=3.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{a=3}.
> $$
>
> The midline is halfway between the maximum and minimum values:
>
> $$
> d=\frac{7+(1)}{2}=4.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{d=4}.
> $$
>
> Since
>
> $$
> \text{period}=\frac{2\pi}{b},
> $$
>
> we have
>
> $$
> 8=\frac{2\pi}{b}.
> $$
>
> Solving for $b$ gives
>
> $$
> \boxed{b=\frac{\pi}{4}}.
> $$
>
> One possible equation is
>
> $$
> \boxed{y=3\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{4}x\right)+4}.
> $$
