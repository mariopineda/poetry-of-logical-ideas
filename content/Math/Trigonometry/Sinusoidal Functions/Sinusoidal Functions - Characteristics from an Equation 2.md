## Question

Consider the sinusoidal function

$$
y=4\sin(3x)-2
$$

for

$$
0\le x\le 2\pi.
$$

1. State the values of $a$, $b$, $c$, and $d$ when the function is written in the form

$$
y=a\sin(bx+c)+d.
$$

2. Determine the:
   - **amplitude**;
   - equation of the **midline**;
   - **period**.

3. Determine the **maximum value**, **minimum value**, and **range**.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1. Parameters
>
> Comparing
>
> $$
> y=4\sin(3x)-2
> $$
>
> with
>
> $$
> y=a\sin(bx+c)+d,
> $$
>
> gives
>
> $$
> \boxed{a=4,\quad b=3,\quad c=0,\quad d=-2}.
> $$
>
> ### 2. Amplitude, midline, and period
>
> The amplitude is
>
> $$
> \boxed{4}.
> $$
>
> The equation of the midline is
>
> $$
> \boxed{y=-2}.
> $$
>
> The period is
>
> $$
> \frac{2\pi}{b}
> =\frac{2\pi}{3}
> =\frac{2\pi}{3}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{period}=\frac{2\pi}{3}}.
> $$
>
> ### 3. Maximum, minimum, and range
>
> The maximum value is
>
> $$
> d+a=-2+4=2.
> $$
>
> The minimum value is
>
> $$
> d-a=-2-4=-6.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{maximum}=2}
> $$
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{minimum}=-6}
> $$
>
> and the range is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\{y\mid -6\le y\le 2,\ y\in\mathbb{R}\}}.
> $$
