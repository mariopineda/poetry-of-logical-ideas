An autonomous garden cart is tethered at the outside corner of a rectangular greenhouse. The greenhouse blocks one quadrant of its movement, so the cart traces **three-quarters of a circular path**. Its travelled arc measures $18\pi$ metres.

![[diploma-2016-arc-route-original.svg]]

Determine the **radius of the complete circle** (the tether length). Explain how the restricted arc changes the circumference calculation.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The cart moves through $\tfrac34$ of a complete circle. With radius $r$ the total circumference would be $2\pi r$, so its accessible arc has length
>
> $$s=\frac34(2\pi r)=\frac{3\pi r}{2}.$$
>
> Set the arc equal to $18\pi$ metres:
>
> $$18\pi=\frac{3\pi r}{2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\boxed{r=12\text{ m}}.$$
>
> Check: $\tfrac34(2\pi\cdot12)=18\pi$ m, matching the stated arc.
