Draw and label the unit circle. Each quadrant should include the standard exact angles based on $\frac{\pi}{6}$, $\frac{\pi}{4}$, and $\frac{\pi}{3}$, together with their exact coordinates.

Then evaluate exactly:

$$
\cos^2\left(\frac{\pi}{6}\right)
-2\tan\left(\frac{9\pi}{4}\right)
+3\sin(-7\pi).
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> On the unit circle, the first-quadrant reference coordinates are
>
> $$
> \left(\frac{\sqrt3}{2},\frac12\right),
> \quad
> \left(\frac{\sqrt2}{2},\frac{\sqrt2}{2}\right),
> \quad
> \left(\frac12,\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\right),
> $$
>
> with the appropriate signs in the other quadrants.
>
> Now,
>
> $$
> \cos^2\left(\frac{\pi}{6}\right)=\frac34,
> $$
>
> $$
> \tan\left(\frac{9\pi}{4}\right)=1,
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> \sin(-7\pi)=0.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \frac34-2+0
> =\boxed{-\frac54}.
> $$
