A learner attempts to solve the following equation over $0\le x<2\pi$:

$$2\sin^2x+\cos^2x+\sin x-1=0.$$

Their steps are:

1. $\sin^2x+\sin x=0$
2. $\sin x(\sin x+1)=0$
3. $\sin x=0$ or $\sin x=-1$
4. $x=0$ or $x=3\pi/2$

Identify the **first incorrect or incomplete step**, explain the error, and give the complete solution set.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Use $\sin^2x+\cos^2x=1$:
>
> $$2\sin^2x+\cos^2x+\sin x-1=\sin^2x+\sin x=\sin x(\sin x+1).$$
>
> Steps 1–3 are correct. **Step 4 is incomplete** because $\sin x=0$ at both $x=0$ and $x=\pi$ in the specified interval. Meanwhile, $\sin x=-1$ only at $x=3\pi/2$.
>
> $$\boxed{x\in\{0,\pi,3\pi/2\}}.$$
>
> Each listed angle makes at least one factor zero, and these are all possibilities on $[0,2\pi)$.
