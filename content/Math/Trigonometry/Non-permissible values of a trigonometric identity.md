Determine the non-permissible values of $\theta$ for the identity

$$\frac{1-\sin^2\theta}{1-\cos^2\theta}=\frac{\cos^2\theta}{\sin^2\theta}.$$

Write all excluded angles in radians using an integer parameter and explain why these values must be excluded **before** simplifying.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The denominators in the original equation are $1-\cos^2\theta=\sin^2\theta$ and $\sin^2\theta$. Both vanish precisely when $\sin\theta=0$.
>
> Thus
>
> $$\boxed{\theta=n\pi,\qquad n\in\mathbb Z}$$
>
> are excluded. The numerator identity $1-\sin^2\theta=\cos^2\theta$ holds everywhere, but it does **not** permit division by zero at the excluded angles.
