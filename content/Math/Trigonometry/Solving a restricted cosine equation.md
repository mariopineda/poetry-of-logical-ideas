Solve $\cos(x-15^\circ)=\frac12$ for $0^\circ<x<90^\circ$. Give the answer to the nearest degree and justify the solution in the specified interval.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Let $u=x-15^\circ$. The stated interval $0^\circ<x<90^\circ$ means $-15^\circ<u<75^\circ$.
>
> Solutions to $\cos u=\tfrac12$ are $u=\pm60^\circ+360^\circ k$, $k\in\mathbb Z$. In the interval $(-15^\circ,75^\circ)$ only $u=60^\circ$ is allowed, so
>
> $$x-15^\circ=60^\circ\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\boxed{x=75^\circ}.$$
>
> Check: $\cos(75^\circ-15^\circ)=\cos60^\circ=1/2$.
