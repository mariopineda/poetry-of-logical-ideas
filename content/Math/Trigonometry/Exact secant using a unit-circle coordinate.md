A point $P(k,\tfrac5{13})$ lies on the terminal arm of a standard-position angle $\theta$ on the **unit circle**, as shown.

![[diploma-batch05-2016-mc17-unit-circle.svg]]

Determine $k$ and the exact value of $\sec\theta$. Explain how the quadrant determines the sign.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> On the unit circle, $k^2+(5/13)^2=1$, so
>
> $$
> k^2=1-\frac{25}{169}=\frac{144}{169}.
> $$
>
> Quadrant II has a negative $x$-coordinate, hence $\boxed{k=-\frac{12}{13}}$. Since $\cos\theta=k$,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\sec\theta=\frac{1}{\cos\theta}=-\frac{13}{12}}.
> $$
