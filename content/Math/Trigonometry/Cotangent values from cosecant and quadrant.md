Suppose $\csc\theta=\frac53$ and $0\le\theta<2\pi$.

Determine every possible quadrant of $\theta$ and the **exact value of $\cot\theta$ in each quadrant**. Show how you determine the signs.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Since $\csc\theta=5/3$, $\sin\theta=3/5>0$, so $\theta$ is in Quadrant I or II (it is not on an axis because $\sin\theta\ne0,1$).
>
> By the Pythagorean identity,
>
> $$
> \cos^2\theta=1-\frac9{25}=\frac{16}{25},
> \qquad\cos\theta=\pm\frac45.
> $$
>
> Therefore
>
> $$
> \cot\theta=\frac{\cos\theta}{\sin\theta}
> =\begin{cases}
> \boxed{\frac43},&\text{Quadrant I},\\[2pt]
> \boxed{-\frac43},&\text{Quadrant II}.
> \end{cases}
> $$
