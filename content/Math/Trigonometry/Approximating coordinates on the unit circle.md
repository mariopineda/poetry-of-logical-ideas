The terminal arm of an angle of $35^\circ$ intersects the unit circle at $P(x,y)$. Determine the coordinates to the nearest hundredth, showing the sine and cosine relationship.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The coordinates on the unit circle at an angle of $35^\circ$ are
>
> $$x=\cos35^\circ\approx0.8191520,\qquad y=\sin35^\circ\approx0.5735764.$$
>
> Rounding each coordinate separately to the nearest hundredth yields
>
> $$\boxed{P(0.82,\,0.57)}.$$
>
> Both coordinates are positive because the angle lies in Quadrant I; $x^2+y^2=1$ before rounding.
