Find the unique angle in the interval $[0,2\pi)$ that is coterminal with

$$
-\frac{17\pi}{6}.
$$

Show how you used complete revolutions to choose the specified representative.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> A full revolution is $2\pi=\frac{12\pi}{6}$. Adding one revolution gives $-\frac{5\pi}{6}$, still negative. Adding another gives
>
> $$
> -\frac{17\pi}{6}+\frac{24\pi}{6}
> =\boxed{\frac{7\pi}{6}}.
> $$
>
> Because $0\le\frac{7\pi}{6}<2\pi$, the answer is in the required interval and is the unique representative there.
