## Question

1. Draw and label $\triangle ABC$ with $\angle B=27^\circ$, $a=10 \mbox{ cm}$, $\angle A=90^\circ$.
2. Determine $c$ to the nearest tenth.
3. Determine $\angle C$ to the nearest whole degree.
4. Determine $b$ to the nearest tenth.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> ![[Images/Legacy/solvingtriangles.png]]
>
> ### B.
>
> $$
> \begin{array}{rcl}
> \cos{27^\circ} & = & \frac{\displaystyle c}{\displaystyle a} = \frac{\displaystyle c}{\displaystyle 10} \\
> c & = & 10 \times \cos{27^\circ}\\
> c & = & 8.9100... \approx 8.9 \mbox{ cm}
> \end{array}
> $$
>
> ### C.
>
> $$
> \begin{array}{rcl}
> \sin{\angle C} & = & \frac{\displaystyle c}{\displaystyle a} = \frac{\displaystyle 8.9...}{\displaystyle 10} \\
> \angle C & = & \sin^{-1}{\left( \frac{\displaystyle 8.9...}{\displaystyle 10} \right)} \\
> \angle C & = & 63^\circ
> \end{array}
> $$
>
> ### D.
>
> The length of edge $b$ can be determined by using the Pythagorean Theorem or a primary trigonometric ratio. Here we are using the Pythagorean Theorem,
> $$
> \begin{array}{rcl}
> b^2 + 8.9...^2 & = & 10^2 \\
> b^2 & = & 10^2-8.9...^2 \\
> b & = & \sqrt{10^2-8.9...^2} \\
> b & = & 4.5399... \approx 4.5 \mbox{ cm}
> \end{array}
> $$
>
