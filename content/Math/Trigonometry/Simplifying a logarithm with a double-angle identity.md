For $0^\circ<x<90^\circ$, simplify the expression below into a difference of logarithms. State the trigonometric identity used and justify the logarithmic domain.

$$
\log\left(\frac{3\sin x}{\sin(2x)}\right)
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Use the double-angle identity $\sin(2x)=2\sin x\cos x$:
>
> $$
> \frac{3\sin x}{\sin(2x)}
> =\frac{3\sin x}{2\sin x\cos x}
> =\frac{3}{2\cos x}.
> $$
>
> The quotient law gives
>
> $$
> \boxed{\log\!\left(\frac32\right)-\log(\cos x)}.
> $$
>
> On $(0^\circ,90^\circ)$ both $\sin x$ and $\cos x$ are positive, so no denominator is zero and each logarithm argument is positive.
