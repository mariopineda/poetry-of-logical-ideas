The universal set is $U=\{1,2,\ldots,150\}$. A number is a **perfect cube** if it equals $k^3$ for some positive integer $k$.

Find how many members of U are:

### A. both **even** and perfect cubes;
### B. both **odd** and perfect cubes;
### C. **not** perfect cubes.

List the perfect cubes before counting.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The cubes not exceeding 150 are
>
> $$1^3=1,\quad2^3=8,\quad3^3=27,\quad4^3=64,\quad5^3=125.$$
>
> Because $6^3=216>150$, there are five cubes in U.
>
> ### A.
>
> Even cubes: $\{8,64\}$, so $\boxed{2}$.
>
> ### B.
>
> Odd cubes: $\{1,27,125\}$, so $\boxed{3}$.
>
> ### C.
>
> There are 150 integers altogether and five cubes:
>
> $$150-5=\boxed{145}.$$
