The Venn diagram shows two sets $P$ and $Q$ within the universal set $U$.

![[diploma-batch08-2016-mc4-nested-sets.svg]]

Determine $P\cup Q$, $P\cap Q$, and whether $Q\subseteq P$. Justify each statement from the diagram rather than relying only on a symbolic rule.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Every point in $Q$ is also inside $P$, so $\boxed{Q\subseteq P}$. The union includes everything in either circle, which adds nothing to $P$ when $Q$ is inside it:
>
> $$\boxed{P\cup Q=P}.$$
>
> The intersection includes the points in both sets. Since all points of $Q$ are also in $P$,
>
> $$\boxed{P\cap Q=Q}.$$
>
> The circles do intersect: $P\cap Q\ne\varnothing$.
