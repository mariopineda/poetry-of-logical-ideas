Fifty people answer whether they like **wildlife documentaries (W)** and **history documentaries (H)**.

- 21 like only wildlife;
- 14 like only history;
- 9 like neither.

### A. Draw a Venn diagram.
![[Counting - SV-09 - Question.png]]
### B. Determine how many like (i) both genres, (ii) wildlife in total, and (iii) history in total.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> The missing overlap is
>
> $$50-21-14-9=\boxed{6}.$$
>
> ![[Counting - SV-09 - Solution.png]]
>
> ### B.
>
> 1. Both genres: $\boxed{6}$.
> 2. Wildlife total: $21+6=\boxed{27}$.
> 3. History total: $14+6=\boxed{20}$.
