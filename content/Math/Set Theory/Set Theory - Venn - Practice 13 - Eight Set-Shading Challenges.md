For each expression, **shade exactly the indicated region** in a Venn diagram. The rectangle represents the universe U. Complement notation $X'$ always means $U\setminus X$.

Use a two-circle diagram for parts A–D and a three-circle diagram for parts E–H.

### A. $A'$
### B. $A\cap B'$
### C. $B\cap\varnothing$
### D. $A\cup B$
### E. $(A\cap C)\cup B$
### F. $(A\cup B)'$
### G. $C\setminus(A\cap B)$
### H. $(A\cap B')\cup C'$

![Question diagram](../../Assets/Counting%20-%20SV-13%20-%20Question.png)

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The eight solved diagrams shade **only** regions satisfying the expression; areas inside the universe but outside the shaded result are excluded.
>
> ![Solution diagram](../../Assets/Counting%20-%20SV-13%20-%20Solution.png)
>
> ### A.
>
> $A'$: everything in U **outside** A.
>
> ### B.
>
> $A\cap B'$: inside A and **not** B (A-only).
>
> ### C.
>
> $B\cap\varnothing=\boxed{\varnothing}$, so **nothing is shaded**.
>
> ### D.
>
> $A\cup B$: everything in A or B or both.
>
> ### E.
>
> $(A\cap C)\cup B$: all of B, plus any remaining part of the A–C overlap.
>
> ### F.
>
> $(A\cup B)'$: everything outside **both** A and B.
>
> ### G.
>
> $C\setminus(A\cap B)$: the part of C except the **three-way overlap**.
>
> ### H.
>
> $(A\cap B')\cup C'$: everything outside C, together with the part of A outside B.
