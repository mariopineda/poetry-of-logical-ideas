The universe $U$ contains books numbered 1–24. A library creates these sets:

$$V=\{1,\ldots,18\},\quad M=\{1,\ldots,8\},$$
$$F=\{9,10,11,12\},\quad N=\{13,14,15,16,17,18\}.$$

![[Counting - SS-03 - Question.png]]

### A. Write all of the subset statements among $V,M,F,N$ that follow directly from these lists.
### B. Propose meaningful categories the four letters might represent.
### C. Is $M'=V$, where the complement is taken in U?
### D. Identify disjoint pairs among $M,F,N$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ![[Counting - SS-03 - Solution.png]]
>
> ### A.
>
> $M\subseteq V$, $F\subseteq V$ and $N\subseteq V$. Also, all four are subsets of U.
>
> ### B.
>
> For example, V could represent 18 fiction titles; M eight mystery books, F four fantasy books, and N six novels of another genre. Books 19–24 are nonfiction. The diagram would be valid because the three categories are mutually exclusive here.
>
> ### C.
>
> **No.** $M'=\{9,10,\ldots,24\}$, whereas $V=\{1,\ldots,18\}$. The complement includes numbers outside V and excludes all members of M.
>
> ### D.
>
> $$\boxed{M\cap F=\varnothing,\quad M\cap N=\varnothing,\quad F\cap N=\varnothing}.$$
