Let $U$ be the integers from $-12$ through $12$, inclusive. Define

$$P=\{1,2,\ldots,12\},\qquad N=\{-12,-11,\ldots,-1\}.$$

### A. Draw a Venn diagram with P, N and the element 0.
![[Counting - SS-08 - Question.png]]
### B. Which of P and N are disjoint?
### C. State true or false and explain:
1. $P\subseteq N$
2. $N\subseteq P$
3. $P'=N$
4. $n(P)=n(N)$
5. There are elements of U between -20 and -15.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> ![[Counting - SS-08 - Solution.png]]
>
> ### B.
>
> $$\boxed{P\cap N=\varnothing}.$$
>
> ### C.
>
> 1. **False:** positive integers are not negative integers.
> 2. **False:** negative integers are not positive integers.
> 3. **False:** $P'=N\cup\{0\}$, which includes zero.
> 4. **True:** both sets contain exactly 12 integers.
> 5. **False:** all integers from -20 to -15 lie outside U, so their intersection with U is the empty set.
