Consider the universal set of prime numbers less than $20$:

$$
U=\{2,3,5,7,11,13,17,19\}.
$$

Let

$$
A=\{3,5,7,11,19\}
$$

and

$$
B=\{2,3,7,13\}.
$$

1. Draw a Venn diagram to illustrate this information.
2. List the elements of $A\cap B$.
3. List the elements of $A\cup B$.
4. List the elements of $A^c$.
5. List the elements of $(A\cup B)^c$.
6. List the elements of $(A\cap B)^c$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> For the Venn diagram:
>
> - $A\cap B=\{3,7\}$ goes in the overlap;
> - $A$ only contains $\{5,11,19\}$;
> - $B$ only contains $\{2,13\}$;
> - $17$ lies outside both circles but inside $U$.
>
> ### 2.
>
> $$
> \boxed{A\cap B=\{3,7\}}
> $$
>
> ### 3.
>
> $$
> \boxed{A\cup B=\{2,3,5,7,11,13,19\}}
> $$
>
> ### 4.
>
> $$
> A^c=U\setminus A,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> \boxed{A^c=\{2,13,17\}}.
> $$
>
> ### 5.
>
> $$
> (A\cup B)^c
> =
> U\setminus(A\cup B),
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> \boxed{(A\cup B)^c=\{17\}}.
> $$
>
> ### 6.
>
> $$
> (A\cap B)^c
> =
> U\setminus(A\cap B),
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> \boxed{(A\cap B)^c=\{2,5,11,13,17,19\}}.
> $$
