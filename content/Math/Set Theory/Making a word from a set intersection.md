Two library catalogues list the letters used in their sorting codes:

$$M=\{a,e,l,m,n,r,t\},\qquad N=\{a,c,e,l,n,o,t\}.$$

Determine $M\cap N$. Then construct a meaningful **four-letter word** from distinct letters of this intersection, using no letter more than once. Justify your choice.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The common elements are exactly
>
> $$M\cap N=\boxed{\{a,e,l,n,t\}}.$$
>
> One valid four-letter word is **LATE**: its letters $l,a,t,e$ belong to the intersection and none repeats. Other valid words are acceptable if they satisfy both conditions.
