A set of 12 labelled design icons is

$$U=\{A,B,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,J,K,L\}.$$

Two inspectors classify them by **independent features**:

$$S=\{A,B,D,E,F,H,K\}\quad\text{(includes a straight segment)},$$
$$C=\{B,C,D,G,H,J\}\quad\text{(includes a curved segment)}.$$

### A. List \(S\cap C\), \(S\setminus C\), and \(C\setminus S\).
### B. Is the claim \(C=S'\) true? Explain using at least one element.
### C. Identify icons that have neither recorded feature.
![[Counting - SS-07 - Question.svg]]

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> $$S\cap C=\boxed{\{B,D,H\}},$$
> $$S\setminus C=\boxed{\{A,E,F,K\}},$$
> $$C\setminus S=\boxed{\{C,G,J\}}.$$
>
> ### B.
>
> **False.** For example, B belongs to **both** S and C, so it cannot belong to S'. Also I and L belong to S' but not to C.
>
> $$S'=\{C,G,I,J,L\}\ne C.$$
>
> ### C.
>
> $$U\setminus(S\cup C)=\boxed{\{I,L\}}.$$
>
