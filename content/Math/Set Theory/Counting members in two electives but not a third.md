A school records participation in pottery ($P$), dance ($D$), and robotics ($R$). The diagram shows the number of students in each **disjoint** region.

![[diploma-batch08-2016-mc5-three-electives.svg]]

How many students participate in **pottery or dance, but not robotics**? Name the qualifying regions and show that no student is counted twice.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> We seek $(P\cup D)\cap R^c$. This includes the **pottery-only** region ($37$), **dance-only** ($29$), and the shared pottery-and-dance region **outside robotics** ($12$).
>
> The regions are mutually exclusive, so their counts add:
>
> $$37+29+12=\boxed{78\text{ students}}.$$
>
> The pottery-and-robotics ($9$), dance-and-robotics ($7$), all-three ($5$), and robotics-only ($41$) regions are excluded.
