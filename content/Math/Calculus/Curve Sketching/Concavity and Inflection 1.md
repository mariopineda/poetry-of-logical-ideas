Using the Second Derivative Test, find the local maximum and minimum values for

$$
f(x)=x^3-12x+5.
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> First find the critical numbers:
>
> $$
> f'(x)=3x^2-12=3(x-2)(x+2).
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> x=-2,\ 2.
> $$
>
> The second derivative is
>
> $$
> f''(x)=6x.
> $$
>
> At $x=-2$,
>
> $$
> f''(-2)=-12<0,
> $$
>
> so $f$ has a local maximum. Its value is
>
> $$
> f(-2)=-8+24+5=21.
> $$
>
> At $x=2$,
>
> $$
> f''(2)=12>0,
> $$
>
> so $f$ has a local minimum. Its value is
>
> $$
> f(2)=8-24+5=-11.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{local maximum }(-2,21)}
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{local minimum }(2,-11)}.
> $$
