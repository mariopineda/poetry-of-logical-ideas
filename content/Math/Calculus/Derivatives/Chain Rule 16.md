Find the equation, in general form, of the tangent line to

$$
y=\sqrt{x^2-5x+2}
$$

at $x=-2$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Differentiate:
>
> $$
> y'
> =
> \frac{2x-5}{2\sqrt{x^2-5x+2}}.
> $$
>
> At $x=-2$,
>
> $$
> y(-2)=\sqrt{4+10+2}=4
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> m
> =
> \frac{-4-5}{2(4)}
> =
> -\frac98.
> $$
>
> Using point-slope form,
>
> $$
> y-4
> =
> -\frac98(x+2).
> $$
>
> Multiply by $8$ and rearrange:
>
> $$
> 8y-32=-9x-18,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> 9x+8y-14=0
> }.
> $$
