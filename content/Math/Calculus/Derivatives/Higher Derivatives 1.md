Determine the first four derivatives of

$$
y=ax^3+bx^2+cx+d,
$$

where

$$
a,b,c,d\in\mathbb R.
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Differentiate successively:
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> y'=3ax^2+2bx+c
> }
> $$
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> y''=6ax+2b
> }
> $$
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> y'''=6a
> }
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> y^{(4)}=0
> }.
> $$
