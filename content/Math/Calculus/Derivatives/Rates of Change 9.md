For the linear relation

$$
6x+8y+48=0,
$$

if $x$ decreases by $24$, what is the change in $y$?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Solve for $y$:
>
> $$
> 8y=-6x-48
> $$
>
> $$
> y=-\frac34x-6.
> $$
>
> For a linear relation,
>
> $$
> \Delta y
> =
> -\frac34\Delta x.
> $$
>
> Since $x$ decreases by $24$,
>
> $$
> \Delta x=-24.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \Delta y
> =
> -\frac34(-24)
> =
> \boxed{18}.
> $$
>
> So $y$ increases by $18$.
