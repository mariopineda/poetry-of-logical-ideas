Differentiate $f(x) = x^2(x+1)$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Use the Product Rule:
>
> $$
> f(x)=x^2(x+1).
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> f'(x)
> =
> 2x(x+1)+x^2(1).
> $$
>
> Simplify:
>
> $$
> f'(x)
> =
> 2x^2+2x+x^2
> =
> 3x^2+2x.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{f'(x)=x(3x+2)}.
> $$
