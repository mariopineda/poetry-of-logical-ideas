For

$$
f(x)=2x^3-5x^2+Ax-7,
$$

if

$$
f'(-1)=-6,
$$

determine $A$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Differentiate:
>
> $$
> f'(x)=6x^2-10x+A.
> $$
>
> At $x=-1$,
>
> $$
> f'(-1)=6+10+A.
> $$
>
> Since $f'(-1)=-6$,
>
> $$
> 16+A=-6.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{A=-22}.
> $$
