If $f(4)=2$, $f'(4)=3$, $g(4)=-2$, and $g'(4)=7$, then evaluate $(fg)'(4)$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Using the Product Rule,
>
> $$
> (fg)'(x)
> =
> f'(x)g(x)+f(x)g'(x).
> $$
>
> At $x=4$,
>
> $$
> (fg)'(4)
> =
> f'(4)g(4)+f(4)g'(4).
> $$
>
> Substitute the given values:
>
> $$
> (fg)'(4)
> =
> 3(-2)+2(7).
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{(fg)'(4)=8}.
> $$
>
