Find the slopes of the two tangent lines to

$$
x^2+y^2=169
$$

at $x=5$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> When $x=5$,
>
> $$
> 25+y^2=169,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> y=\pm12.
> $$
>
> Differentiate implicitly:
>
> $$
> 2x+2yy'=0,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> y'=-\frac{x}{y}.
> $$
>
> At $(5,12)$,
>
> $$
> y'=-\frac{5}{12}.
> $$
>
> At $(5,-12)$,
>
> $$
> y'=\frac{5}{12}.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the two tangent slopes are
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> -\frac{5}{12}
> \quad\text{and}\quad
> \frac{5}{12}
> }.
> $$
