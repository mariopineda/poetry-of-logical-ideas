Find the second derivative.

### A.

$$
y=5x^3-6x^2+8x-11
$$

### B.

$$
f(x)=4-\frac1x+\frac{2}{x^2}-\frac{2}{x^3}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> First,
>
> $$
> y'=15x^2-12x+8.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> y''=30x-12
> }.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> Rewrite as
>
> $$
> f(x)=4-x^{-1}+2x^{-2}-2x^{-3}.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> f'(x)=x^{-2}-4x^{-3}+6x^{-4}.
> $$
>
> Differentiate again:
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> f''(x)
> =
> -2x^{-3}
> +12x^{-4}
> -24x^{-5}
> }.
> $$
>
> Equivalently,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> f''(x)
> =
> -\frac{2}{x^3}
> +\frac{12}{x^4}
> -\frac{24}{x^5}
> }.
> $$
