## Question

Consider the function $ f(x) =
\begin{cases}
4x+5 & \mbox{, } x \leq 3 \\
ax^2-1 & \mbox{, } x > 3 \\
\end{cases}$
, where $a \in \mathbb{R}$. Determine the value(s) for $a$ to make $f'(x)$ continuous at $x=3$.
