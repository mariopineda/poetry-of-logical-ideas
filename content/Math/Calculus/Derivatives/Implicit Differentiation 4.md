Find $y'$.

### A.

$$
y^3+y=4x-9
$$

### B.

$$
5y^2-8y-9=x
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> Differentiate implicitly:
>
> $$
> 3y^2y'+y'=4.
> $$
>
> Factor:
>
> $$
> (3y^2+1)y'=4.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> y'=\frac{4}{3y^2+1}
> }.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> Differentiate:
>
> $$
> 10yy'-8y'=1.
> $$
>
> Factor:
>
> $$
> (10y-8)y'=1.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> y'=\frac{1}{10y-8}
> }.
> $$
