Differentiate $y = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3x^2-4x+1}}$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Rewrite:
>
> $$
> y=(3x^2-4x+1)^{-1/2}.
> $$
>
> Using the Chain Rule,
>
> $$
> y'
> =
> -\frac12(3x^2-4x+1)^{-3/2}(6x-4).
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> y'
> =
> \frac{2-3x}{(3x^2-4x+1)^{3/2}}
> }.
> $$
