Differentiate $y = \frac{3x-5}{1-2x}$. Then, determine the tangent slope at $x=6$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Using the Quotient Rule,
>
> $$
> y
> =
> \frac{3x-5}{1-2x}.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> y'
> =
> \frac{
> 3(1-2x)
> -
> (3x-5)(-2)
> }{
> (1-2x)^2
> }.
> $$
>
> Simplify the numerator:
>
> $$
> 3-6x+6x-10=-7.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> y'
> =
> -\frac{7}{(1-2x)^2}
> }.
> $$
>
> At $x=6$,
>
> $$
> y'(6)
> =
> -\frac{7}{(1-12)^2}
> =
> -\frac{7}{121}.
> $$
>
> Thus the tangent slope is
>
> $$
> \boxed{-\frac{7}{121}}.
> $$
