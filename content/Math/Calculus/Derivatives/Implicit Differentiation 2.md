Find the two tangent slopes to the circle

$$
x^2+y^2=100
$$

at $x=-8$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> When $x=-8$,
>
> $$
> 64+y^2=100,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> y=\pm6.
> $$
>
> Differentiate implicitly:
>
> $$
> 2x+2yy'=0.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> y'=-\frac{x}{y}.
> $$
>
> At $(-8,6)$,
>
> $$
> y'=\frac{8}{6}=\frac43.
> $$
>
> At $(-8,-6)$,
>
> $$
> y'=\frac{8}{-6}=-\frac43.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the two tangent slopes are
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> \frac43
> \quad\text{and}\quad
> -\frac43
> }.
> $$
