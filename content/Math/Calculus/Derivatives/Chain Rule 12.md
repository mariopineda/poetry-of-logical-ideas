If

$$
f(-3)=4,\qquad f'(6)=-2,\qquad g(-3)=6,\qquad g'(-3)=5,
$$

evaluate

$$
(f\circ g)'(-3).
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> By the Chain Rule,
>
> $$
> (f\circ g)'(x)=f'(g(x))g'(x).
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> (f\circ g)'(-3)
> =
> f'(g(-3))g'(-3).
> $$
>
> Using
>
> $$
> g(-3)=6,\qquad f'(6)=-2,\qquad g'(-3)=5,
> $$
>
> we get
>
> $$
> (f\circ g)'(-3)=(-2)(5)=\boxed{-10}.
> $$
