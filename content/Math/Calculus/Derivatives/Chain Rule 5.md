Differentiate $y = \frac{f(ax^2)}{a}$, where $a \in \mathbb{R}$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The expression is defined only when
>
> $$
> a\neq0.
> $$
>
> For $a\neq0$,
>
> $$
> y=\frac1a f(ax^2).
> $$
>
> Differentiate using the Chain Rule:
>
> $$
> y'
> =
> \frac1a f'(ax^2)(2ax).
> $$
>
> The factor $a$ cancels, giving
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> y'=2x\,f'(ax^2)
> }.
> $$
