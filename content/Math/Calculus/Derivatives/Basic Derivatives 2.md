Differentiate.

#### A.

$$
y=8x^5+11x^2-7x+9
$$

#### B.

$$
g(u)=\frac{1}{8u^2}-4\sqrt{u^3}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> Differentiate term by term:
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> y'=40x^4+22x-7
> }.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> Rewrite:
>
> $$
> g(u)
> =
> \frac18u^{-2}-4u^{3/2}.
> $$
>
> Differentiate:
>
> $$
> g'(u)
> =
> -\frac14u^{-3}-6u^{1/2}.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> g'(u)
> =
> -\frac{1}{4u^3}-6\sqrt u
> }.
> $$
