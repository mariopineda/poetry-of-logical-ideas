Differentiate.

### A.

$$
f(x)=\frac{1}{(5x^3+7x-9)^2}
$$

### B.

$$
h(t)=\frac{-2}{\sqrt{16-t^4}}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> Rewrite as
>
> $$
> f(x)=(5x^3+7x-9)^{-2}.
> $$
>
> Using the Chain Rule,
>
> $$
> f'(x)
> =
> -2(5x^3+7x-9)^{-3}(15x^2+7).
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> f'(x)=
> -\frac{2(15x^2+7)}{(5x^3+7x-9)^3}
> }.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> Rewrite as
>
> $$
> h(t)=-2(16-t^4)^{-1/2}.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> h'(t)
> =
> -2\left(-\frac12\right)
> (16-t^4)^{-3/2}(-4t^3).
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> h'(t)=
> -\frac{4t^3}{(16-t^4)^{3/2}}
> }.
> $$
