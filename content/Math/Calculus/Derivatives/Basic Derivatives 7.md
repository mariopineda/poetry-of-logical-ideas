Find the equation of the tangent line at the indicated point. Answer in general form.

### A.

$$
y=7-3x+2x^2
$$

at $x=4$.

### B.

$$
y=\frac{1}{x}-\frac{3}{x^2}
$$

at $x=-2$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> Differentiate:
>
> $$
> y'=-3+4x.
> $$
>
> At $x=4$,
>
> $$
> m=13
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> y(4)=27.
> $$
>
> Using point-slope form,
>
> $$
> y-27=13(x-4).
> $$
>
> Therefore, in general form,
>
> $$
> \boxed{13x-y-25=0}.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> Differentiate:
>
> $$
> y'=-\frac{1}{x^2}+\frac{6}{x^3}.
> $$
>
> At $x=-2$,
>
> $$
> m=-1
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> y(-2)
> =
> -\frac12-\frac34
> =
> -\frac54.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> y+\frac54=-(x+2).
> $$
>
> In general form,
>
> $$
> \boxed{4x+4y+13=0}.
> $$
