Differentiate

$$
y=2x(4x-1)^3
$$

using more than one rule. Factor the answer.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Use the Product Rule and Chain Rule:
>
> $$
> y'
> =
> 2(4x-1)^3
> +
> 2x\left[3(4x-1)^2(4)\right].
> $$
>
> Factor out $2(4x-1)^2$:
>
> $$
> y'
> =
> 2(4x-1)^2\left[(4x-1)+12x\right].
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> y'=2(4x-1)^2(16x-1)
> }.
> $$
