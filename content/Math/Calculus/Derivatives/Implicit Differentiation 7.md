Find the indicated rate of change. Assume all variables are functions of time.

### A.

For

$$
A=Lw,
$$

find

$$
\frac{dA}{dt}.
$$

### B.

For

$$
V=\frac43\pi r^3,
$$

find

$$
\frac{dV}{dt}.
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> Using the Product Rule,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> \frac{dA}{dt}
> =
> w\frac{dL}{dt}
> +
> L\frac{dw}{dt}
> }.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> Differentiate with respect to time:
>
> $$
> \frac{dV}{dt}
> =
> \frac43\pi(3r^2)\frac{dr}{dt}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> \frac{dV}{dt}
> =
> 4\pi r^2\frac{dr}{dt}
> }.
> $$
