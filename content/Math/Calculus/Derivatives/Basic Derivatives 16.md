For

$$
f(x)=x^4+Ax^3+Bx^2-2x+12,
$$

if $f'(1)=15$ and $f'(-1)=23$, determine $A$ and $B$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Differentiate:
>
> $$
> f'(x)=4x^3+3Ax^2+2Bx-2.
> $$
>
> Using $f'(1)=15$:
>
> $$
> 4+3A+2B-2=15
> $$
>
> $$
> 3A+2B=13.
> $$
>
> Using $f'(-1)=23$:
>
> $$
> -4+3A-2B-2=23
> $$
>
> $$
> 3A-2B=29.
> $$
>
> Add the equations:
>
> $$
> 6A=42
> $$
>
> $$
> A=7.
> $$
>
> Substitute into $3A+2B=13$:
>
> $$
> 21+2B=13
> $$
>
> $$
> B=-4.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{A=7,\qquad B=-4}.
> $$
