If
$$
f(x)=(x^2-5x+7)^3,
$$
evaluate
$$
f'(2).
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Differentiate using the Chain Rule:
>
> $$
> f'(x)=3(x^2-5x+7)^2(2x-5).
> $$
>
> At $x=2$,
>
> $$
> x^2-5x+7=4-10+7=1
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> 2x-5=4-5=-1.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> f'(2)=3(1)^2(-1)=\boxed{-3}.
> $$
