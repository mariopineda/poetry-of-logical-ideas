Differentiate

$$
y=\frac{1}{4(8-7x^3-x^5)}.
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Rewrite as
>
> $$
> y=\frac14(8-7x^3-x^5)^{-1}.
> $$
>
> Differentiate:
>
> $$
> y'
> =
> \frac14(-1)(8-7x^3-x^5)^{-2}(-21x^2-5x^4).
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> y'
> =
> \frac{x^2(21+5x^2)}
> {4(8-7x^3-x^5)^2}
> }.
> $$
