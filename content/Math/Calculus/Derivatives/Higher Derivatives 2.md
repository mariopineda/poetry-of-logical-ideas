Determine the second derivative of

$$
h(x)=\frac{5-4x}{3x-7}.
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> First differentiate using the Quotient Rule:
>
> $$
> h'(x)
> =
> \frac{-4(3x-7)-3(5-4x)}
> {(3x-7)^2}.
> $$
>
> Simplify the numerator:
>
> $$
> -12x+28-15+12x=13.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> h'(x)=13(3x-7)^{-2}.
> $$
>
> Differentiate again:
>
> $$
> h''(x)
> =
> 13(-2)(3x-7)^{-3}(3).
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> h''(x)=
> -\frac{78}{(3x-7)^3}
> }.
> $$
