Find the two tangent slopes to the hyperbola

$$
x^2-y^2=-64
$$

at $x=-6$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> When $x=-6$,
>
> $$
> 36-y^2=-64,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> y=\pm10.
> $$
>
> Differentiate implicitly:
>
> $$
> 2x-2yy'=0.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> y'=\frac{x}{y}.
> $$
>
> At $(-6,10)$,
>
> $$
> y'=-\frac{6}{10}=-\frac35.
> $$
>
> At $(-6,-10)$,
>
> $$
> y'=\frac{-6}{-10}=\frac35.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the two tangent slopes are
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> -\frac35
> \quad\text{and}\quad
> \frac35
> }.
> $$
