For the linear function $32x+12y-19=0$, if $x$ decreases by 2, how does $y$ change?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Rewrite the equation:
>
> $$
> 32x+12y-19=0.
> $$
>
> Solve for $y$:
>
> $$
> 12y=-32x+19
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> y=-\frac83x+\frac{19}{12}.
> $$
>
> The slope is
>
> $$
> -\frac83.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \Delta y
> =
> -\frac83\Delta x.
> $$
>
> If $x$ decreases by $2$, then
>
> $$
> \Delta x=-2.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \Delta y
> =
> -\frac83(-2)
> =
> \frac{16}{3}.
> $$
>
> Therefore, $y$
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{increases by }\frac{16}{3}}.
> $$
