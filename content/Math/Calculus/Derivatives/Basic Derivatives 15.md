Find the point on the curve

$$
y=x^2+6x-8
$$

where the tangent line is perpendicular to

$$
x+4y+11=0.
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Rewrite the line:
>
> $$
> y=-\frac14x-\frac{11}{4}.
> $$
>
> Its slope is
>
> $$
> -\frac14.
> $$
>
> A perpendicular line has slope
>
> $$
> 4.
> $$
>
> For the curve,
>
> $$
> y'=2x+6.
> $$
>
> Set
>
> $$
> 2x+6=4.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> x=-1.
> $$
>
> The corresponding point is
>
> $$
> y(-1)=1-6-8=-13.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the point is
>
> $$
> \boxed{(-1,-13)}.
> $$
