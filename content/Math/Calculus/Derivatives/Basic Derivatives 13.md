Find the equation of the tangent line at the indicated point. Answer in general form.

### A.

$$
y=\sqrt{x}-\frac{6}{\sqrt{x}}
$$

at $x=9$.

### B.

$$
y=\frac{x^3-1}{x}
$$

at $x=-2$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> Differentiate:
>
> $$
> y'
> =
> \frac{1}{2\sqrt{x}}
> +
> \frac{3}{x^{3/2}}.
> $$
>
> At $x=9$,
>
> $$
> m=\frac16+\frac19=\frac{5}{18}
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> y(9)=3-2=1.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> y-1=\frac{5}{18}(x-9).
> $$
>
> In general form,
>
> $$
> \boxed{5x-18y-27=0}.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> Simplify:
>
> $$
> y=x^2-\frac1x.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> y'=2x+\frac{1}{x^2}.
> $$
>
> At $x=-2$,
>
> $$
> m=-4+\frac14=-\frac{15}{4}
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> y(-2)=\frac92.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> y-\frac92
> =
> -\frac{15}{4}(x+2).
> $$
>
> In general form,
>
> $$
> \boxed{15x+4y+12=0}.
> $$
