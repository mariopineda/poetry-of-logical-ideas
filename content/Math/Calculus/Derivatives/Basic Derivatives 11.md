Differentiate.

### A.

$$
g(x)=\frac{x^\pi}{x^3}
$$

### B.

$$
f(x)=x^2\sqrt[3]{x}
$$

### C.

$$
h(x)=\frac{1}{\sqrt[9]{x^4}}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> $$
> g(x)=x^{\pi-3},
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> g'(x)=(\pi-3)x^{\pi-4}
> }.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> $$
> f(x)=x^{2+1/3}=x^{7/3}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> f'(x)=\frac73x^{4/3}
> }.
> $$
>
> ### C.
>
> $$
> h(x)=x^{-4/9}.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> h'(x)=-\frac49x^{-13/9}
> }.
> $$
