The position of an object is

$$
s(t)=t^3-7t^2-4t,
\qquad
t\geq1,
$$

where $s$ is measured in metres, $t$ is measured in seconds, East is positive, and West is negative.

### A.

Determine the velocities when the object is $28$ m West of the origin.

### B.

Determine the positions when the object's velocity is $12\text{ m/s}$ West.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Differentiate:
>
> $$
> v(t)=3t^2-14t-4.
> $$
>
> ### A.
>
> $28$ m West means
>
> $$
> s(t)=-28.
> $$
>
> So
>
> $$
> t^3-7t^2-4t+28=0.
> $$
>
> Factor:
>
> $$
> (t-7)(t-2)(t+2)=0.
> $$
>
> Since $t\geq1$,
>
> $$
> t=2,\ 7.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> v(2)=12-28-4=-20
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> v(7)=147-98-4=45.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{v=-20\text{ m/s}}
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> \boxed{v=45\text{ m/s}}.
> $$
>
> That is, $20\text{ m/s}$ West and $45\text{ m/s}$ East.
>
> ### B.
>
> $12\text{ m/s}$ West means
>
> $$
> v(t)=-12.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> 3t^2-14t-4=-12
> $$
>
> $$
> 3t^2-14t+8=0
> $$
>
> $$
> (3t-2)(t-4)=0.
> $$
>
> The solutions are $t=\frac23$ and $t=4$, but the domain requires $t\geq1$. Therefore,
>
> $$
> t=4.
> $$
>
> The position is
>
> $$
> s(4)=64-112-16=-64.
> $$
>
> Hence,
>
> $$
> \boxed{s=-64\text{ m}},
> $$
>
> or $64$ m West of the origin.
