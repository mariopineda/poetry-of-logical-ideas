## Question

$s(t) = t^3 - 7t^2 -4t$, $t \geq 1$, where $s$ is in metres, $t$ is in seconds and East + and West -.

#### A.

Determine the velocities when the position is 28 m West of the origin.

#### B.

Determine the positions when the object's velocity is 12 m/sec. West.
