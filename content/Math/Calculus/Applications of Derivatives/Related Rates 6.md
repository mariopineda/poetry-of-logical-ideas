A circular ring is heated so that it expands. If the rate of increase of the radius is 0.01 cm/s, determine the rate at which the circumference is increasing.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The circumference of a circle is
>
> $$
> C=2\pi r.
> $$
>
> Differentiate with respect to time:
>
> $$
> \frac{dC}{dt}
> =
> 2\pi\frac{dr}{dt}.
> $$
>
> Given
>
> $$
> \frac{dr}{dt}=0.01\text{ cm/s},
> $$
>
> we obtain
>
> $$
> \frac{dC}{dt}
> =
> 2\pi(0.01)
> =
> \boxed{0.02\pi\text{ cm/s}}.
> $$
>
> Approximately,
>
> $$
> \boxed{0.0628\text{ cm/s}}.
> $$
