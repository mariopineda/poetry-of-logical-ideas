Water is being poured into a rectangular aquarium that is $60$ cm long and $30$ cm wide.

Determine the relationship between the rate at which the volume of water is increasing and the rate at which the water depth $h$ is increasing.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The water volume is
>
> $$
> V=60(30)h=1800h.
> $$
>
> Differentiate with respect to time:
>
> $$
> \frac{dV}{dt}
> =
> 1800\frac{dh}{dt}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> \frac{dV}{dt}
> =
> 1800\frac{dh}{dt}
> }.
> $$
>
> Equivalently,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> \frac{dh}{dt}
> =
> \frac{1}{1800}\frac{dV}{dt}
> }.
> $$
>
> If $\frac{dV}{dt}$ is measured in $\text{cm}^3/\text{s}$, then $\frac{dh}{dt}$ is measured in $\text{cm}/\text{s}$.
