## Question

$s(t) t^3-10t^2+6t-11$, where $t$ is in seconds and $s$ is in metres, and East + and West -.

#### A.

Determine the average acceleration from $t=3$ sec. to $t=7$ sec.

#### B.

Determine its location, to the nearest hundredth, when its acceleration is 7 m/sec.$^2$ West.
