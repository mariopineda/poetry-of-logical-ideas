## Question

The position function of an object is $s(t) = 12t-3t^2$, where $t$ in seconds, $s$ in metres, and North +, South -.

#### A.

Determine the objects average velocity from $t=2$ s to $t=5$ s.

#### B.

Determine the objects velocity when it is located 63 m South of the origin.
