For each position function, where $s$ is measured in metres and $t$ in seconds, determine exactly the **magnitude** of the average velocity from $t=0$ s to $t=6$ s.

### A.

$$
s(t)=\frac{t^2+1}{2t-1}
$$

### B.

$$
s(t)=t\sqrt{4t+1}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The magnitude of the average velocity is
>
> $$
> \left|
> \frac{s(6)-s(0)}{6}
> \right|.
> $$
>
> ### A.
>
> $$
> s(6)=\frac{37}{11}
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> s(0)=-1.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \left|
> \frac{\frac{37}{11}-(-1)}{6}
> \right|
> =
> \left|
> \frac{48}{66}
> \right|
> =
> \boxed{\frac{8}{11}\text{ m/s}}.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> $$
> s(6)=6\sqrt{25}=30
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> s(0)=0.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \left|
> \frac{30-0}{6}
> \right|
> =
> \boxed{5\text{ m/s}}.
> $$
