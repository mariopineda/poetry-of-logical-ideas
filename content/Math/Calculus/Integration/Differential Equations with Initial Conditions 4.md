Find the curve $y=F(x)$ that passes through $(-1,0)$ and satisfies

$$
\frac{dy}{dx}=6x^2+6x.
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Integrate:
>
> $$
> F(x)=\int(6x^2+6x)\,dx
> =2x^3+3x^2+C.
> $$
>
> Use $F(-1)=0$:
>
> $$
> 0=-2+3+C
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> C=-1.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{F(x)=2x^3+3x^2-1}.
> $$
