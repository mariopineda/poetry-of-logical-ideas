Numerically estimate each limit. Answer to three decimal places.

### A.

$$
\lim_{x\to1}
\frac{\sqrt[5]{x}-1}{x-1}
$$

### B.

$$
\lim_{h\to0}
\frac{h}{9^h-1}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> Evaluate the expression for values of $x$ close to $1$.
>
> For example:
>
> | $x$ | Value |
> |---:|---:|
> | $0.99$ | $0.201$ |
> | $0.999$ | $0.200$ |
> | $1.001$ | $0.200$ |
> | $1.01$ | $0.199$ |
>
> The values approach
>
> $$
> \boxed{0.200}.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> Evaluate the expression for values of $h$ close to $0$.
>
> For example:
>
> | $h$ | Value |
> |---:|---:|
> | $-0.01$ | $0.460$ |
> | $-0.001$ | $0.456$ |
> | $0.001$ | $0.455$ |
> | $0.01$ | $0.450$ |
>
> The values approach
>
> $$
> \boxed{0.455}.
> $$
