## Question

Evalue the following limits, if possible.

1. $$\lim\_{x \to 4} \frac{9-x^2}{x+5}$$
2. $$\lim\_{x \to 8} \frac{\sqrt{1-x}}{x-8}$$
3. $$\lim\_{x \to 7} \frac{x^2-49}{x^2+7x}$$
4. $$\lim\_{x \to -1} \frac{x+1}{x^2-1}$$
5. $$\lim\_{x \to 5} \frac{\frac{1}{x}-\frac{1}{5}}{x-5}$$
6. $$\lim\_{x \to 10} \frac{\sqrt{x+6}+4}{x-10}$$
