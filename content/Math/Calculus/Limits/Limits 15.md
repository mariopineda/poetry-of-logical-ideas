Evalue $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sqrt{19x+121}-11}{x} = \frac{ab}{cd}$ where the answer is $abcd$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Evaluate
>
> $$
> \lim_{x\to0}
> \frac{\sqrt{19x+121}-11}{x}.
> $$
>
> Rationalize the numerator:
>
> $$
> \frac{\sqrt{19x+121}-11}{x}
> \cdot
> \frac{\sqrt{19x+121}+11}
> {\sqrt{19x+121}+11}.
> $$
>
> This gives
>
> $$
> \frac{19x}
> {x\left(\sqrt{19x+121}+11\right)}.
> $$
>
> Cancel $x$:
>
> $$
> \frac{19}
> {\sqrt{19x+121}+11}.
> $$
>
> Now let
>
> $$
> x\to0:
> $$
>
> $$
> \frac{19}{11+11}
> =
> \boxed{\frac{19}{22}}.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> a=1,\quad b=9,\quad c=2,\quad d=2,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> \boxed{abcd=1922}.
> $$
