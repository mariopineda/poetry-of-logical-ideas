Evalue, if possible.

1.

$$
\lim_{x \to 9^{+}} \sqrt{9-x}
$$
2.

$$
\lim_{x \to 9^{-}} \sqrt{9-x}
$$
3.

$$
\lim_{x \to -5^{-}} \frac{x+5}{|x+5|}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> $$
> \lim_{x\to9^+}\sqrt{9-x}
> $$
>
> For real-valued $x$, the expression
>
> $$
> \sqrt{9-x}
> $$
>
> is not defined for $x>9$.
>
> Therefore, the real right-hand limit
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{does not exist}}.
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> As
>
> $$
> x\to9^-,
> $$
>
> we have
>
> $$
> 9-x\to0^+.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> \lim_{x\to9^-}\sqrt{9-x}=0
> }.
> $$
>
> ### 3.
>
> For
>
> $$
> x<-5,
> $$
>
> we have
>
> $$
> x+5<0,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> |x+5|=-(x+5).
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \frac{x+5}{|x+5|}
> =
> -1.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{-1}.
> $$
