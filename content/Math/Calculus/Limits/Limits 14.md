## Question

Evalue the following limits, if possible.

1. $$\lim\_{x \to -7} \frac{x^2-7x}{x^2-49}$$
2. $$\lim\_{x \to 4} \frac{x^2+2x-24}{x^3-5x^2+4x}$$
3. $$\lim\_{x \to -2} \frac{40-10x^2}{5x^3+40}$$
4. $$\lim\_{x \to -3} \frac{x^2+3x}{5-\sqrt{1-8x}}$$
5. $$\lim\_{x \to 8} \frac{\frac{6}{x}-\frac{3}{4}}{x^3-64x}$$
6. $$\lim\_{x \to 1} \frac{x-1}{\sqrt[3]{x}-1}$$
7. If $\lim\_{x \to a} \frac{\frac{1}{a}-\frac{1}{x}}{x-a} = \frac{9}{16}$, then determine the value(s) of $a$.
