## Question

Evaluate the limits.

1. $$\lim\_{x \to 4} \frac{x^2-16}{x-4}$$
2. $$\lim\_{x \to 1} \frac{x-1}{x}$$
3. $$\lim\_{x \to 0} \frac{\sqrt{x+4}-2}{x}$$
4. $$\lim\_{x \to -6} \frac{x}{x+6}$$
5. $$\lim\_{x \to 7} \frac{x-7}{\frac{1}{x}-\frac{1}{7}}$$
