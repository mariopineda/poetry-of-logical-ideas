## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> (a) $\left(\dfrac12\right)^3=\boxed{\dfrac18}$
>
> (b) $\left(\dfrac34\right)^2=\boxed{\dfrac9{16}}$
>
> (c) $\left(\dfrac15\right)^{-1}=\boxed{5}$
