# Exponent Laws 7

Rewrite using the indicated base.

(a) $64$ as a power of $2$  
(b) $125$ as a power of $5$  
(c) $\frac{1}{27}$ as a power of $3$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> (a) $64=\boxed{2^6}$
>
> (b) $125=\boxed{5^3}$
>
> (c) $\dfrac{1}{27}=\boxed{3^{-3}}$
