For $b>1$, a model satisfies

$$y=\frac{b^{3x}}{b^{x-8}},\qquad \log_b y=5x.$$

Determine $x$. Explain why the logarithm can be simplified.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The quotient law gives $y=b^{2x+8}$, so $\log_by=2x+8$. Thus $2x+8=5x$, which gives $\boxed{x=\frac83}$.
