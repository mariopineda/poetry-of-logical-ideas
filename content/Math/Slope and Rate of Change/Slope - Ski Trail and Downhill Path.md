# Slope - Ski Trail and Downhill Path

**A. Ski trail.** A ski trail drops $18\text{ m}$ vertically over a horizontal run of $12\text{ m}$.

Calculate its **signed slope**, to the nearest tenth, and explain what the sign means.

**B. Downhill path.** A path has a signed slope of $-0.6$ and a horizontal run of $15\text{ m}$.

Determine its **vertical change (rise)** and explain the result.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A. Ski trail
>
> A drop is a negative rise:
>
> $$
> m=\frac{-18}{12}=\boxed{-1.5}.
> $$
>
> The negative sign means the trail goes downhill when moving left to right.
>
> ### B. Downhill path
>
> Since $m=\dfrac{\text{rise}}{\text{run}}$, rearrange to get $\text{rise}=m\times\text{run}$.
>
> $$
> \text{rise}=(-0.6)(15)=\boxed{-9\text{ m}}.
> $$
>
> The vertical change is $-9\text{ m}$, so the path **drops by $9\text{ m}$**.
