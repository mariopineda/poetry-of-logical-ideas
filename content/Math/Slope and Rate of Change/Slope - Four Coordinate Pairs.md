# Slope - Four Coordinate Pairs

Calculate the slope of **each line segment** using signed rise divided by run. Graphing is not required. Round to the nearest tenth.

1. $E(-6,3)$ and $F(-2,-5)$
2. $G(-4,-4)$ and $H(2,2)$
3. $J(1,5)$ and $K(6,3)$
4. $L(-3,1)$ and $M(3,1)$

Then identify which segments have **positive**, **negative**, and **zero** slope.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> | Segment | Signed rise | Run | Slope |
> |---|---:|---:|---:|
> | EF | $-5-3=-8$ | $-2-(-6)=4$ | $\boxed{-2.0}$ |
> | GH | $2-(-4)=6$ | $2-(-4)=6$ | $\boxed{1.0}$ |
> | JK | $3-5=-2$ | $6-1=5$ | $\boxed{-0.4}$ |
> | LM | $1-1=0$ | $3-(-3)=6$ | $\boxed{0.0}$ |
>
> **Positive:** GH. **Negative:** EF and JK. **Zero:** LM (horizontal line).
