# Slope - Roof Pitch and Comparing Hills

**A. Roof pitch.** The roof shown is symmetrical. Its total horizontal width is $12\text{ m}$ and its vertical rise from the eaves to the ridge is $2.4\text{ m}$.

![[Slope - Symmetrical Roof Profile.svg]]

Determine the slope of the **left side**, moving from eave to ridge, to the nearest tenth. Show how you determine the run.

**B. Comparing hills.** Hill A rises $16\text{ m}$ over an $80\text{ m}$ horizontal run. Hill B rises $24\text{ m}$ over a $60\text{ m}$ horizontal run.

Calculate both slopes and state which hill is steeper.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A. Roof pitch
>
> The roof is symmetrical, so the run to the ridge is half its full width:
>
> $$
> \text{run}=\frac{12}{2}=6\text{ m}.
> $$
>
> $$
> m=\frac{2.4}{6}=\boxed{0.4}.
> $$
>
> The left side rises when moving toward the ridge.
>
> ### B. Comparing hills
>
> $$
> m_A=\frac{16}{80}=\boxed{0.2},\qquad m_B=\frac{24}{60}=\boxed{0.4}.
> $$
>
> **Hill B** is steeper because its slope is greater ($0.4>0.2$) and both hills rise from left to right.
