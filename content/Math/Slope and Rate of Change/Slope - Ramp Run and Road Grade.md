# Slope - Ramp Run and Road Grade

1. **Ramp:** A ramp has a slope of $\dfrac{1}{12}$ and must rise $0.75\text{ m}$. How much **horizontal run** is needed?
2. **Uphill road:** A road rises $2.4\text{ m}$ over a horizontal run of $40\text{ m}$. Calculate its **percent grade**.
3. **Downhill road:** A road falls $3\text{ m}$ over a horizontal run of $75\text{ m}$. Find both its **signed slope** and its **signed percent grade**.

Use:

$$
\text{Percent grade}=\frac{\text{rise}}{\text{run}}\times100\%.
$$

Explain what a **negative** percent grade indicates.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1. Ramp
>
> $$
> \frac{1}{12}=\frac{0.75}{\text{run}}
> \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
> \text{run}=0.75(12)=\boxed{9\text{ m}}.
> $$
>
> ### 2. Uphill road
>
> $$
> \text{grade}=\frac{2.4}{40}\times100\%=\boxed{6\%}.
> $$
>
> ### 3. Downhill road
>
> A drop has a negative rise:
>
> $$
> m=\frac{-3}{75}=\boxed{-0.04}.
> $$
>
> $$
> \text{signed grade}=\frac{-3}{75}\times100\%=\boxed{-4\%}.
> $$
>
> The grade has a **magnitude of $4\%$**, with a negative sign to show that the road descends in the direction being considered.
