# Slope - Underground Drainage Installation

A plumber is installing a gravity-fed drainage pipe beneath a commercial building. Two points along the installed pipe are given. Both coordinates are measured in metres.

$$A(-8,1.20),\quad B(12,0.70)$$

The pipe must extend to point $C$, at a horizontal coordinate of $28$ m, while maintaining its existing slope. An apprentice proposes $C(28,0.35)$.

![[Slope - Underground Drainage Installation - Diagram.png]]

**a)** Calculate the slope of the existing pipe.

**b)** Determine whether the proposed extension maintains the existing slope.

**c)** Determine the correct vertical coordinate of $C$.

> [!info]- Hint 1
> For part (c), think about the vertical change required over the additional horizontal distance.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> **(a)** The pipe drops $0.50$ m over $20$ m horizontally. The signed slope must be negative.
>
> $$m_{AB}=\frac{0.70-1.20}{12-(-8)}=\frac{-0.50}{20}=-0.025$$
>
> **(b)** For the proposed extension to maintain the same inclination, its slope must equal that of $AB$.
>
> $$m_{BC}=\frac{0.35-0.70}{28-12}=\frac{-0.35}{16}=-0.021875$$
>
> The slopes are different. The proposed extension descends more gradually than the installed pipe.
>
> **(c)** The additional run is $28-12=16$ m. Rearrange slope = rise divided by run to find the required change in elevation.
>
> $$\text{rise}=m\times\text{run}=(-0.025)(16)=-0.40\text{ m}$$
>
> Starting at $B$, the required elevation is $0.70-0.40=0.30$ m. Thus the correct point is $\boxed{C(28,0.30)}$.
