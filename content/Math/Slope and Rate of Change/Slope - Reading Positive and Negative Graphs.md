# Slope - Reading Positive and Negative Graphs

The four graphs below show line segments on a coordinate grid.

| Graph A | Graph B |
|:---:|:---:|
| ![[Slope - Reading Graph A.svg]] | ![[Slope - Reading Graph B.svg]] |

| Graph C | Graph D |
|:---:|:---:|
| ![[Slope - Reading Graph C.svg]] | ![[Slope - Reading Graph D.svg]] |

For **each graph**:

1. Record the signed **rise** when moving from left to right.
2. Record the positive **run**.
3. Calculate the slope, $m=\dfrac{\text{rise}}{\text{run}}$, to the nearest tenth.

Which two line segments have **negative slopes**? Explain how you can tell from the graphs.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> | Graph | Rise | Run | Slope |
> |---|---:|---:|---:|
> | A | $4-(-2)=6$ | $2-(-4)=6$ | $\boxed{1.0}$ |
> | B | $0-4=-4$ | $4-(-4)=8$ | $\boxed{-0.5}$ |
> | C | $-3-5=-8$ | $1-(-1)=2$ | $\boxed{-4.0}$ |
> | D | $1-(-3)=4$ | $4-(-4)=8$ | $\boxed{0.5}$ |
>
> The line segments in **Graphs B and C** have negative slopes. As you move from left to right, these segments move **down**: their rise is negative and their run is positive.
