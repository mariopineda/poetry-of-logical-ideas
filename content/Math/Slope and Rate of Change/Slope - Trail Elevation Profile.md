# Slope - Trail Elevation Profile

A trail follows the points $S$, $A$, $B$, $C$, and $D$ on the elevation profile below. Both axes are measured in units of **$100\text{ m}$**.

![[Slope - Trail Elevation Profile.svg]]

1. Complete a rise/run/slope table for the four segments $SA$, $AB$, $BC$, and $CD$. Indicate **uphill** or **downhill** for each.
2. Rank the **signed slopes** from **smallest** to **largest**.
3. Which segment has the **steepest descent**?
4. Explain why a downhill segment has a negative slope.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Read the points in graph units: $S(0,2)$, $A(3,5)$, $B(7,3)$, $C(9,7)$ and $D(12,1)$.
>
> | Segment | Rise | Run | Slope | Direction |
> |---|---:|---:|---:|---|
> | SA | $+3$ | $3$ | $\boxed{1.0}$ | Uphill |
> | AB | $-2$ | $4$ | $\boxed{-0.5}$ | Downhill |
> | BC | $+4$ | $2$ | $\boxed{2.0}$ | Uphill |
> | CD | $-6$ | $3$ | $\boxed{-2.0}$ | Downhill |
>
> **Smallest to largest:** $CD(-2.0)$, $AB(-0.5)$, $SA(1.0)$, $BC(2.0)$.
>
> **Steepest descent:** $CD$, because it drops by $2$ units vertically for each unit of horizontal movement.
>
> Downhill travel from left to right has a **negative vertical change** and a **positive horizontal change**, so its slope is negative.
