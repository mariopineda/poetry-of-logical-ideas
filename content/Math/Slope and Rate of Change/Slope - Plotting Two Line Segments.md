# Slope - Plotting Two Line Segments

**Plot** the following pairs of points on the separate grids. Draw and label the line segment joining each pair.

**A.** $A(-4,3)$ and $B(2,-1)$

![[Slope - Plot Segment AB Grid.svg]]

**B.** $C(-3,-4)$ and $D(1,4)$

![[Slope - Plot Segment CD Grid.svg]]

For **each** segment, determine its signed rise, run, and slope to the nearest tenth. State whether it rises or falls when moving from left to right.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### Segment AB
>
> The rise is $-1-3=-4$ and the run is $2-(-4)=6$.
>
> $$
> m=\frac{-4}{6}=-\frac23\approx\boxed{-0.7}.
> $$
>
> **AB falls** when moving from left to right.
>
> ### Segment CD
>
> The rise is $4-(-4)=8$ and the run is $1-(-3)=4$.
>
> $$
> m=\frac{8}{4}=\boxed{2.0}.
> $$
>
> **CD rises** when moving from left to right. Both plotted line segments must pass through the supplied endpoints.
