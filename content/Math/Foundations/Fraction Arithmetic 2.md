## Question

Evaluate without a calculator.

1. $$\displaystyle\frac{3}{12} + \displaystyle\frac{5}{12}$$
2. $$\displaystyle\frac{11}{6} + \displaystyle\frac{2}{6}$$
3. $$\displaystyle\frac{5}{2} - \displaystyle\frac{7}{3}$$
4. $$\displaystyle\frac{5}{17} - \displaystyle\frac{2}{7}$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> 1. $$\displaystyle\frac{3}{12} + \displaystyle\frac{5}{12} = \frac{8}{12} = \frac{2}{3}$$
> 2. $$\displaystyle\frac{11}{6} + \displaystyle\frac{2}{6} = \frac{13}{6}$$
> 3. $$\displaystyle\frac{5}{2} - \displaystyle\frac{7}{3} = \frac{15}{6} + \frac{14}{6} = \frac{1}{6}$$
> 4. $$\displaystyle\frac{5}{17} - \displaystyle\frac{2}{7} = \frac{35}{119} - \frac{34}{119} = \frac{1}{119}$$
>
