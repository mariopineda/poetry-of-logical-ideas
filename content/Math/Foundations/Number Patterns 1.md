## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> (a) $5,10,20,40,\boxed{80},\boxed{160}$ — multiply by $2$.
>
> (b) $80,40,20,10,\boxed{5},\boxed{2.5}$ — divide by $2$.
>
> (c) $3,8,13,18,\boxed{23},\boxed{28}$ — add $5$.
