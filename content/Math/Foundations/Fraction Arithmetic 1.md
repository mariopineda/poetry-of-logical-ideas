## Question

Convert each of the improper fractions below into a decimal to nearest hundreth, a mixed fraction and find an equivalent fraction that has a denominator of 24.

1. $$\frac{15}{6}$$
2. $$\frac{7}{2}$$
3. $$\frac{100}{48}$$
4. $$\frac{48}{36}$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> 1. Decimal: $$\frac{15}{6} = 2.50$$
>    Mixed fraction: $$\frac{15}{6} = 2\frac{3}{6} = 2\frac{1}{2}$$
>    Equivalent fraction: $$\frac{15}{6} = \frac{60}{24}$$
> 2. Decimal: $$\frac{7}{2} = 3.50$$
>    Mixed fractions: $$\frac{7}{2} = 3\frac{1}{2}$$
>    Equivalent fraction: $$\frac{7}{2} = \frac{84}{24}$$
> 3. Decimal: $$\frac{100}{48} = 2.08$$
>    Mixed fraction: $$\frac{100}{48} = 2\frac{4}{48} = 2\frac{1}{12}$$
>    Equivalent fraction: $$\frac{100}{48} = \frac{50}{24}$$
> 4. Decimal: $$\frac{48}{36} \approx 1.33$$
>    Mixed fraction: $$\frac{48}{36} = 1\frac{12}{36} = 1\frac{1}{3}$$
>    Equivalen fraction: $$\frac{48}{36} = \frac{32}{24}$$
