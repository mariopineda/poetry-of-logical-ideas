## Question

Evaluate without a calculator:

1. $$ (10+2-5) \times (6^2 \div (8-4)) $$
2. $$ 10+8-6^2 \div (3^2 \times 4) $$
3. $$ 8 \div (10-9)^3 \times 7 + 4^2 $$
4. $$ (10 \times (6+4)) \div (2^3-7)^2 $$

## Solution

Evaluate using BEDMAS order (brackets, exponents, division/multiplication, addition/subtraction).

### A.

$$
\begin{array}{rcl}
& & (10+2-5) \times (6^2 \div (8-4)) \\
& = & 7 \times (36 \div 4) \\
& = & 7 \times 9 \\
& = & 63
\end{array}
$$

### B.

$$
\begin{array}{rcl}
& & 10+8-6^2 \div (3^2 \times 4) \\
& = & 10+8-36 \div (9 \times 4) \\
& = & -18 \div 36 \\
& = & \frac{\displaystyle -18}{\displaystyle 36} = -\frac{\displaystyle 1}{\displaystyle 2}
\end{array}
$$

### C.

$$
\begin{array}{rcl}
& & 8 \div (10-9)^3 \times 7 + 4^2 \\
& = & 8 \div 1 \times 7 + 16 \\
& = & 8 \times 7 + 16 \\
& = & 56 + 16 \\
& = & 72
\end{array}
$$

### D.

$$
\begin{array}{rcl}
& & (10 \times (6+4)) \div (2^3-7)^2 \\
& = & 10 \times 10 \div (8-7)^2 \\
& = & 100 \div 1^2\\
& = & 100 \div 1\\
& = & 100
\end{array}
$$
