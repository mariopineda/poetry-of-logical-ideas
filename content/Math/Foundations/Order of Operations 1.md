Evaluate without a calculator:

1. $(10+2-5)\times\left(6^2\div(8-4)\right)$
2. $10+8-6^2\div(3^2\times4)$
3. $8\div(10-9)^3\times7+4^2$
4. $(10\times(6+4))\div(2^3-7)^2$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Using BEDMAS:
>
> 1.
> $$
> 7\times(36\div4)=7\times9=\boxed{63}.
> $$
>
> 2.
> $$
> 18-36\div36=18-1=\boxed{17}.
> $$
>
> 3.
> $$
> 8\div1\times7+16=56+16=\boxed{72}.
> $$
>
> 4.
> $$
> 100\div1^2=\boxed{100}.
> $$
