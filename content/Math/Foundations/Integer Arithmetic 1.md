## Question

Evaluate without a calculator:

1. $$ 3-(-5) $$
2. $$ -12 \div (-4) $$
3. $$ -5 + (-4) $$
4. $$ 12(-2) $$
5. $$ (-14)(-0.5) $$
6. $$ -2+3 \times 5 $$
7. $$ (-3)(4)-(-7) $$
8. $$ (-4+1)-(-2 \times 5) $$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> $$
> 3-(-5) = 3+5 = 8
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> $$
> -12 \div (-4) = \frac{\displaystyle -12}{\displaystyle -4} = \frac{\displaystyle 12}{\displaystyle 4} = 3
> $$
>
> ### C.
>
> $$
> -5 + (-4) = -5-4 = -9
> $$
>
> ### D.
>
> $$
> 12(-2) = -24
> $$
>
> ### E.
>
> $$
> (-14)(-0.5) = 14 \times 0.5 = 7
> $$
>
> ### F.
>
> $$
> -2+3 \times 5 = -2 + 15 = 13
> $$
>
> ### G.
>
> $$
> (-3)(4)-(-7) = -12 + 7 = -5
> $$
>
> ### H.
>
> $$
> (-4+1)-(-2 \times 5) = -3-(-10)=-3+10=7
> $$
>
