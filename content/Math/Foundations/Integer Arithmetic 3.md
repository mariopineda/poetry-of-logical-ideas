Evaluate without a calculator:

1.

$$
8 \div (7-9) \times (4+(-4))
$$
2.

$$
(8 \times (-4)-(-9) + (-7)) \div 3
$$
3.

$$
10+6 \div ((-7)-(-5)) \times ((-10)+5)
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> $$
> 8\div(7-9)\times(4+(-4))
> $$
>
> $$
> =8\div(-2)\times0
> =
> \boxed{0}
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> $$
> (8(-4)-(-9)+(-7))\div3
> $$
>
> $$
> =\frac{-32+9-7}{3}
> =
> \frac{-30}{3}
> =
> \boxed{-10}
> $$
>
> ### C.
>
> $$
> 10+6\div((-7)-(-5))\times((-10)+5)
> $$
>
> $$
> =10+6\div(-2)\times(-5)
> $$
>
> $$
> =10+(-3)(-5)
> $$
>
> $$
> =10+15
> =
> \boxed{25}
> $$
