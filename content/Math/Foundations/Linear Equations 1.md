## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> (a)
>
> $$
> 4x+7=31\implies4x=24\implies\boxed{x=6}
> $$
>
> (b)
>
> $$
> 3(x-2)=15\implies x-2=5\implies\boxed{x=7}
> $$
>
> (c)
>
> $$
> 2x+5=x+12\implies x=7\implies\boxed{x=7}
> $$
