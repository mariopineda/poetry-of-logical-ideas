Evaluate without a calculator:

1.

$$
8 \div (7-9) \times (4+(4+(-4)))
$$
2.

$$
(8 \times (-4)-(-9)+(-7)) \div 3
$$
3.

$$
10+5 \div ((-7)-(-5)) \times ((-10)+5)
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> $$
> 8\div(7-9)\times(4+(4+(-4)))
> $$
>
> $$
> =8\div(-2)\times4
> $$
>
> $$
> =(-4)(4)
> =
> \boxed{-16}
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> $$
> (8(-4)-(-9)+(-7))\div3
> $$
>
> $$
> =\frac{-32+9-7}{3}
> =
> \frac{-30}{3}
> =
> \boxed{-10}
> $$
>
> ### 3.
>
> $$
> 10+5\div((-7)-(-5))\times((-10)+5)
> $$
>
> $$
> =10+5\div(-2)\times(-5)
> $$
>
> $$
> =10+\frac{25}{2}
> =
> \boxed{\frac{45}{2}}
> $$
>
> or
>
> $$
> \boxed{22.5}.
> $$
