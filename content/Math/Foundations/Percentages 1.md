## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> (a) $8\%=\boxed{0.08}$
>
> (b) $0.5\%=\boxed{0.005}$
>
> (c) $125\%=\boxed{1.25}$
>
> (d) $37.5\%=\boxed{0.375}$
