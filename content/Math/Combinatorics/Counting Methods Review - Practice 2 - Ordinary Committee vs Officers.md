Eleven community volunteers are available.

### A. A four-person advisory group is chosen with no officer titles. Is this a permutation or combination, and how many groups are possible?
### B. Instead, four distinct positions—chair, vice-chair, recorder and treasurer—are filled. Is this a permutation or combination, and how many assignments are possible?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> A **combination**: no member has a special position.
>
> $$\binom{11}4=\boxed{330}.$$
>
> ### B.
>
> A **permutation**: the four offices are different and the order of assignment matters.
>
> $${}_{11}P_4=11\cdot10\cdot9\cdot8=\boxed{7920}.$$
