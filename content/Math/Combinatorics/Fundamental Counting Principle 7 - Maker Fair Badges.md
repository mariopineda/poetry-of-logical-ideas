A maker fair offers personalized badges. Each badge has **one symbol** and **one background colour**.

The available symbols are **Leaf, Moon, Wave, and Star**. The available background colours are **navy, coral, and cream**.

### A.

Complete an **outcome table** showing every possible badge design. Use a descriptive label such as *Leaf–navy* for each outcome.

### B.

Use the **Fundamental Counting Principle** to verify the total number of different designs.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A. Outcome table
>
> | Symbol | Navy | Coral | Cream |
> |:--|:--|:--|:--|
> | Leaf | Leaf–navy | Leaf–coral | Leaf–cream |
> | Moon | Moon–navy | Moon–coral | Moon–cream |
> | Wave | Wave–navy | Wave–coral | Wave–cream |
> | Star | Star–navy | Star–coral | Star–cream |
>
> The table lists $4$ outcomes in each of $3$ columns, giving $12$ distinct designs.
>
> ### B. Fundamental Counting Principle
>
> There are $4$ choices for the symbol and $3$ choices for the background colour. For every symbol, any of the three colours can be selected.
>
> $$
> 4\times3=\boxed{12}
> $$
>
> Therefore, **12 different badge designs** are possible. This agrees with the outcome table.
