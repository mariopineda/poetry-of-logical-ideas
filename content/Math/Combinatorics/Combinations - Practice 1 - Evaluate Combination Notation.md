Evaluate **by hand**, showing the combination formula and any useful simplification.

### A. $\binom72$
### B. $\binom{11}4$
### C. $\binom90$
### D. $\binom{10}1$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The combination formula is $\displaystyle\binom nr=\frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!}$.
>
> ### A.
>
> $$\binom72=\frac{7\cdot6}{2\cdot1}=\boxed{21}.$$
>
> ### B.
>
> $$\binom{11}4=\frac{11\cdot10\cdot9\cdot8}{4\cdot3\cdot2\cdot1}=\boxed{330}.$$
>
> ### C.
>
> There is exactly one way to select nothing:
>
> $$\binom90=\frac{9!}{0!9!}=\boxed{1}.$$
>
> ### D.
>
> Choose any one of ten objects:
>
> $$\binom{10}1=\boxed{10}.$$
