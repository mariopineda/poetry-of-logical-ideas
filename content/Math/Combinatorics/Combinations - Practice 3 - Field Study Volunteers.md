Seventeen students volunteer for a coastal-cleanup study. The organizer needs to choose **eight students**, and all selected students will perform the same task.

### A.

Should the selection be counted as a permutation or combination? Explain.

### B.

How many different teams can be chosen?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> This is a **combination** because students have identical roles. Changing the order in which their names are selected does not change the team.
>
> ### B.
>
> $$\binom{17}8=\frac{17!}{8!9!}=\boxed{24\,310}.$$
