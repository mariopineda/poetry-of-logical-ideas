Fourteen distinct points are positioned on the circumference of a circle. Each straight chord joins **two distinct points**.

How many different chords can be formed?

![Question diagram](../../Assets/Counting%20-%20MR-21%20-%20Question.png)

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Choose two endpoints without order:
>
> $$\binom{14}2=\frac{14\cdot13}2=\boxed{91}.$$
