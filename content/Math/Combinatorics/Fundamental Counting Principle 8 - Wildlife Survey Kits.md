A wildlife research team is assembling portable survey kits. Each kit must contain exactly one item from each category:

- $3$ camera models;
- $4$ memory-card capacities;
- $2$ mounting-bracket styles;
- $5$ protective-cover colours.

The choices in one category do not restrict the choices in another category.

How many different survey kits can the team assemble? **Show the multiplication statement and explain why multiplication applies.**

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> A complete kit requires a choice from **all four** independent categories. By the Fundamental Counting Principle, multiply the number of options at each stage:
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> N&=3\times4\times2\times5\\
> &=12\times10\\
> &=\boxed{120}.
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> **120 different survey kits** are possible. We multiply rather than add because each selection of a camera can be combined with every selection from the other three categories.
