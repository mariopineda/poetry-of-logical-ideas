Thirteen distinct points are marked on a circle. How many different straight **chords** can be drawn if every chord connects two distinct marked points? Explain why order does not matter.

![[Counting - CO-11 - Question.png]]

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Each chord is determined by an unordered pair of points. Reversing the endpoints gives the same segment, so use a combination:
>
> $$\binom{13}2=\frac{13\cdot12}2=\boxed{78}.$$
