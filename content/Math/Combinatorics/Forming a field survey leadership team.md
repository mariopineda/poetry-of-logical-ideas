A field survey employs six trained leaders and seven assistants. A five-person field team must include **at least four leaders**. Calculate the number of distinct teams and explain why the cases are mutually exclusive.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> There are two and only two cases: exactly four leaders and one assistant, or exactly five leaders.
>
> $$\binom64\binom71+\binom65=15(7)+6=\boxed{111}.$$
>
> The cases are disjoint because a single team cannot have exactly four and exactly five leaders simultaneously.
