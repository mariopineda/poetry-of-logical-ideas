A trail-mix recipe must contain **one kind of nut**, **two different dried fruits**, and **three different seeds**. The supply includes 3 kinds of nuts, 6 dried fruits and 8 seeds.

How many distinct recipes satisfy these requirements? Ingredients are selected, not arranged.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Choose independently from each ingredient category and multiply the numbers of combinations:
>
> $$\binom31\binom62\binom83=3\cdot15\cdot56=\boxed{2\,520}.$$
