A conference has five teacher volunteers and seven parent volunteers. It needs a **teacher chair**, a **parent vice-chair** and **four additional unranked helpers**, with no person filling two roles.

How many distinct staffing groups and assignments are possible?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Choose the two distinct officers first, then choose four unranked helpers from the ten remaining people:
>
> $$5\cdot7\cdot\binom{10}4=35\cdot210=\boxed{7350}.$$
