Five students—Aisha, Ben, Chloe, Diego and Eva—volunteer to serve as **chair** and **secretary** of an eco council. No student may hold both offices.

### A.

Make an organized list or draw a tree diagram to determine all possible officer pairs. Write the chair first.

### B.

Verify your result using permutation notation.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> For chair Aisha, the secretary can be Ben, Chloe, Diego or Eva: **AB, AC, AD, AE**. Likewise the other chairs give **BA, BC, BD, BE; CA, CB, CD, CE; DA, DB, DC, DE; EA, EB, EC, ED**. There are four distinct pairs for each of five chairs.
>
> $$5\cdot4=\boxed{20}.$$
>
> ### B.
>
> Two different offices are assigned to two of five candidates, so order matters:
>
> $$\displaystyle {}_5P_2=\frac{5!}{(5-2)!}=5\cdot4=\boxed{20}.$$
