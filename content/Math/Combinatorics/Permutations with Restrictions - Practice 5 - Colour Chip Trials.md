Seven distinct coloured chips are available. A student draws **three chips in order** and records their colours.

### A.

How many sequences are possible if each chip is **kept out** after it is drawn?

### B.

How many sequences are possible if the chip is **replaced** after each draw?

### C.

Explain why one count is greater than the other.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> Without replacement, the number of choices decreases:
>
> $$7\cdot6\cdot5={}_7P_3=\boxed{210}.$$
>
> ### B.
>
> With replacement, each of the three draws has seven choices:
>
> $$7^3=\boxed{343}.$$
>
> ### C.
>
> The replacement count is larger because repeated colours are allowed. Those additional sequences cannot occur without replacement.
