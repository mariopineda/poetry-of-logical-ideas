An exhibition sells **150 individually numbered tickets**. Four **different** prizes are awarded by drawing a ticket for each prize in a fixed order.

### A. How many prize outcomes are possible **without replacement**?
### B. How many are possible **with replacement**, so a ticket can win more than once?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> There are 150 choices for the first prize, then 149, 148 and 147. These are ordered results:
>
> $${}_{150}P_4=150\cdot149\cdot148\cdot147=\boxed{486\,246\,600}.$$
>
> ### B.
>
> Replacement makes 150 choices available at each of the four draws:
>
> $$150^4=\boxed{506\,250\,000}.$$
