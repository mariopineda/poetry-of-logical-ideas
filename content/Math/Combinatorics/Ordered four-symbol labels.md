A jewellery workshop makes four-letter product tags using distinct letters from the word **SILVER**. No letter may be repeated. How many different tags are possible? Explain why order matters.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> All six letters in SILVER are distinct. Four ordered positions without replacement can be filled in $6$, $5$, $4$, and $3$ ways.
>
> $$ {}_6P_4=6\cdot5\cdot4\cdot3=\boxed{360}.$$
>
> A permutation is appropriate because, for example, SILV and SVIL are different tags.
