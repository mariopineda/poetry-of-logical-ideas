Using the permutation formula, simplify $_nP_2$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Using
>
> $$
> {}_nP_r=\frac{n!}{(n-r)!},
> $$
>
> we have
>
> $$
> {}_nP_2
> =
> \frac{n!}{(n-2)!}.
> $$
>
> Since
>
> $$
> n!=n(n-1)(n-2)!,
> $$
>
> it follows that
>
> $$
> {}_nP_2
> =
> \boxed{n(n-1)}.
> $$
