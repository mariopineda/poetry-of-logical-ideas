Eleven named campers each receive one frozen treat. The treats consist of **4 identical mango**, **3 identical lemon** and **4 identical berry** flavours. **Nadia must receive mango**.

How many distinct flavour assignments are possible?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> First give Nadia a mango treat. Ten named campers remain, and the available flavours are 3 mango, 3 lemon and 4 berry. Assigning indistinguishable flavours gives
>
> $$\frac{10!}{3!3!4!}=\boxed{4\,200}.$$
>
> The specific treats within a flavour are not distinguishable, but the campers are.
