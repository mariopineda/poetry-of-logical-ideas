All seven different letters of **ASTRIDE** are placed in a row. How many arrangements begin with **two vowels**? Explain how the restriction changes the choices.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The vowels are A, I and E, so there are 3 choices for the first position and 2 for the second. The remaining 5 different letters can be arranged freely:
>
> $$3\cdot2\cdot5!=6\cdot120=\boxed{720}.$$
