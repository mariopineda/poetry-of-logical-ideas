A spinner has \(4\) equal sections labelled red, blue, green, and yellow. The spinner is spun once and a regular 6-sided die is rolled once.

### A.

How many possible outcomes are there for each individual event?

### B.

Use the Fundamental Counting Principle to determine the total number of elements in the sample space. Show your multiplication statement.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> The spinner has \(4\) possible outcomes.
>
> The die has \(6\) possible outcomes.
>
> ### B.
>
> $$
> 4 \times 6 = 24
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> n(S)=24
> $$
