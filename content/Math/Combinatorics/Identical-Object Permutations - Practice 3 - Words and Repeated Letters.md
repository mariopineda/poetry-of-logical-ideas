For each word, determine the number of **different arrangements of all its letters**, whether or not the arrangement is a real word. Show how repeated letters affect the count.

### A. CLOUD
### B. SEATTLE
### C. BALLOON
### D. WILDLIFE

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A. CLOUD
>
> All five letters differ:
>
> $$5!=\boxed{120}.$$
>
> ### B. SEATTLE
>
> There are 7 letters: E occurs twice and T occurs twice.
>
> $$\frac{7!}{2!2!}=\boxed{1260}.$$
>
> ### C. BALLOON
>
> There are 7 letters: L occurs twice and O occurs twice.
>
> $$\frac{7!}{2!2!}=\boxed{1260}.$$
>
> ### D. WILDLIFE
>
> There are 8 letters: I occurs twice and L occurs twice.
>
> $$\frac{8!}{2!2!}=\boxed{10080}.$$
>
> We divide by factorials of duplicate counts because exchanging identical letters does not create a new arrangement.
