A soccer team finishes 18 matches with **12 wins**, **4 losses**, and **2 draws**. How many distinct chronological sequences of results could produce this final record?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> There are 18 result positions, with 12 identical W symbols, 4 identical L symbols and 2 identical D symbols. Thus
>
> $$\frac{18!}{12!4!2!}=\boxed{278\,460}.$$
>
> Each distinct sequence gives the same final record but a different order of results.
