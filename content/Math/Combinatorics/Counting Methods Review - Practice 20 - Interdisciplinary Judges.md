A jury of **four people** must be selected from four scientists and seven artists (eleven people total).

### A. How many juries include a particular scientist, Dr. Lin?
### B. How many juries include **at least one artist**?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> Dr. Lin is mandatory; choose the other three from the remaining ten people:
>
> $$\binom{10}3=\boxed{120}.$$
>
> ### B.
>
> Start with all four-person juries, then subtract the one jury consisting of all four scientists:
>
> $$\binom{11}4-\binom44=330-1=\boxed{329}.$$
