A concert has 3 potential lead presenters, 5 sound technicians and 6 ushers available. An event team requires **one presenter, two technicians and two ushers**.

How many different teams can be formed if the two technician slots and two usher slots are interchangeable?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Choose a presenter, a pair of technicians and a pair of ushers; multiply the independent categories:
>
> $$\binom31\binom52\binom62=3\cdot10\cdot15=\boxed{450}.$$
