The probability that a contestant wins a gallery door prize is $\frac{7}{15}$. Find the odds **against** winning and explain the difference between odds and probability.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Losing probability is $1-\frac7{15}=\frac8{15}$. Odds against winning compare losing with winning, $\boxed{8:7}$. Probability compares the favourable count with the total; odds compare two competing counts.
