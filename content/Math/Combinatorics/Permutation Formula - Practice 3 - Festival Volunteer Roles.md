Eight volunteers are qualified for four **different** roles: entrance host, equipment manager, stage coordinator and photographer. Each person can take at most one role.

How many assignments of people to the four roles are possible? Show the Fundamental Counting Principle and permutation notation.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Each job is different, so exchanging two volunteers changes the assignment. We have 8 choices for the first role, then 7, 6 and 5:
>
> $$8\cdot7\cdot6\cdot5=\boxed{1680}.$$
>
> Equivalently,
>
> $$\displaystyle {}_8P_4=\frac{8!}{4!}=\boxed{1680}.$$
