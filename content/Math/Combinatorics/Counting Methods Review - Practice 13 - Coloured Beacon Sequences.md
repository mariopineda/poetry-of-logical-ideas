Eight uniquely coloured beacons can be switched on in a specified **order**, using each beacon at most once. A message must use at least one beacon.

### A. How many messages use **at least six** beacons?
### B. How many use **at most two** beacons?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> Messages can use exactly 6, 7 or 8 beacons:
>
> $${}_8P_6+{}_8P_7+{}_8P_8=20160+40320+40320=\boxed{100\,800}.$$
>
> ### B.
>
> Messages can use exactly 1 or 2 beacons:
>
> $${}_8P_1+{}_8P_2=8+56=\boxed{64}.$$
