Nine distinct points are marked on the circumference of a circle. No three points are collinear.

### A.

How many straight **chords** can be drawn by joining two of the points?

### B.

How many different triangles have their vertices among those points?

![Question diagram](../../Assets/Counting%20-%20CO-06%20-%20Question.png)

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> Choose two endpoints. Their order does not change the chord:
>
> $$\binom92=\boxed{36}.$$
>
> ### B.
>
> Choose any three distinct points. Three distinct points on a circle form a triangle:
>
> $$\binom93=\boxed{84}.$$
