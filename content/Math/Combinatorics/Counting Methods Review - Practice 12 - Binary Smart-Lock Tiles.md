A smart-lock prototype has a **3-by-4 grid of 12 positions**. Each position may independently be set to **on** or **off**.

How many different settings are possible? Explain what the exponent represents.

![[Counting - MR-12 - Question.svg]]

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Each of the 12 positions has exactly two states. The Fundamental Counting Principle gives
>
> $$\underbrace{2\cdot2\cdots2}_{12\text{ positions}}=2^{12}=\boxed{4096}.$$
>
> The exponent 12 counts the **independent positions**, not the number of rows or columns.
