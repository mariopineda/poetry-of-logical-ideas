A sculpture uses **12 wooden blocks** stacked in one vertical tower: 3 identical red, 4 identical blue, 3 identical yellow and 2 identical green. The **top and bottom blocks must both be green**.

How many different visible colour sequences are possible?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Both green blocks are fixed at the ends. The ten remaining slots contain 3 red, 4 blue and 3 yellow blocks:
>
> $$\frac{10!}{3!4!3!}=\boxed{4\,200}.$$
