Eight tokens, each either copper-coloured or silver-coloured, are arranged in a row. Tokens of the same colour are indistinguishable. There are exactly $56$ distinguishable arrangements.

Determine **all possible** numbers of copper and silver tokens, and demonstrate the counting method.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> If there are $k$ copper tokens, choosing their $k$ positions among eight determines the arrangement:
>
> $$\binom8k=56.$$
>
> The binomial coefficients are $1,8,28,56,70,56,28,8,1$, so $k=3$ or $k=5$.
>
> Hence the possibilities are $\boxed{3\text{ copper, }5\text{ silver}}$ or $\boxed{5\text{ copper, }3\text{ silver}}$.
