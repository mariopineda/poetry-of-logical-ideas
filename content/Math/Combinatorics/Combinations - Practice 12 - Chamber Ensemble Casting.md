A chamber-music festival needs **one conductor, two violinists, one cellist and two flautists**. Available musicians are 3 conductors, 7 violinists, 4 cellists and 6 flautists. Nobody is in two categories.

How many different ensembles can be chosen? The two positions for the same instrument are interchangeable.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Use combinations for interchangeable instrument roles and multiply across categories:
>
> $$\binom31\binom72\binom41\binom62
> =3\cdot21\cdot4\cdot15
> =\boxed{3\,780}.$$
