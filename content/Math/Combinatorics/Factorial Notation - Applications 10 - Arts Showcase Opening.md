Eight **different acts** will perform at an arts showcase. The organizers have already chosen the spoken-word performance to be the **opening act**. Every other act will perform exactly once afterward.

In how many different orders can all eight acts appear? Explain your reasoning and use factorial notation.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The opening act has been fixed, so there is **one choice** for the first position.
>
> The remaining seven different acts can be arranged in any order:
>
> $$
> 7\cdot6\cdot5\cdot4\cdot3\cdot2\cdot1
> =7!
> =\boxed{5040}.
> $$
>
> Thus, **5040 complete performance orders** are possible. The opening act is included in each schedule, but its position does not vary.
