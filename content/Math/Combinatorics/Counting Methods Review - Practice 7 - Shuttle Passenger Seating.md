A community shuttle has **nine distinct passenger seats**. The driver is fixed and is **not** included among these seats. The named passengers are all distinct.

### A. How many ways can nine passengers occupy all nine seats?
### B. How many ways can seven passengers occupy any seven seats?
### C. Twelve passengers arrive, but only nine can ride this shuttle. How many seated passenger arrangements are possible (the other three travel separately)?

![Question diagram](../../Assets/Counting%20-%20MR-07%20-%20Question.png)

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> $$9!=\boxed{362\,880}.$$
>
> ### B.
>
> Seven passengers occupy seven of nine distinct seats:
>
> $${}_9P_7=\frac{9!}{2!}=\boxed{181\,440}.$$
>
> ### C.
>
> Choose and arrange nine of the twelve passengers in distinct seats:
>
> $${}_{12}P_9=\frac{12!}{3!}=\boxed{79\,833\,600}.$$
>
> A permutation is needed because who sits in which seat matters.
