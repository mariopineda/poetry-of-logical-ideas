A community league has four separate divisions of six teams. In each division, every team plays every other team exactly once, and no matches take place between divisions. Determine the total number of matches scheduled.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Each division has six teams, and a match is an unordered pair of different teams. The number of matches in one division is
>
> $$\binom62=\frac{6\cdot5}{2}=15.$$
>
> There are four separate divisions and **no inter-division games**, giving
>
> $$\boxed{4\binom62=60\text{ matches}}.$$
