## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> 1.
> $$
> \begin{align*}
> \frac{(n+1)!}{n!}& = 6 \\ \\
> \frac{(n+1)(n)!}{n!} & = 6 \\ \\
> n+1 & = 6 \\\\
> n & = 5
> \end{align*}
> $$
> 2. $$
> \begin{align*}
> (n+1)! & = 6(n-1)! \\ \\
> \frac{(n+1)!}{(n-1)!} & = 6 \\\\
> \frac{(n+1)(n)(n-1)!}{(n-1)!} & = 6 \\\\
> (n+1)n & = 6 \\\\
> n^2+n - 6 & = 0 \\\\
> (n+3)(n-2) = 0 \\\\
> n = -3, n=2
> \end{align*}
> $$
