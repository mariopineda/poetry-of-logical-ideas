### A.

Simplify:

$$
\frac{(n+5)!}{(n+3)!}
$$

### C.

Solve:

$$
\frac{(n+1)!}{(n-1)!}=30
$$

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> \frac{(n+5)!}{(n+3)!}
> &=
> \frac{(n+5)(n+4)(n+3)!}{(n+3)!}\\
> &=(n+5)(n+4)
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> $$
> \boxed{(n+5)(n+4)}
> $$
>
> ### C.
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> \frac{(n+1)!}{(n-1)!}&=30\\
> \frac{(n+1)n(n-1)!}{(n-1)!}&=30\\
> n(n+1)&=30\\
> n^2+n-30&=0\\
> (n+6)(n-5)&=0
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> Since factorials require valid non-negative integer inputs,
>
> $$
> \boxed{n=5}
> $$
