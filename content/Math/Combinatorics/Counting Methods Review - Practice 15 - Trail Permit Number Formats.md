A field station issues permit numbers consisting of **one of three region prefixes**, **one of four site prefixes**, and **four digits** from 0–9. A code may begin with zero.

### A. How many permits are possible if the last four digits must all be different?
### B. How many if digits may repeat?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> $$3\cdot4\cdot{}_{10}P_4=12\cdot5040=\boxed{60\,480}.$$
>
> ### B.
>
> $$3\cdot4\cdot10^4=\boxed{120\,000}.$$
