A gallery is arranging **five different astronomy photographs** and **four different nature photographs** in one row. Every nature photograph must have astronomy photographs immediately on both sides.

How many different alternating arrangements are possible?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Because there are 5 astronomy photographs and 4 nature photographs, the only alternating pattern is
>
> $$\text{A N A N A N A N A}.$$
>
> Arrange the 5 different astronomy photographs in the A slots and the 4 different nature photographs in the N slots:
>
> $$5!\cdot4!=120\cdot24=\boxed{2880}.$$
