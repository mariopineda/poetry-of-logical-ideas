Four members of a robotics club and four members of a photography club will sit in eight seats in one straight row. All eight people are distinct.

How many seating arrangements are possible if a person from one club must sit beside people from the other club wherever possible—that is, **the clubs alternate**?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> There are two club patterns: R P R P R P R P or P R P R P R P R. Within each pattern the robotics members can be arranged in $4!$ ways and the photography members in $4!$ ways:
>
> $$2\cdot4!\cdot4!=2\cdot24\cdot24=\boxed{1152}.$$
