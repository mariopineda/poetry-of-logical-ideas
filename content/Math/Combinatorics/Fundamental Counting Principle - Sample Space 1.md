A coin is tossed once and an 8-sided die is rolled once.

### A.

How many possible outcomes are there for each individual event?

### B.

Use the Fundamental Counting Principle to determine the total number of elements in the sample space. Show your multiplication statement.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> The coin has \(2\) possible outcomes.
>
> The 8-sided die has \(8\) possible outcomes.
>
> ### B.
>
> $$
> 2 \times 8 = 16
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> n(S)=16
> $$
