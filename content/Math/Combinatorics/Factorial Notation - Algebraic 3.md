### A.

Simplify:

$$
\frac{(n+3)!}{(n+1)!}
$$

### C.

Solve:

$$
\frac{(n+2)!}{n!}=56
$$

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> \frac{(n+3)!}{(n+1)!}
> &=
> \frac{(n+3)(n+2)(n+1)!}{(n+1)!}\\
> &=(n+3)(n+2)
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> $$
> \boxed{(n+3)(n+2)}
> $$
>
> ### C.
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> \frac{(n+2)!}{n!}&=56\\
> \frac{(n+2)(n+1)n!}{n!}&=56\\
> (n+2)(n+1)&=56\\
> n^2+3n-54&=0\\
> (n+9)(n-6)&=0
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> Since factorials require valid non-negative integer inputs,
>
> $$
> \boxed{n=6}
> $$
