Using all seven distinct letters of **EQUATOR**, how many arrangements begin with a **vowel**, followed immediately by a **consonant**? The other five letters may appear in any order.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The vowels are E, U, A and O (4 choices), and the consonants are Q, T and R (3 choices). The first two categories cannot overlap. Arrange the remaining 5 letters:
>
> $$4\cdot3\cdot5!=12\cdot120=\boxed{1440}.$$
