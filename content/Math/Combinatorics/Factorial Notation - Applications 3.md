### A.

Manually calculate:

$$
\frac{8!}{5!}
$$

### B.

Nine musicians are arranging themselves in a row on stage. One particular musician must stand in the last position.

How many different arrangements are possible?

### C.

Six students compete for first, second, and third place in a contest.

How many different arrangements are possible for the top three positions?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> $$
> \frac{8!}{5!}
> =
> \frac{8(7)(6)(5!)}{5!}
> =
> 8(7)(6)
> =
> 336
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> One position is fixed, leaving \(8\) musicians to arrange.
>
> $$
> 8!=40\,320
> $$
>
> ### C.
>
> $$
> 6(5)(4)=120
> $$
>
> Equivalently,
>
> $$
> \frac{6!}{3!}=120
> $$
