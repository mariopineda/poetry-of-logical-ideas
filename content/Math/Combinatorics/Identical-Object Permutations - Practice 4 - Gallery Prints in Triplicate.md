A photo gallery mounts **four identical prints of each of three different images** along one long wall. Prints of the same image look identical.

How many distinguishable sequences of the 12 prints are possible?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Without duplicates there would be \(12!\) arrangements. Permuting the four copies of any one photograph does not change the display. Divide by \(4!\) for each of the three photograph types:
>
> $$\frac{12!}{4!\,4!\,4!}=\boxed{34\,650}.$$
