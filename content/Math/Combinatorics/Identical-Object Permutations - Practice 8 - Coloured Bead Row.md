A bracelet designer lines up **10 beads**: four indistinguishable red beads, four indistinguishable blue beads and two indistinguishable green beads.

### A.

How many different colour sequences are possible?

### B.

How many sequences have a green bead at **both ends**?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> Because beads of each colour are indistinguishable,
>
> $$\frac{10!}{4!4!2!}=\boxed{3150}.$$
>
> ### B.
>
> Both green beads are fixed at the ends. Arrange the four red and four blue beads in the remaining eight positions:
>
> $$\frac{8!}{4!4!}=\boxed{70}.$$
