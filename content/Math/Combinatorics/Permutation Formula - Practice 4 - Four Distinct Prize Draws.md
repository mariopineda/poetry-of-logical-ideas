An animal-rescue fundraiser sells **1,200 distinct tickets**. Four prizes (a bicycle, camping set, telescope and gift card) are awarded in that order. A winning ticket is **not returned** before the next draw.

Write the exact number of possible prize-draw outcomes in **permutation notation**; you do not need to evaluate the enormous number.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Each prize is distinguishable, and no ticket can win twice. Thus the ordered outcomes are
>
> $$\boxed{{}_{1200}P_4=1200\cdot1199\cdot1198\cdot1197}.$$
>
> It is a permutation, not a combination, because the **order determines which prize** each winner receives.
