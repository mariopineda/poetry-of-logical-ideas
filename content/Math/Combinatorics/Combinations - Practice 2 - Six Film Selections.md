A public library lets each patron choose **six different short films** from a catalogue of **45** for a free weekend screening. The order in which films are chosen is irrelevant.

How many different selections are possible? State why a combination is appropriate.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> A selection contains six distinct films, but no screening order is specified; the same six films form just one selection regardless of the order picked.
>
> $$\binom{45}6=\frac{45!}{6!39!}=\boxed{8\,145\,060}.$$
