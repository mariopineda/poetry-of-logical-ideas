A visitor walks from the **northwest corner** A of a rectangular street grid to the **southeast corner** B. Every shortest route requires exactly **five east** steps and **four south** steps. The visitor may move only east or south.

How many shortest routes are possible? Use factorial notation for repeated steps.

![[Counting - MR-18 - Question.svg]]

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Each shortest route is an arrangement of five indistinguishable E moves and four indistinguishable S moves:
>
> $$\frac{9!}{5!4!}=\binom94=\boxed{126}.$$
