A science museum has **seven different mineral specimens** and wishes to give distinct specimens to visitors, with no visitor receiving more than one specimen.

### A.

How many ways can specimens be given to **three named visitors**?

### B.

How many ways can a specimen be given to **one named visitor**?

Show permutation notation in each part.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> The visitors are distinguishable, so which visitor receives which specimen matters. There are 7 choices, then 6, then 5:
>
> $$\displaystyle {}_7P_3=7\cdot6\cdot5=\boxed{210}.$$
>
> ### B.
>
> There are 7 choices for the only visitor:
>
> $$\displaystyle {}_7P_1=\boxed{7}.$$
