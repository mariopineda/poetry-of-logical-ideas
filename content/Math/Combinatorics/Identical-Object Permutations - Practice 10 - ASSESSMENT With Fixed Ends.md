How many distinguishable arrangements of all the letters in **ASSESSMENT** begin with **A** and end with **T**?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ASSESSMENT has ten letters: A once, S four times, E twice, M once, N once, T once. Fix A first and T last. The eight middle positions contain four identical S and two identical E, plus M and N:
>
> $$\frac{8!}{4!2!}=\boxed{840}.$$
