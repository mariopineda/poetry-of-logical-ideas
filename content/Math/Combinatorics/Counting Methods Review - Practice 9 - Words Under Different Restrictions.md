Use **all letters** each time. An arrangement need not be an English word.

### A. How many arrangements of **BRAVEST** begin with B and end with T?
### B. How many distinct arrangements are possible of **BALLOON**, without restrictions?
### C. How many arrangements of **RABBIT** begin and end with B?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> B and T are fixed, leaving five distinct letters:
>
> $$5!=\boxed{120}.$$
>
> ### B.
>
> BALLOON has seven letters: L and O each appear twice:
>
> $$\frac{7!}{2!2!}=\boxed{1260}.$$
>
> ### C.
>
> The two identical Bs are fixed at the ends. Arrange the four remaining distinct letters R, A, I and T:
>
> $$4!=\boxed{24}.$$
