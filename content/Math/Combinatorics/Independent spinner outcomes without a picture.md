Two spinners are divided into **equal-sized sectors** and are spun independently once each. The sectors are shown below.

![[batch11-independent-spinners.svg]]

Find the probability that **Spinner A** lands on a letter from the word **RAIN** and **Spinner B** lands on a prime number. Show the favourable outcomes on each spinner and explain why the probabilities may be multiplied.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Spinner A has eight equal sectors, and the distinct letters $R,A,I,N$ give four favourable sectors: $4/8$. Spinner B has six sectors, and the primes shown are $2$ and $3$: $2/6$. Because the two spins are independent,
>
> $$P(\text{RAIN letter and prime})=\frac48\cdot\frac26=\boxed{\frac16}.$$
