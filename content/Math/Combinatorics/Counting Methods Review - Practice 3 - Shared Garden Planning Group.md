Seven hikers and eight cyclists have volunteered for a **five-person** garden-planning group. The selected people have equal roles.

### A. How many groups are possible with no restrictions?
### B. How many have exactly two hikers and three cyclists?
### C. How many contain no hikers?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> $$\binom{15}5=\boxed{3003}.$$
>
> ### B.
>
> $$\binom72\binom83=\boxed{1176}.$$
>
> ### C.
>
> Choose all five from eight cyclists:
>
> $$\binom85=\boxed{56}.$$
