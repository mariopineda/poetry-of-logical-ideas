A decorator has **six different lantern colours**, with exactly **two indistinguishable lanterns of each colour**. All 12 lanterns will be hung in two **labelled** rows, each containing six positions.

How many distinguishable arrangements are possible across the 12 positions?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Treat the six positions in the top row followed by the six positions in the bottom row as 12 distinguishable slots. Every colour is repeated twice, so divide by \(2!\) six times:
>
> $$\frac{12!}{(2!)^6}=\boxed{7\,484\,400}.$$
