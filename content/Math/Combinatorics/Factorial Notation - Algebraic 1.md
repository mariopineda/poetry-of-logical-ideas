### A.

Simplify:

$$
\frac{(n+4)!}{(n+2)!}
$$

### C.

Solve:

$$
\frac{n!}{(n-2)!}=42
$$

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> \frac{(n+4)!}{(n+2)!}
> &=
> \frac{(n+4)(n+3)(n+2)!}{(n+2)!}\\
> &=(n+4)(n+3)
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> $$
> \boxed{(n+4)(n+3)}
> $$
>
> ### C.
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> \frac{n!}{(n-2)!}&=42\\
> \frac{n(n-1)(n-2)!}{(n-2)!}&=42\\
> n(n-1)&=42\\
> n^2-n-42&=0\\
> (n-7)(n+6)&=0
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> Since factorials require valid non-negative integer inputs,
>
> $$
> \boxed{n=7}
> $$
