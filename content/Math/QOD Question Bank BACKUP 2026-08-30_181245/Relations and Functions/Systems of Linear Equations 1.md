## Question

Solve by graphing and check your answer algebraically

1. $$
   \begin{array}{rcl}
   x+y & = & 5 \\
   3x-y & = & 3
   \end{array}
   $$
2. $$
   \begin{array}{rcl}
   x+y & = & 7 \\
   3x+4y & = & 24
   \end{array}
   $$

## Solution

### A.

Rewrite the system in slope y-intercept form.
$$
\begin{array}{rcl}
y & = & -x+5 \\
y & = & 3x-3
\end{array}
$$
Graph the two equations and visually determine the coordinates of the point of intersection.

![[Images/Legacy/solvingsystemsofeqsgraphically1.png]]

The solution to this system is $(2,3)$. Verify by substituting solution into the original system and evaluate LHS and RHS.
$$
\begin{array}{rcl}
x+y & = & 5 \\
2+3 & = & 5 \\
5 & = & 5 \\
\mbox{LHS} & = & \mbox{RHS} \\
& & \\
3x-y & = & 3 \\
3 \times 2 - 3 & = & 3 \\
3 & = & 3 \\
\mbox{LHS} & = & \mbox{RHS}
\end{array}
$$

### B.

Rewrite the system in slope y-intercept form.
$$
\begin{array}{rcl}
y & = & -x+7 \\
y & = & -\frac{\displaystyle 3}{\displaystyle 4}x+6
\end{array}
$$

![[Images/Legacy/solvingsystemsofeqsgraphically2.png]]

Solution: $(4,3)$. Verify by substituting solution into the original system and evaluate LHS and RHS.
$$
\begin{array}{rcl}
x+y & = & 7 \\
4+5 & = & 7 \\
7 & = & 7 \\
\mbox{LHS} & = & \mbox{RHS} \\
& & \\
3x+4y & = & 24 \\
3 \times 4+4 \times 3 & = & 24 \\
24 & = & 24 \\
\mbox{LHS} & = & \mbox{RHS}
\end{array}
$$
