## Question

1. A box has a surface area of 432 cm$^2$. Its length and width are 8 cm and 6 cm respectivelly. What is its height, rounded to the nearest whole number?
2. A cylinder has a surface area of 1800 mm$^2$ and a radius of 12 mm. What is its height, rounded to the nearest hundreth?
3. A square-based pyramid has a surface area of 129.5 in$^2$. The length of its base is 4.5 in. What is its slant height, rounded to the nearest hundreth?
4. An ice cream cone has a surface area of 198.6 cm$^2$. The diameter of the cone is 10.2 cm. What is the slant height of the cone, rounded to the nearest tenth?
5. The surface area of a cone is 400.2 ft$^2$. The radius of the cone is 6.0 ft. Determine the slant height of the cone, rounded to the nearest tenth?
6. A sphere has a surface area of 6.4 m$^2$. What is the diameter of the sphere, rounded to the nearest hundreth?
7. A sphere has a surface area of 10.1 m$^2$. What is the radius, rounded to the nearest tenth?

## Solution

### A.

12 cm

### B.

17.87 mm

### C.

12.14 in

### D.

7.3 cm

### E.

15.2 ft

### F.

1.43 m

### G.

0.9 m
