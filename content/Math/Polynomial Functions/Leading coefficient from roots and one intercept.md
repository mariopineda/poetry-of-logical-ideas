The graph shows a quartic function $f(x)$ with three distinct $x$-intercepts and the labelled $y$-intercept.

![[diploma-batch05-2016-mc11-quartic.svg]]

The graph crosses at the two negative zeros and touches at the positive zero. Determine the **leading coefficient** and write the rule in factored form. Explain the multiplicity of each factor.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The crossing zeros $x=-3$ and $x=-1$ have multiplicity $1$, while the touch at $x=2$ has multiplicity $2$. A quartic model therefore has the form
>
> $$
> f(x)=a(x+3)(x+1)(x-2)^2.
> $$
>
> The labelled $y$-intercept is $(0,6)$, giving
>
> $$
> 6=a(3)(1)(4)=12a
> \quad\Longrightarrow\quad a=\frac12.
> $$
>
> Thus
>
> $$
> \boxed{f(x)=\frac12(x+3)(x+1)(x-2)^2},
> $$
>
> with leading coefficient $\boxed{\tfrac12}$.
