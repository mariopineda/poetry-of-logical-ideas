A metalworker folds up both long edges of a sheet of metal that is **36 cm wide** to make an open rectangular rain channel. Each upright side has height $x$ cm, leaving a horizontal bottom width of $36-2x$ cm.

![[diploma-batch08-2013-nr12-gutter.svg]]

The channel's cross-sectional area is $A(x)=x(36-2x)$. Determine the side height that **maximizes** its area, and find that maximum area. State the physical domain.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> For a usable open channel the side height must be positive and the remaining bottom width positive:
>
> $$x>0,\qquad36-2x>0\quad\Rightarrow\quad\boxed{0<x<18}.$$
>
> Expand the area function:
>
> $$A(x)=-2x^2+36x.$$
>
> Because its leading coefficient is negative, the vertex is a maximum. Its $x$-coordinate is
>
> $$x=-\frac b{2a}=-\frac{36}{2(-2)}=\boxed{9\text{ cm}}.$$
>
> The maximum area is $A(9)=9(36-18)=\boxed{162\text{ cm}^2}$. Both dimensions are positive and the vertex lies inside the physical domain.
