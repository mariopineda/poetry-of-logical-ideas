An open-topped rectangular container is produced by cutting equal squares of side $x$ from the four corners of a square sheet measuring $30$ cm by $30$ cm, then folding up the sides. Its volume is

$$V(x)=x(30-2x)^2.$$

State the physical domain, and use **graphing technology or a table** to determine the value of $x$ that maximizes $V$ and the maximum volume. Explain how the values and graph support a maximum rather than a minimum.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The height is $x$ and the square base has side $30-2x$. Both must be positive, so $\boxed{0<x<15}$.
>
> Graph $V=x(30-2x)^2$ for $0\le x\le15$, with $V$ from $0$ to $2100$. The graph's single interior peak is at $\boxed{(5,2000)}$.
>
> To check the scale of the maximum, a table gives $V(4)=1936$, $V(5)=2000$, and $V(6)=1944$, in cubic centimetres.
>
> Therefore the maximizing cut size is $\boxed{x=5\text{ cm}}$ and the greatest volume is $\boxed{2000\text{ cm}^3}$. Near either endpoint, the volume approaches $0$, whereas the graph has a maximum within the physical domain.
