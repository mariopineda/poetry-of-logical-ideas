## Question

For each scenario...

- identify the independent and dependent variable,
- identify if variables are discrete or continuous,
- sketch a graph which best illustrates the given scenario,
  > [!info]- Hint
> Think about how discrete vs. continuous data will affect the graph.
  , and
- describe the graphed scenario using words.

Label the axes and include units (you need to decide on appropriate units).

###### A.

how a person's height above ground varies over time during a Ferris wheel ride.

###### B.

number of jumps made by a frog and the total distance it travels.

###### C.

height of a bouncy ball after being dropped from the top of a building.

###### D.

number of houses sold by a realtor in a year.

###### E.

distance from home as you drive to Calgary to visit a friend.

###### F.

your speed as you bike up and down a hill.

###### G.

revenue from selling eggs at the Farmer's market.

###### H.

your level as you start the campain in a new game on your XBox.
