A biodegradable material has a **half-life of 24 days**. A sample contains $18$ grams after $72$ days.

Determine the original mass of the sample. Explain how many half-lives have elapsed and verify your result.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The number of half-lives is
>
> $$
> \frac{72}{24}=3.
> $$
>
> Let $M_0$ be the initial mass. The half-life model gives
>
> $$
> 18=M_0\left(\frac12\right)^3=\frac{M_0}{8}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{M_0=144\text{ g}}.
> $$
>
> Check: $144\to72\to36\to18$ grams after three successive half-lives.
