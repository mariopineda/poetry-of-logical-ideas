A simplified model for per-capita GDP, $G(t)$ dollars, as a function of an increase of $t^\circ\text{C}$ in average temperature is

$$
G(t)=22588-168.05472t^2.
$$

1. Graph $G(t)$ for $0\leq t\leq10$.
2. Determine $G(6)$. Explain what the result represents in context. Round to the nearest whole dollar.
3. According to the model, what is the per-capita GDP when there is no temperature increase?
4. What temperature increase results in the modeled per-capita GDP being reduced to half of its value at $t=0$? Round to the nearest tenth of a degree Celsius.

> [!info]- Source
> Model retained from the original QOD, which cites Howard & Sterner (2017).

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> Graph
>
> $$
> G(t)=22588-168.05472t^2
> $$
>
> over $0\leq t\leq10$.
>
> ### 2.
>
> $$
> G(6)=22588-168.05472(6)^2=16538.03008.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{G(6)\approx \$16\,538}.
> $$
>
> ### 3.
>
> $$
> G(0)=22588,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> \boxed{\$22\,588}.
> $$
>
> ### 4.
>
> Half of $G(0)$ is $11294$. Solve
>
> $$
> 11294=22588-168.05472t^2.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> t^2\approx67.204
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> t\approx8.197.
> $$
>
> Since temperature increase is nonnegative,
>
> $$
> \boxed{t\approx8.2^\circ\text{C}}.
> $$
