If $f(x)=5-3x$ and $g(x)=4x+1$, then solve $f(2-5x)=g(4x+6)$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Compute each side.
>
> $$
> f(2-5x)
> =
> 5-3(2-5x)
> =
> 15x-1.
> $$
>
> Also,
>
> $$
> g(4x+6)
> =
> 4(4x+6)+1
> =
> 16x+25.
> $$
>
> Set them equal:
>
> $$
> 15x-1=16x+25.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> -x=26
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=-26}.
> $$
