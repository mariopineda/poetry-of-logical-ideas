Solve the rational equation

$$
\frac{x+4}{3x}=\frac54.
$$

State the original non-permissible value and **verify** the solution by substitution.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The original denominator requires $x\ne0$.
>
> Cross-multiply:
>
> $$
> 4(x+4)=5(3x)
> \quad\Longrightarrow\quad4x+16=15x
> \quad\Longrightarrow\quad\boxed{x=\frac{16}{11}}.
> $$
>
> This value is nonzero, so it is permitted. Substitute it into the left side:
>
> $$
> \frac{\frac{16}{11}+4}{3\left(\frac{16}{11}\right)}
> =\frac{60/11}{48/11}=\frac{60}{48}=\frac54.
> $$
>
> The substitution agrees with the right side, completing the verification.
