A signal reading is modelled by

$$
S(t)=7^{2t-3}.
$$

Find the value of $t$ when $S(t)=20$. First rewrite the equation in **logarithmic form**, then solve and round $t$ to the nearest hundredth.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Set $7^{2t-3}=20$. By the definition of logarithms,
>
> $$
> 2t-3=\log_7 20.
> $$
>
> Add $3$ and divide by $2$:
>
> $$
> t=\frac{3+\log_7 20}{2}
> =\frac{3+\dfrac{\ln20}{\ln7}}{2}
> \approx\boxed{2.27}.
> $$
>
> The base $7$ is valid because $7>0$ and $7\ne1$.
