## Question

The speed, $v$, in metres per second, of a freely falling object after it has fallen $h$ metres from its point of release is modelled by

$$
v=4.4\sqrt{h}.
$$

State the contextual domain and range in both **set-builder notation** and **interval notation**. Explain each restriction.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ![[Domain and Range - Free-Fall Speed Model Graph.png]]
>
> The distance fallen cannot be negative, so $h\ge0$. The square root is therefore defined for every contextual value of $h$.
>
> **Domain**
>
> - Set-builder notation: $\{h\in\mathbb{R}\mid h\ge0\}$
> - Interval notation: $[0,\infty)$
>
> The model gives a speed of $0$ m/s when $h=0$. As the distance fallen increases, the speed increases without an upper bound in the model.
>
> **Range**
>
> - Set-builder notation: $\{v\in\mathbb{R}\mid v\ge0\}$
> - Interval notation: $[0,\infty)$
