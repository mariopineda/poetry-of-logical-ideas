An object is dropped from a platform $100$ m above the ground. The speed, $v$, in metres per second, of the object after it has fallen $h$ metres from its point of release is modelled by

$$
v=4.4\sqrt{h}.
$$

State the contextual domain and range in both **set notation** and **interval notation**. Explain each restriction.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ![[Domain and Range - Free-Fall Speed Model Graph.png]]
>
> The object begins its fall when $h=0$ and can fall a maximum of $100$ m before reaching the ground. Therefore,
>
> $$
> 0\le h\le100.
> $$
>
> **Domain**
>
> - Set notation: $\{h\mid 0\le h\le100,\ h\in\mathbb{R}\}$
> - Interval notation: $[0,100]$
>
> The minimum speed occurs when the object is released:
>
> $$
> v=4.4\sqrt{0}=0.
> $$
>
> The maximum speed predicted by the model occurs after the object has fallen $100$ m:
>
> $$
> v=4.4\sqrt{100}=44.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> 0\le v\le44.
> $$
>
> **Range**
>
> - Set notation: $\{v\mid 0\le v\le44,\ v\in\mathbb{R}\}$
> - Interval notation: $[0,44]$
