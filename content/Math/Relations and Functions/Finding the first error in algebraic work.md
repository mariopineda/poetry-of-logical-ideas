A learner simplifies $\dfrac{x-2}{x(x+4)}-\dfrac{x+1}{x}$ ($x\ne0,-4$) using these steps:

1. $\dfrac{(x-2)-(x+1)(x+4)}{x(x+4)}$
2. $\dfrac{x-2-(x^2+5x+4)}{x(x+4)}$
3. $\dfrac{x^2+4x+6}{x(x+4)}$

Identify the **first incorrect step**, explain the algebraic error, and give the correct simplified form.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Steps 1 and 2 are correct. In step 3 the leading minus sign was distributed incorrectly. The correct numerator is $x-2-x^2-5x-4=-x^2-4x-6$. Thus first error is **step 3**, and the correct result is $\boxed{\frac{-x^2-4x-6}{x(x+4)}}$, $x\ne0,-4$.
