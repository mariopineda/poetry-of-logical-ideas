Solve $|x^2-5x+2|=2$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Solve by considering both cases.
>
> ### Case 1
>
> $$
> x^2-5x+2=2.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> x^2-5x=0
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> x(x-5)=0.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> x=0,\ 5.
> $$
>
> ### Case 2
>
> $$
> x^2-5x+2=-2.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> x^2-5x+4=0
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> (x-1)(x-4)=0.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> x=1,\ 4.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=0,\ 1,\ 4,\ 5}.
> $$
