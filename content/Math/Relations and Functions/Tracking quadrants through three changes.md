A profile $y=f(x)$ is shown on the coordinate grid. Its entire plotted graph has $-9\le x\le-6$ and $2\le y\le4$.

![[diploma-batch04-2017-nr1-quadrants.svg]]

For **each** of the following transformations, determine the quadrant containing the entire transformed graph. Justify your conclusions by tracking its $x$- and $y$-values.

1. $g(x)=f(x)-7$
2. $h(x)=f(x+12)$
3. $m(x)=f(-x)$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The original $x$-values are negative, between $-9$ and $-6$, and all $y$-values are positive, between $2$ and $4$.
>
> 1. $g(x)=f(x)-7$ leaves the $x$-values negative and changes the $y$-values to $[-5,-3]$. Thus the graph lies entirely in **Quadrant III**.
> 2. $h(x)=f(x+12)$ translates the graph **12 units left**, not right. The transformed $x$-values are $[-21,-18]$, and $y$ is still positive, so the graph lies entirely in **Quadrant II**.
> 3. $m(x)=f(-x)$ reflects the original graph across the $y$-axis. New $x$-values are $[6,9]$, with positive $y$-values, so the graph lies entirely in **Quadrant I**.
>
> $$\boxed{\text{(1) III,\quad (2) II,\quad (3) I}}.$$
