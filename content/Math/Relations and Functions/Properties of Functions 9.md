For $f(x)=1-2x$, determine (in simplfied form):

1.

$$
\frac{f(a)-f(-2)}{a+2}
$$
2.

$$
\frac{f(3+h)-f(3)}{h}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> For
>
> $$
> f(x)=1-2x,
> $$
>
> ### 1.
>
> $$
> f(a)=1-2a
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> f(-2)=5.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \frac{f(a)-f(-2)}{a+2}
> =
> \frac{-2a-4}{a+2}
> =
> \frac{-2(a+2)}{a+2}
> =
> \boxed{-2},
> $$
>
> for $a\neq-2$.
>
> ### 2.
>
> $$
> f(3+h)=1-2(3+h)=-5-2h
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> f(3)=-5.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \frac{f(3+h)-f(3)}{h}
> =
> \frac{-2h}{h}
> =
> \boxed{-2},
> $$
>
> for $h\neq0$.
