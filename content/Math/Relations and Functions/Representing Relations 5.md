The following table shows how the temperature affected the number of people visiting an outdoor pool on five consequtive weekends.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Temperature ($^\circ$C) | Number of people |
| $16$ | $8$ |
| $12$ | $8$ |
| $18$ | $15$ |
| $22$ | $18$ |
| $25$ | $26$ |

### A.

List the ordered pairs of the relation.

### B.

Represent the table of values on graph. Give the graph a title and label the axes. State the independent and dependent variables.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> The ordered pairs are
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> (16,8),\ (12,8),\ (18,15),\ (22,18),\ (25,26)
> }.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> For the graph:
>
> - horizontal axis: **Temperature ($^\circ$C)**
> - vertical axis: **Number of people**
> - plot the five ordered pairs as separate points
> - an appropriate title is **Pool Attendance vs. Temperature**
>
> The independent variable is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{temperature}},
> $$
>
> and the dependent variable is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{number of people visiting the pool}}.
> $$
>
> Because the number of people is counted in whole numbers, the attendance variable is discrete.
