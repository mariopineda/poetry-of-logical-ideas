For each table:

1. describe the relationship in words;
2. write an equation for $y$ in terms of $x$;
3. represent the relation as a mapping diagram by listing each input-to-output arrow.

### A.

| $x$ | $-2$ | $-1$ | $0$ | $1$ | $2$ |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| $y$ | $-\frac83$ | $-\frac73$ | $-2$ | $-\frac53$ | $-\frac43$ |

### B.

| $x$ | $-5$ | $-1$ | $0$ | $3$ | $7$ |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| $y$ | $16$ | $4$ | $1$ | $-8$ | $-20$ |

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> As $x$ increases by $1$, $y$ increases by
>
> $$
> \frac13.
> $$
>
> When $x=0$,
>
> $$
> y=-2.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{y=\frac13x-2}.
> $$
>
> In words:
>
> > $y$ is two less than one-third of $x$.
>
> Mapping arrows:
>
> $$
> -2\to-\frac83,
> \quad
> -1\to-\frac73,
> \quad
> 0\to-2,
> \quad
> 1\to-\frac53,
> \quad
> 2\to-\frac43.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> The slope is
>
> $$
> \frac{4-16}{-1-(-5)}
> =
> \frac{-12}{4}
> =
> -3.
> $$
>
> Since $y=1$ when $x=0$,
>
> $$
> \boxed{y=-3x+1}.
> $$
>
> In words:
>
> > $y$ is one more than negative three times $x$.
>
> Mapping arrows:
>
> $$
> -5\to16,
> \quad
> -1\to4,
> \quad
> 0\to1,
> \quad
> 3\to-8,
> \quad
> 7\to-20.
> $$
