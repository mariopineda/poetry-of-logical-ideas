The original functions $f$ and $g$ are shown below, with rules

$$f(x)=\sqrt{x+6},\qquad g(x)=|x-3|-5.$$

![[diploma-batch06-2016-nr1-two-original-graphs.svg]]

Both graphs undergo a **horizontal stretch by a factor of $3$** about the $y$-axis, yielding $p(x)=f(x/3)$ and $q(x)=g(x/3)$.

Determine the domain and range of each transformed function. Use the graphs to explain why the domain may change while the range stays unchanged.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> For $f$, the endpoint occurs at $x=-6$ and its outputs are nonnegative. A horizontal stretch multiplies each $x$-coordinate by $3$, so the endpoint moves to $x=-18$:
>
> $$p(x)=\sqrt{x/3+6},\qquad \boxed{D_p=[-18,\infty)},\qquad\boxed{R_p=[0,\infty)}.$$
>
> The absolute-value graph has a minimum of $-5$ and domain all real numbers. Its vertex shifts from $x=3$ to $x=9$:
>
> $$q(x)=|x/3-3|-5,\qquad\boxed{D_q=\mathbb R},\qquad\boxed{R_q=[-5,\infty)}.$$
>
> A horizontal stretch modifies inputs, not attainable outputs, so each range is unchanged.
