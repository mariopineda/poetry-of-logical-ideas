## Question

Determine the **domain and range** of each relation in both **set-builder notation** and **interval notation**.

![[Domain and Range - Reading Graphs 1 Graph.png]]

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A
>
> The relation has an open endpoint at $(0,4)$ and continues indefinitely down and to the left.
>
> **Domain**
>
> - Set-builder notation: $\{x\in\mathbb{R}\mid x<0\}$
> - Interval notation: $(-\infty,0)$
>
> **Range**
>
> - Set-builder notation: $\{y\in\mathbb{R}\mid y<4\}$
> - Interval notation: $(-\infty,4)$
>
> The open endpoint means that neither $x=0$ nor $y=4$ is included.
>
> ### B
>
> The relation begins at the closed endpoint $(-4,-2)$ and continues indefinitely up and to the right.
>
> **Domain**
>
> - Set-builder notation: $\{x\in\mathbb{R}\mid x\ge-4\}$
> - Interval notation: $[-4,\infty)$
>
> **Range**
>
> - Set-builder notation: $\{y\in\mathbb{R}\mid y\ge-2\}$
> - Interval notation: $[-2,\infty)$
>
> The closed endpoint means that $x=-4$ and $y=-2$ are included.
>
> ### C
>
> The relation has open endpoints at $(-2,2)$ and $(2,0)$ and an included minimum point at $(0,-3)$.
>
> **Domain**
>
> - Set-builder notation: $\{x\in\mathbb{R}\mid -2<x<2\}$
> - Interval notation: $(-2,2)$
>
> **Range**
>
> - Set-builder notation: $\{y\in\mathbb{R}\mid -3\le y<2\}$
> - Interval notation: $[-3,2)$
