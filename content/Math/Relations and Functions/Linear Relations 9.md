A ball is thrown into the air from the balcony of an apartment and falls to the ground. The height $h$ meters of the ball relative to the ground $t$ seconds after being thrown is given by $h=-5t^2+10t+35$.

1. What is the maximum height of the ball?
2. What are the x- and y-intercepts? Round to the nearest tenth if necessary. Explain what thease mean.
3. What is the height of the ball after 3 seconds?
4. At what time does the ball reach a height of 38 m? Round to the nearest tenth.
5. When is the ball above 15 m? Round to the nearest tenth?
6. Explain whether this is a linear or non-linear scenario.
7. What is the domain and range, in set notation, of the relation in this context?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The height is
>
> $$
> h(t)=-5t^2+10t+35.
> $$
>
> ### 1. Maximum height
>
> The vertex occurs at
>
> $$
> t=-\frac{10}{2(-5)}=1.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> h(1)=-5+10+35=40.
> $$
>
> So the maximum height is
>
> $$
> \boxed{40\text{ m}}.
> $$
>
> ### 2. Intercepts
>
> The $y$-intercept is
>
> $$
> \boxed{(0,35)},
> $$
>
> the initial height of the ball.
>
> For the $x$-intercepts,
>
> $$
> -5t^2+10t+35=0
> $$
>
> gives
>
> $$
> t=1\pm2\sqrt2.
> $$
>
> Numerically,
>
> $$
> t\approx-1.8,\ 3.8.
> $$
>
> Only
>
> $$
> \boxed{(3.8,0)}
> $$
>
> is meaningful in context: the ball reaches the ground about $3.8$ s after being thrown.
>
> ### 3.
>
> $$
> h(3)=-45+30+35=\boxed{20\text{ m}}.
> $$
>
> ### 4.
>
> Set $h=38$:
>
> $$
> -5t^2+10t+35=38.
> $$
>
> This gives
>
> $$
> 5t^2-10t+3=0
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> t=1\pm\frac{\sqrt{10}}5.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \boxed{t\approx0.4\text{ s},\ 1.6\text{ s}}.
> $$
>
> ### 5.
>
> Solve
>
> $$
> -5t^2+10t+35>15.
> $$
>
> The relevant boundary time is
>
> $$
> t=1+\sqrt5\approx3.24.
> $$
>
> In context the ball is above $15$ m for
>
> $$
> \boxed{0\leq t<3.2\text{ s}}
> $$
>
> to the nearest tenth.
>
> ### 6.
>
> The relation is **non-linear** because it is quadratic.
>
> ### 7.
>
> The ball is modeled from release until it hits the ground:
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{Domain }=\{t\mid0\leq t\leq1+2\sqrt2\}}
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{Range }=\{h\mid0\leq h\leq40\}}.
> $$
