Let

$$
f(x)=|x-2|-1.
$$

Evaluate or solve:

1. $f(0)$
2. $f(1)$
3. $f(x)=1$
4. $f(x)=-1$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> $$
> f(0)=|0-2|-1=2-1=\boxed{1}.
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> $$
> f(1)=|1-2|-1=1-1=\boxed{0}.
> $$
>
> ### 3.
>
> Solve
>
> $$
> |x-2|-1=1.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> |x-2|=2,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> x-2=\pm2.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=0,\ 4}.
> $$
>
> ### 4.
>
> Solve
>
> $$
> |x-2|-1=-1.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> |x-2|=0,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=2}.
> $$
