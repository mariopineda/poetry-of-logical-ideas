An outdoor installation is modelled by a function $f$ whose graph is shown. A digital display plots the transformed function $g(x)=-2f(x)+7$. Determine the **range** of $g$, using the smallest value of $f$ visible on the graph. Explain how the reflection and vertical scale factor affect the range.

![[diploma-2017-new-ramp-profile.svg]]

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The graph has a lowest point with ordinate $2$, included in the graph, and its right-hand ray increases indefinitely. Thus $\mathrm{range}(f)=[2,\infty)$. Since $g=-2f+7$, the least value of $f$ produces the **largest** value of $g$:
>
> $$g_{\max}=-2(2)+7=3.$$
>
> As $f\to\infty$, $g\to-\infty$. Both the endpoint inclusion and unbounded direction matter. Hence
>
> $$\boxed{\mathrm{range}(g)=(-\infty,3]}.$$
