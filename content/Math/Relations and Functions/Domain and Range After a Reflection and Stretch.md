A function $f$ is defined for $-3\leq x\leq5$ and has a range of $-2\leq y\leq7$. A new function is defined by

$$g(x)=-2f\!\left(\frac{x}{3}\right).$$

Determine the **domain and range** of $g$. Explain how each transformation changes these intervals.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> For the domain, require $-3\leq x/3\leq 5$, so $-9\leq x\leq15$. For the range, multiply every original output by $-2$, reversing the inequalities:
>
> $$-14\leq -2f(x/3)\leq 4.$$
> Thus the domain is $\boxed{[-9,15]}$ and the range is $\boxed{[-14,4]}$.
