A landscape cross-section is modelled by

$$
f(x)=3x^2-4x+7.
$$

A new graph is defined by $g(x)=-f(-x)$. Expand and simplify $g(x)$ and explain the two reflections that produce it.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> First replace $x$ with $-x$:
>
> $$
> f(-x)=3(-x)^2-4(-x)+7=3x^2+4x+7.
> $$
>
> Then negate every output:
>
> $$
> \boxed{g(x)=-3x^2-4x-7}.
> $$
>
> The inner minus sign reflects the original graph in the **$y$-axis**, and the outer minus sign reflects that image in the **$x$-axis**. These reflections are also equivalent to a half-turn about the origin.
