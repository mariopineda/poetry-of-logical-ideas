For $x>0$, condense the expression

$$
\frac12\log_3x-\log_37
$$

into **one logarithm**. State the domain of the original expression and explain which laws you used.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Apply the power law:
>
> $$
> \frac12\log_3x=\log_3\left(x^{1/2}\right)=\log_3\sqrt{x}.
> $$
>
> Then apply the quotient law:
>
> $$
> \log_3\sqrt{x}-\log_37
> =\boxed{\log_3\left(\frac{\sqrt{x}}7\right)}.
> $$
>
> The original domain is $\boxed{x>0}$. On this domain the condensed argument $\sqrt{x}/7$ is also positive, so the two expressions are equivalent.
