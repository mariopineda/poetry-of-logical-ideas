The domain of $f$ is $[1,5]$ and its range is $[-3,7]$. Define $g(x)=-2f\left(\frac{x}{3}\right)$. Determine the domain and range of $g$, showing how each transformation acts.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The expression $x/3$ must lie in $[1,5]$, hence $3\le x\le15$. The range values are multiplied by $-2$, sending $[-3,7]$ to $[-14,6]$. Therefore $\boxed{D_g=[3,15]}$ and $\boxed{R_g=[-14,6]}$.
