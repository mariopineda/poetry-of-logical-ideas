Roughly sketch.

1.

$$
y=2|x-3|-8
$$
2.

$$
y=-x^3+5
$$
3.

$$
y=\frac{-1}{x+6}
$$
4.

$$
y=\sqrt{7-x}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> $$
> y=2|x-3|-8
> $$
>
> Parent function:
>
> $$
> y=|x|.
> $$
>
> Transformations:
>
> - vertical stretch by factor $2$;
> - right $3$;
> - down $8$.
>
> Vertex:
>
> $$
> \boxed{(3,-8)}.
> $$
>
> $x$-intercepts:
>
> $$
> 2|x-3|-8=0
> $$
>
> gives
>
> $$
> |x-3|=4,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=-1,\ 7}.
> $$
>
> $y$-intercept:
>
> $$
> y=2|0-3|-8=-2,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> \boxed{(0,-2)}.
> $$
>
> Range:
>
> $$
> \boxed{y\geq-8}.
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> $$
> y=-x^3+5
> $$
>
> Parent function:
>
> $$
> y=x^3.
> $$
>
> Transformations:
>
> - reflection in the $x$-axis;
> - up $5$.
>
> Inflection point:
>
> $$
> \boxed{(0,5)}.
> $$
>
> $x$-intercept:
>
> $$
> -x^3+5=0
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=\sqrt[3]{5}\approx1.71}.
> $$
>
> Domain and range are both
>
> $$
> \boxed{\mathbb R}.
> $$
>
> ### 3.
>
> $$
> y=-\frac{1}{x+6}
> $$
>
> Parent function:
>
> $$
> y=\frac1x.
> $$
>
> Transformations:
>
> - reflection in the $x$-axis;
> - left $6$.
>
> Vertical asymptote:
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=-6}.
> $$
>
> Horizontal asymptote:
>
> $$
> \boxed{y=0}.
> $$
>
> $y$-intercept:
>
> $$
> \boxed{\left(0,-\frac16\right)}.
> $$
>
> There is no $x$-intercept.
>
> ### 4.
>
> $$
> y=\sqrt{7-x}
> $$
>
> Parent function:
>
> $$
> y=\sqrt{x}.
> $$
>
> This can be written as
>
> $$
> y=\sqrt{-(x-7)}.
> $$
>
> The graph is reflected horizontally and shifted right $7$.
>
> Endpoint:
>
> $$
> \boxed{(7,0)}.
> $$
>
> Domain:
>
> $$
> \boxed{x\leq7}.
> $$
>
> Range:
>
> $$
> \boxed{y\geq0}.
> $$
>
> $y$-intercept:
>
> $$
> \boxed{(0,\sqrt7)}.
> $$
