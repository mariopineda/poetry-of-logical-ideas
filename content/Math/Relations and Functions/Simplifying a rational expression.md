Simplify the rational expression and state the non-permissible value of the **original** expression:

$$
\frac{10x^2-15x}{5x}.
$$

Explain why the excluded value remains excluded after simplification.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Factor the numerator:
>
> $$
> \frac{10x^2-15x}{5x}=\frac{5x(2x-3)}{5x}.
> $$
>
> Provided $x\ne0$, the common factor $5x$ may be cancelled:
>
> $$
> \boxed{2x-3},\qquad\boxed{x\ne0}.
> $$
>
> The original denominator $5x$ vanishes at $x=0$, so this value remains excluded even though the simplified expression $2x-3$ is defined there.
