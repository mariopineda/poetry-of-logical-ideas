A continuous function $f$ has domain $-2\leq x\leq 7$ and range $-5\leq y\leq 4$. Define

$$g(x)=-3f\left(\frac{x}{4}\right).$$

Determine the domain and range of $g$, explaining each transformation.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> For $g$, the argument $x/4$ must remain within $[-2,7]$, so $-8\leq x\leq28$. Multiplying the original outputs by $-3$ reverses the inequalities: $-12\leq y\leq15$.
>
> **Domain:** $\boxed{[-8,28]}$. **Range:** $\boxed{[-12,15]}$.
