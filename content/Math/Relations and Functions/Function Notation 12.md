Sketch the following functions. State the range in set notation.

1.

$$
f(x)=-2x+3$ for the domain $\{-3,-2,-1,0,1,2,3\}
$$
2.

$$
g(x)=\frac{1}{2}x-5$ for the domain ${x \in \mathbb{R}}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> For
>
> $$
> f(x)=-2x+3
> $$
>
> with domain
>
> $$
> \{-3,-2,-1,0,1,2,3\},
> $$
>
> the corresponding points are
>
> $$
> (-3,9),\ (-2,7),\ (-1,5),\ (0,3),\ (1,1),\ (2,-1),\ (3,-3).
> $$
>
> The range is therefore
>
> $$
> \boxed{\{-3,-1,1,3,5,7,9\}}.
> $$
>
> Because the domain is discrete, the graph consists of these seven separate points.
>
> ### 2.
>
> For
>
> $$
> g(x)=\frac12x-5
> $$
>
> with domain $\mathbb R$, the graph is a straight line with slope $\frac12$ and $y$-intercept $-5$.
>
> A non-horizontal linear function with domain $\mathbb R$ has every real number as an output, so the range is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\{y\mid y\in\mathbb R\}}.
> $$
