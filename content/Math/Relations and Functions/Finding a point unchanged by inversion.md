The function $f(x)=2x-3$ has an inverse. Determine the point on its graph that is unchanged when the graph is reflected across $y=x$. Verify your result using an equation for $f^{-1}(x)$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The inverse graph is the reflection in $y=x$. A point stays fixed under this reflection exactly when its two coordinates are equal. Intersect $f(x)=2x-3$ with $y=x$:
>
> $$2x-3=x\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x=3.$$
>
> Therefore the invariant point is $\boxed{(3,3)}$. Separately, interchange $x$ and $y$ in $y=2x-3$ and solve:
>
> $$f^{-1}(x)=\frac{x+3}{2};\qquad f^{-1}(3)=3.$$
>
> The original and inverse graphs indeed share the claimed fixed point.
