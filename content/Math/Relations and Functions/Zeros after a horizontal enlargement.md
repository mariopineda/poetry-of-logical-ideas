The function $P(x)=(x+4)(x-1)(2x-3)$ is transformed into $N(x)=P\!\left(\frac{x}{3}\right)$. Determine **all zeros** of $N$, showing how the horizontal transformation changes each zero.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The zeros of $P$ follow from its linear factors:
>
> $$x=-4,\quad x=1,\quad x=\frac32.$$
>
> Now $N(x)=P(x/3)$ is zero exactly when $x/3$ equals one of the three original zeros. Multiplying all three roots by $3$ gives
>
> $$\boxed{x=-12,\quad 3,\quad \frac92}.$$
>
> Each result can be substituted into $P(x/3)$ to give a zero factor.
