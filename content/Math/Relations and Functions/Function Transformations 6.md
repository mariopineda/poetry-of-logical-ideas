Without using graphing technology, sketch each function. Identify the transformations from its parent function and state important features.

### 1.

$$
y=(x-3)^3+1
$$

### 2.

$$
y=-\frac14x^2-9
$$

### 3.

$$
y=\sqrt{25-x}+3
$$

### 4.

$$
y=|x^2-4|
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> $$
> y=(x-3)^3+1
> $$
>
> Parent:
>
> $$
> y=x^3.
> $$
>
> Transformations:
>
> - right $3$;
> - up $1$.
>
> The inflection point is
>
> $$
> \boxed{(3,1)}.
> $$
>
> The $x$-intercept is found from
>
> $$
> (x-3)^3+1=0,
> $$
>
> giving
>
> $$
> \boxed{(2,0)}.
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> $$
> y=-\frac14x^2-9
> $$
>
> Parent:
>
> $$
> y=x^2.
> $$
>
> Transformations:
>
> - vertical compression by factor $\frac14$;
> - reflection in the $x$-axis;
> - down $9$.
>
> Vertex:
>
> $$
> \boxed{(0,-9)}.
> $$
>
> Axis of symmetry:
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=0}.
> $$
>
> Range:
>
> $$
> \boxed{y\leq-9}.
> $$
>
> ### 3.
>
> $$
> y=\sqrt{25-x}+3
> =
> \sqrt{-(x-25)}+3.
> $$
>
> Parent:
>
> $$
> y=\sqrt{x}.
> $$
>
> Transformations:
>
> - reflection horizontally;
> - right $25$;
> - up $3$.
>
> Endpoint:
>
> $$
> \boxed{(25,3)}.
> $$
>
> Domain:
>
> $$
> \boxed{x\leq25}.
> $$
>
> Range:
>
> $$
> \boxed{y\geq3}.
> $$
>
> ### 4.
>
> $$
> y=|x^2-4|.
> $$
>
> Start with
>
> $$
> y=x^2-4.
> $$
>
> The part of the parabola below the $x$-axis is reflected above the $x$-axis.
>
> Important points:
>
> $$
> \boxed{(-2,0),\ (0,4),\ (2,0)}.
> $$
>
> The function is symmetric about the $y$-axis and has range
>
> $$
> \boxed{y\geq0}.
> $$
