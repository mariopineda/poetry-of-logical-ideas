If $f(x) = x^2-x$ and $g(x)=x+5$, then find the following. State the domains.

1.

$$
(f-g)(x)
$$
2.

$$
\left( \frac{f}{g} \right)(x)
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Given
>
> $$
> f(x)=x^2-x,
> \qquad
> g(x)=x+5,
> $$
>
> ### 1.
>
> $$
> (f-g)(x)
> =
> x^2-x-(x+5)
> =
> \boxed{x^2-2x-5}.
> $$
>
> Both original functions are defined for all real $x$, so
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{Domain }=\mathbb R}.
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> $$
> \left(\frac fg\right)(x)
> =
> \frac{x^2-x}{x+5}
> =
> \boxed{\frac{x(x-1)}{x+5}}.
> $$
>
> The denominator cannot be zero, so
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{Domain }=\mathbb R\setminus\{-5\}}.
> $$
