Multiply the following two rational expressions and express the result as a single fraction with an expanded numerator:

$$\frac{2x+3}{x+5}\cdot\frac{x+2}{x}.$$

Write the result as $\dfrac{Ax^2+Bx+C}{x(x+5)}$, determine the **single-digit whole numbers** $A,B,C$, and state all non-permissible values.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Multiplying the numerators and retaining the common denominator gives
>
> $$\frac{(2x+3)(x+2)}{x(x+5)}=\frac{2x^2+7x+6}{x(x+5)}.$$
>
> Therefore $\boxed{A=2,\quad B=7,\quad C=6}$, all single-digit whole numbers. From the original denominators, $\boxed{x\ne0,-5}$. These restrictions remain even if the expression could later be simplified.
