## Question

A Ferris wheel takes $3$ minutes to complete one rotation. Riders board at the lowest point, $2$ m above the ground, and reach a maximum height of $64$ m.

State the domain and range for **two complete rotations** in both **set-builder notation** and **interval notation**. Use $t$ for time in minutes and $h$ for height in metres.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> One rotation takes $3$ minutes, so two rotations take
>
> $$
> 2(3)=6\text{ minutes}.
> $$
>
> ![[Domain and Range - Ferris Wheel Model Graph.png]]
>
> Time begins when the riders board at $t=0$ and ends after the second rotation at $t=6$.
>
> **Domain**
>
> - Set-builder notation: $\{t\in\mathbb{R}\mid 0\le t\le6\}$
> - Interval notation: $[0,6]$
>
> The riders move between the minimum height of $2$ m and the maximum height of $64$ m.
>
> **Range**
>
> - Set-builder notation: $\{h\in\mathbb{R}\mid 2\le h\le64\}$
> - Interval notation: $[2,64]$
