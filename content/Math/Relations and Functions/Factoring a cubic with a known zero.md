The polynomial $P(x)=2x^3-3x^2-11x+6$ has a zero at $x=3$. Write

$$P(x)=(x-3)(ax^2+bx+c),$$

and determine $a$, $b$, and $c$ by division or coefficient comparison.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> First verify $P(3)=2(27)-3(9)-11(3)+6=0$. Synthetic division of $2,-3,-11,6$ by $3$ gives quotient coefficients $2,3,-2$ and remainder $0$. Therefore
>
> $$P(x)=(x-3)(2x^2+3x-2),$$
>
> so $\boxed{a=2,\ b=3,\ c=-2}$.
