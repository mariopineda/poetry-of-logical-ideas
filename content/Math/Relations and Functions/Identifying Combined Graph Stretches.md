The blue graph $y=f(x)$ is transformed into the orange graph $y=g(x)$, shown below.

![[diploma-30-1-transformations-original.svg]]

Determine an equation for $g(x)$ in terms of $f(x)$ and justify it using corresponding coordinates.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The original peak $(-2,8)$ maps to $(-3,4)$: the $x$-coordinate is multiplied by $3/2$ and the $y$-coordinate by $1/2$. A horizontal stretch by $3/2$ corresponds to replacing $x$ with $2x/3$.
>
> $$g(x)=\frac12 f\!\left(\frac{2x}{3}\right).$$
