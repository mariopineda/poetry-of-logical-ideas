A scale drawing undergoes a horizontal compression by a factor of $\frac15$ about the $y$-axis, a vertical compression by a factor of $\frac23$ about the $x$-axis, and a translation $6$ units upward. Write a single mapping rule $(x,y)\mapsto(x',y')$, and determine the image of $(-10,9)$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Horizontal compression by a factor of $1/5$ maps $x$ to $x/5$. Vertical compression by $2/3$ maps $y$ to $2y/3$; then the vertical translation adds $6$. Their combined mapping is
>
> $$\boxed{(x,y)\longmapsto\left(\frac x5,\frac{2y}{3}+6\right)}.$$
>
> Substituting $(-10,9)$ gives $x'=-10/5=-2$ and $y'=(2/3)(9)+6=12$, hence
>
> $$\boxed{(-10,9)\mapsto(-2,12)}.$$
