## Question

Simplify:

1. $$\left( \frac{m^5}{m} \right) \left( \frac{m^7}{m^9} \right)$$
2. $$\left( \frac{10a^7}{b^5} \right) \left( \frac{b^9}{2a^6} \right)$$
3. $$\left( \frac{f}{f^{-3}} \right) \left( \frac{f^{-2}}{f^5} \right)$$
4. $$\left( \frac{q^7r^{-1}}{s^{-2}t^5} \right)^{-3}$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> 1. \[
>    \begin{align}
>    & \left( \frac{m^5}{m} \right) \left( \frac{m^7}{m^9} \right) \\
>    = & m^{5-1}m^{7-9} \mbox{ (Quotient Law)}\\
>    = & m^4m^{-2} \\
>    = & m^{4+(-2)} \mbox{ (Product Law)}\\
>    = & \boxed{m^2}
>    \end{align}
>    \]
> 2. \[
>    \begin{align}
>    & \left( \frac{10a^7}{b^5} \right) \left( \frac{b^9}{2a^6} \right) \\
>    = & \frac{10a^7b^9}{2a^6b^5} \\
>    = & 5a^{7-6}b^{9-5} \mbox{ (Quotient Law)}\\
>    = & \boxed{5ab^4}
>    \end{align}
>    \]
> 3. \[
>    \begin{align}
>    & \left( \frac{f}{f^{-3}} \right) \left( \frac{f^{-2}}{f^5} \right) \\
>    = & f^{1-(-3)} f^{-2-5} \mbox{ (Quotient Law)}\\
>    = & f^{1+3} f^{-7}\\
>    = & f^4f^{-7} \\
>    = & f^{4+(-7)} \\
>    = & f^{-3} \\
>    = & \boxed{\frac{1}{f^3}} \mbox{ (Negative Exponent Law)}
>    \end{align}
>    \]
> 4. \[
>    \begin{align}
>    & \left( \frac{q^7r^{-1}}{s^{-2}t^5} \right)^{-3} \\
>    = & \frac{q^{-3(7)}r^{-1(-3)}}{s^{-2(-3)}t^{5(-3)}} \mbox{ (Power of a Power/Quotient Law)}\\
>    = & \frac{q^{-21}r^3}{s^6t^{-15}} \\
>    = & \boxed{\frac{t^{15}r^3}{s^6q^{21}}} \mbox{ (Negative Exponent Law)} \\
>    \end{align}
>    \]
>
