Consider the functions $k(x)=6x-4$, $m(x)=-\frac{3}{4}x+3$ and $n(x)=x+8$. determine the value of $x$ for the following. Leave answers as fractions if necessary. Verify using your graphing calculator or [Desmos](https://www.desmos.com/calculator).

1.

$$
k(x)=20
$$
2.

$$
m(x)=15
$$
3.

$$
n(x)=-5
$$
4.

$$
k(x)=0
$$
5.

$$
m(x)=\frac{1}{2}
$$
6.

$$
n(x)=-\frac{3}{4}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Given
>
> $$
> k(x)=6x-4,
> \qquad
> m(x)=-\frac34x+3,
> \qquad
> n(x)=x+8,
> $$
>
> solve each equation.
>
> 1.
>
> $$
> 6x-4=20
> \Rightarrow
> 6x=24
> \Rightarrow
> \boxed{x=4}.
> $$
>
> 2.
>
> $$
> -\frac34x+3=15
> \Rightarrow
> -\frac34x=12
> \Rightarrow
> \boxed{x=-16}.
> $$
>
> 3.
>
> $$
> x+8=-5
> \Rightarrow
> \boxed{x=-13}.
> $$
>
> 4.
>
> $$
> 6x-4=0
> \Rightarrow
> 6x=4
> \Rightarrow
> \boxed{x=\frac23}.
> $$
>
> 5.
>
> $$
> -\frac34x+3=\frac12
> \Rightarrow
> -\frac34x=-\frac52
> \Rightarrow
> \boxed{x=\frac{10}{3}}.
> $$
>
> 6.
>
> $$
> x+8=-\frac34
> \Rightarrow
> \boxed{x=-\frac{35}{4}}.
> $$
