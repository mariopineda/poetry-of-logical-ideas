## Question

For each relation:

- Create a table of values between $x\_{min}=-10$ and $x\_{max}=10$
- Graph the relations with a graphing calculator or with [Desmos](https://www.desmos.com/calculator)
- Identify relation as linear or non-linear
- Visually identify the x and y-intercepts

1. $y=4-x$
2. $y=x^2+3x-5$
3. $y=\displaystyle\frac{1}{4}x$
4. $y=(x+3)(x-2)$
5. $-3y+2x=9$
