For each relation:

- Create a table of values between $x_{min}=-10$ and $x_{max}=10$
- Graph the relations with a graphing calculator or with [Desmos](https://www.desmos.com/calculator)
- Identify relation as linear or non-linear
- Visually identify the x and y-intercepts

1.

$$
y=4-x
$$
2.

$$
y=x^2+3x-5
$$
3.

$$
y=\displaystyle\frac{1}{4}x
$$
4.

$$
y=(x+3)(x-2)
$$
5.

$$
-3y+2x=9
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> A representative table using $x=-10,0,10$ is enough to establish the shape of each relation.
>
> ### 1. $y=4-x$
>
> $$
> (-10,14),\ (0,4),\ (10,-6)
> $$
>
> This relation is **linear**.
>
> - $x$-intercept: $\boxed{(4,0)}$
> - $y$-intercept: $\boxed{(0,4)}$
>
> ### 2. $y=x^2+3x-5$
>
> $$
> (-10,65),\ (0,-5),\ (10,125)
> $$
>
> This relation is **non-linear**.
>
> - $y$-intercept: $\boxed{(0,-5)}$
> - $x$-intercepts:
>
> $$
> x=\frac{-3\pm\sqrt{29}}{2}
> $$
>
> so approximately
>
> $$
> \boxed{(-4.19,0),(1.19,0)}.
> $$
>
> ### 3. $y=\frac14x$
>
> $$
> (-10,-2.5),\ (0,0),\ (10,2.5)
> $$
>
> This relation is **linear**.
>
> Both intercepts are
>
> $$
> \boxed{(0,0)}.
> $$
>
> ### 4. $y=(x+3)(x-2)$
>
> $$
> (-10,84),\ (0,-6),\ (10,104)
> $$
>
> This relation is **non-linear**.
>
> - $x$-intercepts: $\boxed{(-3,0),(2,0)}$
> - $y$-intercept: $\boxed{(0,-6)}$
>
> ### 5. $-3y+2x=9$
>
> Solve for $y$:
>
> $$
> y=\frac23x-3.
> $$
>
> Representative points are
>
> $$
> (-10,-\tfrac{29}{3}),\ (0,-3),\ (10,\tfrac{11}{3}).
> $$
>
> This relation is **linear**.
>
> - $x$-intercept: $\boxed{\left(\frac92,0\right)}$
> - $y$-intercept: $\boxed{(0,-3)}$
