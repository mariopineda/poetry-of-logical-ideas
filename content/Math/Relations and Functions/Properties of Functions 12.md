Determine the domain of the following combined functions. Answer in interval notation.

1.

$$
y=\frac{4}{x\sqrt{x+8}}
$$
2.

$$
y=\frac{\sqrt{6-x}}{x^2-x}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> $$
> y=\frac{4}{x\sqrt{x+8}}.
> $$
>
> Because the square root is in the denominator, we require
>
> $$
> x+8>0.
> $$
>
> Also,
>
> $$
> x\neq0.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{(-8,0)\cup(0,\infty)}.
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> $$
> y=\frac{\sqrt{6-x}}{x^2-x}.
> $$
>
> The radicand must satisfy
>
> $$
> 6-x\geq0,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> x\leq6.
> $$
>
> Also,
>
> $$
> x^2-x=x(x-1)\neq0,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> x\neq0,1.
> $$
>
> Hence the domain is
>
> $$
> \boxed{(-\infty,0)\cup(0,1)\cup(1,6]}.
> $$
