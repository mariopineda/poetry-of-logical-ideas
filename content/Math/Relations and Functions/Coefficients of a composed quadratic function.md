Let $f(x)=x^2+2x+7$ and $g(x)=3x+2$. Express $(f\circ g)(x)$ in the form $ax^2+bx+c$, then determine $a$, $b$, and $c$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Function composition means **substitute the entire expression** $g(x)=3x+2$ into $f$:
>
> $$\begin{aligned}
> f(g(x))&=(3x+2)^2+2(3x+2)+7\\
> &=9x^2+12x+4+6x+4+7\\
> &=\boxed{9x^2+18x+15}.
> \end{aligned}$$
>
> Comparison with $ax^2+bx+c$ yields $\boxed{a=9,\ b=18,\ c=15}$. The $y$-intercept is independently checked using $f(g(0))=f(2)=4+4+7=15$.
