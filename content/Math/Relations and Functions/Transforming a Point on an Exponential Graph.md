A point $Q(2,25)$ lies on the exponential function $y=c^x$, where $c>1$. First determine $c$. Then find the coordinates of the corresponding point after the graph is transformed to

$$y-4=c^{x-3}.$$

Explain the effect of each translation.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Since $c^2=25$ and $c>1$, $c=5$. The equation can be rewritten as $y=c^{x-3}+4$. This shifts the original graph **3 units right** and **4 units up**.
>
> $$Q(2,25)\longrightarrow Q'(2+3,25+4)=(5,29).$$
