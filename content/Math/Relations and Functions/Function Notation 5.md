Consider the functions $f(x)=-2x-1$, $g(x)=x+2$ and $h(x)=\frac{1}{4}x-3$. Evaluate the following. Leave answers as fractions if necessary. Verify using your graphing calculator or [Desmos](https://www.desmos.com/calculator).

1.

$$
g(1)
$$
2.

$$
f(a)
$$
3.

$$
h(12)
$$
4.

$$
f \left( \frac{1}{4} \right)
$$
5.

$$
g(-2)
$$
6.

$$
h(0)
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Given
>
> $$
> f(x)=-2x-1,
> \qquad
> g(x)=x+2,
> \qquad
> h(x)=\frac14x-3,
> $$
>
> we obtain:
>
> 1.
>
> $$
> g(1)=1+2=\boxed{3}.
> $$
>
> 2.
>
> $$
> f(a)=-2a-1=\boxed{-2a-1}.
> $$
>
> 3.
>
> $$
> h(12)=\frac14(12)-3=3-3=\boxed{0}.
> $$
>
> 4.
>
> $$
> f\left(\frac14\right)
> =-2\left(\frac14\right)-1
> =-\frac12-1
> =\boxed{-\frac32}.
> $$
>
> 5.
>
> $$
> g(-2)=-2+2=\boxed{0}.
> $$
>
> 6.
>
> $$
> h(0)=\frac14(0)-3=\boxed{-3}.
> $$
