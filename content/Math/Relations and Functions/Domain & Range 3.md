## Question

Sketch examples of relations with the following domain and range.

1. Domain: $\{x|x\in\mathbb{R} \}$, Range: $\{y|y\in\mathbb{R}\}$
2. Domain: $\{x|x\geq 0, x \in \mathbb{R}\}$, Range: $\{y|y\geq 0, y \in \mathbb{R}\}$
3. Domain: $\{x|x\geq 0, x \in \mathbb{R}\}$, Range: $\{y|y\in\mathbb{R}\}$
4. Domain: $\{x|x \geq -2, x\in\mathbb{R}\}$, Range: $\{y| y \leq -2, y\in\mathbb{R} \}$
5. Domain: $(-\infty, \infty)$, Range: $(-\infty, \infty) $
6. Domain: $(-\infty, 0)$, Range: $(-\infty, \infty)$
7. Domain: $(-\infty, \infty)$, Range: $[1, \infty)$
8. Domain: $[5,10]$, Range: $[-3,3] $
