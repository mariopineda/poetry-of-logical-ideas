## Question

Draw a relation that satisfies the given domain and range.

1. line where $\{ x | x>3, x\in\mathbb{R} \}$ and $\{ y | y<-1, y\in\mathbb{R} \}$
2. oval where the domain is $[-3,1]$ and the range is $[2,8]$
3. U-shaped where $x\in\mathbb{R}$ and $\{ y | y\leq2, y\in\mathbb{R} \}$
4. wave (sinusoidal shape) where the domain is $(-\infty, \infty)$ and the range is $[-5,10]$
