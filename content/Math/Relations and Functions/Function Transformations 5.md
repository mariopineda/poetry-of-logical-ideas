Find the vertex, axis of symmetry, max/min value and intercepts for:

1.

$$
y=-2x^2-12x+14
$$
2.

$$
y=3x^2-10x
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> Complete the square:
>
> $$
> y=-2x^2-12x+14
> =-2(x+3)^2+32.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> - vertex: $\boxed{(-3,32)}$
> - axis: $\boxed{x=-3}$
> - maximum value: $\boxed{32}$
>
> For the $x$-intercepts,
>
> $$
> -2x^2-12x+14=0
> $$
>
> gives
>
> $$
> x^2+6x-7=0
> =(x+7)(x-1).
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \boxed{(-7,0),(1,0)}.
> $$
>
> The $y$-intercept is
>
> $$
> \boxed{(0,14)}.
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> For
>
> $$
> y=3x^2-10x,
> $$
>
> the axis is
>
> $$
> x=\frac{10}{6}=\frac53.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> y\left(\frac53\right)
> =
> 3\left(\frac{25}{9}\right)
> -
> 10\left(\frac53\right)
> =
> -\frac{25}{3}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> - vertex: $\boxed{\left(\frac53,-\frac{25}{3}\right)}$
> - axis: $\boxed{x=\frac53}$
> - minimum value: $\boxed{-\frac{25}{3}}$
>
> Factor:
>
> $$
> y=x(3x-10),
> $$
>
> so the $x$-intercepts are
>
> $$
> \boxed{(0,0),\left(\frac{10}{3},0\right)}.
> $$
>
> The $y$-intercept is also $\boxed{(0,0)}$.
