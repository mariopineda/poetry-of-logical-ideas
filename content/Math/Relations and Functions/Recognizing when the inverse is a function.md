Four functions with domain $\mathbb{R}$ are graphed below.

![[diploma-batch05-2016-mc5-inverse-graphs.svg]]

Determine **which two graphs** have inverse relations that are functions on their full domains. Justify your choices using the horizontal-line test and explain why the other two fail.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Graphs **A** and **C** are strictly monotonic (A increases throughout; C decreases throughout). Each horizontal line intersects either graph at most once. Therefore $\boxed{A\text{ and }C}$ have inverses that are functions.
>
> Graph **B** has a local maximum and minimum, so some horizontal lines meet it more than once. Graph **D** is V-shaped; a horizontal line above its vertex meets it twice. Both fail the horizontal-line test, so their inverse relations fail the vertical-line test.
