A wildlife-research team is preparing for a regional training meeting.

**(a)** State every non-permissible value for the expression

$$\frac{x^2-9}{x-3}\div\frac{3x}{x+4}.$$

Explain why the divisor itself must not be zero.

**(b)** Solve algebraically, checking the original restrictions:

$$\frac{3x+5}{x}+\frac12=5.$$

**(c)** Two drivers travel the same 210-km route. The return trip is made at a speed 14 km/h faster than the outbound trip and takes 0.5 hours less. If the outbound speed is $v$ km/h, complete a distance–rate–time comparison and write the rational equation representing the difference in travel times. **Do not solve it.**

**(d)** Four identical prizes are awarded to four *different* participants out of 15. Should the number of possible groups be calculated using permutations or combinations? **Justify** and calculate the number.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> **(a)** The original denominator $x-3$ excludes $x=3$. The divisor has denominator $x+4$, excluding $x=-4$, and numerator $3x$, so $x=0$ makes the divisor zero and division undefined. The complete restrictions are
>
> $$\boxed{x\ne-4,\ 0,\ 3}.$$
>
> **(b)** With $x\ne0$, multiply by $2x$:
>
> $$2(3x+5)+x=10x\implies7x+10=10x\implies\boxed{x=\frac{10}{3}}.$$
>
> This is nonzero and satisfies the original equation.
>
> **(c)** Outbound time $=210/v$ hours; return time $=210/(v+14)$ hours. The completed comparison is:
>
> | Trip | Distance (km) | Speed (km/h) | Time (h) |
> |---|---:|---:|---:|
> | Outbound | 210 | $v$ | $210/v$ |
> | Return | 210 | $v+14$ | $210/(v+14)$ |
>
> The outbound trip takes half an hour longer, giving
>
> $$\boxed{\frac{210}{v}-\frac{210}{v+14}=0.5},\qquad v>0.$$
>
> **(d)** The prizes are identical, so the selected group does not depend on order. Use **combinations**:
>
> $$\binom{15}{4}=\frac{15!}{4!\,11!}=\boxed{1365}.$$
