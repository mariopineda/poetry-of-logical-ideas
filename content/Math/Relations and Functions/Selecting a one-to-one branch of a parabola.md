An arch profile is modeled by $f(x)=-3(x+4)^2+7$. Provide two different maximal domain restrictions that make its inverse a function. Justify using the shape of the parabola.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The vertex is at $x=-4$, and the parabola is symmetric about this vertical line. Restricting the domain to either monotonic branch makes each output correspond to only one input. The maximal restrictions are $\boxed{x\le -4}$ and $\boxed{x\ge -4}$.
