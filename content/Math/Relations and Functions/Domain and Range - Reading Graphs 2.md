## Question

Determine the **domain and range** of each relation in both **set-builder notation** and **interval notation**.

![[Domain and Range - Reading Graphs 2 Graph.png]]

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A
>
> The parabola continues indefinitely to the left and right and has a minimum value of $y=0$.
>
> **Domain**
>
> - Set-builder notation: $\{x\mid x\in\mathbb{R}\}$
> - Interval notation: $(-\infty,\infty)$
>
> **Range**
>
> - Set-builder notation: $\{y\in\mathbb{R}\mid y\ge0\}$
> - Interval notation: $[0,\infty)$
>
> ### B
>
> The cubic relation continues indefinitely in both horizontal and vertical directions.
>
> **Domain**
>
> - Set-builder notation: $\{x\mid x\in\mathbb{R}\}$
> - Interval notation: $(-\infty,\infty)$
>
> **Range**
>
> - Set-builder notation: $\{y\mid y\in\mathbb{R}\}$
> - Interval notation: $(-\infty,\infty)$
>
> ### C
>
> The sideways parabola has a minimum horizontal value of $x=4$ and extends indefinitely upward and downward.
>
> **Domain**
>
> - Set-builder notation: $\{x\in\mathbb{R}\mid x\ge4\}$
> - Interval notation: $[4,\infty)$
>
> **Range**
>
> - Set-builder notation: $\{y\mid y\in\mathbb{R}\}$
> - Interval notation: $(-\infty,\infty)$
