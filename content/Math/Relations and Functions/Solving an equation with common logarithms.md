Solve

$$
9=10^{x-4}
$$

for $x$, rounded to the nearest tenth. Show the common-logarithm step and explain the rounding.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Take base-ten logarithms of both sides:
>
> $$
> \log_{10}9=\log_{10}\left(10^{x-4}\right)=x-4.
> $$
>
> Thus
>
> $$
> x=4+\log_{10}9\approx4.9542425.
> $$
>
> The hundredths digit is $5$, so rounding to the nearest tenth gives
>
> $$
> \boxed{x\approx5.0}.
> $$
