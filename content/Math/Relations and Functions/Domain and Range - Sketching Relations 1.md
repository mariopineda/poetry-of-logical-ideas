## Question

Sketch one possible relation for each domain and range.

1. Domain: $\{x\mid x\in\mathbb{R}\}$; Range: $\{y\mid y\in\mathbb{R}\}$
2. Domain: $\{x\in\mathbb{R}\mid x\ge0\}$; Range: $\{y\in\mathbb{R}\mid y\ge0\}$
3. Domain: $\{x\in\mathbb{R}\mid x\ge0\}$; Range: $\{y\mid y\in\mathbb{R}\}$
4. Domain: $\{x\in\mathbb{R}\mid x\ge-2\}$; Range: $\{y\in\mathbb{R}\mid y\le-2\}$

Use arrows to show where a relation continues indefinitely and open or closed endpoints where required.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Answers will vary. The graphs below show one possible relation for each domain and range.
>
> ![[Domain and Range - Sketching Relations 1 Graph.png]]
>
> 1. The line $y=x$ has domain $(-\infty,\infty)$ and range $(-\infty,\infty)$.
> 2. The ray $y=x$, restricted to $x\ge0$, has domain $[0,\infty)$ and range $[0,\infty)$.
> 3. The sideways parabola $x=y^2$ has domain $[0,\infty)$ and range $(-\infty,\infty)$.
> 4. The ray $y=-x-4$, restricted to $x\ge-2$, has domain $[-2,\infty)$ and range $(-\infty,-2]$.
