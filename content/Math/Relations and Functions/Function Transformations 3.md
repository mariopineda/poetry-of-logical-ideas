Find the vertex, axis of symmetry, max/min values and intercepts for:

1.

$$
y=-(x-4)^2+16
$$
2.

$$
y=x^2-10x-24
$$
3.

$$
y=4x^2+8x-60
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> $$
> y=-(x-4)^2+16
> $$
>
> is in vertex form.
>
> - vertex: $\boxed{(4,16)}$
> - axis of symmetry: $\boxed{x=4}$
> - maximum value: $\boxed{16}$
> - $x$-intercepts:
>
> $$
> 0=-(x-4)^2+16
> \Rightarrow
> (x-4)^2=16
> \Rightarrow
> x=0,\ 8
> $$
>
> so $\boxed{(0,0),(8,0)}$
>
> - $y$-intercept: $\boxed{(0,0)}$
>
> ### 2.
>
> $$
> y=x^2-10x-24
> $$
>
> has axis
>
> $$
> x=\frac{-(-10)}{2}=5.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> y(5)=25-50-24=-49.
> $$
>
> - vertex: $\boxed{(5,-49)}$
> - axis: $\boxed{x=5}$
> - minimum value: $\boxed{-49}$
>
> Factor:
>
> $$
> x^2-10x-24=(x-12)(x+2),
> $$
>
> so the $x$-intercepts are
>
> $$
> \boxed{(-2,0),(12,0)}.
> $$
>
> The $y$-intercept is
>
> $$
> \boxed{(0,-24)}.
> $$
>
> ### 3.
>
> $$
> y=4x^2+8x-60
> $$
>
> has axis
>
> $$
> x=-\frac{8}{8}=-1.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> y(-1)=4-8-60=-64.
> $$
>
> - vertex: $\boxed{(-1,-64)}$
> - axis: $\boxed{x=-1}$
> - minimum value: $\boxed{-64}$
>
> Factor:
>
> $$
> 4x^2+8x-60=4(x+5)(x-3),
> $$
>
> so the $x$-intercepts are
>
> $$
> \boxed{(-5,0),(3,0)}.
> $$
>
> The $y$-intercept is
>
> $$
> \boxed{(0,-60)}.
> $$
