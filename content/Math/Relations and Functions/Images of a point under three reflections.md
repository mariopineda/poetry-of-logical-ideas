A marked point $P(-4,7)$ lies on a graph, as shown.

![[diploma-batch06-2016-nr2-original-point.svg]]

Determine the image of $P$ after each of these **separate, non-cumulative** transformations:

1. Reflection in the line $y=x$.
2. Reflection in the $y$-axis.
3. Reflection in the $x$-axis.

Explain each coordinate rule.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The transformations act on the original point separately:
>
> 1. In $y=x$: $(x,y)\mapsto(y,x)$, so $\boxed{(7,-4)}$.
> 2. In the $y$-axis: $(x,y)\mapsto(-x,y)$, so $\boxed{(4,7)}$.
> 3. In the $x$-axis: $(x,y)\mapsto(x,-y)$, so $\boxed{(-4,-7)}$.
>
> The answers are not cumulative; performing all reflections in succession would give different images.
