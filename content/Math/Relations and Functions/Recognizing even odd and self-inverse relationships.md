The four numbered graphs show different functions. Determine **all graph numbers** that have each of the following properties. A graph may satisfy more than one property.

1. $f(-x)=f(x)$ (symmetry about the $y$-axis)
2. $f(-x)=-f(x)$ (symmetry about the origin)
3. $f^{-1}(x)=f(x)$ (symmetry about the line $y=x$)

![[diploma-batch04-2017-nr3-four-symmetries.svg]]

Explain how the relevant symmetries can be seen from the graphs. You may verify your conclusions algebraically if you infer an equation.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> **Evenness** means reflection across the $y$-axis does not change the curve. Graph 3 has that symmetry: its left and right halves are mirror images. None of the other plots is symmetric about the $y$-axis.
>
> **Oddness** means a $180^\circ$ rotation about the origin leaves the graph unchanged. Both Graph 1 (the centred cubic) and Graph 4 (the two negative-reciprocal branches) have this rotational symmetry.
>
> **Self-inverse** means reflection across $y=x$ maps the graph onto itself. Graph 4 has that property: its branches coincide with the graph of $y=-1/x$, and algebraically
>
> $$r(r(x))=-\frac1{-1/x}=x\quad(x\ne0).$$
>
> Therefore the full matches are
>
> $$\boxed{\text{even: 3;\quad odd: 1 and 4;\quad self-inverse: 4}}.$$
>
> Graph 2 is deliberately shifted, so it does not satisfy any of the listed symmetries.
