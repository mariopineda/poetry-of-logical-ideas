A suspension cable is modelled by $f(x)=(x+3)^2-2$. A redesigned cable follows $g(x)=f(x-5)+4$.

Find the vertex of $g$, identifying the horizontal and vertical shifts. Explain how the signs in the transformation affect the direction.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The original vertex is $(-3,-2)$. Replacing $x$ by $x-5$ shifts the graph **right $5$**; adding $4$ shifts it **up $4$**. Consequently
>
> $$
> (-3,-2)\longrightarrow(-3+5,-2+4)
> =\boxed{(2,2)}.
> $$
>
> Equivalently, $g(x)=((x-5)+3)^2-2+4=(x-2)^2+2$, which confirms the vertex.
