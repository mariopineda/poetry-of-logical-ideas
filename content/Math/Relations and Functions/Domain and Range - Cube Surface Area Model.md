## Question

The surface area of a cube is modelled by

$$
A=6s^2,
$$

where $s$ is the edge length in units and $A$ is the surface area in square units. Consider cubes with an edge length of at least $4$ units.

1. Identify the independent and dependent variables.
2. State the contextual domain and range in both set-builder notation and interval notation.
3. Explain what the ordered pair $(9,486)$ means in this context.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ![[Domain and Range - Cube Surface Area Model Graph.png]]
>
> ### 1. Variables
>
> The edge length, $s$, is the **independent variable**. The surface area, $A$, is the **dependent variable** because its value depends on $s$.
>
> ### 2. Domain and range
>
> The smallest permitted edge length is $4$ units:
>
> $$
> s\ge4
> $$
>
> When $s=4$,
>
> $$
> A=6(4)^2=96.
> $$
>
> The surface area increases as the edge length increases.
>
> **Domain**
>
> - Set-builder notation: $\{s\in\mathbb{R}\mid s\ge4\}$
> - Interval notation: $[4,\infty)$
>
> **Range**
>
> - Set-builder notation: $\{A\in\mathbb{R}\mid A\ge96\}$
> - Interval notation: $[96,\infty)$
>
> ### 3. Meaning of $(9,486)$
>
> The ordered pair means that a cube with an edge length of **$9$ units** has a surface area of **$486$ square units**:
>
> $$
> A=6(9)^2=486.
> $$
