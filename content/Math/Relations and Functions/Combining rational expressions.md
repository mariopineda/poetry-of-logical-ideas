Simplify the rational expression and state **all non-permissible values** of the original expression:

$$
\frac{x+5}{x-2}-\frac{x-1}{x+5}.
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The original denominators require
>
> $$
> x-2\ne0,\quad x+5\ne0
> \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \boxed{x\ne2,-5}.
> $$
>
> Use the common denominator $(x-2)(x+5)$:
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> \frac{x+5}{x-2}-\frac{x-1}{x+5}
> &=\frac{(x+5)^2-(x-1)(x-2)}{(x-2)(x+5)}\\
> &=\frac{(x^2+10x+25)-(x^2-3x+2)}{(x-2)(x+5)}\\
> &=\boxed{\frac{13x+23}{(x-2)(x+5)}}.
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> The expression is defined only for $x\ne2$ and $x\ne-5$.
