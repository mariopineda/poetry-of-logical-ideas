Let $b>1$. Express

$$
\log_b288+\log_b25-\log_b45
$$

as a single logarithm $\log_b M$, and determine $M$. Show the logarithm laws used.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Apply the product law to the sum and the quotient law to the difference:
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> \log_b288+\log_b25-\log_b45
> &=\log_b(288\cdot25)-\log_b45\\
> &=\log_b\left(\frac{288\cdot25}{45}\right)\\
> &=\boxed{\log_b160}.
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> Thus $\boxed{M=160}$. The base is valid because $b>1$, and all log arguments are positive.
