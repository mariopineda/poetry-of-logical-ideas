## Question

Sketch one possible relation of the specified shape for each domain and range.

1. A ray with domain $(3,\infty)$ and range $(-\infty,-1)$
2. An oval with domain $[-3,1]$ and range $[2,8]$
3. An inverted U-shaped relation with domain $(-\infty,\infty)$ and range $(-\infty,2]$
4. A sinusoidal relation with domain $(-\infty,\infty)$ and range $[-5,10]$

Use arrows to show where a relation continues indefinitely and open or closed endpoints where required.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Answers will vary. The graphs below show one possible relation of each required shape.
>
> ![[Domain and Range - Sketching Relations 2 Graph.png]]
>
> 1. A ray beginning with an open endpoint at $(3,-1)$ and extending down and to the right has the required domain and range.
> 2. An oval centred at $(-1,5)$ with horizontal radius $2$ and vertical radius $3$ has the required domain and range.
> 3. The parabola $y=-x^2+2$ has domain $(-\infty,\infty)$ and range $(-\infty,2]$.
> 4. The function $y=7.5\sin x+2.5$ has domain $(-\infty,\infty)$ and range $[-5,10]$.
