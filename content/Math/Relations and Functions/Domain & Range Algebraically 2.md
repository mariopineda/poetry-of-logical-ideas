## Question

State the domain in interval notation.

1. $y=|4x-13|$
2. $y=-\frac{3}{2x}+5$
3. $y=\frac{4}{8x-x^2}$
4. $y=\sqrt{22-4x}$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1. $y=|4x-13|$
>
> The absolute-value expression is defined for every real value of $x$.
>
> $$
> \boxed{(-\infty,\infty)}
> $$
>
> ### 2. $y=-\dfrac{3}{2x}+5$
>
> The denominator cannot equal zero:
>
> $$
> 2x\ne0 \quad\Rightarrow\quad x\ne0.
> $$
>
> $$
> \boxed{(-\infty,0)\cup(0,\infty)}
> $$
>
> ### 3. $y=\dfrac{4}{8x-x^2}$
>
> The denominator cannot equal zero:
>
> $$
> 8x-x^2=x(8-x)\ne0.
> $$
>
> Therefore, $x\ne0$ and $x\ne8$.
>
> $$
> \boxed{(-\infty,0)\cup(0,8)\cup(8,\infty)}
> $$
>
> ### 4. $y=\sqrt{22-4x}$
>
> The expression under the square root must be greater than or equal to zero:
>
> $$
> 22-4x\ge0
> $$
>
> $$
> x\le\frac{11}{2}.
> $$
>
> $$
> \boxed{\left(-\infty,\frac{11}{2}\right]}
> $$
