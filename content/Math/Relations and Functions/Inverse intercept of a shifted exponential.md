A measurement model is given by

$$f(x)=6\left(\frac14\right)^x-18.$$

Determine the $y$-intercept of $f^{-1}$ to the nearest hundredth, and explain how the inverse interchanges coordinates.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> If $f^{-1}(0)=a$, then $f(a)=0$. Solve
>
> $$6(1/4)^a-18=0\Rightarrow(1/4)^a=3\Rightarrow a=\frac{\ln3}{\ln(1/4)}\approx-0.79.$$
>
> The inverse has $\boxed{y\text{-intercept }-0.79}$. Its point $(0,-0.79)$ corresponds to $(-0.79,0)$ on the original function.
