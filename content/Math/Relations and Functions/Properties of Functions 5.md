If $f(x) = x+1$ and $g(x)=2x^2-3$, then evaluate:

1.

$$
f(3)
$$
2.

$$
g(-1)
$$
3.

$$
f(4)+g(3)
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Given
>
> $$
> f(x)=x+1,
> \qquad
> g(x)=2x^2-3,
> $$
>
> 1.
>
> $$
> f(3)=3+1=\boxed{4}.
> $$
>
> 2.
>
> $$
> g(-1)=2(-1)^2-3=2-3=\boxed{-1}.
> $$
>
> 3.
>
> $$
> f(4)+g(3)
> =
> 5+(18-3)
> =
> \boxed{20}.
> $$
