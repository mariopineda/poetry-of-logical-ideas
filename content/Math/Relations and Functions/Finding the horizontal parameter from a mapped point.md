A sensor-response curve $y=f(x)$ is transformed to $g(x)=3f(b(x+2))$, where $b>0$. The point $(6,-2)$ on $f$ corresponds to $(-1,-6)$ on $g$. Determine $b$ and explain why the $y$-coordinate is consistent with the transformation.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> A point $(u,v)$ on $f$ corresponds to $\bigl(u/b-2,\,3v\bigr)$ on $g$ because $b(x+2)=u$. For the supplied original point, $u=6$, so
>
> $$-1=\frac6b-2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\frac6b=1\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\boxed{b=6}.$$
>
> The matching ordinate is $3(-2)=-6$, verifying the correspondence. The restriction $b>0$ is satisfied.
