## Question

The volume of a cube is modelled by

$$
V=s^3,
$$

where $s$ is the edge length in units and $V$ is the volume in cubic units. Consider cubes with edge lengths greater than $0$ units but less than $10$ units.

State the contextual domain and range in both **set-builder notation** and **interval notation**.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ![[Domain and Range - Cube Volume Model Graph.png]]
>
> The edge length must satisfy
>
> $$
> 0<s<10.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the contextual domain is:
>
> - Set-builder notation: $\{s\in\mathbb{R}\mid 0<s<10\}$
> - Interval notation: $(0,10)$
>
> The function $V=s^3$ is increasing for positive values of $s$. As $s$ approaches $0$, $V$ approaches $0$. As $s$ approaches $10$, $V$ approaches
>
> $$
> 10^3=1000.
> $$
>
> Neither boundary is included, so the contextual range is:
>
> - Set-builder notation: $\{V\in\mathbb{R}\mid 0<V<1000\}$
> - Interval notation: $(0,1000)$
