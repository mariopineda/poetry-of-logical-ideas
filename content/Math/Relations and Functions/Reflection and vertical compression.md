A profile is modelled by $y=f(x)$. A transformation produces a new function satisfying

$$
5g(x)=f(-x).
$$

Describe the two graph transformations, identify their reflection axis, and give the **vertical scale factor** from $f$ to $g$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Solving for $g$ gives
>
> $$
> g(x)=\frac15 f(-x).
> $$
>
> The input change $x\mapsto-x$ reflects the graph in the **$y$-axis**. The outside multiplier $\frac15$ scales every $y$-coordinate by $\frac15$ relative to the $x$-axis, a **vertical compression**. The scale factor is $\boxed{\frac15}$.
