The functions $f$ and $g$ are defined by the **closed line segments** shown below.

![[diploma-batch05-2016-mc15-line-segments.svg]]

For $h(x)=f(x)-g(x)$, determine the domain and range of $h$. Explain how the slopes and endpoint values can be found from the two graphs.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Both segments have domain $[-2,3]$, so $h$ has the same domain. Reading the endpoints, $f$ rises from $(-2,-3)$ to $(3,7)$, giving slope $2$ and rule $f(x)=2x+1$ on this interval.
>
> The line $g$ falls from $(-2,5)$ to $(3,-5)$, giving slope $-2$ and rule $g(x)=-2x+1$. Therefore
>
> $$
> h(x)=(2x+1)-(-2x+1)=4x.
> $$
>
> Since this is increasing, its minimum is $h(-2)=-8$ and maximum is $h(3)=12$. Both endpoint values are included:
>
> $$
> \boxed{D_h=[-2,3]},\qquad\boxed{R_h=[-8,12]}.
> $$
