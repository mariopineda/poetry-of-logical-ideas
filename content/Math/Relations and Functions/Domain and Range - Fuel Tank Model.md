## Question

The volume of fuel, $v$, in litres, remaining in a car's gas tank after the car has travelled $d$ kilometres is modelled by

$$
v=60.5-0.072d.
$$

1. Graph the relation using a graphing calculator. Label both axes, including units.
2. Determine how much fuel remains after the car has travelled $165$ km. Round to the nearest hundredth of a litre.
3. If $15$ L of fuel remains, determine how far the car has travelled. Round to the nearest tenth of a kilometre.
4. State the contextual domain and range in both set-builder notation and interval notation. Round boundary values to the nearest tenth where necessary.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1. Graph
>
> Enter $Y_1=60.5-0.072X$ into the graphing calculator. Only the portion of the graph in the first quadrant represents the situation.
>
> ![[Domain and Range - Fuel Tank Model Graph.png]]
>
> ### 2. Fuel remaining after $165$ km
>
> $$
> v=60.5-0.072(165)=48.62
> $$
>
> Approximately **$48.62$ L** of fuel remains.
>
> ### 3. Distance travelled when $15$ L remains
>
> $$
> 15=60.5-0.072d
> $$
>
> $$
> 0.072d=45.5
> $$
>
> $$
> d=631.944\ldots
> $$
>
> The car has travelled approximately **$631.9$ km**.
>
> ### 4. Contextual domain and range
>
> The tank is empty when $v=0$:
>
> $$
> 0=60.5-0.072d
> $$
>
> $$
> d=840.277\ldots\approx840.3
> $$
>
> **Domain**
>
> - Set-builder notation: $\{d\in\mathbb{R}\mid 0\le d\le840.3\}$
> - Interval notation: $[0,840.3]$
>
> **Range**
>
> - Set-builder notation: $\{v\in\mathbb{R}\mid 0\le v\le60.5\}$
> - Interval notation: $[0,60.5]$
