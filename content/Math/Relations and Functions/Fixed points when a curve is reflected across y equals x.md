The original graph of $y=f(x)$ is shown below. It will be reflected across the line $y=x$.

![[diploma-batch02-invariant-unmarked.svg]]

**How many points remain unchanged by the reflection?** Draw or imagine the line $y=x$, explain your reasoning, and estimate the coordinates of all invariant points to the nearest integer.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> An invariant point under reflection across $y=x$ must satisfy $f(x)=x$; graphically it is an intersection of $y=f(x)$ with $y=x$. Drawing that diagonal on the given grid gives **three** intersections, approximately at $(-3,-3)$, $(1,1)$, and $(4,4)$.
>
> The plotted curve was generated independently from $f(x)=x+0.08(x+3)(x-1)(x-4)$. This provides an exact check: $f(x)-x=0$ at $x=-3,1,4$, with no other real solutions.
>
> Therefore there are $\boxed{3}$ invariant points. The coordinates are $\boxed{(-3,-3),\ (1,1),\ (4,4)}$.
