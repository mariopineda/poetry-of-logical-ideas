An airplane at an altitude of 10000 m begins to descend at 300 m/min.

1. State the independent and dependent variables.
2. Using your graphing calculator or [Desmos](https://www.desmos.com/calculator) draw a graph showing the altitude of the airplane over time for the first 10 minutes of descent.
3. Determine the equation of this relation in terms of $h$, height in meters, and $t$, time in minutes.
4. Explain the meaning of the x and y-intercepts.
5. State the domain and range, in set notation, of the airplane from when it starts its descent until it reaches the ground. Round to the nearest whole number.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> The independent variable is **time** $t$ in minutes.
>
> The dependent variable is **altitude** $h$ in metres.
>
> ### 2–3.
>
> The airplane begins at $10000$ m and loses $300$ m of altitude each minute, so
>
> $$
> \boxed{h=10000-300t}.
> $$
>
> For the first $10$ minutes the graph is the line segment from
>
> $$
> (0,10000)
> $$
>
> to
>
> $$
> (10,7000).
> $$
>
> ### 4.
>
> The $y$-intercept is
>
> $$
> \boxed{(0,10000)},
> $$
>
> meaning the airplane begins its descent at $10000$ m.
>
> The $x$-intercept occurs when $h=0$:
>
> $$
> 10000-300t=0
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> t=\frac{100}{3}\approx33.33.
> $$
>
> This means the model predicts the airplane reaches ground level after about $33.33$ minutes.
>
> ### 5.
>
> In context,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\{t\mid 0\leq t\leq \tfrac{100}{3}\}}
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> \boxed{\{h\mid 0\leq h\leq10000\}}.
> $$
>
> If the time endpoint is rounded to the nearest whole minute, it is approximately $33$ min.
