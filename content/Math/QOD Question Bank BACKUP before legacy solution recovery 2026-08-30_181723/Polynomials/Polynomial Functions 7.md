1. Using an appropriate regression function, determine the equation describing the height of the ball as a function of time. Round parameters to the nearest hundreth. Justify your choice of regression function.
2. Determine the maximum height of the ball above the ground. Is this an extrapolation or interpolation? Explain.
3. Determine, to the nearest tenth of a second, the time it takes for the ball to hit the ground. Is this an extrapolation or interpolation? Explain.
