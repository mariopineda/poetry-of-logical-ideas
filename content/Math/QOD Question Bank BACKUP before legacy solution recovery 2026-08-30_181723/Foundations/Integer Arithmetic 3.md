## Question

Evaluate without a calculator:

1. $$8 \div (7-9) \times (4+(-4))$$
2. $$(8 \times (-4)-(-9) + (-7)) \div 3$$
3. $$10+6 \div ((-7)-(-5)) \times ((-10)+5)$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> \[
> \begin{array}{cl}
> & 8 \div (7-9) \times (4+(-4)) \\
> = & 8 \div (-2) \times 0 \\
> = & \frac{8}{-2} \times 0 = 0
> \end{array}
> \]
>
> ### B.
>
> \[
> \begin{array}{cl}
> & (8 \times (-4)-(-9) + (-7)) \div 3 \\
> = & \frac{8 \times (-4)+9 -7}{3} \\
> = & \frac{-32+-7}{3} \\
> = & \frac{-30}{3} = -10
> \end{array}
> \]
>
> ### C.
>
> \[
> \begin{array}{cl}
> & 10+6 \div ((-7)-(-5)) \times ((-10)+5) \\
> = & 10+6 \div (-7+5) \times (-5) \\
> = & 10+6 \div (-2) \times (-5) \\
> = & 10+ \frac{6}{-2} \times (-5) \\
> = & 10 + \frac{-30}{-2} \\
> = & 10 + 15 = 25
> \end{array}
> \]
>
