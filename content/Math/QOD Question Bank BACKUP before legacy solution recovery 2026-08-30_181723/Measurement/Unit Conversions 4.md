## Question

Convert the following unit as indicated, rounded to the nearest hundredth when applicable. For metric units, provide answer in decimal and in scientific notation when above or below $\times 1000$ the base unit (i.e. if you answer has three or more leading or trailing zeros).

1. $1140 \mbox{ m} \Rightarrow \mbox{km}$
2. $0.0611 \mbox{ m} \Rightarrow \mbox{dm}$
3. $0.000075 \mbox{ L} \Rightarrow \mbox{mL}$
4. $640000 \mbox{ m} \Rightarrow \mbox{hm}$
5. $90 \mbox{ in} \Rightarrow \mbox{ft and in}$
6. $0.005 \mbox{ mi} \Rightarrow \mbox{yd and feet and in}$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> All conversions are done using proportional reasoning.
>
> ### A.
>
> $$
> \begin{array}{rcl}
> \frac{\displaystyle x \mbox{ km}}{\displaystyle 1140 \mbox{ m}} & = & \frac{\displaystyle 1 \mbox{ km}}{\displaystyle 1000 \mbox{ m}} \\
> x & = & \frac{\displaystyle 1}{\displaystyle 1000} \times 1140 \\
> x & = & 1.140 \mbox{ km} \approx 1.14 \mbox{ km}
> \end{array}
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> $$
> \begin{array}{rcl}
> \frac{\displaystyle x \mbox{ dm}}{\displaystyle 0.0611 \mbox{ m}} & = & \frac{\displaystyle 10 \mbox{ dm}}{\displaystyle 1 \mbox{ m}} \\
> x & = & \frac{\displaystyle 10}{\displaystyle 1} \times 0.0611 \\
> x & = & 0.611 \mbox{ dm} \approx 0.61 \mbox{ dm}
> \end{array}
> $$
>
> ### C.
>
> $$
> \begin{array}{rcl}
> \frac{\displaystyle x \mbox{ mL}}{\displaystyle 0.000075 \mbox{ L}} & = & \frac{\displaystyle 1000 \mbox{ mL}}{\displaystyle 1 \mbox{ L}} \\
> x & = & \frac{\displaystyle 1000}{\displaystyle 1} \times 0.000075 \\
> x & = & 0.075 \mbox{ mL} \approx 0.07 \mbox{ mL}
> \end{array}
> $$
>
> ### D.
>
> $$
> \begin{array}{rcl}
> \frac{\displaystyle x \mbox{ hm}}{\displaystyle 640000 \mbox{ m}} & = & \frac{\displaystyle 1 \mbox{ hm}}{\displaystyle 100 \mbox{ m}} \\
> x & = & \frac{\displaystyle 1}{\displaystyle 100} \times 640000 \\
> x & = & 6400 \mbox{ hm} = 6.4 \times 10^3 \mbox{ hm}
> \end{array}
> $$
>
> ### E.
>
> $$
> \begin{array}{rcl}
> \frac{\displaystyle x \mbox{ ft}}{\displaystyle 90 \mbox{ in}} & = & \frac{\displaystyle 1 \mbox{ ft}}{\displaystyle 12 \mbox{ in}} \\
> x & = & \frac{\displaystyle 1}{\displaystyle 12} \times 90 \\
> x & = & 7.5 \mbox{ ft} \mbox{ (Convert decimal part to inches)} \\
> \frac{\displaystyle y \mbox{ in}}{\displaystyle 0.5 \mbox{ ft}} & = & \frac{\displaystyle 12 \mbox{ in}}{\displaystyle 1 \mbox{ ft}} \\
> y & = & \frac{\displaystyle 12}{\displaystyle 1} \times 0.5 \\
> y & = & 6 \mbox{ in}\\
> \end{array}
> $$
> Solution: 7 ft and 6 in.
>
> ### F.
>
> $$
> \begin{array}{rcl}
> \frac{\displaystyle x \mbox{ yd}}{\displaystyle 0.005 \mbox{ mi}} & = & \frac{\displaystyle 1760 \mbox{ yd}}{\displaystyle 1 \mbox{ mi}} \\
> x & = & \frac{\displaystyle 1760}{\displaystyle 1} \times 0.005 \\
> x & = & 8.8 \mbox{ yd} \mbox{ (Convert decimal part to feet)} \\
> \frac{\displaystyle y \mbox{ ft}}{\displaystyle 0.8 \mbox{ yd}} & = & \frac{\displaystyle 3 \mbox{ ft}}{\displaystyle 1 \mbox{ yd}} \\
> y & = & \frac{\displaystyle 3}{\displaystyle 1} \times 0.8 \\
> y & = & 2.4 \mbox{ ft} \mbox{ (Convert decimal part to inches)} \\
> \frac{\displaystyle z \mbox{ in}}{\displaystyle 0.4 \mbox{ ft}} & = & \frac{\displaystyle 12 \mbox{ in}}{\displaystyle 1 \mbox{ ft}} \\
> z & = & \frac{\displaystyle 12}{\displaystyle 1} \times 0.4 \\
> z & = & 4.8 \mbox{ in} \mbox{ (Convert decimal part to a fraction)}\\

> \end{array}
> $$
