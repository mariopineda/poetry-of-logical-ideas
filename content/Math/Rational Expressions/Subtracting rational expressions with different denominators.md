Simplify $\dfrac{x+4}{x-2}-\dfrac{x-1}{x+3}$ as one rational expression. State all non-permissible values.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Use the common denominator $(x-2)(x+3)$. The numerator is $(x+4)(x+3)-(x-1)(x-2)=x^2+7x+12-(x^2-3x+2)=10x+10$. Thus the result is $\boxed{\frac{10x+10}{(x-2)(x+3)}}$, with $\boxed{x\ne 2,-3}$.
