Simplify $\dfrac{12x^2+8x}{4x}$, and state the non-permissible value of the original expression.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Factor $4x$ from the numerator: $\frac{4x(3x+2)}{4x}=\boxed{3x+2}$, provided $x\ne0$. The original denominator is zero at $\boxed{x=0}$, which remains non-permissible even after cancellation.
