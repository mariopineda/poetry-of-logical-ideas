## Question

Without using a calculator, determine the exact value of the following.

1.

$$
5^{-2}
$$
2.

$$
8^{2 / 3}
$$
3.

$$
\left( \frac{\displaystyle 4}{\displaystyle 9} \right)^{\frac{1}{2}}
$$
4.

$$
125^{\frac{1}{3}} - 10^0
$$
5.

$$
\left( \frac{\displaystyle 1}{\displaystyle 4} \right)^{-2}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> 1.
>
> $$
> 5^{-2}=\frac{1}{5^2}=\boxed{\frac{1}{25}}
> $$
>
> 2.
>
> $$
> 8^{2/3}=\left(\sqrt[3]{8}\right)^2=2^2=\boxed{4}
> $$
>
> 3.
>
> $$
> \left(\frac49\right)^{1/2}
> =
> \sqrt{\frac49}
> =
> \boxed{\frac23}
> $$
>
> 4.
>
> $$
> 125^{1/3}-10^0=5-1=\boxed{4}
> $$
>
> 5.
>
> $$
> \left(\frac14\right)^{-2}
> =
> 4^2
> =
> \boxed{16}
> $$
