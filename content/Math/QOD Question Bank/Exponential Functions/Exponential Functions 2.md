## Question

Solve for $x$

1.

$$
2^x = 16 \sqrt{2}
$$
2.

$$
8^{3x} = 4^{1-x}
$$
3.

$$
9^{3x+1} = 27^{3x}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> $$
> 2^x
> =
> 16\sqrt2
> =
> 2^4\cdot2^{1/2}
> =
> 2^{9/2}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=\frac92}.
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> $$
> 8^{3x}=4^{1-x}.
> $$
>
> Write both sides with base $2$:
>
> $$
> 2^{9x}=2^{2-2x}.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> 9x=2-2x,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=\frac{2}{11}}.
> $$
>
> ### 3.
>
> $$
> 9^{3x+1}=27^{3x}.
> $$
>
> Write both sides with base $3$:
>
> $$
> 3^{6x+2}=3^{9x}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> 6x+2=9x,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=\frac23}.
> $$
