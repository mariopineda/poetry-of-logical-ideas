## Question

Solve for $x$

1.

$$
x^{\frac{1}{2}} = 5
$$
2.

$$
x^{-\frac{1}{2}} = 5
$$
3.

$$
x^{\frac{1}{3}} = -5
$$
4.

$$
4x^{-\frac{2}{3}} = 16
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> $$
> x^{1/2}=5
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=25}.
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> $$
> x^{-1/2}=5
> $$
>
> means
>
> $$
> \frac{1}{\sqrt{x}}=5.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \sqrt{x}=\frac15
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=\frac{1}{25}}.
> $$
>
> ### 3.
>
> $$
> x^{1/3}=-5
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=-125}.
> $$
>
> ### 4.
>
> $$
> 4x^{-2/3}=16.
> $$
>
> Divide by $4$:
>
> $$
> x^{-2/3}=4.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> x^{2/3}=\frac14.
> $$
>
> Let
>
> $$
> u=x^{1/3}.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> u^2=\frac14,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> u=\pm\frac12.
> $$
>
> Hence,
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=\pm\frac18}.
> $$
