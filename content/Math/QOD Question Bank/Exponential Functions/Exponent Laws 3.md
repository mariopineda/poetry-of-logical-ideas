## Question

Use exponent laws to simplify the following:

1.

$$
4x^{-3}
$$
2.

$$
\frac{15y^{-3}}{5y}
$$
3.

$$
(3x^3y)(5x^{-2}y^4)
$$
4.

$$
\frac{24p^{-8}}{16p^{-3}}
$$
5.

$$
\frac{b^{4x+y}}{b^{x-2y}}
$$
6.

$$
\frac{x^{5a+7b} \times x^{3a+b}}{x^a \times x^{2a-7b}}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> 1.
>
> $$
> 4x^{-3}=\boxed{\frac{4}{x^3}}
> $$
>
> 2.
>
> $$
> \frac{15y^{-3}}{5y}
> =
> 3y^{-4}
> =
> \boxed{\frac{3}{y^4}}
> $$
>
> 3.
>
> $$
> (3x^3y)(5x^{-2}y^4)
> =
> 15x^{3-2}y^5
> =
> \boxed{15xy^5}
> $$
>
> 4.
>
> $$
> \frac{24p^{-8}}{16p^{-3}}
> =
> \frac32p^{-5}
> =
> \boxed{\frac{3}{2p^5}}
> $$
>
> 5.
>
> $$
> \frac{b^{4x+y}}{b^{x-2y}}
> =
> b^{4x+y-(x-2y)}
> =
> \boxed{b^{3x+3y}}
> $$
>
> 6.
>
> $$
> \frac{x^{5a+7b}x^{3a+b}}{x^ax^{2a-7b}}
> =
> x^{8a+8b-(3a-7b)}
> =
> \boxed{x^{5a+15b}}
> $$
