## Question

Simplify. Express in exponential form.

1.

$$
\left( \frac{48a^{-\frac{2}{3}} b^{\frac{5}{4}} c^{-\frac{1}{2}} }{50ab^{-1}c^{\frac{1}{2}}} \right) \left( \frac{35 a^\frac{1}{3} b^{-\frac{3}{4}} c^{-\frac{3}{2}} }{ 42 a^{-\frac{4}{3}} b^{\frac{1}{4}} c}\right)
$$
2.

$$
\sqrt{ \frac{32 x^{-5} y^{\frac{3}{2}} \times 18 x^2 y^{-\frac{1}{4}} }{4 x y^{-\frac{3}{4}}} }
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Assume the variables are positive so that the fractional powers are real.
>
> ### 1.
>
> Combine the numerical coefficients:
>
> $$
> \frac{48}{50}\cdot\frac{35}{42}
> =
> \frac45.
> $$
>
> Combine like bases:
>
> $$
> a^{-2/3-1+1/3+4/3}=a^0,
> $$
>
> $$
> b^{5/4+1-3/4-1/4}=b^{5/4},
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> c^{-1/2-1/2-3/2-1}=c^{-7/2}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac45b^{5/4}c^{-7/2}}
> $$
>
> or, using positive exponents,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{4b^{5/4}}{5c^{7/2}}}.
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> First simplify inside the radical:
>
> $$
> \frac{32x^{-5}y^{3/2}\cdot18x^2y^{-1/4}}
> {4xy^{-3/4}}
> =
> 144x^{-4}y^2.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \sqrt{144x^{-4}y^2}
> =
> 12x^{-2}y
> =
> \boxed{\frac{12y}{x^2}}.
> $$
