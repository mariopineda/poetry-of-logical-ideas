## Question

Simplify:

1.

$$
z^2 \times z^7
$$
2.

$$
\left( a^3 b^4\right) \left( a^8 b^3\right)
$$
3.

$$
\left(-2xy\right) \left(-5x^7y^3\right)
$$
4.

$$
\frac{a^6}{a^3}
$$
5.

$$
\frac{-12x^6y^5}{4xy^3}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> 1.
>
> $$
> z^2z^7=\boxed{z^9}
> $$
>
> 2.
>
> $$
> (a^3b^4)(a^8b^3)=\boxed{a^{11}b^7}
> $$
>
> 3.
>
> $$
> (-2xy)(-5x^7y^3)=\boxed{10x^8y^4}
> $$
>
> 4.
>
> $$
> \frac{a^6}{a^3}=\boxed{a^3}
> $$
>
> 5.
>
> $$
> \frac{-12x^6y^5}{4xy^3}
> =
> \boxed{-3x^5y^2}
> $$
