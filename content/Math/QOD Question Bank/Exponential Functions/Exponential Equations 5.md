## Question

How many times more intense is the sound of a referee's whistle (125 dB) than a train whistle at 200 m (90 dB)? Answer to the nearest whole number.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The decibel scale is
>
> $$
> \beta
> =
> 10\log_{10}\left(\frac{I}{I_0}\right).
> $$
>
> The difference in sound level is
>
> $$
> 125-90=35\text{ dB}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> 35
> =
> 10\log_{10}\left(\frac{I_{\text{ref}}}{I_{\text{train}}}\right).
> $$
>
> Divide by $10$:
>
> $$
> 3.5
> =
> \log_{10}\left(\frac{I_{\text{ref}}}{I_{\text{train}}}\right).
> $$
>
> Hence,
>
> $$
> \frac{I_{\text{ref}}}{I_{\text{train}}}
> =
> 10^{3.5}
> \approx3162.28.
> $$
>
> To the nearest whole number, the referee's whistle is
>
> $$
> \boxed{3162\text{ times as intense}}.
> $$
