## Question

Joe invests \$5000 into a high-interest savings bond with an annual interest rate of $9\%$, compounded monthly.

### A.

Write the exponential equation

$$
A=P(1+i)^n
$$

that represents this situation. What does the parameter $n$ represent?

### B.

How much will the bond be worth after $1$ year?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> The principal is
>
> $$
> P=5000.
> $$
>
> The monthly interest rate is
>
> $$
> i=\frac{0.09}{12}=0.0075.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{A=5000(1.0075)^n}.
> $$
>
> Here, $n$ is the number of monthly compounding periods.
>
> If $t$ is measured in years, then $n=12t$, so an equivalent model is
>
> $$
> \boxed{A=5000(1.0075)^{12t}}.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> After $1$ year,
>
> $$
> n=12.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> A=5000(1.0075)^{12}
> \approx 5469.0345.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the bond will be worth approximately
>
> $$
> \boxed{\$5469.03}.
> $$
