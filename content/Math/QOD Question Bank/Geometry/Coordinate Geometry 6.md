## Question

Determine the equation of the line. Answer in general form.

1. through $(1,-6)$, parallel to $x+2y=6$
2. through $(-1,-2)$, perpendicular to $2x+5y+8=0$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> A line parallel to
>
> $$
> x+2y=6
> $$
>
> has the form
>
> $$
> x+2y=C.
> $$
>
> Substitute $(1,-6)$:
>
> $$
> 1+2(-6)=-11.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{x+2y+11=0}.
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> For
>
> $$
> 2x+5y+8=0,
> $$
>
> the slope is
>
> $$
> -\frac25.
> $$
>
> A perpendicular line has slope
>
> $$
> \frac52.
> $$
>
> Using point-slope form through $(-1,-2)$,
>
> $$
> y+2=\frac52(x+1).
> $$
>
> Multiply by $2$ and rearrange:
>
> $$
> 2y+4=5x+5
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> \boxed{5x-2y+1=0}.
> $$
