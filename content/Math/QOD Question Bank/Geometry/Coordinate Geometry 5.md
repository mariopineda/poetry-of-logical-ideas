## Question

Determine the shortest distance between $P(7,6)$ and the line $y=-2x+5$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Rewrite the line in standard form:
>
> $$
> y=-2x+5
> $$
>
> becomes
>
> $$
> 2x+y-5=0.
> $$
>
> The distance from a point $(x_0,y_0)$ to the line
>
> $$
> Ax+By+C=0
> $$
>
> is
>
> $$
> d=
> \frac{|Ax_0+By_0+C|}
> {\sqrt{A^2+B^2}}.
> $$
>
> Using $P(7,6)$,
>
> $$
> d
> =
> \frac{|2(7)+6-5|}
> {\sqrt{2^2+1^2}}
> =
> \frac{15}{\sqrt5}.
> $$
>
> Rationalize:
>
> $$
> d=3\sqrt5.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{3\sqrt5\approx6.71}
> $$
>
> units.
