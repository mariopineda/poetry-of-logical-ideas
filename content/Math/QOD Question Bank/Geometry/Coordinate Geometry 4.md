## Question

Determine the equation of the line that is parallel to $5x+7y-35=0$ and goes through the point $P(2,9)$. Answer in general form.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> A parallel line has the same $x$- and $y$-coefficients, so write
>
> $$
> 5x+7y+C=0.
> $$
>
> Substitute $(2,9)$:
>
> $$
> 5(2)+7(9)+C=0.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> 10+63+C=0
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> C=-73.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{5x+7y-73=0}.
> $$
