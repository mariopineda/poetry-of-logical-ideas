## Question

### A.

Manually calculate:

$$
\frac{10!}{7!}
$$

### B.

Seven different books are arranged on a shelf. One particular book must be placed first.

How many different arrangements are possible?

### C.

Eight finalists compete for gold, silver, and bronze medals.

How many different arrangements are possible for the three medals?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> $$
> \frac{10!}{7!}
> =
> \frac{10(9)(8)(7!)}{7!}
> =
> 10(9)(8)
> =
> 720
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> One position is fixed, leaving \(6\) books to arrange.
>
> $$
> 6!=720
> $$
>
> ### C.
>
> $$
> 8(7)(6)=336
> $$
>
> Equivalently,
>
> $$
> \frac{8!}{5!}=336
> $$
