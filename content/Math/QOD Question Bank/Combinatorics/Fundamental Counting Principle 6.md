## Question

In a war zone, households experience severe shortages of electricity, water, and fuel. Each day:

- Electricity: Available in 2 disjoint 4-hour blocks (for example, 8 AM–12 PM or 4 PM–8 PM).
- Water: Running for 1 hour, with 3 possible time slots (6 AM, 12 PM, or 8 PM).
- Fuel: Available for cooking in 1 time slot, either 12 PM or 6 PM.

How many unique daily schedules can a household have for accessing all three utilities?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> There are:
>
> - $2$ choices for electricity,
> - $3$ choices for water,
> - $2$ choices for fuel.
>
> By the Fundamental Counting Principle,
>
> $$
> 2\times3\times2=12.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the number of unique daily schedules is
>
> $$
> \boxed{12}.
> $$
