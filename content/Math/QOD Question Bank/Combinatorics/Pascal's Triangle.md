## Question

Complete the missing entries in this row of Pascal's Triangle:

$$
1,\ 8,\ 28,\ \square,\ 70,\ \square,\ 28,\ 8,\ 1.
$$

Explain how Pascal's Triangle can be used to determine the missing values.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Each interior entry in Pascal's Triangle is the sum of the two entries directly above it. The row shown is the row of binomial coefficients for
>
> $$
> (a+b)^8.
> $$
>
> Those coefficients are
>
> $$
> 1,\ 8,\ 28,\ 56,\ 70,\ 56,\ 28,\ 8,\ 1.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the missing entries are
>
> $$
> \boxed{56\text{ and }56}.
> $$
