## Question

A security code consists of four digits, each chosen from \(0\) through \(9\). Digits may be repeated.

### A.

How many different security codes are possible?

### B.

How many security codes are possible if the second digit must be \(7\)?

### C.

How many security codes are possible if the first digit must be \(4\) and the last digit must be \(9\)?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> $$
> 10(10)(10)(10)=10\,000
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> Address the restriction first:
>
> $$
> 10(1)(10)(10)=1000
> $$
>
> ### C.
>
> Address the restrictions first:
>
> $$
> 1(10)(10)(1)=100
> $$
