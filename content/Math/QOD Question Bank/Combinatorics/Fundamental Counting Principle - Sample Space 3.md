## Question

One spinner has \(3\) equal sections labelled A, B, and C. A second spinner has \(8\) equal sections numbered \(1\) through \(8\). Each spinner is spun once.

### A.

How many possible outcomes are there for each individual event?

### B.

Use the Fundamental Counting Principle to determine the total number of elements in the sample space. Show your multiplication statement.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> The first spinner has \(3\) possible outcomes.
>
> The second spinner has \(8\) possible outcomes.
>
> ### B.
>
> $$
> 3 \times 8 = 24
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> n(S)=24
> $$
