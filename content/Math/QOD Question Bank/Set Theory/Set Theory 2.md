## Question

Consider the universal set

$$
\mathbf U=\{1,2,3,\ldots,20\}.
$$

Define

$$
\mathbf O=\{\text{odd natural numbers less than or equal to }15\},
$$

$$
\mathbf P=\{\text{prime numbers less than }15\},
$$

$$
\mathbf E=\{\text{even prime numbers less than }15\},
$$

and

$$
\mathbf Q=\{\text{natural numbers greater than }15\text{ and less than or equal to }20\}.
$$

1. List the elements of $\mathbf O$ and determine $n(\mathbf O)$.
2. List the elements of $\mathbf P$ and determine $n(\mathbf P)$.
3. List the elements of $\mathbf O\cap\mathbf P$ and determine $n(\mathbf O\cap\mathbf P)$.
4. Identify the subset relationships among $\mathbf U$, $\mathbf O$, $\mathbf P$, $\mathbf E$, and $\mathbf Q$.
5. List the elements of $\mathbf E$ and determine $n(\mathbf E)$.
6. Draw a Venn diagram representing $\mathbf U$, $\mathbf O$, $\mathbf P$, $\mathbf E$, and $\mathbf Q$.
7. List the elements of $\mathbf E\cap\mathbf O$ and determine $n(\mathbf E\cap\mathbf O)$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> $$
> \mathbf O=\{1,3,5,7,9,11,13,15\},
> \qquad
> \boxed{n(\mathbf O)=8}.
> $$
>
> $$
> \mathbf P=\{2,3,5,7,11,13\},
> \qquad
> \boxed{n(\mathbf P)=6}.
> $$
>
> $$
> \mathbf O\cap\mathbf P=\{3,5,7,11,13\},
> \qquad
> \boxed{n(\mathbf O\cap\mathbf P)=5}.
> $$
>
> $$
> \mathbf E=\{2\},
> \qquad
> \boxed{n(\mathbf E)=1}.
> $$
>
> $$
> \mathbf Q=\{16,17,18,19,20\}.
> $$
>
> Important subset relationships include
>
> $$
> \mathbf E\subset\mathbf P\subset\mathbf U,
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> \mathbf O\subset\mathbf U,
> \qquad
> \mathbf Q\subset\mathbf U.
> $$
>
> In the Venn diagram, $\mathbf O$ overlaps $\mathbf P$, $\mathbf E$ lies entirely inside $\mathbf P$, and $\mathbf Q$ is disjoint from $\mathbf O$, $\mathbf P$, and $\mathbf E$.
>
> Finally,
>
> $$
> \mathbf E\cap\mathbf O=\varnothing,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> \boxed{n(\mathbf E\cap\mathbf O)=0}.
> $$
