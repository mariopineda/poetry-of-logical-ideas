## Question

Convert the following unit as indicated using unit analysis. Provide all decimal answers in fractional form.

1.

$$
5 \mbox{ ft} \Rightarrow \mbox{in}
$$
2.

$$
32560 \mbox{ yd} \Rightarrow \mbox{mi}
$$
3.

$$
9 \mbox{ mi} \Rightarrow \mbox{ft}
$$
4.

$$
180 \mbox{ yd} \Rightarrow \mbox{ft}
$$
5.

$$
12 \mbox{ ft} 2 \mbox{ in} \Rightarrow \mbox{in}
$$
6.

$$
207 \mbox{ in} \Rightarrow \mbox{yd}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Use
>
> $$
> 1\text{ ft}=12\text{ in},
> \qquad
> 1\text{ yd}=3\text{ ft},
> \qquad
> 1\text{ mi}=1760\text{ yd}=5280\text{ ft}.
> $$
>
> ### 1.
>
> $$
> 5\text{ ft}
> \left(\frac{12\text{ in}}{1\text{ ft}}\right)
> =
> \boxed{60\text{ in}}
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> $$
> 32560\text{ yd}
> \left(\frac{1\text{ mi}}{1760\text{ yd}}\right)
> =
> \boxed{\frac{37}{2}\text{ mi}}
> $$
>
> ### 3.
>
> $$
> 9\text{ mi}
> \left(\frac{5280\text{ ft}}{1\text{ mi}}\right)
> =
> \boxed{47520\text{ ft}}
> $$
>
> ### 4.
>
> $$
> 180\text{ yd}
> \left(\frac{3\text{ ft}}{1\text{ yd}}\right)
> =
> \boxed{540\text{ ft}}
> $$
>
> ### 5.
>
> $$
> 12\text{ ft}\,2\text{ in}
> =
> 12(12)\text{ in}+2\text{ in}
> =
> \boxed{146\text{ in}}
> $$
>
> ### 6.
>
> $$
> 207\text{ in}
> \left(\frac{1\text{ yd}}{36\text{ in}}\right)
> =
> \boxed{\frac{23}{4}\text{ yd}}
> $$
