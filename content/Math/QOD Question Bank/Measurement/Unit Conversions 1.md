## Question

Convert the following unit as indicated, rounded to the nearest hundredth when applicable.

1.

$$
26 \mbox{ mm} \Rightarrow \mbox{cm}
$$
2.

$$
8.174 \mbox{ km} \Rightarrow \mbox{m}
$$
3.

$$
1.6 \mbox{ cm} \Rightarrow \mbox{m}
$$
4.

$$
572 \mbox{ mm} \Rightarrow \mbox{m}
$$
5.

$$
5 \mbox{ m} \Rightarrow \mbox{mm}
$$
6.

$$
98 \mbox{ m} \Rightarrow \mbox{km}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> 1.
>
> $$
> 26\text{ mm}
> \left(\frac{1\text{ cm}}{10\text{ mm}}\right)
> =
> \boxed{2.6\text{ cm}}
> $$
>
> 2.
>
> $$
> 8.174\text{ km}
> \left(\frac{1000\text{ m}}{1\text{ km}}\right)
> =
> \boxed{8174\text{ m}}
> $$
>
> 3.
>
> $$
> 1.6\text{ cm}
> \left(\frac{1\text{ m}}{100\text{ cm}}\right)
> =
> \boxed{0.016\text{ m}}
> $$
>
> 4.
>
> $$
> 572\text{ mm}
> \left(\frac{1\text{ m}}{1000\text{ mm}}\right)
> =
> 0.572\text{ m}
> \approx
> \boxed{0.57\text{ m}}
> $$
>
> 5.
>
> $$
> 5\text{ m}
> \left(\frac{1000\text{ mm}}{1\text{ m}}\right)
> =
> \boxed{5000\text{ mm}}
> $$
>
> 6.
>
> $$
> 98\text{ m}
> \left(\frac{1\text{ km}}{1000\text{ m}}\right)
> =
> 0.098\text{ km}
> \approx
> \boxed{0.10\text{ km}}
> $$
