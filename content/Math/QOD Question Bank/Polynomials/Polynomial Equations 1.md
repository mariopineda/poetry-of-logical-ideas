## Question

Solve $x^3+x^2 = 24x+24$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Solve
>
> $$
> x^3+x^2=24x+24.
> $$
>
> Move everything to one side:
>
> $$
> x^3+x^2-24x-24=0.
> $$
>
> Factor by grouping:
>
> $$
> x^2(x+1)-24(x+1)=0.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> (x+1)(x^2-24)=0.
> $$
>
> So
>
> $$
> x=-1
> $$
>
> or
>
> $$
> x^2=24.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=-1,\ -2\sqrt6,\ 2\sqrt6}.
> $$
