## Question

For each polynomial, state the name, degree, leading coefficient, constant, domain and y-intercept.


1.

$$
f(x)=8x+9
$$
2.

$$
g(x)=8x^3+6x^2-10x
$$
4.

$$
h(x)=\sqrt{3}x-19
$$
5.

$$
m(y)=-7y^4-2y^2+y-\sqrt{5}
$$
6.

$$
y=\frac{\displaystyle x^2}{\displaystyle 5}-\frac{\displaystyle 2}{\displaystyle 5}
$$
7.

$$
f(x) = 3(x-3)^2
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> $$
> f(x)=8x+9
> $$
>
> - name: linear polynomial
> - degree: $1$
> - leading coefficient: $8$
> - constant: $9$
> - domain: $\mathbb R$
> - $y$-intercept: $\boxed{(0,9)}$
>
> ### 2.
>
> $$
> g(x)=8x^3+6x^2-10x
> $$
>
> - name: cubic polynomial
> - degree: $3$
> - leading coefficient: $8$
> - constant: $0$
> - domain: $\mathbb R$
> - $y$-intercept: $\boxed{(0,0)}$
>
> ### 4.
>
> $$
> h(x)=\sqrt3\,x-19
> $$
>
> - name: linear polynomial
> - degree: $1$
> - leading coefficient: $\sqrt3$
> - constant: $-19$
> - domain: $\mathbb R$
> - $y$-intercept: $\boxed{(0,-19)}$
>
> ### 5.
>
> $$
> m(y)=-7y^4-2y^2+y-\sqrt5
> $$
>
> - name: quartic polynomial
> - degree: $4$
> - leading coefficient: $-7$
> - constant: $-\sqrt5$
> - domain: $\mathbb R$
> - at input $0$: $m(0)=-\sqrt5$, so the intercept in input-output coordinates is $\boxed{(0,-\sqrt5)}$
>
> ### 6.
>
> $$
> y=\frac15x^2-\frac25
> $$
>
> - name: quadratic polynomial
> - degree: $2$
> - leading coefficient: $\frac15$
> - constant: $-\frac25$
> - domain: $\mathbb R$
> - $y$-intercept: $\boxed{\left(0,-\frac25\right)}$
>
> ### 7.
>
> $$
> f(x)=3(x-3)^2=3x^2-18x+27
> $$
>
> - name: quadratic polynomial
> - degree: $2$
> - leading coefficient: $3$
> - constant: $27$
> - domain: $\mathbb R$
> - $y$-intercept: $\boxed{(0,27)}$
