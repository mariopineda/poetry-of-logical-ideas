## Question

### 1. Exponent Laws

Simplify using exponent laws. Express your answer with positive exponents.

$$
\frac{(3a^2b^3)^2}{9ab^2}
$$

### 2. Polynomial Operations

Simplify. Express your answer in descending order.

$$
2(2x^2-3x+4)-(x^2+5x-1)
$$

### 3. Factoring

Factor fully.

$$
12x^4-75x^2
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1. Exponent Laws
>
> Apply the power of a product and power of a power rules:
>
> $$
> (3a^2b^3)^2=9a^4b^6.
> $$
>
> Then divide and subtract exponents on like bases:
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> \frac{9a^4b^6}{9ab^2}
> &=a^{4-1}b^{6-2}\\
> &=a^3b^4.
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> **Answer:** $\boxed{a^3b^4}$
>
> ### 2. Polynomial Operations
>
> Distribute:
>
> $$
> 4x^2-6x+8-x^2-5x+1.
> $$
>
> Combine like terms:
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> (4x^2-x^2)+(-6x-5x)+(8+1)
> &=3x^2-11x+9.
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> **Answer:** $\boxed{3x^2-11x+9}$
>
> ### 3. Factoring
>
> First factor out the greatest common factor:
>
> $$
> 12x^4-75x^2=3x^2(4x^2-25).
> $$
>
> The remaining binomial is a difference of squares:
>
> $$
> 4x^2-25=(2x)^2-5^2=(2x-5)(2x+5).
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> 12x^4-75x^2=3x^2(2x-5)(2x+5).
> $$
>
> **Answer:** $\boxed{3x^2(2x-5)(2x+5)}$
