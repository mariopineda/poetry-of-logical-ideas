## Question

Solve each equation by factoring.

### A.

$$
x^2+5x+6=0
$$

### B.

$$
2x^2+7x+3=0
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> $$
> x^2+5x+6=0
> $$
>
> $$
> (x+2)(x+3)=0
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> x=-2,\,-3
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> $$
> 2x^2+7x+3=0
> $$
>
> $$
> 2x^2+6x+x+3=0
> $$
>
> $$
> 2x(x+3)+(x+3)=0
> $$
>
> $$
> (2x+1)(x+3)=0
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> x=-\frac{1}{2},\,-3
> $$
