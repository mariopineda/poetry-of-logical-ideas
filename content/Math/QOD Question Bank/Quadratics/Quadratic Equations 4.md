## Question

The roots of the equation $x(x-1)=2$ are

1. 0 and 1
2. 2 and 3
3. -1 and 2
4. -2 and 1

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Start with
>
> $$
> x(x-1)=2.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> x^2-x-2=0.
> $$
>
> Factor:
>
> $$
> (x-2)(x+1)=0.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=-1,\ 2},
> $$
>
> which is **choice 3**.
