## Question

Which of the following functions is not a quadratic function?

1.

$$
f(x) = x^2-5x+8
$$
2.

$$
g(x) = -3(x+5)^2-7
$$
3.

$$
h(x) = x(x^2+4)-2
$$
4.

$$
P(X) = 1-x^2
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> A quadratic function has degree $2$.
>
> 1.
>
> $$
> f(x)=x^2-5x+8
> $$
>
> is quadratic.
>
> 2.
>
> $$
> g(x)=-3(x+5)^2-7
> $$
>
> is quadratic.
>
> 3.
>
> $$
> h(x)=x(x^2+4)-2=x^3+4x-2
> $$
>
> has degree $3$, so it is **not** quadratic.
>
> 4.
>
> $$
> P(x)=1-x^2
> $$
>
> is quadratic.
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{h(x)=x(x^2+4)-2}
> $$
>
> is the function that is not quadratic, so the answer is **choice 3**.
