## Question

Indicate which of the following functions are quadratic. For the quadratic functions, expand and state the coordinates of the vertex.

1.

$$
y=(x-4)(x-12)(x+2)
$$
2.

$$
y=3(x+8)-2(x+2)(x-1)+3x
$$
3.

$$
y=2(x-6)(x-3)-x+5
$$
4.

$$
y=(x-3)(3x^2+6x-1)
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> $$
> y=(x-4)(x-12)(x+2)
> $$
>
> is a product of three linear factors, so it is degree $3$.
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{Not quadratic}}
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> Expand:
>
> $$
> y=3(x+8)-2(x+2)(x-1)+3x
> $$
>
> $$
> =3x+24-2(x^2+x-2)+3x
> $$
>
> $$
> =-2x^2+4x+28.
> $$
>
> This is quadratic.
>
> Complete the square:
>
> $$
> y=-2(x-1)^2+30.
> $$
>
> Vertex:
>
> $$
> \boxed{(1,30)}.
> $$
>
> ### 3.
>
> Expand:
>
> $$
> y=2(x-6)(x-3)-x+5
> $$
>
> $$
> =2x^2-19x+41.
> $$
>
> This is quadratic.
>
> The vertex occurs at
>
> $$
> x=-\frac{b}{2a}
> =
> \frac{19}{4}.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> y\left(\frac{19}{4}\right)
> =
> -\frac{33}{8}.
> $$
>
> Vertex:
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> \left(\frac{19}{4},-\frac{33}{8}\right)
> }.
> $$
>
> ### 4.
>
> $$
> y=(x-3)(3x^2+6x-1)
> $$
>
> has degree $3$.
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{Not quadratic}}
> $$
