## Question

When the equation $6x^2-12x-18$ is fully factored, one factor is:

1.

$$
x-1
$$
2.

$$
x+3
$$
3.

$$
x+6
$$
4.

$$
x-3
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Factor:
>
> $$
> 6x^2-12x-18
> =
> 6(x^2-2x-3)
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> x^2-2x-3=(x-3)(x+1).
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> 6x^2-12x-18
> =
> 6(x-3)(x+1).
> $$
>
> Therefore one factor is
>
> $$
> \boxed{x-3},
> $$
>
> which is **choice 4**.
