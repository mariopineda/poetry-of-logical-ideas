## Question

The zeros of the quadratic function $f(x) = 6x^2+2x-1$ are

1.

$$
\frac{-1 \pm \sqrt{14}}{6}
$$
2.

$$
\frac{-1 \pm 2\sqrt{7}}{6}
$$
3.

$$
\frac{-1 \pm \sqrt{7}}{6}
$$
4.

$$
\frac{-2 \pm \sqrt{7}}{6}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> For
>
> $$
> 6x^2+2x-1=0,
> $$
>
> use the quadratic formula:
>
> $$
> x=
> \frac{-2\pm\sqrt{2^2-4(6)(-1)}}{12}.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> x=
> \frac{-2\pm\sqrt{28}}{12}
> =
> \frac{-2\pm2\sqrt7}{12}
> =
> \boxed{\frac{-1\pm\sqrt7}{6}}.
> $$
>
> Therefore the correct answer is **choice 3**.
