## Question

Write each quadratic equation in vertex form by completing the square. State the coordinates of the vertex.

1.

$$
y=x^2+2x+3
$$
2.

$$
y=x^2+12x+20
$$
3.

$$
y=-x^2+8x-7
$$
4.

$$
y=-x^2-10x-31
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> $$
> y=x^2+2x+3
> $$
>
> $$
> y=(x+1)^2+2.
> $$
>
> Vertex:
>
> $$
> \boxed{(-1,2)}.
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> $$
> y=x^2+12x+20
> $$
>
> $$
> y=(x+6)^2-16.
> $$
>
> Vertex:
>
> $$
> \boxed{(-6,-16)}.
> $$
>
> ### 3.
>
> $$
> y=-x^2+8x-7
> $$
>
> $$
> y=-(x-4)^2+9.
> $$
>
> Vertex:
>
> $$
> \boxed{(4,9)}.
> $$
>
> ### 4.
>
> $$
> y=-x^2-10x-31
> $$
>
> $$
> y=-(x+5)^2-6.
> $$
>
> Vertex:
>
> $$
> \boxed{(-5,-6)}.
> $$
