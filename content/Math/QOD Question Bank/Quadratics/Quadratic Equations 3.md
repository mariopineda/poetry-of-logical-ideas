## Question

Solve $2x^2=6x-3$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Solve
>
> $$
> 2x^2=6x-3.
> $$
>
> Rearrange:
>
> $$
> 2x^2-6x+3=0.
> $$
>
> Using the Quadratic Formula,
>
> $$
> x
> =
> \frac{6\pm\sqrt{(-6)^2-4(2)(3)}}{2(2)}.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> x
> =
> \frac{6\pm\sqrt{12}}{4}
> =
> \frac{6\pm2\sqrt3}{4}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> x=\frac{3\pm\sqrt3}{2}
> }.
> $$
