## Question

Solve each equation by factoring.

### A.

$$
x^2-x-12=0
$$

### B.

$$
3x^2-10x-8=0
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> $$
> x^2-x-12=0
> $$
>
> $$
> (x-4)(x+3)=0
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> x=4,\,-3
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> $$
> 3x^2-10x-8=0
> $$
>
> $$
> 3x^2-12x+2x-8=0
> $$
>
> $$
> 3x(x-4)+2(x-4)=0
> $$
>
> $$
> (3x+2)(x-4)=0
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> x=-\frac{2}{3},\,4
> $$
