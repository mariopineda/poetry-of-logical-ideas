## Question

If $f(x) = 2x+1$ and $g(x)=5x$, then determine $3f(x)-2g(x+1)$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Given
>
> $$
> f(x)=2x+1
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> g(x)=5x,
> $$
>
> we have
>
> $$
> 3f(x)-2g(x+1)
> =
> 3(2x+1)-2[5(x+1)].
> $$
>
> Simplify:
>
> $$
> 6x+3-10x-10
> =
> \boxed{-4x-7}.
> $$
