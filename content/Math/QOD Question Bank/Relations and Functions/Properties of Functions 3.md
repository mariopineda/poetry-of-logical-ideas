## Question

Determine the domain of $y=\left( \frac{f}{g}\right)(x) = \frac{\sqrt{x+7}}{x-3} $. Answer in interval notation.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The function is
>
> $$
> y=
> \frac{\sqrt{x+7}}{x-3}.
> $$
>
> For the square root to be real,
>
> $$
> x+7\geq0,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> x\geq-7.
> $$
>
> Also, the denominator cannot equal zero:
>
> $$
> x\neq3.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the domain is
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> [-7,3)\cup(3,\infty)
> }.
> $$
>
