## Question

Express the following without absolute values: $|x+7|$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The expression inside the absolute value changes sign at
>
> $$
> x+7=0,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> x=-7.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> |x+7|=
> \begin{cases}
> x+7, & x\geq -7,\\
> -x-7, & x<-7.
> \end{cases}
> }
> $$
>
