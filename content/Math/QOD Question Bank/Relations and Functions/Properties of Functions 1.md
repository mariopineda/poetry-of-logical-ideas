## Question

If $f(x)=2x-x^2$ and $g(x)=4x-11$, then evaluate $4f(3)-2g(-1)$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Evaluate
>
> $$
> f(3)=2(3)-3^2=6-9=-3.
> $$
>
> Also,
>
> $$
> g(-1)=4(-1)-11=-15.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> 4f(3)-2g(-1)
> =
> 4(-3)-2(-15).
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> -12+30
> =
> \boxed{18}.
> $$
>
