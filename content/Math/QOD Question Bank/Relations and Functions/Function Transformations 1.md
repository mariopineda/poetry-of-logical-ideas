## Question

Fully sketch $y=-2x^2+12x+32$. Include vertex and intercepts.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Complete the square:
>
> $$
> y=-2x^2+12x+32.
> $$
>
> Factor $-2$ from the quadratic and linear terms:
>
> $$
> y=-2(x^2-6x)+32.
> $$
>
> Complete the square:
>
> $$
> y=-2[(x-3)^2-9]+32.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> y=-2(x-3)^2+50
> }.
> $$
>
> ### Vertex
>
> $$
> \boxed{(3,50)}.
> $$
>
> ### Axis of symmetry
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=3}.
> $$
>
> ### $x$-intercepts
>
> Set $y=0$:
>
> $$
> -2x^2+12x+32=0.
> $$
>
> Divide by $-2$:
>
> $$
> x^2-6x-16=0.
> $$
>
> Factor:
>
> $$
> (x-8)(x+2)=0.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{(-2,0),\ (8,0)}.
> $$
>
> ### $y$-intercept
>
> At $x=0$,
>
> $$
> y=32.
> $$
>
> So,
>
> $$
> \boxed{(0,32)}.
> $$
>
> The parabola opens downward, with domain
>
> $$
> \mathbb R
> $$
>
> and range
>
> $$
> \boxed{y\leq50}.
> $$
