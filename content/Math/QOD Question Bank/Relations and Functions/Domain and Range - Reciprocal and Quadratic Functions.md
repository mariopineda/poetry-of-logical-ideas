## Question

State the domain and range of each function in both **set-builder notation** and **interval notation**.

1.

$$
f(x)=\dfrac{1}{x}
$$
2.

$$
g(x)=x^2
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ![[Domain and Range - Reciprocal and Quadratic Functions Graph.png]]
>
> ### 1. $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{x}$
>
> Division by zero is undefined, so $x=0$ is excluded from the domain. The function can never produce $y=0$, so zero is also excluded from the range.
>
> **Domain**
>
> - Set-builder notation: $\{x\in\mathbb{R}\mid x\ne0\}$
> - Interval notation: $(-\infty,0)\cup(0,\infty)$
>
> **Range**
>
> - Set-builder notation: $\{y\in\mathbb{R}\mid y\ne0\}$
> - Interval notation: $(-\infty,0)\cup(0,\infty)$
>
> ### 2. $g(x)=x^2$
>
> Any real number can be squared, so there is no restriction on $x$. Squaring a real number never produces a negative result.
>
> **Domain**
>
> - Set-builder notation: $\{x\mid x\in\mathbb{R}\}$
> - Interval notation: $(-\infty,\infty)$
>
> **Range**
>
> - Set-builder notation: $\{y\in\mathbb{R}\mid y\ge0\}$
> - Interval notation: $[0,\infty)$
