## Question

In 2015 the provincial government in Alberta increased the minimum wage for students under 18 to \$15/hour. In 2019 the newly elected provincial government rolled back the minimum salary to \$13/hour.

### A.

Express the relation between the number of hours worked, $h$, and total earnings, $E$, for the \$15/hour minimum wage as an equation.

### B.

Express the relation between the number of hours worked, $h$, and total earnings, $E$, for the \$13/hour minimum wage as an equation.

### C.

State the independent and dependent variables.

### D.

Is the relation between hours worked and earnings discrete or continuous? Explain your reasoning.

### E.

Using [Desmos](https://www.desmos.com/calculator), graph the relations on the same graph using two colours, one for each equation, for a $40$-hour work week. Label the axes and adjust the domain and range to realistic values for this context.

### F.

What would your earnings be for a $40$-hour work week under the two pay rates? What is the difference in earnings between the two pay rates? How much less would you earn in a week after the rollback?

### G.

How many more hours would you have to work to earn \$1000 after the rollback?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> At \$15 per hour,
>
> $$
> \boxed{E=15h}.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> At \$13 per hour,
>
> $$
> \boxed{E=13h}.
> $$
>
> ### C.
>
> The independent variable is the number of hours worked, $h$.
>
> The dependent variable is total earnings, $E$.
>
> ### D.
>
> The relation is **continuous** because a worker can work fractional portions of an hour, so $h$ can take any real value in the relevant interval.
>
> ### E.
>
> For a $40$-hour work week, graph
>
> $$
> E=15h,\qquad 0\le h\le40
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> E=13h,\qquad 0\le h\le40.
> $$
>
> A suitable viewing window is approximately
>
> $$
> 0\le h\le40,
> \qquad
> 0\le E\le600.
> $$
>
> ### F.
>
> At \$15 per hour,
>
> $$
> E=15(40)=\$600.
> $$
>
> At \$13 per hour,
>
> $$
> E=13(40)=\$520.
> $$
>
> The difference is
>
> $$
> 600-520=\boxed{\$80}.
> $$
>
> So the weekly earnings are \$80 lower after the rollback.
>
> ### G.
>
> At \$15 per hour, the time required to earn \$1000 is
>
> $$
> \frac{1000}{15}
> =
> 66.\overline6\text{ h}.
> $$
>
> At \$13 per hour, the time required is
>
> $$
> \frac{1000}{13}
> \approx76.92\text{ h}.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the additional time required is
>
> $$
> 76.92-66.67
> \approx
> \boxed{10.26\text{ h}}.
> $$
