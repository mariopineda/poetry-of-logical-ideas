## Question

For each qualitative graph description, give one realistic scenario that could produce that graph. Clearly identify the independent and dependent variables.

### A.

A straight line increasing through the origin.

### B.

A straight line decreasing until it reaches zero.

### C.

A horizontal line.

### D.

A U-shaped parabola.

### E.

A smooth periodic curve that repeatedly rises and falls.

### F.

An increasing step graph.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Many answers are possible. One valid set is:
>
> ### A.
>
> **Scenario:** total cost at a fixed price per item.
>
> - independent variable: number of items;
> - dependent variable: total cost.
>
> ### B.
>
> **Scenario:** litres of fuel remaining while a vehicle consumes fuel at a constant rate.
>
> - independent variable: time;
> - dependent variable: fuel remaining.
>
> ### C.
>
> **Scenario:** the temperature of a perfectly controlled room.
>
> - independent variable: time;
> - dependent variable: temperature.
>
> ### D.
>
> **Scenario:** height above the ground of a point on a parabolic ramp cross-section, measured relative to horizontal position.
>
> - independent variable: horizontal position;
> - dependent variable: height.
>
> ### E.
>
> **Scenario:** height of a rider on a Ferris wheel.
>
> - independent variable: time;
> - dependent variable: height above the ground.
>
> ### F.
>
> **Scenario:** cumulative number of completed assignments during a semester.
>
> - independent variable: time;
> - dependent variable: number completed.
>
> The dependent variable changes only when another assignment is completed, producing steps.
