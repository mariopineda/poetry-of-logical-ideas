## Question

If $f(x)=x^2-3x+5$, then determine:

1.

$$
\frac{f(a)-f(6)}{a-6}
$$
2.

$$
\frac{f(2+h)-f(2)}{h}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The function is
>
> $$
> f(x)=x^2-3x+5.
> $$
>
> ### 1.
>
> First,
>
> $$
> f(6)=36-18+5=23.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> \frac{f(a)-f(6)}{a-6}
> =
> \frac{a^2-3a+5-23}{a-6}.
> $$
>
> Factor the numerator:
>
> $$
> a^2-3a-18
> =
> (a-6)(a+3).
> $$
>
> Therefore, for $a\neq6$,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> \frac{f(a)-f(6)}{a-6}
> =
> a+3
> }.
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> First,
>
> $$
> f(2)=4-6+5=3.
> $$
>
> Now,
>
> $$
> f(2+h)
> =
> (2+h)^2-3(2+h)+5.
> $$
>
> Simplify:
>
> $$
> f(2+h)=3+h+h^2.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \frac{f(2+h)-f(2)}{h}
> =
> \frac{h+h^2}{h}.
> $$
>
> For $h\neq0$,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> \frac{f(2+h)-f(2)}{h}
> =
> 1+h
> }.
> $$
