## Question

Explain whether each relation is a function of $x$.

### A.

$$
y=2x+3
$$

### B.

$$
x=y^2
$$

### C.

$$
\{(-2,5),(0,1),(3,7),(5,7)\}
$$

### D.

$$
\{(1,2),(1,4),(2,5),(3,6)\}
$$

### E.

$$
x^2+y^2=9
$$

### F.

$$
x=2
$$

### G.

$$
\{(2,1),(3,5),(3,6),(4,-2),(5,-1)\}
$$

### H.

$$
\{(3,-2),(4,1),(5,1),(6,0),(7,-2)\}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> A relation is a function of $x$ if every allowed input $x$ has exactly one output $y$.
>
> ### A.
>
> $$
> y=2x+3
> $$
>
> is a function: every $x$ produces exactly one $y$.
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{Function}}
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> $$
> x=y^2
> $$
>
> is not a function of $x$. For example, if $x=4$, then
>
> $$
> y=2
> \quad\text{or}\quad
> y=-2.
> $$
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{Not a function}}
> $$
>
> ### C.
>
> No input is paired with two different outputs.
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{Function}}
> $$
>
> ### D.
>
> The input $x=1$ is paired with both $2$ and $4$.
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{Not a function}}
> $$
>
> ### E.
>
> The circle
>
> $$
> x^2+y^2=9
> $$
>
> fails the vertical-line test.
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{Not a function}}
> $$
>
> ### F.
>
> The vertical line
>
> $$
> x=2
> $$
>
> contains many $y$-values for the same input.
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{Not a function}}
> $$
>
> ### G.
>
> The input $x=3$ has outputs $5$ and $6$.
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{Not a function}}
> $$
>
> ### H.
>
> Every input occurs only once.
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{Function}}
> $$
