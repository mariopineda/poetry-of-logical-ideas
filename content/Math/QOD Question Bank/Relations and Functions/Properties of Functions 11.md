## Question

1. For $f(x)=3x^2-4x$, then determine (in simplified form):
   1.

   $$
   \frac{f(a)-f(-2)}{a+2}
   $$
   2. $\frac{f(3+h)-f(3)}{h}$- For $f(x)=\frac{x}{x+1}$, determine (in simplified form):
   1.

   $$
   \frac{f(a)-f(-2)}{a+2}
   $$
   2.

   $$
   \frac{f(3+h)-f(3)}{h}
   $$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### For $f(x)=3x^2-4x$
>
> First,
>
> $$
> f(-2)=20.
> $$
>
> #### 1.
>
> $$
> \frac{f(a)-f(-2)}{a+2}
> =
> \frac{3a^2-4a-20}{a+2}.
> $$
>
> Factor:
>
> $$
> 3a^2-4a-20=(a+2)(3a-10).
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{3a-10},
> \qquad a\neq-2.
> $$
>
> #### 2.
>
> $$
> f(3+h)=3(3+h)^2-4(3+h)
> =15+14h+3h^2.
> $$
>
> Since $f(3)=15$,
>
> $$
> \frac{f(3+h)-f(3)}h
> =
> \frac{14h+3h^2}{h}
> =
> \boxed{14+3h},
> \qquad h\neq0.
> $$
>
> ### For $f(x)=\frac{x}{x+1}$
>
> Here,
>
> $$
> f(-2)=2.
> $$
>
> #### 1.
>
> $$
> \frac{f(a)-f(-2)}{a+2}
> =
> \frac{\frac{a}{a+1}-2}{a+2}.
> $$
>
> The numerator simplifies to
>
> $$
> -\frac{a+2}{a+1}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{-\frac1{a+1}},
> $$
>
> with the original restrictions
>
> $$
> a\neq-1,-2.
> $$
>
> #### 2.
>
> $$
> f(3+h)=\frac{3+h}{4+h},
> \qquad
> f(3)=\frac34.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> \frac{f(3+h)-f(3)}h
> =
> \frac{1}{4(h+4)}.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{1}{4(h+4)}},
> $$
>
> with
>
> $$
> h\neq0,-4.
> $$
