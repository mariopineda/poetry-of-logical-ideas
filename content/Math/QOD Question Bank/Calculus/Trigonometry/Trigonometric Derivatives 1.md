## Question

Find the equation of the tangent line to

$$
y=\frac{\sin x}{\cos 2x}
$$

at $x=\frac{\pi}{6}$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> At $x=\frac{\pi}{6}$,
>
> $$
> y=\frac{\sin(\pi/6)}{\cos(\pi/3)}=1.
> $$
>
> Differentiate:
>
> $$
> y'
> =
> \frac{\cos x\cos2x+2\sin x\sin2x}{\cos^2 2x}.
> $$
>
> At $x=\frac{\pi}{6}$,
>
> $$
> y'=3\sqrt3.
> $$
>
> Therefore the tangent line is
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> y-1=3\sqrt3\left(x-\frac{\pi}{6}\right)
> }.
> $$
