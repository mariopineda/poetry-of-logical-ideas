## Question

Evaluate the limits.

1.

$$
\lim_{x \to 4} \frac{x^2-16}{x-4}
$$
2.

$$
\lim_{x \to 1} \frac{x-1}{x}
$$
3.

$$
\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sqrt{x+4}-2}{x}
$$
4.

$$
\lim_{x \to -6} \frac{x}{x+6}
$$
5.

$$
\lim_{x \to 7} \frac{x-7}{\frac{1}{x}-\frac{1}{7}}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> $$
> \lim_{x\to4}
> \frac{x^2-16}{x-4}
> $$
>
> Factor:
>
> $$
> x^2-16=(x-4)(x+4).
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{8}.
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> $$
> \lim_{x\to1}\frac{x-1}{x}
> =
> \boxed{0}.
> $$
>
> ### 3.
>
> Rationalize:
>
> $$
> \frac{\sqrt{x+4}-2}{x}
> \cdot
> \frac{\sqrt{x+4}+2}
> {\sqrt{x+4}+2}.
> $$
>
> This becomes
>
> $$
> \frac{1}{\sqrt{x+4}+2}.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac14}.
> $$
>
> ### 4.
>
> $$
> \lim_{x\to-6}\frac{x}{x+6}.
> $$
>
> From the left,
>
> $$
> \frac{x}{x+6}\to+\infty.
> $$
>
> From the right,
>
> $$
> \frac{x}{x+6}\to-\infty.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{the two-sided limit does not exist}}.
> $$
>
> ### 5.
>
> $$
> \frac{x-7}
> {\frac1x-\frac17}.
> $$
>
> Simplify the denominator:
>
> $$
> \frac1x-\frac17
> =
> \frac{7-x}{7x}
> =
> -\frac{x-7}{7x}.
> $$
>
> Therefore, for $x\neq7$,
>
> $$
> \frac{x-7}
> {-\frac{x-7}{7x}}
> =
> -7x.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \boxed{-49}.
> $$
