## Question

Sketch the piecewise function $y =
\begin{cases}
x+5 & \mbox{, if } x < -2 \\
x^2-1 & \mbox{, if } -2 \leq x<1 \\
|x-3| & \mbox{, if } x \geq 1
\end{cases}$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The function is
>
> $$
> y=
> \begin{cases}
> x+5, & x<-2,\\
> x^2-1, & -2\leq x<1,\\
> |x-3|, & x\geq1.
> \end{cases}
> $$
>
> Sketch each branch only on its stated interval.
>
> ### First branch
>
> $$
> y=x+5,\qquad x<-2.
> $$
>
> At
>
> $$
> x=-2,
> $$
>
> the corresponding point is
>
> $$
> (-2,3),
> $$
>
> but this branch has an **open** endpoint there.
>
> ### Second branch
>
> $$
> y=x^2-1,\qquad -2\leq x<1.
> $$
>
> It has a **closed** point at
>
> $$
> (-2,3)
> $$
>
> and an **open** point at
>
> $$
> (1,0).
> $$
>
> The vertex is
>
> $$
> (0,-1).
> $$
>
> ### Third branch
>
> $$
> y=|x-3|,\qquad x\geq1.
> $$
>
> It begins with a **closed** point at
>
> $$
> (1,2)
> $$
>
> and has vertex
>
> $$
> \boxed{(3,0)}.
> $$
>
> The graph is continuous at
>
> $$
> x=-2
> $$
>
> but has a jump at
>
> $$
> x=1.
> $$
