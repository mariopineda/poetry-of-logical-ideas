## Question

Given $V=\frac{4}{3} \pi r^3$, determine the derivative of the volume.

#### A.

With respect to radius.

#### B.

With respect to time.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The volume is
>
> $$
> V=\frac43\pi r^3.
> $$
>
> ### A. With respect to radius
>
> Differentiate with respect to $r$:
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> \frac{dV}{dr}
> =
> 4\pi r^2
> }.
> $$
>
> ### B. With respect to time
>
> If the radius changes with time, use the Chain Rule:
>
> $$
> \frac{dV}{dt}
> =
> \frac{dV}{dr}\frac{dr}{dt}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> \frac{dV}{dt}
> =
> 4\pi r^2\frac{dr}{dt}
> }.
> $$
