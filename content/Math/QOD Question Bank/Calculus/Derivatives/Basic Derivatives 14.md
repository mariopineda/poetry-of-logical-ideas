## Question

For what real values of $x$ does

$$
y=x^6-18x^4+2x^3-72x+5
$$

have a horizontal tangent?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> A horizontal tangent occurs when
>
> $$
> y'=0.
> $$
>
> Differentiate:
>
> $$
> y'
> =
> 6x^5-72x^3+6x^2-72.
> $$
>
> Factor:
>
> $$
> y'
> =
> 6(x+1)(x^2-12)(x^2-x+1).
> $$
>
> The quadratic
>
> $$
> x^2-x+1
> $$
>
> has discriminant
>
> $$
> 1-4=-3<0,
> $$
>
> so it has no real roots.
>
> Therefore, the real solutions are
>
> $$
> x=-1
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> x=\pm\sqrt{12}=\pm2\sqrt3.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=-2\sqrt3,\ -1,\ 2\sqrt3}.
> $$
