## Question

Differentiate.

#### A.

$$
y=x^2 \times x^8
$$

#### B.

$$
g(t)=\frac{1}{t^4}
$$

#### C.

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\sqrt[5]{x^2}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> First simplify:
>
> $$
> y=x^2x^8=x^{10}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{y'=10x^9}.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> Rewrite:
>
> $$
> g(t)=t^{-4}.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \boxed{g'(t)=-4t^{-5}=-\frac{4}{t^5}}.
> $$
>
> ### C.
>
> Rewrite:
>
> $$
> \sqrt[5]{x^2}=x^{2/5}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> \frac{d}{dx}\sqrt[5]{x^2}
> =
> \frac25x^{-3/5}
> =
> \frac{2}{5x^{3/5}}
> }
> $$
>
> for $x\neq0$.
>
