## Question

Differentiate $f(x) = (x^3-8x+2)^6$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Using the Chain Rule,
>
> $$
> f'(x)
> =
> 6(x^3-8x+2)^5(3x^2-8).
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> f'(x)
> =
> 6(3x^2-8)(x^3-8x+2)^5
> }.
> $$
>
