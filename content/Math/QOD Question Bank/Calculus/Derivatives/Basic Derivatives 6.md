## Question

Differentiate.

### A.

$$
y=5x^4-6x^3+7x^2-\frac{x}{3}+9
$$

### B.

$$
h(x)=9x^2-8^3+\frac{1}{4x^2}-\frac{1}{6\sqrt[3]{x^4}}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> Differentiate term by term:
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> y'=20x^3-18x^2+14x-\frac13
> }.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> Rewrite the variable terms using exponents:
>
> $$
> h(x)
> =
> 9x^2-512+\frac14x^{-2}-\frac16x^{-4/3}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> h'(x)
> =
> 18x-\frac12x^{-3}+\frac29x^{-7/3}.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> h'(x)
> =
> 18x-\frac{1}{2x^3}
> +\frac{2}{9x^{7/3}}
> }.
> $$
