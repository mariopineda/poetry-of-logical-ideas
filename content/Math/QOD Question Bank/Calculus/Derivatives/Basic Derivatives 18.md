## Question

Determine the $y$-intercept of the tangent line to

$$
y=5x-x^2
$$

at

$$
x=\frac13.
$$

Round to the nearest hundredth.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Differentiate:
>
> $$
> y'=5-2x.
> $$
>
> At $x=\frac13$,
>
> $$
> m=5-\frac23=\frac{13}{3}.
> $$
>
> The point on the curve is
>
> $$
> \left(
> \frac13,\,
> 5\left(\frac13\right)-\frac19
> \right)
> =
> \left(
> \frac13,\,
> \frac{14}{9}
> \right).
> $$
>
> Using
>
> $$
> y=mx+b,
> $$
>
> we get
>
> $$
> \frac{14}{9}
> =
> \frac{13}{3}\left(\frac13\right)+b.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> b=\frac19\approx0.1111.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the $y$-intercept is
>
> $$
> \boxed{0.11}.
> $$
