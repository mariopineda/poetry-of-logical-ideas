## Question

Differentiate $f(x) = (x-3)(x+1)$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Using the Product Rule,
>
> $$
> f'(x)
> =
> 1(x+1)+(x-3)(1).
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> f'(x)
> =
> x+1+x-3
> =
> \boxed{2x-2}.
> $$
