## Question

A line is perpendicular to

$$
3x+2y-8=0
$$

and has a $y$-intercept of $5$.

Determine its equation in general form.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Rewrite the given line:
>
> $$
> 2y=-3x+8
> $$
>
> $$
> y=-\frac32x+4.
> $$
>
> Its slope is
>
> $$
> -\frac32.
> $$
>
> A perpendicular line has slope
>
> $$
> \frac23.
> $$
>
> Since its $y$-intercept is $5$,
>
> $$
> y=\frac23x+5.
> $$
>
> Multiply by $3$ and rearrange:
>
> $$
> \boxed{2x-3y+15=0}.
> $$
