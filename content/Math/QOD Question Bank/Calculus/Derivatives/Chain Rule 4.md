## Question

If $g(3)=6$, $g'(3)=5$, $f(5)=2$, and $f'(6)=8$, then evaluate $(f \circ g)'(3)$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> By the Chain Rule,
>
> $$
> (f\circ g)'(x)
> =
> f'(g(x))g'(x).
> $$
>
> At $x=3$,
>
> $$
> (f\circ g)'(3)
> =
> f'(g(3))g'(3).
> $$
>
> Since
>
> $$
> g(3)=6,\qquad f'(6)=8,\qquad g'(3)=5,
> $$
>
> we get
>
> $$
> (f\circ g)'(3)
> =
> 8(5)
> =
> \boxed{40}.
> $$
>
> The given value $f(5)=2$ is not needed.
