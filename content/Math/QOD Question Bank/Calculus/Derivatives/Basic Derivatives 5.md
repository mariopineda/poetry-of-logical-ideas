## Question

Differentiate.

### A.

$$
y=x^3\cdot x^9
$$

### B.

$$
y=\frac{1}{x^7}
$$

### C.

$$
f(t)=\sqrt[6]{t^{11}}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> First simplify:
>
> $$
> y=x^{12}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{y'=12x^{11}}.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> $$
> y=x^{-7},
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> \boxed{y'=-7x^{-8}=-\frac{7}{x^8}}.
> $$
>
> ### C.
>
> $$
> f(t)=t^{11/6},
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> f'(t)=\frac{11}{6}t^{5/6}
> }.
> $$
