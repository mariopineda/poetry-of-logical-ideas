## Question

Differentiate

$$
y=(x-2)^3\sqrt{2x-1}.
$$

Answer in fully factored form.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Write
>
> $$
> y=(x-2)^3(2x-1)^{1/2}.
> $$
>
> Using the Product Rule and Chain Rule,
>
> $$
> y'
> =3(x-2)^2(2x-1)^{1/2}
> +(x-2)^3(2x-1)^{-1/2}.
> $$
>
> Factor:
>
> $$
> y'
> =\frac{(x-2)^2}{\sqrt{2x-1}}
> \left[3(2x-1)+(x-2)\right].
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> y'=\frac{(x-2)^2(7x-5)}{\sqrt{2x-1}}
> }.
> $$
