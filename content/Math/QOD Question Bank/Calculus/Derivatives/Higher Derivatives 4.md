## Question

Roughly sketch a function $y=f(x)$ satisfying each set of conditions. Answers will vary.

### A.

$$
f'(x)>0
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
f''(x)<0
$$

### B.

$$
f(x)<0,
\qquad
f'(x)<0,
\qquad
f''(x)>0
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Answers will vary. One valid example for each part is enough.
>
> ### A.
>
> Take
>
> $$
> f(x)=1-e^{-x}.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> f'(x)=e^{-x}>0
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> f''(x)=-e^{-x}<0.
> $$
>
> So the graph is increasing and concave down.
>
> ### B.
>
> For $x>0$, take
>
> $$
> f(x)=-\sqrt{x}.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> f(x)<0,
> $$
>
> $$
> f'(x)=-\frac{1}{2\sqrt{x}}<0,
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> f''(x)=\frac{1}{4x^{3/2}}>0.
> $$
>
> So on $x>0$, the graph is below the $x$-axis, decreasing, and concave up.
