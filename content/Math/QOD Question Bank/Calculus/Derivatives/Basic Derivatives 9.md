## Question

Determine the $x$-coordinates of the points on

$$
y=-\frac{2}{x}+4
$$

where the tangent line is parallel to

$$
x-8y+24=0.
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Rewrite the line in slope-intercept form:
>
> $$
> y=\frac18x+3.
> $$
>
> Its slope is
>
> $$
> m=\frac18.
> $$
>
> For the curve,
>
> $$
> y=-2x^{-1}+4,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> y'=\frac{2}{x^2}.
> $$
>
> Set the tangent slope equal to $\frac18$:
>
> $$
> \frac{2}{x^2}=\frac18.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> x^2=16
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=-4,\ 4}.
> $$
