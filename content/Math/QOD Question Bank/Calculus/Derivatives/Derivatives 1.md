## Question

Determine the derivative of $y=x^2-3x-8$ using first principles. Then, determine the tangent slope at $x=-4$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Let
>
> $$
> f(x)=x^2-3x-8.
> $$
>
> Using first principles,
>
> $$
> f'(x)
> =
> \lim_{h\to0}
> \frac{f(x+h)-f(x)}{h}.
> $$
>
> Now,
>
> $$
> f(x+h)
> =
> (x+h)^2-3(x+h)-8.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> f'(x)
> =
> \lim_{h\to0}
> \frac{
> x^2+2xh+h^2-3x-3h-8
> -
> (x^2-3x-8)
> }{h}.
> $$
>
> Simplify:
>
> $$
> f'(x)
> =
> \lim_{h\to0}
> \frac{2xh+h^2-3h}{h}.
> $$
>
> Factor and cancel $h$:
>
> $$
> f'(x)
> =
> \lim_{h\to0}
> (2x+h-3).
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \boxed{f'(x)=2x-3}.
> $$
>
> At $x=-4$,
>
> $$
> f'(-4)
> =
> 2(-4)-3
> =
> -11.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the tangent slope is
>
> $$
> \boxed{-11}.
> $$
