## Question

For the linear function

$$
y=\frac45x-9,
$$

if $y$ increases by $20$, what is the corresponding change in $x$?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> For a linear function,
>
> $$
> \Delta y
> =
> \frac45\Delta x.
> $$
>
> Given
>
> $$
> \Delta y=20,
> $$
>
> we have
>
> $$
> 20=\frac45\Delta x.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \Delta x
> =
> 20\left(\frac54\right)
> =
> \boxed{25}.
> $$
