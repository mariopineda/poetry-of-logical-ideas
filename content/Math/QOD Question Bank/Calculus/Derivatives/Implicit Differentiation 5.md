## Question

Find the slope of the tangent line to

$$
x^2-5y^2=-1
$$

at the point $(-2,-1)$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Differentiate implicitly:
>
> $$
> 2x-10yy'=0.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> y'=\frac{x}{5y}.
> $$
>
> At $(-2,-1)$,
>
> $$
> y'
> =
> \frac{-2}{5(-1)}
> =
> \boxed{\frac25}.
> $$
