## Question

Differentiate $y = \left[ x^3-7+(5x+1)^4 \right]^9$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Let
>
> $$
> u=x^3-7+(5x+1)^4.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> y=u^9,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> \frac{dy}{dx}
> =
> 9u^8\frac{du}{dx}.
> $$
>
> Now,
>
> $$
> \frac{du}{dx}
> =
> 3x^2+20(5x+1)^3.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> y'
> =
> 9\left[x^3-7+(5x+1)^4\right]^8
> \left[3x^2+20(5x+1)^3\right]
> }.
> $$
>
