## Question

If

$$
y=u^2-u
$$

and

$$
u=2x-7,
$$

evaluate

$$
\left.\frac{dy}{dx}\right|_{x=3}.
$$

Use Leibniz notation.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Using Leibniz notation,
>
> $$
> \frac{dy}{dx}
> =
> \frac{dy}{du}\frac{du}{dx}.
> $$
>
> Now,
>
> $$
> \frac{dy}{du}=2u-1
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> \frac{du}{dx}=2.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \frac{dy}{dx}
> =
> 2(2u-1).
> $$
>
> At $x=3$,
>
> $$
> u=2(3)-7=-1.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \left.\frac{dy}{dx}\right|_{x=3}
> =
> 2[2(-1)-1]
> =
> \boxed{-6}.
> $$
