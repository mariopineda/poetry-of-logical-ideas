## Question

Differentiate

$$
g(x)=(6x+5)^4(4x-7)^3
$$

using more than one rule. Fully factor the answer.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Use the Product Rule and Chain Rule:
>
> $$
> g'(x)
> =
> 24(6x+5)^3(4x-7)^3
> +
> 12(6x+5)^4(4x-7)^2.
> $$
>
> Factor out the common factor:
>
> $$
> g'(x)
> =
> 12(6x+5)^3(4x-7)^2
> \left[2(4x-7)+(6x+5)\right].
> $$
>
> Simplify the bracket:
>
> $$
> 2(4x-7)+(6x+5)=14x-9.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> g'(x)
> =
> 12(6x+5)^3(4x-7)^2(14x-9)
> }.
> $$
