## Question

For what real values of $x$ does

$$
y=4x^3-33x+20
$$

have a horizontal tangent?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> A horizontal tangent occurs when
>
> $$
> y'=0.
> $$
>
> Differentiate:
>
> $$
> y'=12x^2-33.
> $$
>
> Set the derivative equal to zero:
>
> $$
> 12x^2-33=0.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> 12x^2=33
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> x^2=\frac{11}{4}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=\pm\frac{\sqrt{11}}{2}}.
> $$
