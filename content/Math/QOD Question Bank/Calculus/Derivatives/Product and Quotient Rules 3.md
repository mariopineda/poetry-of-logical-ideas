## Question

Differentiate $f(t) = 2t^5 \times h(t)$, where $f$ and $h$ are differentiable functions.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Differentiate
>
> $$
> f(t)=2t^5h(t).
> $$
>
> Using the Product Rule,
>
> $$
> f'(t)
> =
> 10t^4h(t)
> +
> 2t^5h'(t).
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> f'(t)
> =
> 10t^4h(t)+2t^5h'(t)
> }.
> $$
>
> Equivalently,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> f'(t)
> =
> 2t^4\left[5h(t)+th'(t)\right]
> }.
> $$
