## Question

Determine the x-coordinates where the function $y=\frac{2}{x}-\frac{6}{x^3}$ has a horizontal tangent.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Rewrite the function as
>
> $$
> y=2x^{-1}-6x^{-3}.
> $$
>
> Differentiate:
>
> $$
> y'
> =
> -2x^{-2}+18x^{-4}.
> $$
>
> A horizontal tangent occurs when
>
> $$
> y'=0.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> -\frac{2}{x^2}+\frac{18}{x^4}=0.
> $$
>
> Multiply by $x^4$:
>
> $$
> -2x^2+18=0.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> x^2=9
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=-3,\ 3}.
> $$
