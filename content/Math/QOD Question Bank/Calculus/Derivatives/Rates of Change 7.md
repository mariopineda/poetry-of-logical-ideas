## Question

A line is parallel to

$$
4x-7y+11=0
$$

and passes through $C(-14,-6)$.

Determine its equation in general form.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Rewrite the given line in slope-intercept form:
>
> $$
> -7y=-4x-11
> $$
>
> $$
> y=\frac47x+\frac{11}{7}.
> $$
>
> Its slope is
>
> $$
> m=\frac47.
> $$
>
> A parallel line has the same slope. Using the point $(-14,-6)$,
>
> $$
> y+6=\frac47(x+14).
> $$
>
> Simplify:
>
> $$
> y=\frac47x+2.
> $$
>
> Multiply by $7$ and rearrange:
>
> $$
> \boxed{4x-7y+14=0}.
> $$
