## Question

Find $y'$ using implicit differentiation.

### A.

$$
y^2-5x=7-y+x^2
$$

### B.

$$
4y^3-9y=x^2+3xy-2
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> Differentiate both sides:
>
> $$
> 2yy'-5=-y'+2x.
> $$
>
> Collect the $y'$ terms:
>
> $$
> (2y+1)y'=2x+5.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> y'=\frac{2x+5}{2y+1}
> }.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> Differentiate both sides:
>
> $$
> (12y^2-9)y'
> =
> 2x+3(xy'+y).
> $$
>
> Expand:
>
> $$
> (12y^2-9)y'
> =
> 2x+3xy'+3y.
> $$
>
> Collect the $y'$ terms:
>
> $$
> (12y^2-3x-9)y'
> =
> 2x+3y.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> y'=
> \frac{2x+3y}
> {12y^2-3x-9}
> }.
> $$
