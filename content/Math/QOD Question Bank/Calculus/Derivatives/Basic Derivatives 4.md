## Question

Determine the equation, in general form, of the tangent line to $y=\sqrt[3]{x^2}-\frac{1}{\sqrt[3]{x}}$ at $x=-1$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Rewrite the function as
>
> $$
> y=x^{2/3}-x^{-1/3}.
> $$
>
> Differentiate:
>
> $$
> y'
> =
> \frac23x^{-1/3}
> +
> \frac13x^{-4/3}.
> $$
>
> At $x=-1$,
>
> $$
> m
> =
> \frac23(-1)^{-1/3}
> +
> \frac13(-1)^{-4/3}
> =
> -\frac23+\frac13
> =
> -\frac13.
> $$
>
> The point on the curve is
>
> $$
> y(-1)
> =
> \sqrt[3]{1}
> -
> \frac{1}{\sqrt[3]{-1}}
> =
> 1-(-1)
> =
> 2.
> $$
>
> So the tangent line is
>
> $$
> y-2
> =
> -\frac13(x+1).
> $$
>
> Multiply by $3$ and rearrange:
>
> $$
> 3y-6=-x-1.
> $$
>
> Therefore, in general form,
>
> $$
> \boxed{x+3y-5=0}.
> $$
