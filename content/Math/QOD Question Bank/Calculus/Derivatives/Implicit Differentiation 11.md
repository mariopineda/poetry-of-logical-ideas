## Question

Find the indicated rate of change. Assume all variables are functions of time.

### A.

For

$$
V=\pi r^2h,
$$

find

$$
\frac{dV}{dt}.
$$

### B.

For

$$
A=2\pi r^2+2\pi rh,
$$

find

$$
\frac{dA}{dt}.
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> Differentiate using the Product Rule:
>
> $$
> \frac{dV}{dt}
> =
> \pi\left(
> 2rh\frac{dr}{dt}
> +
> r^2\frac{dh}{dt}
> \right).
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> \frac{dV}{dt}
> =
> 2\pi rh\frac{dr}{dt}
> +
> \pi r^2\frac{dh}{dt}
> }.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> Differentiate term by term:
>
> $$
> \frac{dA}{dt}
> =
> 4\pi r\frac{dr}{dt}
> +
> 2\pi
> \left(
> h\frac{dr}{dt}
> +
> r\frac{dh}{dt}
> \right).
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> \frac{dA}{dt}
> =
> 4\pi r\frac{dr}{dt}
> +
> 2\pi h\frac{dr}{dt}
> +
> 2\pi r\frac{dh}{dt}
> }.
> $$
