## Question

Differentiate. Assume $f$ is differentiable.

### A.

$$
y=f(x^9)
$$

### B.

$$
y=\frac{f(ax)}{a},
\qquad a\in\mathbb R,\ a\neq0
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> Using the Chain Rule,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> y'=9x^8f'(x^9)
> }.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> Since $a$ is constant and $a\neq0$,
>
> $$
> y'
> =
> \frac{1}{a}f'(ax)\cdot a.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> y'=f'(ax)
> }.
> $$
