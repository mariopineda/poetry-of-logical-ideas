## Question

The position function of an object is $s(t)=t^3-3t^2$, where $t$ in seconds $s$ in metres, and North + and South -. When is it moving North and towards the origin at the same time.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The position function is
>
> $$
> s(t)=t^3-3t^2
> =
> t^2(t-3).
> $$
>
> For $t\geq0$, the object is South of the origin when
>
> $$
> s(t)<0,
> $$
>
> which occurs for
>
> $$
> 0<t<3.
> $$
>
> Differentiate:
>
> $$
> v(t)=3t^2-6t
> =
> 3t(t-2).
> $$
>
> The object is moving North when
>
> $$
> v(t)>0.
> $$
>
> For $t\geq0$, this occurs when
>
> $$
> t>2.
> $$
>
> To be moving North **toward** the origin, the object must be South of the origin while moving North.
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{2<t<3}.
> $$
