## Question

The position of an object is

$$
s(t)=5t^2-8t+3,
$$

where $s$ is measured in kilometres, $t$ is measured in hours, upward is positive, and downward is negative.

### A.

Determine the average velocity of the object during the first $3$ hours.

### B.

Determine the instantaneous velocity at $t=3$ h.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> The average velocity from $t=0$ to $t=3$ is
>
> $$
> v_{\text{avg}}
> =
> \frac{s(3)-s(0)}{3-0}.
> $$
>
> Now,
>
> $$
> s(3)=45-24+3=24
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> s(0)=3.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> v_{\text{avg}}
> =
> \frac{24-3}{3}
> =
> \boxed{7\text{ km/h}}.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> Differentiate:
>
> $$
> v(t)=s'(t)=10t-8.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> v(3)=30-8
> =
> \boxed{22\text{ km/h}}.
> $$
>
> Both velocities are positive, so the direction is upward.
