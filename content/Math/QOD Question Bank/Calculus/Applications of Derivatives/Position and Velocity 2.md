## Question

A rock is thrown upward at 35 m/s and at a height of 22 m. Its position function is $s(t) = 22+35t-4.7t^2$, where up + and down -. Determine its max height to nearest hundredth.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The position function is
>
> $$
> s(t)=22+35t-4.7t^2.
> $$
>
> The maximum height occurs when the velocity is zero.
>
> Differentiate:
>
> $$
> v(t)=s'(t)=35-9.4t.
> $$
>
> Set
>
> $$
> v(t)=0:
> $$
>
> $$
> 35-9.4t=0.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> t=\frac{35}{9.4}\approx3.7234\text{ s}.
> $$
>
> Now evaluate the position:
>
> $$
> s(3.7234)
> \approx87.1596.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the maximum height is
>
> $$
> \boxed{87.16\text{ m}}.
> $$
