## Question

A car is initially moving at $20\text{ m/s}$. Its brakes are applied and it begins to slow down. Its position is

$$
s(t)=20t-2t^2,
\qquad
0\leq t\leq5,
$$

where $s$ is measured in metres and $t$ in seconds.

### A.

Determine the braking distance from the instant the brakes are applied until the car comes to rest.

### B.

Determine the speed of the car after it has travelled $42$ m while braking.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Differentiate:
>
> $$
> v(t)=s'(t)=20-4t.
> $$
>
> ### A.
>
> The car comes to rest when
>
> $$
> 20-4t=0,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> t=5.
> $$
>
> Since $s(0)=0$,
>
> $$
> s(5)=100-50=50.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the braking distance is
>
> $$
> \boxed{50\text{ m}}.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> Set
>
> $$
> s(t)=42:
> $$
>
> $$
> 20t-2t^2=42.
> $$
>
> Rearranging,
>
> $$
> t^2-10t+21=0
> $$
>
> $$
> (t-3)(t-7)=0.
> $$
>
> The model has domain $0\leq t\leq5$, so
>
> $$
> t=3.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> v(3)=20-12=8.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the speed is
>
> $$
> \boxed{8\text{ m/s}}.
> $$
