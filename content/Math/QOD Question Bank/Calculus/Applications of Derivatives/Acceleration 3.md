## Question

The velocity of an object is

$$
v(t)=5t^2-4t-25,
$$

where $v$ is measured in m/s, East is positive, and West is negative.

### A.

Determine the object's velocity when its acceleration is $26\text{ m/s}^2$ East.

### B.

Is the object's velocity increasing or decreasing when $t=1$ s?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Differentiate the velocity function:
>
> $$
> a(t)=v'(t)=10t-4.
> $$
>
> ### A.
>
> Set the acceleration equal to $26$:
>
> $$
> 10t-4=26.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> t=3.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> v(3)=5(3)^2-4(3)-25=8.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the velocity is
>
> $$
> \boxed{8\text{ m/s East}}.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> At $t=1$,
>
> $$
> a(1)=10(1)-4=6>0.
> $$
>
> Therefore the velocity is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{increasing}}.
> $$
>
> At this instant $v(1)=-24\text{ m/s}$, so the object is moving West while its speed is decreasing.
