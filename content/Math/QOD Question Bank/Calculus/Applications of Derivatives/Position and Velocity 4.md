## Question

The position of an object is

$$
s(t)=t^2-6t+5,
$$

where $s$ is measured in metres, $t$ is measured in seconds, North is positive, and South is negative.

### A.

Determine the position of the object when it comes to rest.

### B.

Determine whether the object is moving toward or away from the origin at $t=2$ s.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Differentiate:
>
> $$
> v(t)=s'(t)=2t-6.
> $$
>
> ### A.
>
> The object is at rest when
>
> $$
> 2t-6=0,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> t=3.
> $$
>
> Its position is
>
> $$
> s(3)=9-18+5=-4.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{s=-4\text{ m}},
> $$
>
> which is $4$ m South of the origin.
>
> ### B.
>
> At $t=2$,
>
> $$
> s(2)=4-12+5=-3
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> v(2)=4-6=-2.
> $$
>
> Both position and velocity are negative, so the object is moving farther South from the origin.
>
> Therefore, it is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{moving away from the origin}}.
> $$
