## Question

A circular disk is heated and then cooled. During the cooling process, the radius is found to be decreasing at the rate of 0.02 mm/s. At what rate is the area of the disk changing when the radius of the disk is 100 mm?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The area of the disk is
>
> $$
> A=\pi r^2.
> $$
>
> Differentiate:
>
> $$
> \frac{dA}{dt}
> =
> 2\pi r\frac{dr}{dt}.
> $$
>
> During cooling,
>
> $$
> \frac{dr}{dt}=-0.02\text{ mm/s}.
> $$
>
> When
>
> $$
> r=100\text{ mm},
> $$
>
> $$
> \frac{dA}{dt}
> =
> 2\pi(100)(-0.02)
> =
> -4\pi.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> \frac{dA}{dt}
> =
> -4\pi\text{ mm}^2/\text{s}
> }.
> $$
>
> The negative sign means the area is decreasing.
