## Question

The position function of an object is $s(t) = 12t-3t^2$, where $t$ in seconds, $s$ in metres, and North +, South -.

#### A.

Determine the objects average velocity from $t=2$ s to $t=5$ s.

#### B.

Determine the objects velocity when it is located 63 m South of the origin.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The position function is
>
> $$
> s(t)=12t-3t^2.
> $$
>
> ### A.
>
> The average velocity from $t=2$ to $t=5$ is
>
> $$
> v_{\text{avg}}
> =
> \frac{s(5)-s(2)}{5-2}.
> $$
>
> Now,
>
> $$
> s(5)=60-75=-15
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> s(2)=24-12=12.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> v_{\text{avg}}
> =
> \frac{-15-12}{3}
> =
> \boxed{-9\text{ m/s}}.
> $$
>
> The negative sign means the average velocity is $9\text{ m/s}$ South.
>
> ### B.
>
> Being $63$ m South of the origin means
>
> $$
> s(t)=-63.
> $$
>
> So
>
> $$
> 12t-3t^2=-63.
> $$
>
> Rearrange:
>
> $$
> t^2-4t-21=0.
> $$
>
> Factor:
>
> $$
> (t-7)(t+3)=0.
> $$
>
> Since time is non-negative,
>
> $$
> t=7.
> $$
>
> The velocity is
>
> $$
> v(t)=s'(t)=12-6t.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> v(7)=12-42=-30.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{v=-30\text{ m/s}},
> $$
>
> or $30\text{ m/s}$ South.
