## Question

Find the area of the region bounded by the parabolas

$$
y=x^2
$$

and

$$
y=2x-x^2.
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Find the points of intersection:
>
> $$
> x^2=2x-x^2
> $$
>
> $$
> 2x^2-2x=0
> $$
>
> $$
> 2x(x-1)=0,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> x=0,\ 1.
> $$
>
> On $0\leq x\leq1$, the upper curve is $y=2x-x^2$ and the lower curve is $y=x^2$. Therefore,
>
> $$
> A=\int_0^1\left[(2x-x^2)-x^2\right]\,dx
> $$
>
> $$
> =\int_0^1(2x-2x^2)\,dx
> $$
>
> $$
> =\left[x^2-\frac{2}{3}x^3\right]_0^1
> $$
>
> $$
> =1-\frac{2}{3}
> =\boxed{\frac{1}{3}}.
> $$
>
> The bounded area is $\boxed{\frac13\text{ units}^2}$.
