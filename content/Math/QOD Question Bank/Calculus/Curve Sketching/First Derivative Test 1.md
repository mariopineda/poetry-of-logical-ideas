## Question

For the function

$$
f(x)=6x^2-x^3,
$$

identify the local maximum and local minimum. Use the First Derivative Test and make a rough sketch.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Differentiate:
>
> $$
> f'(x)=12x-3x^2=3x(4-x).
> $$
>
> The critical numbers are
>
> $$
> x=0,\ 4.
> $$
>
> Sign analysis gives:
>
> - $f'(x)<0$ on $(-\infty,0)$;
> - $f'(x)>0$ on $(0,4)$;
> - $f'(x)<0$ on $(4,\infty)$.
>
> Therefore, $x=0$ is a local minimum and $x=4$ is a local maximum.
>
> The corresponding function values are
>
> $$
> f(0)=0
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> f(4)=6(16)-64=32.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{local minimum }(0,0)}
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{local maximum }(4,32)}.
> $$
>
> Because the leading term is $-x^3$, the graph rises to the left and falls to the right.
