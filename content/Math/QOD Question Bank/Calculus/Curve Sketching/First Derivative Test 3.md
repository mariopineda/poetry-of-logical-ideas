## Question

The function

$$
f(x)=x^3+bx^2+cx+d
$$

has horizontal tangents at $x=-2$ and $x=5$, and it passes through $(4,10)$.

Determine $b$, $c$, and $d$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Differentiate:
>
> $$
> f'(x)=3x^2+2bx+c.
> $$
>
> A horizontal tangent at $x=-2$ gives
>
> $$
> f'(-2)=12-4b+c=0.
> $$
>
> A horizontal tangent at $x=5$ gives
>
> $$
> f'(5)=75+10b+c=0.
> $$
>
> Subtract the first equation from the second:
>
> $$
> 63+14b=0,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> b=-\frac92.
> $$
>
> Substitute into
>
> $$
> 12-4b+c=0:
> $$
>
> $$
> 12+18+c=0,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> c=-30.
> $$
>
> Since the curve passes through $(4,10)$,
>
> $$
> 10
> =
> 4^3
> -\frac92(4^2)
> -30(4)
> +d.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> 10=64-72-120+d
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> d=138.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> b=-\frac92,\qquad c=-30,\qquad d=138
> }.
> $$
