## Question

Solve $\frac{2x-1}{x} - \frac{x+1}{x+4} = \frac{5x+8}{x^2+4x}$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Restrictions:
>
> $$
> x\neq0,-4.
> $$
>
> Multiply
>
> $$
> \frac{2x-1}{x}
> -
> \frac{x+1}{x+4}
> =
> \frac{5x+8}{x(x+4)}
> $$
>
> by
>
> $$
> x(x+4).
> $$
>
> This gives
>
> $$
> (2x-1)(x+4)-x(x+1)=5x+8.
> $$
>
> Expand:
>
> $$
> x^2+6x-4=5x+8.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> x^2+x-12=0.
> $$
>
> Factor:
>
> $$
> (x+4)(x-3)=0.
> $$
>
> The candidates are
>
> $$
> x=-4,\ 3.
> $$
>
> But $x=-4$ is non-permissible.
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=3}.
> $$
