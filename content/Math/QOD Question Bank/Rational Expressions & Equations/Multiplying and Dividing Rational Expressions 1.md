## Question

Simplify and state the non-permissible value.

#### A.

$$
\frac{8x^2}{25} \times \frac{50}{2x^3}
$$

#### B.

$$
\frac{6x^3}{27x^4} \times \frac{18x}{15}
$$

#### C.

$$
\left( \frac{x}{24x^5} \right) (-14x^2)
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> The original denominator \(2x^3\) requires
>
> $$
> x\ne0.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> \frac{8x^2}{25}\times\frac{50}{2x^3}
> =
> \frac{400x^2}{50x^3}
> =
> \boxed{\frac{8}{x}},
> \qquad
> \boxed{x\ne0}.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> The original denominator \(27x^4\) requires
>
> $$
> x\ne0.
> $$
>
> Multiply and simplify:
>
> $$
> \frac{6x^3}{27x^4}\times\frac{18x}{15}
> =
> \frac{108x^4}{405x^4}
> =
> \boxed{\frac{4}{15}},
> \qquad
> \boxed{x\ne0}.
> $$
>
> ### C.
>
> The denominator \(24x^5\) requires
>
> $$
> x\ne0.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> \left(\frac{x}{24x^5}\right)(-14x^2)
> =
> \frac{-14x^3}{24x^5}
> =
> \boxed{-\frac{7}{12x^2}},
> \qquad
> \boxed{x\ne0}.
> $$
>
