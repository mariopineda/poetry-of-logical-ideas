## Question

Simplify and state the non-permissible value as restrictions on the variable

#### A.

$$
\left( \frac{2y^3-4y^2}{3y^2-9y} \right) \div \left( \frac{y^2-4}{(y+2)(y-3)} \right)
$$

#### B.

$$
\frac{4a^2-1}{4a^2-16} \div \frac{2a-1}{2-a}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> Factor:
>
> $$
> \frac{2y^3-4y^2}{3y^2-9y}
> \div
> \frac{y^2-4}{(y+2)(y-3)}.
> $$
>
> This becomes
>
> $$
> \frac{2y^2(y-2)}{3y(y-3)}
> \cdot
> \frac{(y+2)(y-3)}{(y-2)(y+2)}.
> $$
>
> Cancel common factors:
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{2y}{3}}.
> $$
>
> Restrictions come from all original denominators and from the divisor not being zero:
>
> $$
> \boxed{y\neq-2,\ 0,\ 2,\ 3}.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> Factor:
>
> $$
> \frac{4a^2-1}{4a^2-16}
> \div
> \frac{2a-1}{2-a}.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> \frac{(2a-1)(2a+1)}
> {4(a-2)(a+2)}
> \cdot
> \frac{2-a}{2a-1}.
> $$
>
> Since
>
> $$
> 2-a=-(a-2),
> $$
>
> the expression simplifies to
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> -\frac{2a+1}{4(a+2)}
> }.
> $$
>
> Restrictions:
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> a\neq-2,\ \frac12,\ 2
> }.
> $$
