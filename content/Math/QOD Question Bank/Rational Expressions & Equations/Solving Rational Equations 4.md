## Question

Solve the following equations.

#### A.

$$
\frac{4}{x+2} = 3
$$

#### B.

$$
\frac{5a-3}{a+7} = \frac{5a-14}{a+1}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> $$
> \frac{4}{x+2}=3,
> \qquad x\neq-2.
> $$
>
> Multiply by $x+2$:
>
> $$
> 4=3x+6.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> 3x=-2
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=-\frac23}.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> $$
> \frac{5a-3}{a+7}
> =
> \frac{5a-14}{a+1},
> \qquad
> a\neq-7,-1.
> $$
>
> Cross-multiply:
>
> $$
> (5a-3)(a+1)
> =
> (5a-14)(a+7).
> $$
>
> Expand:
>
> $$
> 5a^2+2a-3
> =
> 5a^2+21a-98.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> 95=19a
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> \boxed{a=5}.
> $$
