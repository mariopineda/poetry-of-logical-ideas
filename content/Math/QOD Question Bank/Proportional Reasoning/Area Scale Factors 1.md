## Question

Complete the table for the following rectangles.

|Original dimensions of figure (cm) | New dimensions of figure (cm) | LSF | ASF | 
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|$3 \times 5$ | $6 \times 10$     |     |     |
|$4\times 9$ | $12\times 27$ | | |
|$14\times 10$ | $21\times 15$ | | |
|$9 \times 6$ | $26 \times 24$ | | |

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> For similar figures,
>
> $$
> \text{ASF}=(\text{LSF})^2.
> $$
>
> 1. From $3\times5$ to $6\times10$:
>
> $$
> \text{LSF}=2,
> \qquad
> \text{ASF}=4.
> $$
>
> 2. From $4\times9$ to $12\times27$:
>
> $$
> \text{LSF}=3,
> \qquad
> \text{ASF}=9.
> $$
>
> 3. From $14\times10$ to $21\times15$:
>
> $$
> \text{LSF}=\frac{21}{14}=\frac32,
> \qquad
> \text{ASF}=\left(\frac32\right)^2=\frac94.
> $$
>
> 4. From $9\times6$ to $26\times24$:
>
> $$
> \frac{26}{9}\neq\frac{24}{6}=4.
> $$
>
> The two dimensions do not have the same linear scale factor, so these rectangles are **not similar**. Therefore there is no single LSF or ASF for this row.
