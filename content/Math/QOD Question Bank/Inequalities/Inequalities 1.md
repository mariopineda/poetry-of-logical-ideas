## Question

If set $R=\{x|x<7, x \in \mathbb{R}\}$ and set $T=\{x|x \geq -4, x \in \mathbb{R}\}$

1. $R \cap T$?
2. $R \cup T$?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The sets are
>
> $$
> R=(-\infty,7)
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> T=[-4,\infty).
> $$
>
> ### 1. Intersection
>
> The numbers common to both sets satisfy
>
> $$
> -4\leq x<7.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{R\cap T=[-4,7)}.
> $$
>
> ### 2. Union
>
> Together the two sets cover all real numbers:
>
> $$
> \boxed{R\cup T=(-\infty,\infty)=\mathbb R}.
> $$
