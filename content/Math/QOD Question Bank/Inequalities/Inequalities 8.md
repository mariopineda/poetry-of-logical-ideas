## Question

Solve the following inequalities, represent the solutions in set notation, interval notation and by graphing.

#### A.

$$
(x+1)(x-2)(x-3) < 0
$$

#### B.

$$
(x+1)(x-2)(x-3) > 0
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The critical values are
>
> $$
> x=-1,\ 2,\ 3.
> $$
>
> The sign pattern of
>
> $$
> (x+1)(x-2)(x-3)
> $$
>
> is
>
> $$
> -,\ +,\ -,\ +
> $$
>
> on the intervals
>
> $$
> (-\infty,-1),\quad(-1,2),\quad(2,3),\quad(3,\infty).
> $$
>
> ### A.
>
> For
>
> $$
> (x+1)(x-2)(x-3)<0,
> $$
>
> the solution is
>
> $$
> \boxed{(-\infty,-1)\cup(2,3)}.
> $$
>
> Set notation:
>
> $$
> \boxed{\{x\in\mathbb R\mid x<-1\text{ or }2<x<3\}}.
> $$
>
> On a number line, use open circles at $-1$, $2$, and $3$, shading left of $-1$ and between $2$ and $3$.
>
> ### B.
>
> For
>
> $$
> (x+1)(x-2)(x-3)>0,
> $$
>
> the solution is
>
> $$
> \boxed{(-1,2)\cup(3,\infty)}.
> $$
>
> Set notation:
>
> $$
> \boxed{\{x\in\mathbb R\mid -1<x<2\text{ or }x>3\}}.
> $$
>
> On a number line, use open circles at $-1$, $2$, and $3$, shading between $-1$ and $2$ and to the right of $3$.
