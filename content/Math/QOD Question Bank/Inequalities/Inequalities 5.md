## Question

Solve $\frac{x^2}{x-5} \leq \frac{16x}{x-5}$. Answer in interval notation.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Move everything to one side:
>
> $$
> \frac{x^2}{x-5}
> -
> \frac{16x}{x-5}
> \leq0.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> \frac{x(x-16)}{x-5}\leq0.
> $$
>
> The critical numbers are
>
> $$
> 0,\ 5,\ 16.
> $$
>
> Note that
>
> $$
> x=5
> $$
>
> is excluded because the original expression is undefined there.
>
> A sign analysis gives:
>
> - negative on $(-\infty,0)$;
> - positive on $(0,5)$;
> - negative on $(5,16)$;
> - positive on $(16,\infty)$.
>
> The zeros $0$ and $16$ are included.
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{(-\infty,0]\cup(5,16]}.
> $$
>
