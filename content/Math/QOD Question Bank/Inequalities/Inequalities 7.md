## Question

Solve $x \leq \frac{12}{x+1}$. Answer in interval notation.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Start with
>
> $$
> x\leq\frac{12}{x+1},
> \qquad x\neq-1.
> $$
>
> Move everything to one side:
>
> $$
> x-\frac{12}{x+1}\leq0.
> $$
>
> Combine:
>
> $$
> \frac{x(x+1)-12}{x+1}\leq0.
> $$
>
> Factor:
>
> $$
> \frac{(x+4)(x-3)}{x+1}\leq0.
> $$
>
> The critical numbers are
>
> $$
> -4,\ -1,\ 3.
> $$
>
> A sign analysis gives:
>
> - negative on $(-\infty,-4)$;
> - positive on $(-4,-1)$;
> - negative on $(-1,3)$;
> - positive on $(3,\infty)$.
>
> The zeros $-4$ and $3$ are included, while $-1$ is excluded.
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{(-\infty,-4]\cup(-1,3]}.
> $$
