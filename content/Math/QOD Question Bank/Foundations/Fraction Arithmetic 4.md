## Question

Evaluate without a calculator by, when possible, reducing the fractions to lowest terms before multiplying.

1.

$$
\frac{10}{12}\times\frac{9}{18}
$$

2.

$$
\frac{3}{20}\times\frac{8}{12}
$$

3.

$$
\frac{2}{4}\times\frac{5}{10}
$$

4.

$$
\frac{4}{18}\times\frac{2}{12}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> 1.
>
> $$
> \frac{10}{12}\times\frac{9}{18}
> =
> \frac56\times\frac12
> =
> \boxed{\frac{5}{12}}
> $$
>
> 2.
>
> $$
> \frac{3}{20}\times\frac{8}{12}
> =
> \frac{3}{20}\times\frac23
> =
> \boxed{\frac{1}{10}}
> $$
>
> 3.
>
> $$
> \frac24\times\frac{5}{10}
> =
> \frac12\times\frac12
> =
> \boxed{\frac14}
> $$
>
> 4.
>
> $$
> \frac4{18}\times\frac2{12}
> =
> \frac29\times\frac16
> =
> \frac2{54}
> =
> \boxed{\frac1{27}}
> $$
