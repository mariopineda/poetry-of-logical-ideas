## Question

How much shorter is it to diagonally cross a rectangular field than around the sides if the field is 400 m long and 120 m wide? Round to the nearest tenth.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The distance around two sides of the rectangular field is
>
> $$
> 400+120=520\text{ m}.
> $$
>
> The diagonal distance is
>
> $$
> d=\sqrt{400^2+120^2}
> \approx417.61\text{ m}.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the diagonal route is shorter by
>
> $$
> 520-417.61
> \approx102.39\text{ m}.
> $$
>
> To the nearest tenth,
>
> $$
> \boxed{102.4\text{ m}}.
> $$
