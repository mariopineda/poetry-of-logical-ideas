## Question

Find an equation of a **sine function** with amplitude $3$, period $\frac{\pi}{2}$, phase shift $5$ units right, and vertical displacement $1$ unit down.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Use
>
> $$
> y=a\sin(b(x-c))+d.
> $$
>
> The amplitude gives $a=3$.
>
> The period satisfies
>
> $$
> \frac{2\pi}{|b|}=\frac{\pi}{2},
> $$
>
> so $b=4$.
>
> The phase shift gives $c=5$, and the vertical displacement gives $d=-1$.
>
> Therefore, one valid equation is
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> y=3\sin\left(4(x-5)\right)-1
> }.
> $$
