## Question

State the following for the function $f(x)=\sin{x}$ defined on the set of real numbers.

1. Domain
2. Range
3. Amplitude
4. Period
5. Maximum value
6. Minimum value

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> For
>
> $$
> f(x)=\sin x,
> $$
>
> the characteristics are:
>
> 1. **Domain**
>
> $$
> \boxed{\mathbb R}
> $$
>
> 2. **Range**
>
> $$
> \boxed{[-1,1]}
> $$
>
> 3. **Amplitude**
>
> $$
> \boxed{1}
> $$
>
> 4. **Period**
>
> $$
> \boxed{2\pi}
> $$
>
> 5. **Maximum value**
>
> $$
> \boxed{1}
> $$
>
> 6. **Minimum value**
>
> $$
> \boxed{-1}
> $$
>
