## Question

Given

$$
y=4\cos\left(2x+\frac{\pi}{2}\right),
$$

1. express the function in the form $y=a\cos(b(x-c))+d$;
2. describe the transformations from $y=\cos x$;
3. compare amplitude, vertical displacement, range, phase shift, period, and domain.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Since
>
> $$
> 2x+\frac{\pi}{2}
> =
> 2\left(x+\frac{\pi}{4}\right),
> $$
>
> the function is
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> y=4\cos\left(2\left(x+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)\right)
> }.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> a=4,\quad b=2,\quad c=-\frac{\pi}{4},\quad d=0.
> $$
>
> Transformations:
>
> - vertical stretch by $4$;
> - horizontal compression by $\frac12$;
> - shift $\frac{\pi}{4}$ left;
> - no vertical displacement.
>
> | Characteristic | $y=\cos x$ | Transformed function |
> |---|---:|---:|
> | Amplitude | $1$ | $4$ |
> | Vertical displacement | $0$ | $0$ |
> | Range | $[-1,1]$ | $[-4,4]$ |
> | Phase shift | $0$ | $-\frac{\pi}{4}$ |
> | Period | $2\pi$ | $\pi$ |
> | Domain | $\mathbb R$ | $\mathbb R$ |
