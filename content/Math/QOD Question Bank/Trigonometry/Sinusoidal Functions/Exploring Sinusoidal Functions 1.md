## Question

Sketch the graphs of $y=\sin x$ and $y=\cos x$, for $-\pi \leq x \leq 2\pi$. Determine:

1. the value of $\cos x$ when $\sin x$ has a maximum value.
2. the value of $\cos x$ when $\sin x$ has a minimum value.
3. the value of $\cos x$ when $\sin x$ has a value of zero.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> For
>
> $$
> -\pi\leq x\leq2\pi,
> $$
>
> compare the standard sine and cosine graphs.
>
> ### 1.
>
> $\sin x$ has its maximum value $1$ at
>
> $$
> x=\frac{\pi}{2}.
> $$
>
> At this point,
>
> $$
> \cos\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right)=\boxed{0}.
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> $\sin x$ has its minimum value $-1$ at
>
> $$
> x=-\frac{\pi}{2}
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> x=\frac{3\pi}{2}.
> $$
>
> At both points,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\cos x=0}.
> $$
>
> ### 3.
>
> $\sin x=0$ at
>
> $$
> x=-\pi,\ 0,\ \pi,\ 2\pi.
> $$
>
> The corresponding cosine values are
>
> $$
> -1,\ 1,\ -1,\ 1.
> $$
>
> Therefore, whenever $\sin x=0$,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\cos x=\pm1}.
> $$
