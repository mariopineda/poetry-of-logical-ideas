## Question

Consider the periodic function $f(x) = 15.3 \sin (\pi x)$

1. Explain how to determine a graphing window that will show at least three complete periods of $f(x)$, starting from $x=0$.
2. Graph three complete cycles using your graphing calculator and state the window settings used.
3. Determine, to the nearest tenth, the value of the function when $x=4.5$.
4. Determine, to the nearest hundreth, two values of $x$ between 3 and 4 for which $f(x)=-8.5.$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> For
>
> $$
> f(x)=15.3\sin(\pi x),
> $$
>
> the amplitude is
>
> $$
> 15.3
> $$
>
> and the period is
>
> $$
> \frac{2\pi}{\pi}=2.
> $$
>
> ### 1.
>
> Three complete periods require an interval of length
>
> $$
> 3(2)=6.
> $$
>
> Starting from $x=0$, a suitable horizontal window is therefore
>
> $$
> \boxed{0\leq x\leq6}.
> $$
>
> Since the amplitude is $15.3$, a suitable vertical window is, for example,
>
> $$
> \boxed{-16\leq y\leq16}.
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> Using the window above, the graph displays three complete cycles on
>
> $$
> 0\leq x\leq6.
> $$
>
> ### 3.
>
> $$
> f(4.5)
> =
> 15.3\sin(4.5\pi)
> =
> 15.3.
> $$
>
> Therefore, to the nearest tenth,
>
> $$
> \boxed{15.3}.
> $$
>
> ### 4.
>
> Solve
>
> $$
> 15.3\sin(\pi x)=-8.5.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> \sin(\pi x)
> =
> -\frac{8.5}{15.3}
> =
> -\frac59.
> $$
>
> For $3<x<4$, the two solutions are approximately
>
> $$
> x\approx3.1875
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> x\approx3.8125.
> $$
>
> Therefore, to the nearest hundredth,
>
> $$
> \boxed{x\approx3.19,\ 3.81}.
> $$
