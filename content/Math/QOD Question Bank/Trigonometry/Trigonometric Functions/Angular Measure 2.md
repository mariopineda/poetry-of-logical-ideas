## Question

### A.

Identify all angles coterminal with $\frac{4\pi}{3}$ in general form.

### B.

Identify the angles coterminal with $\frac{4\pi}{3}$ that satisfy

$$
-4\pi \leq \theta \leq 4\pi.
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> Coterminal angles differ by integer multiples of $2\pi$:
>
> $$
> \boxed{\theta=\frac{4\pi}{3}+2\pi n,\qquad n\in\mathbb Z}.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> Solve
>
> $$
> -4\pi\leq \frac{4\pi}{3}+2\pi n\leq4\pi.
> $$
>
> This gives
>
> $$
> -\frac{8}{3}\leq n\leq\frac{4}{3},
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> n=-2,-1,0,1.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{-\frac{8\pi}{3},\ -\frac{2\pi}{3},\ \frac{4\pi}{3},\ \frac{10\pi}{3}}.
> $$
