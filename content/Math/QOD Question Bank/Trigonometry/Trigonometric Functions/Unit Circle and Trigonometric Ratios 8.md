## Question

Using the unit circle, determine the exact values of

$$
\sin\frac{2\pi}{3},\qquad
\cos\frac{7\pi}{6},\qquad
\csc\frac{11\pi}{6},\qquad
\tan\frac{4\pi}{3}.
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> $$
> \boxed{\sin\frac{2\pi}{3}=\frac{\sqrt3}{2}},
> $$
>
> $$
> \boxed{\cos\frac{7\pi}{6}=-\frac{\sqrt3}{2}},
> $$
>
> $$
> \boxed{\csc\frac{11\pi}{6}=-2},
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> \boxed{\tan\frac{4\pi}{3}=\sqrt3}.
> $$
