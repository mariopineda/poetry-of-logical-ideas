## Question

Sketch the unit circle indicating the reference angles 30$^\circ$, 45$^\circ$ and 60$^\circ$ in each quadrant. Label each angle in both degrees and in radians.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The required angles are obtained by placing the reference angles
>
> $$
> 30^\circ,\quad45^\circ,\quad60^\circ
> $$
>
> in each quadrant.
>
> | Reference angle | Quadrant I | Quadrant II | Quadrant III | Quadrant IV |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | $30^\circ$ | $30^\circ=\frac{\pi}{6}$ | $150^\circ=\frac{5\pi}{6}$ | $210^\circ=\frac{7\pi}{6}$ | $330^\circ=\frac{11\pi}{6}$ |
> | $45^\circ$ | $45^\circ=\frac{\pi}{4}$ | $135^\circ=\frac{3\pi}{4}$ | $225^\circ=\frac{5\pi}{4}$ | $315^\circ=\frac{7\pi}{4}$ |
> | $60^\circ$ | $60^\circ=\frac{\pi}{3}$ | $120^\circ=\frac{2\pi}{3}$ | $240^\circ=\frac{4\pi}{3}$ | $300^\circ=\frac{5\pi}{3}$ |
>
> On the sketch, draw rays from the origin through each of these twelve angles and label each ray in both degrees and radians.
