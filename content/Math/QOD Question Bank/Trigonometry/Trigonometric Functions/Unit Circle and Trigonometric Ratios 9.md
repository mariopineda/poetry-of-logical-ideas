## Question

Use technology to find each approximate value. Round to the nearest ten-thousandth.

1.
$$
\cos\frac{7\pi}{5}
$$

2.
$$
\csc(173^\circ).
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> $$
> \cos\frac{7\pi}{5}\approx-0.30901699,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> \boxed{-0.3090}.
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> $$
> \csc(173^\circ)
> =
> \frac{1}{\sin(173^\circ)}
> \approx8.20550905,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> \boxed{8.2055}.
> $$
