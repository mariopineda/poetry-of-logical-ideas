## Question

A ball is dropped from a height of 23 cm. After each bounce, it rises to only 78% of its previous height. Determine the total distance the ball travels until it comes to rest.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The ball first travels
>
> $$
> 23\text{ cm}
> $$
>
> downward.
>
> After that, each bounce height is $78\%$ of the previous height.
>
> The bounce heights form the infinite geometric series
>
> $$
> 23(0.78)+23(0.78)^2+\cdots.
> $$
>
> Its first term is
>
> $$
> a=23(0.78)
> $$
>
> and its ratio is
>
> $$
> r=0.78.
> $$
>
> The sum of all bounce heights is
>
> $$
> \frac{23(0.78)}{1-0.78}.
> $$
>
> Each bounce height is travelled twice: once upward and once downward.
>
> Therefore, the total distance is
>
> $$
> 23
> +
> 2\left(
> \frac{23(0.78)}{1-0.78}
> \right).
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \boxed{186.09\text{ cm}}
> $$
>
> approximately.
