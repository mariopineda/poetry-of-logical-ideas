## Question

Evaluate, if possible.

#### A.

$$
\lim_{n \to \infty} \left( -\frac{7}{8} \right)^n
$$

#### B.

$$
\lim_{n \to \infty} \left( \frac{5}{3} \right)
$$

#### C.

$$
\lim_{n \to \infty} \left( \frac{100}{n^3} \right)
$$

#### D.

$$
\lim_{n \to \infty} \left( \frac{7}{6} \right)^n
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### A.
>
> $$
> \lim_{n\to\infty}
> \left(-\frac78\right)^n
> $$
>
> Since
>
> $$
> \left|-\frac78\right|<1,
> $$
>
> the powers approach zero:
>
> $$
> \boxed{0}.
> $$
>
> ### B.
>
> The sequence is constant:
>
> $$
> \lim_{n\to\infty}\frac53
> =
> \boxed{\frac53}.
> $$
>
> ### C.
>
> $$
> \lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{100}{n^3}
> =
> \boxed{0}.
> $$
>
> ### D.
>
> Since
>
> $$
> \frac76>1,
> $$
>
> we have
>
> $$
> \left(\frac76\right)^n\to\infty.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\infty}.
> $$
