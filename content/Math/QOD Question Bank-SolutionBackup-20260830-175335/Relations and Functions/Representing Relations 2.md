## Question

Classify the following as representing discrete or continuous data.
> [!info]- Hint
> Discrete data can only take certain values. For example, the number of students in a class (you cannot have half a student) or the results of rolling a dice (only the values 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6 are possible). Continuous data can take any value (within a range). For example, a person's height could be any value (within the range of human heights), not just certain fixed heights or the time in a race can be measured to fractions of a second.

1. The farmers' market sells fresh tomatoes for $1.29 per kilogram.
2. Rachel is saving $10/month to purchase a new watch.
3. The formula $F=1.8C+32$ compares the temperature in degrees Celcius ($C$) to temperatures in degrees Fahrenheit ($F$).
