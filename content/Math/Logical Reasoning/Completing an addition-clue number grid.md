Fill in the unknown digits in this addition-clue grid. Each row and column contains distinct digits from $1$ to $9$. The totals at the right and bottom are fixed. The diagonal from the upper-left to the lower-right also totals $16$.

![[batch09-number-grid.svg]]

Determine $A$, $B$, $C$, and $D$ and verify each row, column, and the indicated diagonal. Explain the order in which you used the clues.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The middle column gives $5+C+4=15$, so $\boxed{C=6}$.
>
> The marked diagonal gives $A+C+6=16$, so $A+6+6=16$ and $\boxed{A=4}$.
>
> The top row gives $A+5+B=17$, so $4+5+B=17$ and $\boxed{B=8}$.
>
> The bottom row gives $D+4+6=13$, so $\boxed{D=3}$.
>
> Thus $\boxed{(A,B,C,D)=(4,8,6,3)}$. Verify rows: $4+5+8=17$, $7+6+2=15$, $3+4+6=13$; columns: $4+7+3=14$, $5+6+4=15$, $8+2+6=16$; diagonal: $4+6+6=16$. No row or column repeats a digit.
