Two nonempty sets $S$ and $T$ represent students registered for different workshops. Suppose $S$ and $T$ are disjoint. State the value of $S\cap T$ and justify why neither $S\subseteq T$ nor $T\subseteq S$ is possible.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Disjointness means there is no element in both sets, so $\boxed{S\cap T=\varnothing}$. If $S\subseteq T$ while $S$ is nonempty, any element of $S$ would also belong to $T$, contradicting disjointness. The same argument applies with $S$ and $T$ interchanged.
