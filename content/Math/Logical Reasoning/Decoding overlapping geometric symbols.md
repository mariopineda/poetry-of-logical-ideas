An exhibition labels arrangements of shapes using three-letter codes. The five labelled examples are shown below.

![[diploma-batch08-2016-mc2-shape-code-evidence.svg]]

Determine **which letter** means that the shapes **overlap**. Give a comparison that isolates this characteristic from the shape type and the number of shapes.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Compare **PXQ** and **PXZ**. Both use two triangles, but the second picture overlaps and the first does not. Their codes differ only in the final letter: $Q$ versus $Z$.
>
> To verify the conclusion is not specific to triangles, compare **RXQ** and **RXZ**. Both use two circles and differ only by the overlapping arrangement. Again the overlapping drawing uses **Z**.
>
> Therefore the uniquely identified overlap symbol is $\boxed{Z}$. The remaining symbols encode shape type ($P,R$) and number ($X,Y$).
