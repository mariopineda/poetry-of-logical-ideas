Convert the following unit as indicated using unit analysis. Round answers to the nearest tenth when applicable.

1.

$$
1 \mbox{ yd} \Rightarrow \mbox{m}
$$
2.

$$
180 \mbox{ cm} \Rightarrow \mbox{in}
$$
3.

$$
3 \mbox{ mi} \Rightarrow \mbox{km}
$$
4.

$$
6 \mbox{ m} \Rightarrow \mbox{ft}
$$
5.

$$
41.2 \mbox{ km} \Rightarrow \mbox{mi}
$$
6.

$$
7 \mbox{ ft} 2 \mbox{ in} \Rightarrow \mbox{m}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Use
>
> $$
> 1\text{ yd}=0.9144\text{ m},
> \qquad
> 1\text{ in}=2.54\text{ cm},
> \qquad
> 1\text{ mi}=1.609344\text{ km}.
> $$
>
> ### 1.
>
> $$
> 1\text{ yd}=0.9144\text{ m}\approx\boxed{0.9\text{ m}}
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> $$
> 180\text{ cm}
> \left(\frac{1\text{ in}}{2.54\text{ cm}}\right)
> \approx
> \boxed{70.9\text{ in}}
> $$
>
> ### 3.
>
> $$
> 3\text{ mi}
> \left(\frac{1.609344\text{ km}}{1\text{ mi}}\right)
> \approx
> \boxed{4.8\text{ km}}
> $$
>
> ### 4.
>
> $$
> 6\text{ m}
> \left(\frac{1\text{ ft}}{0.3048\text{ m}}\right)
> \approx
> \boxed{19.7\text{ ft}}
> $$
>
> ### 5.
>
> $$
> 41.2\text{ km}
> \left(\frac{1\text{ mi}}{1.609344\text{ km}}\right)
> \approx
> \boxed{25.6\text{ mi}}
> $$
>
> ### 6.
>
> First convert to inches:
>
> $$
> 7\text{ ft}\,2\text{ in}
> =
> 86\text{ in}.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> 86\text{ in}
> \left(\frac{0.0254\text{ m}}{1\text{ in}}\right)
> =
> 2.1844\text{ m}
> \approx
> \boxed{2.2\text{ m}}.
> $$
