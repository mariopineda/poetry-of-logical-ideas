A bicycle builder offers $5$ frame designs, $3$ handlebar styles, $2$ brake systems, and $4$ paint finishes. A customer chooses one of each. How many unique combinations are possible? Identify the counting principle and explain why you multiply.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The choices are independent categories and each finished bicycle uses exactly one item from each. By the **fundamental counting principle**,
>
> $$5\times3\times2\times4=\boxed{120}.$$
>
> There are $120$ distinct combinations; the order in which the customer makes the selections is irrelevant because the categories themselves differ.
