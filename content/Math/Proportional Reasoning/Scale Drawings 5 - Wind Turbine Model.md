A small wind-turbine model has a **total height of 24 cm**, measured **from the base to the tip of the highest rotor blade** at a scale of **1:125**. What is the corresponding total height of the full-size turbine, in metres?

![Question diagram](/assets/scale-05-question.svg)

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> **Step 1 — Identify the measured dimension.** The $24$ cm is the **total height from the ground to the tip of the highest blade**, not just the tower height. Therefore, the answer must describe that same tip-to-base measurement.
>
> **Step 2 — Interpret the scale.** At $1:125$, every $1$ cm of model height corresponds to $125$ cm of actual height.
>
> **Step 3 — Set up the proportion and solve.** Let $H$ be the actual tip-to-base height in centimetres.
>
> $$
> \frac{1\text{ cm}}{125\text{ cm}}=\frac{24\text{ cm}}{H}
> $$
>
> $$
> H=24\times125\text{ cm}=3000\text{ cm}
> $$
>
> **Step 4 — Convert to metres.**
>
> $$
> 3000\text{ cm}\times\frac{1\text{ m}}{100\text{ cm}}=\boxed{30\text{ m}}
> $$
>
> **Check:** $30$ m is $3000$ cm; dividing by $125$ returns the model height of $24$ cm.
>
