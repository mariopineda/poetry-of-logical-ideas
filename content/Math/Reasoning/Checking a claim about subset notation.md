A community centre surveyed $36$ members about whether they attend the ceramics club ($C$) and the photography club ($P$). The partially completed Venn diagram shows the numbers attending only ceramics, both clubs, and only photography.

![[batch10-two-club-venn.svg]]

Determine **which statements are true**, and explain why the others are false:

1. $C\subseteq P'$.
2. $|C\cup P|=28$.
3. $|C\cap P|=19$.
4. $|(C\cup P)'|=8$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The union has $9+7+12=28$ members, the intersection has $7$, and the number in neither club is $36-28=8$.
>
> 1. **False.** Seven members attend both clubs, so $C$ is not a subset of the complement of $P$.
> 2. **True.** $|C\cup P|=9+7+12=28$.
> 3. **False.** $|C\cap P|=7$, not $19$.
> 4. **True.** $|(C\cup P)'|=36-28=8$.
>
> The true statements are $\boxed{2\text{ and }4}$.
