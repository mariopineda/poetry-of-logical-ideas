A survey of visitors records interest in **wildlife**, **landscape**, and **portrait** photography. The eight mutually exclusive Venn regions are numbered in the accompanying diagram.

![[diploma-apr2019-venn.svg]]

Identify the numbered region representing each group:

(a) Visitors interested in portrait photography **only**.

(b) Visitors interested in **all three** photography categories.

(c) Visitors interested in wildlife **and** landscape, **but not** portrait photography.

(d) The **complement of the union** of the three interests. Explain why this final group lies outside all three circles.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Reading the diagram gives (a) $\boxed{7}$; (b) $\boxed{5}$; (c) $\boxed{2}$; (d) $\boxed{8}$. The complement of the union consists of people with **none** of the three interests, so they lie inside the universe rectangle but outside every circle.
