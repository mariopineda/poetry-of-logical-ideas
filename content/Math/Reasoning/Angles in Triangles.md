In triangle $ABC$,

$$
\angle A=2x+10^\circ
$$

and

$$
\angle B=3x-5^\circ.
$$

The exterior angle at vertex $C$ is

$$
120^\circ.
$$

Determine $x$ and all three interior angles of the triangle.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> By the Exterior Angle Theorem,
>
> $$
> \angle A+\angle B=120^\circ.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> (2x+10)+(3x-5)=120.
> $$
>
> Simplify:
>
> $$
> 5x+5=120
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> 5x=115
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=23}.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> \angle A=2(23)+10=56^\circ
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> \angle B=3(23)-5=64^\circ.
> $$
>
> The interior angle at $C$ is supplementary to the exterior angle:
>
> $$
> \angle C=180^\circ-120^\circ=60^\circ.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\angle A=56^\circ,\quad\angle B=64^\circ,\quad\angle C=60^\circ}.
> $$
