A nature reserve has the following sets of observations:

$$A=\{\text{elk, moose, deer, fox}\},$$
$$B=\{\text{lynx, wolf, otter, bear}\},$$
$$C=\{\text{moose, wolf, deer, hare}\}.$$

Find **all pairs of sets** that are disjoint. Write all three pairwise intersections in set notation and justify your answer.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The pairwise intersections are
>
> $$A\cap B=\boxed{\varnothing},\qquad A\cap C=\{\text{moose, deer}\},\qquad B\cap C=\{\text{wolf}\}.$$
>
> Only $A$ and $B$ are disjoint because they have no common element.
