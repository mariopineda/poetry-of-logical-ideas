Explain what it means for two nonempty sets $A$ and $B$ to be **disjoint**. Write a set equation describing their intersection. Then explain whether two nonempty disjoint sets can satisfy $A\subseteq B$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Disjoint sets have no elements in common, so $A\cap B=\varnothing$. If $A\subseteq B$, every element of $A$ would also belong to $B$. Because $A$ is nonempty, that would make $A\cap B=A\ne\varnothing$, contradicting disjointness. Therefore two **nonempty** disjoint sets cannot satisfy $A\subseteq B$.
