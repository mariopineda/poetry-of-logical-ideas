Consider the sequence

$$
1,\ 3,\ 6,\ 10,\ 15,\ldots
$$

1. Use inductive reasoning to predict the next three terms.
2. Conjecture a formula for the $n$th term.
3. Determine the $20$th term.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The successive differences are
>
> $$
> 2,\ 3,\ 4,\ 5,\ldots
> $$
>
> so the next differences are
>
> $$
> 6,\ 7,\ 8.
> $$
>
> Therefore the next three terms are
>
> $$
> 15+6=21,
> $$
>
> $$
> 21+7=28,
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> 28+8=36.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \boxed{21,\ 28,\ 36}.
> $$
>
> These are triangular numbers, with
>
> $$
> \boxed{a_n=\frac{n(n+1)}{2}}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> a_{20}
> =
> \frac{20(21)}{2}
> =
> \boxed{210}.
> $$
