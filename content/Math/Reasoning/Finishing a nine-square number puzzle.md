The nine circles in the triangular number puzzle contain the digits $1$ through $9$, each **exactly once**. The four numbers on each side add to $21$. Complete the missing entries $A$, $B$, $C$ and $D$ and explain how the side sums and unused digits determine them.

![[batch10-triangle-number-puzzle.svg]]

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The five fixed entries use the digits $1,4,5,7,8$, leaving $\{2,3,6,9\}$ for the unknowns.
>
> The left side gives $A+7+5+B=21$, so $A+B=9$. The right side gives $A+1+8+D=21$, so $A+D=12$. The bottom side gives $B+C+4+D=21$, so $B+C+D=17$.
>
> Of the unused digits, the only choice satisfying all three conditions, without repetition, is
>
> $$\boxed{A=3,\qquad B=6,\qquad C=2,\qquad D=9.}$$
>
> Check: left $3+7+5+6=21$, bottom $6+2+4+9=21$, and right $9+8+1+3=21$. Each digit appears once.
