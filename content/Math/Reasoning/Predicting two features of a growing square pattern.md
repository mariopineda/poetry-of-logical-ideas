A museum displays a four-panel number pattern. The four positions follow **independent rules** from one stage to the next, as shown in the diagram.

![[batch13-four-sequence-pattern.svg]]

Determine the four missing entries $A$, $B$, $C$, and $D$ at Stage 4. Explain the rule governing **each** position.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The top-left position increases by $3$: $3,6,9,\boxed{12}$, so $A=12$.
>
> The top-right position is divided by $5$: $125,25,5,\boxed{1}$, so $B=1$.
>
> The bottom-left position is divided by $2$: $40,20,10,\boxed{5}$, so $C=5$.
>
> The bottom-right position decreases by $2$: $9,7,5,\boxed{3}$, so $D=3$.
>
> Thus $(A,B,C,D)=\boxed{(12,1,5,3)}$.
