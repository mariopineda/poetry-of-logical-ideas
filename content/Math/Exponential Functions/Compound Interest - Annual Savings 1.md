A student deposits $425 in a savings account earning $4.8\%$ annually, compounded once each year.

### A.

Write the future-value function.

### B.

How much interest accumulates after two years?

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> **Step 1 — Build the compound-interest model.** Annual compounding means one compounding period each year. The interest factor is $1+0.048=1.048$:
> 
> $$A(t)=425(1.048)^t.$$
> 
> **Step 2 — Find the balance after two years.**
> 
> $$A(2)=425(1.048)^2=425(1.098304)=\$466.7792\approx\$466.78.$$
> 
> **Step 3 — Separate balance from interest.** The account contains the original deposit **plus** interest. Subtract the deposit:
> 
> $$I=466.78-425=\boxed{\$41.78}.$$
> 
> **Check by year:** The first year's interest is $425(0.048)=\$20.40$. In the second year interest is earned on $\$445.40$, so the second year's interest is slightly greater.
