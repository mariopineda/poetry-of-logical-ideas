# Exponential Functions Practice T4 Q03 - Plant Growth Regression

A greenhouse records the height of a climbing vine:

| $x$ | 7 | 14 | 21 | 28 | 35 | 42 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $y$ | 12.5 | 23.1 | 41.6 | 74.7 | 134.8 | 242.6 |

a) Create an exponential regression equation, rounding parameters to hundredths. b) Estimate height on day 10. c) Estimate height on day 30 and comment on interpolation. d) Predict day it reaches 60 cm.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Regression $h(t)=7.00(1.09)^t$. With unrounded values: $h(10)\approx16.3$ cm, $h(30)\approx88.4$ cm; $h(t)=60$ at approximately day 25. Day 30 is interpolation, while day 10 also lies within the measured interval.
