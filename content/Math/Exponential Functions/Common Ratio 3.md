## Question

Consider the pattern:

$$
7,\ 14,\ 28,\ 56,\ \ldots
$$

1. Write the next **two** numbers.
2. Briefly explain how you determined your answer.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Each term is multiplied by $2$, so the common ratio is
>
> $$
> r=2
> $$
>
> $$
> 56(2)=112
> $$
>
> $$
> 112(2)=224
> $$
>
> Therefore, the next two terms are
>
> $$
> \boxed{112,\ 224}
> $$
