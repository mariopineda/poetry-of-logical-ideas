## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> | $x$ | $0$ | $1$ | $2$ | $3$ | $4$ |
> |---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
> | $y$ | $6$ | $18$ | $54$ | **$162$** | **$486$** |
>
> The outputs change by a **constant ratio** of $3$, since $18\div6=54\div18=162\div54=486\div162=3$. The differences are not constant.
