# Exponential Functions Practice T2 Q08 - Depreciation and Loan Repayment

A vehicle is purchased for \$28,000 and depreciates according to $V(t)=28000(0.82)^t$. A loan of \$23,000 is repaid by monthly instalments of \$390. The financing model yields $(1.008)^{n}=2.1$, where $n$ is the number of months. a) Find purchase value. b) Find annual depreciation. c) Solve for $n$. d) Estimate the total loan interest. e) Estimate the car value at loan payoff using unrounded $n$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> a) \$28,000. b) $18\%$. c) $n=\ln(2.1)/\ln(1.008)\approx93.11$ months. d) Interest $=390n-23000\approx\$13,313.93$ (idealized model). e) Vehicle value $=28000(0.82)^{n/12}\approx\$6,003.53$.
