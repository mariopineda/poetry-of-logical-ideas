Solve $5=4^{3x+1}$ for $x$. Show how taking a logarithm gives a linear equation in $x$ before obtaining a numerical approximation.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Take natural logarithms: $\ln5=(3x+1)\ln4$, so $3x+1=\frac{\ln5}{\ln4}$. Thus $\boxed{x=\frac{\ln5/\ln4-1}{3}\approx0.054}$ (to three decimals).
