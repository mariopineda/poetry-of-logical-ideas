# Exponential Functions Practice T4 Q05 - Modelling Medicine Concentration from Data

A study estimates a substance remaining in a sample:

| $x$ | 0 | 1 | 3 | 4 | 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $y$ | 54 | 42 | 25 | 20 | 12 |

a) Fit and graph an exponential regression $C(t)=ab^t$ (three decimals). b) Find when the amount falls below 4 mg, nearest hour. c) Calculate the half-life, nearest tenth of an hour.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Regression $C(t)=53.842(0.779)^t$. Using the unrounded regression, $C(t)<4$ after approximately 10 hours; half-life $\ln(0.5)/\ln(b)\approx2.8$ hours.
