# Exponential Functions Practice T4 Q08 - Deriving Equations from Plotted Points

Each graph includes two marked points. Determine its exponential equation in the form $y=ab^x$. Show how you obtain both $a$ and $b$.

![[EF_T4_Q08.png]]

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Graph A has $(0,3)$ and $(1,6)$, so $y=3(2)^x$. Graph B has $(0,12)$ and $(1,6)$, so $y=12(0.5)^x$.
