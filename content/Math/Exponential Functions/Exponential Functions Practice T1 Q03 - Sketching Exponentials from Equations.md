# Exponential Functions Practice T1 Q03 - Sketching Exponentials from Equations

Sketch each function, label the $y$-intercept, and give its $x$-intercepts, end behaviour, domain, and range.

a) $$
y=8(3)^x
$$
b) $$
y=5(2)^x
$$
c) $$
y=18\left(\frac13\right)^x
$$
d) $$
y=7\left(\frac12\right)^x
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> All have no $x$-intercepts, domain $\mathbb{R}$ and range $(0,\infty)$. The $y$-intercepts are 8, 5, 18, and 7. The first two grow toward the right; the last two decrease toward the right. For a sketch, use $x=-1,0,1$ and evaluate each function.
