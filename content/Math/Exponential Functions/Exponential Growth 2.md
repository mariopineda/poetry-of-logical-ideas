# Exponential Growth 2

Two savings accounts each start with \$500. Account A adds \$50 every year. Account B increases by $10\%$ of its current balance every year. Complete the table for years $0$–$3$. Which account has more at the end of year $3$?

| Year | $0$ | $1$ | $2$ | $3$ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Account A | \$500 | | | |
| Account B | \$500 | | | |

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> | Year | $0$ | $1$ | $2$ | $3$ |
> |---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
> | Account A: add $\$50$ each year | $\$500$ | $\$550$ | $\$600$ | **$\$650$** |
> | Account B: increase by $10\%$ each year | $\$500$ | $\$550$ | $\$605$ | **$\$665.50$** |
>
> Account B calculations:
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> \text{Year 1: }&500(1.10)=550\\
> \text{Year 2: }&550(1.10)=605\\
> \text{Year 3: }&605(1.10)=665.50
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> **Account B** has more at the end of year $3$:
>
> $$
> \$665.50-\$650=\boxed{\$15.50}
> $$
