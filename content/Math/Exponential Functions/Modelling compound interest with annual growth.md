A community art fund deposits $2\,400$ into an account that earns $3.5\%$ interest compounded annually. Write a function for its value after $t$ years and calculate its value after six years, to the nearest cent.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The initial balance is $P=2400$ and annual interest is $r=0.035$. After $t$ whole or fractional years, assuming annual compounding as stated:
>
> $$A(t)=P(1+r)^t=\boxed{2400(1.035)^t}.$$
>
> For six years:
>
> $$A(6)=2400(1.035)^6\approx2950.212783\ldots\approx\boxed{\$2\,950.21}.$$
>
> The factor $1.035$ includes the original balance plus $3.5\%$ interest each year; using $0.035$ alone would not model the account balance.
