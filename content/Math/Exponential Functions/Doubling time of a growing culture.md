The number of yeast cells in a laboratory culture is modelled by

$$
N(t)=320\cdot2^{t/15},
$$

where $t$ is measured in hours.

Determine **(a)** the doubling time and **(b)** the predicted cell count after $45$ hours. Justify both directly from the exponential model.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> **(a)** An increase of $15$ hours increases the exponent by one:
>
> $$
> \frac{N(t+15)}{N(t)}=\frac{320\cdot2^{(t+15)/15}}{320\cdot2^{t/15}}=2.
> $$
>
> The doubling time is therefore
>
> $$
> \boxed{15\text{ hours}}.
> $$
>
> **(b)** At $t=45$ hours, three doubling intervals have passed:
>
> $$
> N(45)=320\cdot2^{45/15}=320(8)=\boxed{2560\text{ cells}}.
> $$
