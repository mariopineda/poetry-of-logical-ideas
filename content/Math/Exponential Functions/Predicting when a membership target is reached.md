A conservation group counted $320$ nesting pairs at the start of a study. The number is expected to increase by $6\%$ each year at a constant rate.

Develop an exponential equation that could determine how many years $t$ are needed for the count to reach $510$. Explain your growth factor, and calculate the **first complete year** when the model predicts at least $510$ pairs.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The annual growth factor is $1+0.06=1.06$, so $N(t)=320(1.06)^t$.
>
> To determine the threshold time, solve
>
> $$510=320(1.06)^t,\qquad (1.06)^t=\frac{510}{320}.$$
>
> $$t=\frac{\ln(510/320)}{\ln(1.06)}\approx7.99.$$
>
> Therefore the model first reaches at least $510$ at the end of **year $\boxed{8}$**. Using the factor $0.06$ instead of $1.06$ would model neither this growth nor its initial value.
