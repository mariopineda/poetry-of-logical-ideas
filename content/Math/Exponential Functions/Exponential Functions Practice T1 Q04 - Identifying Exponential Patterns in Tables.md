# Exponential Functions Practice T1 Q04 - Identifying Exponential Patterns in Tables

For every table, decide whether it is exponential, explain using successive ratios, determine the $y$-intercept, and classify any exponential trend as growth or decay.

a) | $x$ | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $y$ | 9 | 27 | 81 | 243 | 729 | 2187 |

b) | $x$ | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $y$ | 2048 | 512 | 128 | 32 | 8 | 2 |

c) | $x$ | -4 | -3 | -2 | -1 | 0 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $y$ | -7 | -4 | -1 | 2 | 5 | 8 |

d) | $x$ | -1 | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $y$ | 500 | 100 | 20 | 4 | 0.8 | 0.16 |

e) | $x$ | -2 | -1 | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $y$ | 8 | 4 | 2 | 5 | 9 | 14 |

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> a: yes, ratio $3$, $y$-intercept $9$, growth. b: yes, ratio $1/4$, intercept $2048$, decay. c: no; intercept $5$. d: yes, ratio $1/5$, intercept $100$, decay. e: no; intercept $2$.
