Solve $5^{x-2}=25^{2x-1}$ algebraically, without using a graph. Verify your answer in the original equation.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Since $25=5^2$,
>
> $$5^{x-2}=25^{2x-1}=(5^2)^{2x-1}=5^{4x-2}.$$
>
> Equal positive bases other than $1$ have equal exponents:
>
> $$x-2=4x-2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \boxed{x=0}.$$
>
> Check in the original equation: $5^{-2}=\tfrac1{25}$ and $25^{-1}=\tfrac1{25}$, so the answer is valid.
