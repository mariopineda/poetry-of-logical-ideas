A wildlife rehabilitation centre tracked an animal population that grew by $4.5\%$ per year for eight years. At the end of the eighth year, the population was approximately $18\,000$. Estimate the initial population to the nearest whole animal using an exponential model.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Let $P_0$ be the initial count. The annual growth factor is $1+0.045=1.045$, and eight periods have elapsed:
>
> $$18\,000=P_0(1.045)^8.$$
>
> Divide by the growth factor:
>
> $$P_0=\frac{18\,000}{(1.045)^8}\approx12\,657.332\ldots.$$
>
> To the nearest whole animal, the estimated initial population is $\boxed{12\,657}$. The model assumes a constant annual percentage growth rate.
