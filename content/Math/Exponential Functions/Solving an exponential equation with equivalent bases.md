Solve $125^{x-1}=25^{2x+1}$ exactly by converting both bases to powers of the same number. Verify your answer by comparing the original expressions.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Because $125=5^3$ and $25=5^2$,
>
> $$(5^3)^{x-1}=(5^2)^{2x+1}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad5^{3x-3}=5^{4x+2}.$$
>
> Equal powers of the same positive base other than $1$ have equal exponents:
>
> $$3x-3=4x+2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\boxed{x=-5}.$$
>
> Substitution gives $125^{-6}=5^{-18}$ and $25^{-9}=5^{-18}$, so the solution checks.
