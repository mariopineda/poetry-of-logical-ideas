# Exponential Functions Practice T3 Q01 - Graphical Solutions at Two Heights

For each graph, estimate the $x$-value at $y=5$ and $y=12$. Show horizontal reading lines and report answers to one decimal place.

![[EF_T3_Q01.png]]

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Graph A: $20(0.7)^x$, giving $x\approx3.9$ at $5$ and $x\approx1.4$ at $12$. Graph B: $4(1.7)^x$, giving $x\approx0.4$ at $5$ and $x\approx2.1$ at $12$.
