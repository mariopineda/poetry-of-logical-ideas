# Exponential Functions Practice T1 Q06 - Intercept and Growth or Decay

Find the $y$-intercept and state whether each function grows or decays.

a) $$
y=7(3)^x
$$
b) $$
y=4\left(\frac13\right)^x
$$
c) $$
y=12(1.25)^x
$$
d) $$
y=(0.6)^x
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> a: $7$, growth; b: $4$, decay; c: $12$, growth; d: $1$, decay. For each, set $x=0$ and compare the base with $1$.
