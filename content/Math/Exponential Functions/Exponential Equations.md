Solve for $x$.

1.

$$
2^{5x+2}=2^{17}
$$

2.

$$
2^x=16\sqrt{2}
$$

3.

$$
8^{3x}=4^{1-x}
$$

4.

$$
9^{x+1}=729
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> Because the bases are equal,
>
> $$
> 5x+2=17.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> 5x=15
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=3}.
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> Write the right side as a power of $2$:
>
> $$
> 16\sqrt2
> =
> 2^4\cdot2^{1/2}
> =
> 2^{9/2}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=\frac92}.
> $$
>
> ### 3.
>
> Rewrite both sides with base $2$:
>
> $$
> (2^3)^{3x}=(2^2)^{1-x}.
> $$
>
> So
>
> $$
> 2^{9x}=2^{2-2x}.
> $$
>
> Hence,
>
> $$
> 9x=2-2x,
> $$
>
> $$
> 11x=2,
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=\frac{2}{11}}.
> $$
>
> ### 4.
>
> Since
>
> $$
> 9=3^2
> \qquad\text{and}\qquad
> 729=3^6,
> $$
>
> we have
>
> $$
> 3^{2(x+1)}=3^6.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> 2x+2=6,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=2}.
> $$
