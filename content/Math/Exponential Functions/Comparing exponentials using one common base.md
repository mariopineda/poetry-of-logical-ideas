Use exponent laws to rewrite the equation with a single base, then determine the constant term $c$ when the result is placed in the form $3x^2+bx+c=0$.

$$
\frac{27^{x(x-1)}}{3^{2x+1}}=9^{x-4}
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Because $27=3^3$ and $9=3^2$,
>
> $$
> 3^{3x(x-1)-(2x+1)}=3^{2(x-4)}.
> $$
>
> The base $3$ is positive and not $1$, so equality of the powers requires equality of their exponents:
>
> $$
> 3x^2-3x-2x-1=2x-8.
> $$
>
> Collect terms:
>
> $$
> 3x^2-7x+7=0.
> $$
>
> Thus the required constant term is $\boxed{c=7}$.
