A lab culture is modelled by $P(t)=200(8)^{t/30}$, where $t$ is measured in hours. Determine the doubling period without graphing.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Because $8=2^3$, $P(t)=200\cdot2^{t/10}$. An increase of $t$ by 10 raises the exponent by 1 and doubles $P$. Thus the doubling time is $\boxed{10\text{ hours}}$.
