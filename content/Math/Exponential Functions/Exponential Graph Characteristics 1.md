## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> **Step 1 — Return to the two exponential curves in [[Recognizing Function Types 1]].** Graph **b** shows growth and graph **e** shows decay.
> 
> **Step 2 — Read the $y$-intercept.** The $y$-intercept occurs where $x=0$. Graph b crosses the vertical axis at $1$; graph e crosses it at $3$.
> 
> | Characteristic | Graph b (growth) | Graph e (decay) |
> |---|---|---|
> | Number of $x$-intercepts | 0 | 0 |
> | $y$-intercept | $(0,1)$ | $(0,3)$ |
> | Domain | $x\in\mathbb{R}$ | $x\in\mathbb{R}$ |
> | Range | $y>0$ | $y>0$ |
> | As $x\to-\infty$ | $y\to0^+$ | $y\to\infty$ |
> | As $x\to\infty$ | $y\to\infty$ | $y\to0^+$ |
> 
> **Why:** Both graphs stay above the $x$-axis for all real $x$, which explains their ranges and the absence of $x$-intercepts. The horizontal asymptote $y=0$ is approached but never reached.
