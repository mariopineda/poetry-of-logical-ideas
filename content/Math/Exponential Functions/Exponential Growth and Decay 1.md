Find the $y$-intercept and state whether each function grows or decays.

A. $$

y=7(3)^x
$$

B. $$

y=4\left(\frac13\right)^x
$$

C. $$

y=12(1.25)^x
$$

D. $$

y=(0.6)^x
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> **Step 1 — Identify $a$ and $b$ in $y=ab^x$.** The initial value is $a$, since $b^0=1$. If $b>1$, multiplying repeatedly makes the output increase; if $0<b<1$, it decreases.
> 
> | Equation | $a=y(0)$ | Base $b$ | Decision and reason |
> |---|---:|---:|---|
> | a) $7(3)^x$ | $7$ | $3$ | Growth, because $3>1$ |
> | b) $4(\frac13)^x$ | $4$ | $\frac13$ | Decay, because $0<\frac13<1$ |
> | c) $12(1.25)^x$ | $12$ | $1.25$ | Growth, because $1.25>1$ |
> | d) $(0.6)^x$ | $1$ | $0.6$ | Decay, because $0<0.6<1$ |
> 
> **Check:** Function c changes by a factor of $1.25$ each time $x$ increases by $1$, so it grows by $25\%$ per step. Function d retains $60\%$ each step, a decrease of $40\%$.
