# Exponential Functions Practice T4 Q07 - Exponential Surface Coverage

The percentage of a wetland pool covered by floating plants is recorded:

| $x$ | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $y$ | 18.0 | 19.8 | 21.8 | 24.0 | 26.4 | 29.0 |

a) Find the weekly percent growth to nearest percent. b) If the model remains valid, calculate how many weeks until coverage reaches $60\%$, to the nearest tenth.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Weekly growth approximately $10\%$. Solve $18(1.10)^t=60$: $t=\ln(60/18)/\ln(1.1)\approx12.6$ weeks.
