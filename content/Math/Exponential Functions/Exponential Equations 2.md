Solve the following equations. Express the solution as an exact value in the form $\frac{\log M}{\log N}$ and to two decimal places.

1.

$$
4^x=60
$$
2.

$$
3^x=9
$$
3.

$$
7^{x+2}+3=444
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ### 1.
>
> $$
> 4^x=60.
> $$
>
> Taking logarithms,
>
> $$
> x\log4=\log60.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> x=\frac{\log60}{\log4}
> \approx2.95
> }.
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> $$
> 3^x=9=3^2.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> x=\frac{\log9}{\log3}=2.00
> }.
> $$
>
> ### 3.
>
> $$
> 7^{x+2}+3=444.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> 7^{x+2}=441.
> $$
>
> Divide by $7^2=49$:
>
> $$
> 7^x=9.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> x=\frac{\log9}{\log7}
> \approx1.13
> }.
> $$
