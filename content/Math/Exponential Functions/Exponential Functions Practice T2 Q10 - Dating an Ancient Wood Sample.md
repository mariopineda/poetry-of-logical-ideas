# Exponential Functions Practice T2 Q10 - Dating an Ancient Wood Sample

A recovered wooden artifact retains $68\%$ of its original carbon-14. Using $C(t)=100(0.5)^{t/5730}$, find its age, to the nearest year.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Solve $68=100(0.5)^{t/5730}$; $t=5730\log(0.68)/\log(0.5)\approx3188$ years.
