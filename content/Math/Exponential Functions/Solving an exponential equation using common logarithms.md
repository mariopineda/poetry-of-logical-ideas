Solve $7=10^{x-5}$ and give $x$ to the nearest hundredth. Show the logarithmic step.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Taking the common logarithm gives $\log 7=x-5$. Therefore $x=5+\log 7\approx\boxed{5.85}$.
