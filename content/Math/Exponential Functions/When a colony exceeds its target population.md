A monitored insect colony contains $1\,800$ individuals and grows by $7\%$ each year. Find the minimum **whole number of completed years** needed for its population to exceed $3\,100$. Set up and solve an exponential inequality, and check the adjacent years.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The population model after $t$ completed years is $P(t)=1800(1.07)^t$. The requirement is strict:
>
> $$1800(1.07)^t>3100\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad t>\frac{\ln(3100/1800)}{\ln(1.07)}\approx8.03468.$$
>
> The first **integer** satisfying this is $t=9$. Check the adjacent years:
>
> $$P(8)\approx3092.74<3100,\qquad P(9)\approx3309.23>3100.$$
>
> Thus $\boxed{9\text{ completed years}}$ are required.
