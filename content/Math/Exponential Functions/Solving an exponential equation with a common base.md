Solve exactly for $x$ and show the common-base step: $$9^{2x-3}=3^{1/2}.$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Since $9=3^2$, $3^{4x-6}=3^{1/2}$. Thus $4x-6=1/2$, so $4x=13/2$ and $\boxed{x=13/8}$.
