# Exponential Functions Practice T1 Q11 - Comparing Caffeine Decay Graphs

Two volunteers consume different quantities of caffeine. The graphs show estimated caffeine remaining over eight hours.

a) Read the initial amount, contextual domain and range for each. b) Explain the downward trends. c) Identify who began with more caffeine and who eliminates it faster proportionally. d) Estimate both amounts after four hours. e) Interpret the intercepts in context.

![[EF_T1_Q11.png]]

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> A starts at 96 mg; B at 120 mg. For measured interval: $0\le t\le 8$; A ranges approximately $10.7\le C\le96$, B approximately $2.3\le C\le120$. Both decay; B initially has more caffeine and has the greater proportional removal rate. After 4 h: A $\approx32.0$ mg, B $\approx16.6$ mg. Each intercept is starting caffeine.
