## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> **Step 1 — Read the horizontal level.** To solve $f(x)=5$ or $f(x)=12$ from a graph, draw a horizontal line at that $y$-value and record the $x$-coordinate where it crosses the curve.
> 
> **Step 2 — Record your estimates to one decimal place.**
> 
> | Function shown | At $y=5$ | At $y=12$ |
> |---|---:|---:|
> | A: $y=20(0.7)^x$ | $x\approx3.9$ | $x\approx1.4$ |
> | B: $y=4(1.7)^x$ | $x\approx0.4$ | $x\approx2.1$ |
> 
> **Step 3 — Verify numerically if needed.** For A at $y=5$: $20(0.7)^x=5$ means $(0.7)^x=0.25$. Thus $x=\ln(0.25)/\ln(0.7)\approx3.9$. A is decreasing, so it reaches the larger $y$-value at a smaller $x$-value; that agrees with the graph.
> 
> **Calculator method:** Enter the exponential as `Y1` and the horizontal target as `Y2`; use `2nd` → `TRACE` → `5:intersect`. Repeat for the other target.
