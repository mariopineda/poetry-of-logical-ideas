# Exponential Functions Practice T1 Q10 - Contrasting Growth and Decay

a) Write an exponential growth function and an exponential decay function that each intersect the $y$-axis at $7$. b) Describe their shared and differing properties.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> For example $f(x)=7(1.4)^x$ and $g(x)=7(0.6)^x$. They share intercept $7$, no $x$-intercepts, domain $\mathbb R$, range $(0,\infty)$, and horizontal asymptote $y=0$. They have opposite monotonicity and reverse end limits.
