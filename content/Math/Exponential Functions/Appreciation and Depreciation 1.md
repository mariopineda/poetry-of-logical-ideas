# Appreciation and Depreciation 1

For each model, state the starting value, whether the asset appreciates or depreciates, and the yearly percentage change.

a) $y=850(1.08)^t$; b) $y=1750(0.92)^t$; c) $y=4200(1.12)^t$; d) $y=2600(0.97)^t$; e) $y=750(0.2)^t$.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> **Step 1 — Read the initial amount.** In $y=ab^t$, $a=y(0)$ because $b^0=1$.
> 
> **Step 2 — Convert the multiplier to a rate.** For growth $r=(b-1)\times100\%$; for decay the percentage loss is $(1-b)\times100\%$.
> 
> | Model | Initial amount | Multiplier | Change each year |
> |---|---:|---:|---|
> | a) $850(1.08)^t$ | $850$ | $1.08$ | Appreciates $8\%$ |
> | b) $1750(0.92)^t$ | $1750$ | $0.92$ | Depreciates $8\%$ |
> | c) $4200(1.12)^t$ | $4200$ | $1.12$ | Appreciates $12\%$ |
> | d) $2600(0.97)^t$ | $2600$ | $0.97$ | Depreciates $3\%$ |
> | e) $750(0.2)^t$ | $750$ | $0.2$ | Depreciates $80\%$ |
> 
> **Common mistake:** A factor of $0.2$ does **not** mean a $20\%$ decrease. It means $20\%$ remains, so $80\%$ has been lost.
