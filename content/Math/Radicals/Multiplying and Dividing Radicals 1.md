## Question

Simplify the following radicals.

1. $(5\sqrt{18})(2\sqrt{6})$
2. $\sqrt{5}(4\sqrt{5}-\sqrt{15}+2\sqrt{3})$
3. $\sqrt{a}(\sqrt{a}-4\sqrt{b})$
4. $\displaystyle\frac{3\sqrt{80}}{2\sqrt{2}}$
5. $\displaystyle\frac{8\sqrt{39} + 8\sqrt{75}}{4\sqrt{4}}$
6. $\displaystyle\frac{20\sqrt{12}}{12\sqrt{20}}$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> ## A.
>
> \[
> \begin{align}
> & 5\sqrt{18})(2\sqrt{6}) \\
> = & 5 \times 2 \sqrt{18 \times 6} \\
> = & 10 \sqrt{108} \\
> = & 10 \sqrt{36 \times 3} \\
> = & 10 \times 6 \sqrt{3} \\
> = & 60\sqrt{3}
> \end{align}
> \]
>
> ## B.
>
> \[
> \begin{align}
> & \sqrt{5}(4\sqrt{5}-\sqrt{15}+2\sqrt{3}) \\
> = & 4\sqrt{5}\sqrt{5} - \sqrt{15}\sqrt+{5} + 2\sqrt{3}\sqrt{5} \\
> = & 4 \times 5 - \sqrt{75} + 2\sqrt{15} \\
> = & 20 - \sqrt{25 \times 3} + 2\sqrt{15} \\
> = & 20 - 5\sqrt{3} + 2\sqrt{15}
> \end{align}
> \]
>
> ## C.
>
> \[
> \begin{align}
> & \sqrt{a}(\sqrt{a}-4\sqrt{b}) \\
> = & \sqrt{a}\sqrt{a} - 4\sqrt{b}\sqrt{a} \\
> = & a - 4\sqrt{ab}
> \end{align}
> \]
>
> ## D.
>
> \[
> \begin{align}
> & \displaystyle\frac{3\sqrt{80}}{2\sqrt{2}} \\
> = & \displaystyle\frac{3}{2}\sqrt{\displaystyle\frac{80}{2}} \\
> = & \displaystyle\frac{3}{3} \sqrt{40} \\
> = & \displaystyle\frac{3}{2} \sqrt{4 \times 10} \\
> = & \displaystyle\frac{3}{2} \times 2 \sqrt{10} \\
> = & 4\sqrt{10}
> \end{align}
> \]
>
> ## E.
>
> \[
> \begin{align}
> & \displaystyle\frac{8\sqrt{39} + 8\sqrt{75}}{4\sqrt{4}} \\
> = & \displaystyle\frac{8\sqrt{39}}{4\sqrt{4}} + \displaystyle\frac{8\sqrt{75}}{4\sqrt{4}} \\
> = & \displaystyle\frac{2\sqrt{39}}{2} + \displaystyle\frac{2\sqrt{25 \times 3}}{2} \\
> = & \sqrt{39} + 5\sqrt{3}
> \end{align}
> \]
>
> ## F.
>
> \[
> \begin{align}
> & \displaystyle\frac{20\sqrt{12}}{12\sqrt{20}} \\
> = & \displaystyle\frac{5\sqrt{4 \times 3}}{3\sqrt{4 \times 5}} \\
> = & \displaystyle\frac{5 \times 2\sqrt{3}}{3 \times 2\sqrt{5}} \\
> = & \displaystyle\frac{5\sqrt{3}}{3\sqrt{5}}
> \end{align}
> \]
>
