Solve $x-2 = \sqrt{2x-3}+1$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Solve
>
> $$
> x-2=\sqrt{2x-3}+1.
> $$
>
> Rearrange:
>
> $$
> x-3=\sqrt{2x-3}.
> $$
>
> Since the right side is non-negative, any solution must satisfy
>
> $$
> x\geq3.
> $$
>
> Square both sides:
>
> $$
> (x-3)^2=2x-3.
> $$
>
> Expand:
>
> $$
> x^2-6x+9=2x-3.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> x^2-8x+12=0.
> $$
>
> Factor:
>
> $$
> (x-2)(x-6)=0.
> $$
>
> The candidates are
>
> $$
> x=2,\ 6.
> $$
>
> But $x=2$ does not satisfy the original equation.
>
> Checking $x=6$:
>
> $$
> 6-2=\sqrt{12-3}+1
> $$
>
> $$
> 4=3+1.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{x=6}.
> $$
