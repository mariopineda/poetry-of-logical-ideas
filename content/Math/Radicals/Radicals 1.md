Convert the following radicals to mixed radicals in simplest form.

1.

$$
\sqrt{50}
$$
2.

$$
\sqrt{60}
$$
3.

$$
\sqrt{54}
$$
4.

$$
7\sqrt{63}
$$
5. -5$\sqrt{162}$

> [!info]- Hint 1
> When factoring the radicand you want one of the factors to be the largest possible perfect square.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> Factor out the largest perfect-square factor.
>
> 1.
>
> $$
> \sqrt{50}
> =
> \sqrt{25\cdot2}
> =
> \boxed{5\sqrt2}.
> $$
>
> 2.
>
> $$
> \sqrt{60}
> =
> \sqrt{4\cdot15}
> =
> \boxed{2\sqrt{15}}.
> $$
>
> 3.
>
> $$
> \sqrt{54}
> =
> \sqrt{9\cdot6}
> =
> \boxed{3\sqrt6}.
> $$
>
> 4.
>
> $$
> 7\sqrt{63}
> =
> 7\sqrt{9\cdot7}
> =
> \boxed{21\sqrt7}.
> $$
>
> 5.
>
> $$
> -5\sqrt{162}
> =
> -5\sqrt{81\cdot2}
> =
> \boxed{-45\sqrt2}.
> $$
