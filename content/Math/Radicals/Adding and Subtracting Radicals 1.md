## Question

Simplify the following radicals.

1. $\sqrt{75} - \sqrt{3}$
2. $2\sqrt{24} + \sqrt{6} - \sqrt{54}$
3. $2\sqrt{90} - 4\sqrt{40} - \sqrt{1000}$
4. $\sqrt{900} - 1 + \sqrt{1}$
5. $\displaystyle\frac{1}{3}\sqrt{63} + \displaystyle\frac{2}{5}\sqrt{700}-\displaystyle\frac{3}{2}\sqrt{28}$

## Solution

## A.

\[
\begin{align}
& \sqrt{75} - \sqrt{3} \\
= & \sqrt{25 \times 3} - \sqrt{3} \\
= & 5\sqrt{3} - \sqrt{3} \\
= & (5-1)\sqrt{3} \\
= & 4\sqrt{3} \\
\end{align}
\]

## B.

\[
\begin{align}
& 2\sqrt{24} + \sqrt{6} - \sqrt{54} \\
= & 2\sqrt{4 \times 6} + \sqrt{6} - \sqrt{9 \times 6} \\
= & 2 \times 2\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{6} - 3\sqrt{6} \\
= & 4\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{6} - 3\sqrt{6} \\
= & (4+1-3)\sqrt{6} \\
= & 2\sqrt{6} \\
\end{align}
\]

## C.

\[
\begin{align}
& 2\sqrt{90} - 4\sqrt{40} - \sqrt{1000} \\
= & 2\sqrt{9 \times 10} - 4\sqrt{4 \times 10} - \sqrt{100 \times 10} \\
= & 2 \times 3\sqrt{10} - 4 \times 2\sqrt{10} - 10\sqrt{10} \\
= & 6\sqrt{10} - 8\sqrt{10} - 10\sqrt{10} \\
= & (6-8-10)\sqrt{10} \\
= & -12\sqrt{10}
\end{align}
\]

## D.

\[
\begin{align}
& \sqrt{900} - 1 + \sqrt{1} \\
= & 30 - 1 + 1 \\
= & 30
\end{align}
\]

## E.

\[
\begin{align}
& \displaystyle\frac{1}{3}\sqrt{63} + \displaystyle\frac{2}{5}\sqrt{700}-\displaystyle\frac{3}{2}\sqrt{28}
= & \displaystyle\frac{1}{3}\sqrt{9 \times 7} + \displaystyle\frac{2}{5}\sqrt{100 \times 7}-\displaystyle\frac{3}{2}\sqrt{4 \times 7} \\
= & \displaystyle\frac{1}{3} \times 3 \sqrt{7} + \displaystyle\frac{2}{5} \times 10 \sqrt{7}-\displaystyle\frac{3}{2} \times 2 \sqrt{7} \\
= & 1\sqrt{7} + 4\sqrt{7} - 3\sqrt{7} \\
= & (1+4-3)\sqrt{7} \\
= & 2\sqrt{7}
\end{align}
\]
