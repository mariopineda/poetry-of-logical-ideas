A designer places seven distinct anchor points around the rim of a circular sign, as shown below.

![[diploma-2019-seven-circle-anchors.svg]]

How many different triangles or quadrilaterals can be made by joining selected anchors? Treat polygons with the same vertices as identical, regardless of drawing order.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Any three chosen anchors form a triangle and any four chosen anchors form a quadrilateral because the points lie on a circle. Count $\binom73+\binom74=35+35=\boxed{70}$ polygons.
