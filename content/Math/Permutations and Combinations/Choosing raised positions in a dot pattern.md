An electronic accessibility display has **eight distinct dot locations**, shown below. A dot pattern is determined only by which locations are turned on; the order in which they are activated does not matter.

![[diploma-batch08-2013-nr11-dot-template.svg]]

How many different patterns have **exactly five** of the eight dots on? Justify why combinations, rather than permutations, are appropriate.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> A pattern is the selection of a set of five locations from eight distinct locations. The order of activation does not change the final pattern, so
>
> $$\binom85=\frac{8!}{5!3!}=\frac{8\cdot7\cdot6}{3\cdot2\cdot1}=\boxed{56}.$$
>
> Permutations would count multiple activation orders for the same five active dot positions.
