Determine the number of distinguishable arrangements of all eight letters in **BALLOONS**. Explain precisely which repeated letters affect the count.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> There are eight letters. The letter L appears twice and O appears twice; the other letters occur once.
>
> Counting all $8!$ permutations would overcount each arrangement $2!$ times for the L's and $2!$ times for the O's. Therefore
>
> $$\frac{8!}{2!\,2!}=\frac{40\,320}{4}=\boxed{10\,080}.$$
