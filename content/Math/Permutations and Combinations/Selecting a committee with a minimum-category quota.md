A science fair has six student researchers and five student artists. A four-person committee is to contain at least three researchers. How many distinct committees are possible? Explain each case.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The committee is unordered, so use combinations. The only eligible compositions of four members are:
>
> - Three researchers and one artist: $\binom63\binom51=20\cdot5=100$.
> - Four researchers and no artists: $\binom64\binom50=15\cdot1=15$.
>
> The cases are disjoint and exhaustive, giving
>
> $$\boxed{100+15=115\text{ committees}}.$$
>
> The available numbers of students make both cases possible.
