A fashion photographer can assemble 30 visually different outfits by choosing **one top**, **one scarf**, and **one pair of shoes**. There are 3 scarf choices and 2 shoe styles. Every combination is allowed.

What is the **minimum number of different tops** needed to offer all 30 outfits? Set up and solve a counting equation.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Let $t$ be the number of tops. Each top can be combined with 3 scarves and 2 shoe styles, for $6t$ outfits. Therefore
>
> $$6t=30\quad\Longrightarrow\quad t=\frac{30}{6}=\boxed{5\text{ tops}}.$$
>
> Check: $5\times3\times2=30$ different outfits. Since the number of tops is a whole number, no rounding is needed.
