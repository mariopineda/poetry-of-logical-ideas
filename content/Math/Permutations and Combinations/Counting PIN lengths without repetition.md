A device permits a PIN consisting of three or four digits chosen from 1 through 7, with no repeats. How many PINs are possible? Count both lengths.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> A PIN is ordered and may be either length. With digits $1,\ldots,7$ and no repetition:
>
> $$N_3={}_7P_3=7\cdot6\cdot5=210,\qquad N_4={}_7P_4=7\cdot6\cdot5\cdot4=840.$$
>
> These cases are disjoint because they have different lengths, so add:
>
> $$\boxed{210+840=1050\text{ PINs}}.$$
>
> A leading digit is always allowed here; these are PINs, not integers subject to a nonzero leading-digit rule.
