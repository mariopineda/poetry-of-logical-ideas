A robotics team enters seven independent rounds. Each round may result in a win, loss, tie, or disqualification. How many different ordered sequences of results are possible? Explain the counting principle used.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Each of the seven rounds has four possible recorded results. Treating the rounds as distinguishable positions, the fundamental counting principle gives
>
> $$\boxed{4\times4\times4\times4\times4\times4\times4=4^7=16\,384}.$$
>
> This counts **ordered sequences**. If a problem asked only for the overall counts of wins, losses, ties, and disqualifications, it would be a different counting task.
