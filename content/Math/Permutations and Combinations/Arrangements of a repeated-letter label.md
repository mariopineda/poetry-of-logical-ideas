A gallery keeps its entrance panel permanently at the far left. To its right, it will display five prints: **two indistinguishable blue-sky prints** and **three different wildlife photographs**.

How many visibly different orders of the five prints are possible? Show why treating all five prints as different would overcount.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The entrance panel is fixed, so only the five prints are arranged. If all five were distinct there would be $5!=120$ orders. Interchanging the two indistinguishable blue-sky prints does not create a new display, so every visible arrangement is counted $2!$ times.
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{5!}{2!}=\frac{120}{2}=60\text{ distinct arrangements}}.
> $$
