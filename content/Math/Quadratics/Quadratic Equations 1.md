When the equation $6x^2-12x-18$ is fully factored, one factor is:

1.

$$
x-1
$$
2.

$$
x+3
$$
3.

$$
x+6
$$
4.

$$
x-3
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> The expression has a greatest common factor of $6$:
>
> $$
> 6x^2-12x-18=6(x^2-2x-3).
> $$
>
> **Inspection ($a=1$).**
>
> Identify the numerical coefficients $a=1$, $b=-2$, and $c=-3$. Thus $ac=-3$; we need two numbers with product $-3$ and sum $-2$. Since $ac$ is negative, the numbers have opposite signs.
>
> - **Sequential division:** Test divisors $1,2,3,\ldots$ of $|ac|=3$ until a factor pair has the required difference: $3\div1=3$ (difference $2$, **match**).
> - **Square-root-guided search:** $\sqrt{3}\approx 1.73$, so the possible smaller divisors end at $1$. Start at $1$ and work downward: $3\div1=3$ (difference $2$, **match**).
>
> The required numbers are $m=-3$ and $n=1$: $-3+(1)=-2$ and $(-3)(1)=-3$.
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> 6x^2-12x-18&=6(x-3)(x+1).
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> Therefore, $\boxed{x-3}$ is a factor, which is **choice 4**.
>
