State the maximum or minimum value of each quadratic function, correct to the nearest hundreth, and the x-value at which it occurs.

1.

$$
y=3x^2-4x-5
$$
2.

$$
y=\frac{1}{3}x^2-4x+10
$$
3.

$$
y=-0.25x^2+2x+3
$$
4.

$$
y=2x^2-\frac{1}{4}x+1
$$

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
>
> For a quadratic
>
> $$
> y=ax^2+bx+c,
> $$
>
> the maximum or minimum occurs at
>
> $$
> x=-\frac{b}{2a}.
> $$
>
> ### 1.
>
> $$
> y=3x^2-4x-5
> $$
>
> Since $a>0$, the function has a minimum.
>
> $$
> x=\frac23\approx0.67
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> y=-\frac{19}{3}\approx-6.33.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{minimum }-6.33\text{ at }x=0.67}.
> $$
>
> ### 2.
>
> $$
> y=\frac13x^2-4x+10
> $$
>
> Since $a>0$, the function has a minimum.
>
> $$
> x=6
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> y=-2.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{minimum }-2.00\text{ at }x=6.00}.
> $$
>
> ### 3.
>
> $$
> y=-0.25x^2+2x+3
> $$
>
> Since $a<0$, the function has a maximum.
>
> $$
> x=4
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> y=7.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{maximum }7.00\text{ at }x=4.00}.
> $$
>
> ### 4.
>
> $$
> y=2x^2-\frac14x+1
> $$
>
> Since $a>0$, the function has a minimum.
>
> $$
> x=\frac{1}{16}=0.0625
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> y=\frac{127}{128}\approx0.99.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{minimum }0.99\text{ at }x=0.06}.
> $$
