A bag contains **3 striped**, **4 plain**, and **2 dotted** marbles. Two marbles are drawn one after the other **without replacement**.

Find the exact probability that the **first** marble is striped and the **second** marble is *not striped*. Explain why the second probability uses a different denominator.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Initially there are $3+4+2=9$ marbles. The probability of striped first is $3/9$. If that occurs, 8 marbles remain and **all 6 non-striped marbles are still present**, so
>
> $$P(\text{striped then non-striped})=\frac39\cdot\frac68=\frac{18}{72}=\boxed{\frac14}.$$
>
> The denominator falls to 8 because the first marble is not replaced. Order is specified; the reverse order is **not** added.
