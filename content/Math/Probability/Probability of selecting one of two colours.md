An art room has a container holding 8 red tokens, 5 black tokens, and 10 yellow tokens. One token is selected at random, with each token equally likely.

Find the probability that the token is **red or black**, rounded to the nearest hundredth. Explain why adding the two favourable counts is justified.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The token colours are mutually exclusive on one draw. There are $8+5+10=23$ tokens, and $8+5=13$ favourable tokens.
>
> $$P(\text{red or black})=\frac{13}{23}\approx0.565217=\boxed{0.57}.$$
>
> The categories do not overlap, so their counts can be added without double-counting.
