At a nature festival, 54 visitors reported participating in archery ($A$), climbing ($B$), or kayaking ($C$). Four took part in all three. In total, 10 participated in both $A$ and $B$, nine in both $A$ and $C$, and eight in both $B$ and $C$; these pairwise totals **include** people in all three activities. Twenty-three participated in $A$. Seven tried only $B$, eighteen tried only $C$, and two tried none.

**Complete a three-set Venn diagram** showing each region's count, and use it to determine how many visitors participated in $B$ altogether. Show how the given survey total checks your answer.

![[batch12-three-set-blank-venn.svg]]

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The triple intersection is $4$. The pairwise-only regions are $AB=10-4=6$, $AC=9-4=5$, and $BC=8-4=4$. The $A$-only region is $23-(6+5+4)=8$.
>
> Fill the regions of the Venn diagram with $A$ only $8$, $B$ only $7$, $C$ only $18$, $AB$ only $6$, $AC$ only $5$, $BC$ only $4$, and $ABC=4$; outside all circles is $2$.
>
> The total in $B$ is $7+6+4+4=\boxed{21}$. The universe check is $8+7+18+6+5+4+4+2=54$, as required.
