A transit agency categorizes delays by **time of day** and separately by **month**. The two summary tables are:

| Time of day | Morning | Afternoon | Evening |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Probability | 0.22 | 0.48 | 0.30 |

| Month category | December | Every other month combined |
|---|---:|---:|
| Probability | 0.16 | 0.84 |

Assuming that the time-of-day and month classifications are **independent**, what is the probability of a delay occurring in the **morning during December**? Round to the nearest hundredth and explain why multiplication is appropriate.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> The two events have probabilities $P(M)=0.22$ and $P(D)=0.16$. Because independence is explicitly assumed,
>
> $$P(M\cap D)=P(M)P(D)=0.22(0.16)=0.0352.$$
>
> Rounded to the nearest hundredth, this is $\boxed{0.04}$. The unrounded product is 0.0352; this is a **joint probability**, not the sum $0.38$.
