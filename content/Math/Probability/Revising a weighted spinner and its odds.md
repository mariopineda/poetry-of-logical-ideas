The **original** game spinner has six equal-area sectors, shown below: one gold, one black, and four white.

![[diploma-batch08-2016-mc12-spinner.svg]]

A redesigned spinner **removes the black sector**, **doubles the area of gold**, and **leaves the total white area unchanged**. Assuming each design has a uniform spinner pointer, determine the odds **in favour of gold on the redesigned spinner**. Justify using area ratios.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> Use one old sector as one unit of area. Originally gold occupies 1 unit, black 1, and white 4. The new design has **gold 2 units** and **white 4 units**, with black removed.
>
> Because landing probability is proportional to sector area, the odds in favour of gold are
>
> $$2:4=\boxed{1:2}.$$
>
> The total new area is $2+4=6$ units, giving $P(\text{gold})=2/6=1/3$. The probability is not the same notation as the odds.
