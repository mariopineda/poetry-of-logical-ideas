A case contains 9 identical-size camera batteries and 5 chargers. Two items are chosen at random without replacement. Determine the probability that both are batteries and show the multiplication of conditional probabilities.

## Solution

> [!example]- Show solution
> There are $14$ objects initially, of which $9$ are batteries. The probability the first is a battery is $9/14$. After that occurs, there are $8$ batteries among $13$ remaining objects:
>
> $$P(\text{both batteries})=\frac9{14}\times\frac8{13}=\frac{72}{182}=\boxed{\frac{36}{91}}\approx0.3956.$$
>
> The second denominator is $13$ (not $14$) because the selections are **without replacement**.
