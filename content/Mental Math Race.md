Create different mental-math worksheets for your class and print them double-sided. Every worksheet has questions on the front and a **STOP** message with a **Name** line on the reverse, so students can write their names before the race begins.

Choose the operations, difficulty, number of worksheets, and question count below. **No answer keys are generated.**
