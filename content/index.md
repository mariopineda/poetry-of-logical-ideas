## A Final Reminder

You are responsible for your own learning.

Your teacher will provide instruction, class time, course materials, opportunities to practise, and opportunities to ask questions.

You are responsible for:

* coming to class;
* arriving on time;
* checking the online class schedule;
* completing your QODs;
* completing assigned practice;
* bringing the materials you need;
* asking for help when you need it;
* catching up when you miss class; and
* following the procedures explained on this site.

**When you are unsure about something, check this site, the online class schedule, Google Classroom, and the course outline first.**
