title: What if I have to leave class early?
created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z

If you need to leave class early, talk to me during class, preferably **before you leave**.

You are responsible for any instruction, examples, announcements, or classwork you miss after leaving.

Leaving early on a regular basis can make it difficult to keep up with the course, just like arriving late or missing class.
