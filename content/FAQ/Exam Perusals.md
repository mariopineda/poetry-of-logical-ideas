## What should I bring to class?

Bring:

* your workbook;
* pencils and other writing tools;
* the appropriate calculator; and
* a willingness to learn and participate.

You are responsible for arriving prepared for class.

## What if I forget my workbook?

You are still responsible for participating in class.

If extra workbooks are available at the back of the classroom, you may use one. Otherwise, take notes and complete your work on separate paper.

Forgetting your workbook is not a reason to sit out of the lesson.

## Do I need permission to use the washroom?

Not during regular class.

You are an adult and do not need to ask permission every time you need to leave briefly. Use this freedom responsibly.

You are responsible for anything you miss while you are outside the classroom.

**This does not apply during exams.** During an exam, you must follow the exam-room procedures.

## What behaviour is expected in class?

Students are expected to behave like **mature, responsible, and respectful adults**.

When your teacher or another student is speaking, give that person your attention.

Do not interrupt. If you want to ask a question or make a comment, raise your hand and wait for your turn.

Avoid side conversations while someone else is speaking. Giving other people your attention when they are speaking is a basic sign of respect.

## Can I eat or drink in class?

Yes, provided it does not disturb other people or create a mess.

Please avoid food that:

* has a strong smell;
* is greasy or messy; or
* has loud or distracting packaging.

Clean up after yourself.

Buying food or waiting in a food line is **not an excuse for arriving late to class**.

## Do we have assigned seats?

Not during regular classes. You may sit wherever you choose.

During exams, seating is assigned and may change from one exam to another.

## Can I record the class?

No. **Audio and video recording are not permitted at any time.**

## Can I take pictures of the whiteboard?

Yes, if you need to.

However, no other person, including your teacher, may appear in the photograph.

## Can I bring a friend, family member, or guest to class?

No.

Only students who are enrolled in the class are permitted in the classroom.

## What if I require an accommodation?

Accommodations must be **formally approved by school administration**.

Speak to your assistant principal about the approval process.

Once an accommodation has been formally approved, speak to me and bring it to my attention. I will follow accommodations that have been formally approved and communicated by the school.

## Can course requirements be changed because of my work schedule, vacation, appointment, or personal commitments?

Generally, no.

This is a **full-time, in-person course with a scheduled class time**.

You are responsible for managing work, appointments, vacations, and other personal commitments around the course.

## What about school-related activities or meetings?

Some attendance exceptions may be possible depending on the circumstances.

I must receive confirmation directly from the appropriate staff member, such as another teacher or an assistant principal.

This may affect how an absence or lateness is recorded, but it does **not** automatically create an exception to the missed-exam rules.

## What if I want to drop or withdraw from the course?

Speak to your **assistant principal**.

Course withdrawals are handled by school administration.

## Where do I find exam dates and other important dates?

Use the **online class schedule**.

Exam dates are posted there from the beginning of the course, but dates may occasionally change.

You are responsible for checking the schedule regularly. If you are absent, you are still responsible for monitoring it for changes.

You should not expect a separate email or message every time the schedule changes.

## How is my final course mark calculated?

The grading breakdown and explanation of how your course mark is calculated are provided in the **course outline**.

Refer to the course outline for this information.
