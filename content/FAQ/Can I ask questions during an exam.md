title: Can I ask questions during an exam?
created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z
modified: 2026-08-27T16:05:07.411Z
---

Yes, you may ask me a question during an exam.

However, my answers will be extremely limited. I cannot give hints, explain how to solve a problem, confirm whether your work is correct, or provide information that would give you an advantage.

If something on the exam is genuinely unclear, raise your hand and ask.
