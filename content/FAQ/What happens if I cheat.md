title: What happens if I cheat?
created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z
modified: 2026-08-27T16:05:07.457Z
---

A first incident of cheating results in a mark of **0** on the affected exam, QOD, or work.

The incident is recorded in your student profile and referred to the department head.

Repeated academic dishonesty will be referred to an assistant principal and may result in further consequences, including removal from the course, suspension, or other academic disciplinary action.
