## Do I need my own calculator?

Yes. You are responsible for bringing the appropriate calculator to class.

Depending on the course, this may be:

* a scientific calculator; or
* a graphing calculator.

Cell phones may **not** be used as calculators.

## Can I borrow a calculator?

Yes, but there is a specific sign-out procedure.

Calculator sign-out happens **only during attendance at the beginning of class**.

To borrow a calculator:

1. Power down your cell phone.
2. Put your name on the phone using a sticky note.
3. Give the phone to your teacher.
4. Receive the calculator.

Your phone will be locked away during class.

During the **last five minutes of class**, return the calculator and receive your phone.

## Can I borrow a calculator if I arrive late?

No.

Calculator sign-out happens only during attendance at the beginning of class.

## Can I return the calculator early so I can get my phone back?

No.

Calculator return takes place during the **last five minutes of class**.

## What happens if I lose, damage, or fail to return a borrowed calculator?

Your phone will not be returned to you at that time.

You will be sent to the department head to resolve the situation.

## Can I use my phone during regular class?

Yes, but use it **responsibly and appropriately**.

Watching movies or videos, playing games, or using your phone for entertainment during class is not appropriate.

Students who use devices inappropriately during class may be asked to leave.

## Can I use a laptop or tablet?

Yes.

Laptops and tablets may be used for legitimate classroom purposes, including:

* Google Classroom;
* course materials;
* notes; and
* assigned work.

They are not to be used for games, movies, videos, or other entertainment during class.

## Can I use headphones or earbuds?

Yes, during **independent work time**.

Do not use them while your teacher is teaching.

Headphones and earbuds are **not permitted during exams**.
