created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z

Yes, if you are using it for a legitimate classroom purpose.

A laptop or tablet can be useful for accessing course materials or completing appropriate schoolwork.

Using it for movies, games, entertainment, or other activities unrelated to class is not appropriate.
