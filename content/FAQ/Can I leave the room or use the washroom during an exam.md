There are **no washroom breaks during the first 30 minutes of an exam**.

After the first 30 minutes, you may leave for the washroom only **after you have finished and handed in your exam**.

Once your exam has been handed in, your attempt is complete. You may **not** return and continue writing.

There are no temporary washroom breaks where you leave the room and then return to continue the exam.

Only **one student at a time** may leave the classroom for the washroom.

You should therefore use the washroom before the exam begins whenever possible.
