---
title: "Can I leave the room or use the washroom during an exam?"
category: "Exams"
order: 5
publish: true
---

You may not leave the room while you are still writing the exam. If you need to use the washroom, you must first finish and hand in your exam.

After handing in the exam, washroom trips are one student at a time. You may not access your bag or phone while you are still in the exam room.
