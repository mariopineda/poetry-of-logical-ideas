publish: true
title: Can one replacement exam replace several unit exams?
created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z
modified: 2026-08-27T15:34:28.981Z
---

No.

Each replacement exam applies to **one specific unit only**.

If your course offers more than one replacement-exam opportunity, each replacement must still be used for a different eligible unit. You cannot use a later replacement exam to replace the mark from a unit that has already been replaced.
