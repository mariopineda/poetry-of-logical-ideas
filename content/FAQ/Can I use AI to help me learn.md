title: Can I use AI to help me learn?
created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z
modified: 2026-08-27T15:34:29.016Z
---

Yes, AI can be a useful learning tool when it helps you **understand** a concept, review an idea, or practise a skill.

The important distinction is whether AI is **supporting your learning or replacing your thinking**.

If AI does the work and you copy or submit that work as your own, that is cheating.

For QODs in particular, you should be able to understand and explain anything you submit.
