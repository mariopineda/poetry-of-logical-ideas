title: What do I do if I am absent?
created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z
modified: 2026-08-27T16:19:40.365Z
---

If you are absent, complete the **Absence Report** in Google Classroom on the **same day** as the absence.

The date on the form must be the date you were absent.

You cannot submit the Absence Report before the absence or on a later day.

If you submit the form correctly on the same day, the absence will be recorded as **excused**. If you do not, the absence will remain **unexcused**.

You are also responsible for finding out what you missed and catching up before you return to class.
