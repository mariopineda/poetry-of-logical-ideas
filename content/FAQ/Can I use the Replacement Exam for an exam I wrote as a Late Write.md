No.

If a unit exam was written as a **Late Write**, that unit is **not eligible for the Replacement Exam**.

Writing a Late Write therefore means that you give up the opportunity to replace that particular unit exam later.

You may still be eligible to use the Replacement Exam for a **different eligible unit**, provided you meet all other Replacement Exam requirements.
