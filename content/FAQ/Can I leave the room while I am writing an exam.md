title: Can I leave the room while I am writing an exam?
created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z
modified: 2026-08-27T16:05:07.420Z

No.

Once you begin writing, you may not leave the classroom and then return to continue the exam.

If you need to use the washroom, you must first **finish and hand in your exam**. After handing it in, one student at a time may leave for the washroom and return before another student goes.

You may not access your bag or phone.
