title: What if I communicate with another student during an exam?
created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z
modified: 2026-08-27T16:19:40.436Z
---

Communicating with another student during an exam is considered **cheating**.

This includes talking, passing information, showing work, using gestures to communicate answers, or communicating electronically.

If you need something during an exam, raise your hand and speak to the teacher instead.
