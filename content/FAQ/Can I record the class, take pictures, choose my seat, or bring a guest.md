Audio or video recording of the class is not allowed.

You may take a photograph of the whiteboard as long as no person, including the teacher, appears in the photograph.

Regular classes normally have open seating. Exams have assigned seating.

Friends, family members, and other guests may not attend class unless they are officially authorized by the school.
