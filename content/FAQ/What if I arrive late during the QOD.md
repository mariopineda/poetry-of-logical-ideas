title: What if I arrive late during the QOD?
created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z
modified: 2026-08-27T16:19:40.376Z
---

The QOD always closes **10 minutes after the scheduled start of class**.

If you arrive late, you only have whatever time remains in that 10-minute window.

For example, if you arrive **5 minutes late**, you have only **5 minutes left** to complete and submit the QOD.

The timer does **not** restart when you arrive.

Once the 10-minute QOD window has ended, the QOD is missed.
