created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z

An exam perusal operates under controlled conditions similar to an exam.

You may not have:

- your phone;
- your bag;
- pencils;
- paper;
- workbooks; or
- other materials.

You may not photograph, record, copy, or remove any part of the exam.
