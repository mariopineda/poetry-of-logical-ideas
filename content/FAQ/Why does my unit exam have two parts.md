title: Why does my unit exam have two parts?
created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z
modified: 2026-08-27T15:34:28.809Z
---

Most unit exams are divided into **Part A** and **Part B**, written during two consecutive class sessions.

Part A is normally written response. Part B is normally multiple choice and numerical response.

The exam is split across two classes so that **every student receives approximately twice the normal class time** to complete the unit exam. This is why a separate “extra time” accommodation is not used for these exams.
