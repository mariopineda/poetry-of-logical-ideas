title: Can I take notes or copy questions from my exam?
created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z
modified: 2026-08-27T16:19:40.420Z
---

No.

You may not write down, photograph, copy, or otherwise record exam questions or answers.

The purpose of the perusal is to review and understand your marked work, not to create a copy of the exam.
