You must be in good standing in the course and receive permission from me each time.

Good standing is based on your overall progress and behaviour in the course. I will consider things such as your attendance and lateness, your QOD record, your course mark, your classroom behaviour, and whether you are keeping up with your work.

Repeated or habitual absences or lateness, a pattern of missed or failing QODs, poor progress in the course, or misuse of the prayer-space privilege may affect whether you are allowed to leave class.

Good standing is based on my judgment. There is no automatic formula. Even if you are in good standing, permission to leave is not automatic. I will decide whether it is an appropriate time for you to leave.

If you are not permitted to leave class for the prayer space, you may still use the school prayer space outside of class time. If your attendance, work, behaviour, or progress improves, permission to leave during class may be restored.

Misuse of the privilege will normally affect only the student who misuses it. That student may lose permission to leave class to use the prayer space. If there is a repeated or organized pattern of misuse involving several students, class-time access may be restricted for a larger group or for all students.

Students are also responsible for knowing and following all school-wide rules for use of the prayer space.
