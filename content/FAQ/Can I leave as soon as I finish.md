title: Can I leave as soon as I finish?
created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z
modified: 2026-08-27T16:19:40.388Z
---

No one may leave the classroom during the **first 30 minutes** of an exam.

If you finish during that time, hand in your exam and remain seated quietly.

After the first 30 minutes, you may leave after handing in your exam. Only one student at a time should be up, collecting belongings, and leaving so that exam conditions are maintained for everyone still writing.
