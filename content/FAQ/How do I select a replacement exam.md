title: How do I select a replacement exam?
created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z

The replacement-exam selection window is usually about **two weeks before the replacement exam** and normally lasts only a few specific class days. The exact dates are shown on the online class schedule.

You must make your selection **in person during class**.

Selections are not accepted by email or message.

If the final unit exam is written too close to the replacement exam for its mark to be available before the selection deadline, you may tell me at the beginning of the replacement exam that you want to replace the final unit. This is the only exception.
