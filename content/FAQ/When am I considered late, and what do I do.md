---
title: "When am I considered late, and what do I do?"
category: "Attendance & Lateness"
order: 1
publish: true
---

Attendance is taken at the scheduled start of class. If you are not physically in the classroom when your name is called, you are late. This is true even if you arrive only a few seconds later. A bag, jacket, or other belongings in the room do not count as attendance.

If you are late, enter the classroom and submit the Late Report in Google Classroom. You must submit it while you are physically inside the classroom and within the first 20 minutes of class. If you do not submit the Late Report, you will remain marked absent.
