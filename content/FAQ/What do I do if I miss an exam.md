title: What do I do if I miss an exam?
created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z
modified: 2026-08-27T16:19:40.401Z
---

If you miss an exam, you must email me **after the missed exam and within 24 hours**.

Your email must include:

- your name;
- your class; and
- the exam you missed.

Telling me before the exam that you will be absent does **not** satisfy this requirement.

Emailing within 24 hours makes you eligible to **request** a late write. It does not automatically mean a late write has been approved.
