Check the online class schedule regularly. The **current online schedule is the official source for course dates**.

Exam dates are posted there from the beginning of the course, but the schedule is tentative and dates may change. I may announce changes in class, but you are responsible for checking the online schedule. Do not rely on receiving a separate message.

An older copy of the schedule, an old screenshot, or information from another student does not override the current online schedule.

Also check this website, Google Classroom, and the course outline. My handwritten in-class notes are not posted online. Shorter online notes are available, but they may use different examples or explanations and are not identical to what happens in class. Classmates are also an important source of information if you miss something.
