Check the online class schedule regularly for exam dates and other important dates. The schedule is tentative, so dates may change and you are responsible for monitoring it.

Also check this website, Google Classroom, and the course outline. My handwritten in-class notes are not posted online. Shorter online notes are available, but they may use different examples or explanations and are not identical to what happens in class. Classmates are also an important source of information if you miss something.
