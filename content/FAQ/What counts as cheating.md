title: What counts as cheating?
created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z

---

Cheating means presenting work as your own when the work is **not actually your own**.

This includes work copied or obtained from:

- another student;
- ChatGPT or another AI system;
- a website or online solution;
- notes or materials that are not permitted; or
- any other source that has done the work for you.

Academic honesty applies to exams, QODs, and classwork.
