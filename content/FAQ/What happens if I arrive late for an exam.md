title: What happens if I arrive late for an exam?
created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z
modified: 2026-08-27T15:34:28.909Z
---

You may begin an exam up to **30 minutes after the scheduled start time**, but you will receive **no extra time**. The exam still ends at the original scheduled time.

Normal attendance procedures also apply. If attendance has already been taken, you must complete the Late Report while physically in the classroom and within the first 20 minutes of class.

If you arrive more than **30 minutes late**, you cannot write the exam.
