---
title: "Can I leave, talk, or use my phone after I finish my exam?"
category: "Exams"
order: 6
publish: true
---

No one may leave during the first 30 minutes of the exam. If you finish during that time, hand in your exam and remain quietly seated.

After the first 30 minutes, you may leave after handing in your exam. Only one student at a time should be up collecting belongings and leaving. Exam conditions continue until you leave the classroom, so you may not talk or use your phone while you remain in the room.
