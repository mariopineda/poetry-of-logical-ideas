A limited QOD catch-up opportunity may be available approximately two weeks before the replacement exam.

QOD catch-up is intended for students who need to address a **small number of missed or low QOD marks**. It is not designed for completing a large backlog, and students should **not rely on catch-up as a way to meet the replacement-exam criterion**.

Catch-up opportunities are limited and require an appointment. The work must be completed **in the classroom during a spare**, not during your regular math class. I will determine which QODs you complete, and QODs completed beforehand cannot be submitted as catch-up work.

Unlike regular QODs, where only selected submissions may be marked, **all QODs completed during a catch-up session are marked**.

Completing catch-up QODs does **not guarantee** that your QOD average will reach the level required for replacement-exam eligibility. The best approach is to maintain your QOD average throughout the course rather than depending on a later catch-up opportunity.
