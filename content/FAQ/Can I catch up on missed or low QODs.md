There may be a limited QOD catch-up opportunity approximately two weeks before a replacement exam.

You must make an appointment. Appointments are limited. The work must be completed in the classroom during a spare, not during your regular math class. I choose which QODs you complete. You cannot bring in QODs completed beforehand.

This opportunity is intended to help with a small number of missed or low QODs, not a large backlog. It does **not** guarantee that your QOD average will reach 50%. You are responsible for maintaining your QOD average throughout the course.
