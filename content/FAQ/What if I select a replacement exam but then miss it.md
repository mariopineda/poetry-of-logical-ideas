title: What if I select a replacement exam but then miss it?
created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z
modified: 2026-08-27T15:34:28.854Z
---

The replacement-exam opportunity is lost.

There is **no late write, make-up date, or alternate sitting for a replacement exam**.

You must be present on the scheduled replacement-exam date.
