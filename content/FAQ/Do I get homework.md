Most assigned practice is started during class, and you are given substantial class time to work on it.

If you do not finish the assigned work during class, it automatically becomes your responsibility to complete it **before the next class**.

I may not specifically announce that something is “homework.” You are responsible for keeping up with the assigned practice.
