title: Can course requirements be changed because of my work schedule, vacation, appointment, or personal commitments?
created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z
modified: 2026-08-27T16:13:45.560Z
---

No.

This is a **full-time, in-person course**, and the course requirements apply to everyone.

Work schedules, vacations, appointments, and other personal commitments do not create special extensions, alternate deadlines, or exceptions to normal course procedures.

You are responsible for managing your commitments around the course.
