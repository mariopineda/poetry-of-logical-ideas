created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z

During regular class, no.

You are an adult and do not need to ask permission to use the washroom. Use that freedom responsibly and remember that you are responsible for anything you miss while you are out of the room.

**Different rules apply during exams.** During an exam, you cannot leave while you are still writing.
