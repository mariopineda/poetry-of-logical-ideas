title: What is an exam perusal?
created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z
modified: 2026-08-27T16:19:40.412Z
---

An **exam perusal** is a scheduled opportunity for you to look at your marked exam after the marks have been released.

During the perusal, you can review your work, see where you lost marks, and ask questions about the marking.

The exam itself does **not** leave the classroom.
