title: What happens if I choose not to complete the classwork?
created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z

You are responsible for your own learning.

If you choose not to complete the assigned practice, the natural consequence is that you may be unprepared for future lessons, QODs, and exams.

Class time gives you an opportunity to practise while help is available. How you use that time will affect your chances of success.
