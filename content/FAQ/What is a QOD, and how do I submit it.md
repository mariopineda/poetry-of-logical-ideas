QOD means Question of the Day. A QOD is normally posted in Google Classroom at the beginning of class.

You have exactly 10 minutes from the scheduled start of class to complete it. Your work must be handwritten on paper, photographed, and submitted through Google Classroom.

You need a functioning phone or other device that can take a picture and upload it to Google Classroom.
