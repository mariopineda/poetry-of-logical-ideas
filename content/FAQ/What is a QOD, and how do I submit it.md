title: "What is a QOD, and how do I submit it?"
category: "QOD & Classwork"

publish: true
---

QOD means Question of the Day. A QOD is posted in Google Classroom at the beginning of class. You have exactly 10 minutes from the scheduled start of class to complete and submit it.

Your work must be handwritten on paper, photographed, and submitted through Google Classroom. You therefore need a functioning phone or other device that can take a picture and upload it to Google Classroom.
