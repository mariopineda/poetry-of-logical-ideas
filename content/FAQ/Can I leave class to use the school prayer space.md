Yes, but you must ask me for permission **every time** before you leave. Permission is not automatic.

Whenever possible, use the prayer space during a spare, break, or before or after class. Leaving class to use the prayer space is a privilege and should not be taken for granted.

You must wait for an appropriate time to leave. You will not normally be permitted to leave during instruction. You may not leave during a QOD or any other assessment, including quizzes, unit exams, late writes, replacement exams, exam perusals, or other assessments.

If you receive permission, go directly to the prayer space, complete your prayer, and return to class promptly. Do not use the trip as an extra break, visit other places, or spend extra time away from class.

Normal attendance, lateness, and QOD rules still apply. You are responsible for catching up on anything you miss while you are away from class.

Each student must ask for permission individually. One student cannot ask for permission for other students.
