title: How is my Part A and Part B exam mark calculated?
created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z
modified: 2026-08-27T15:34:28.946Z
---

Part A and Part B together make **one unit exam**.

The points you earn on both parts are added together. Your percentage is then calculated using the **total number of points available across both parts**.

Part A and Part B are not treated as two separate unit-exam marks.
