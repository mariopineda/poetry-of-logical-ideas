title: What about school-related activities or meetings?
created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z
modified: 2026-08-27T16:05:07.435Z
---

School-related activities may sometimes be treated differently for **attendance purposes**, depending on the circumstances.

Any exception requires direct confirmation from the relevant school staff member or assistant principal.

A school-related activity does **not automatically excuse a missed exam** or replace the normal missed-exam procedures.
