created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z

You are expected to use your phone **responsibly and appropriately**.

Students in this program are adults, so phones are not automatically collected during regular class. However, watching movies or videos, playing games, or using a device for entertainment during class is inappropriate.

If your phone use is disrupting learning, you may be asked to leave the classroom.
