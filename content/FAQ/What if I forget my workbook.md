title: What if I forget my workbook?
created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z
modified: 2026-08-27T15:34:28.868Z
---

You should still participate in class.

If an extra workbook is available at the back of the classroom, you may use it during class. Otherwise, complete the work on separate paper.

Forgetting your workbook does not remove your responsibility to participate and complete the assigned work.
