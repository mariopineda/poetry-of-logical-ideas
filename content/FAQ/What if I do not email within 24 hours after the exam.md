If you do not email Dr. Pineda within 24 hours of the **scheduled end of the missed exam**, you are not eligible to request a Late Write.

The missed exam mark remains a zero.

You may still be able to replace that zero later using the Replacement Exam if you meet all Replacement Exam requirements and the unit is otherwise eligible for replacement.
