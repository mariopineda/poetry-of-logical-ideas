title: What do I do if I am late?
created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z
modified: 2026-08-27T16:19:40.349Z
---

If you arrive after attendance has been taken, complete the **Late Report** in Google Classroom.

You must submit the Late Report **while you are physically in the classroom** and within the first **20 minutes of class**.

You cannot submit the Late Report before you arrive.

If you arrive within the first 20 minutes but do not submit the Late Report, you will remain marked absent.

If you arrive more than 20 minutes after class begins, you are considered absent. Submitting the Late Report after that time will not change your attendance.
