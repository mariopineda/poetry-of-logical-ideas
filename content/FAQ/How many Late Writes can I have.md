A maximum of **one Late Write may be approved during the course**. This is a hard limit.

The one-Late-Write limit does **not** mean that every student is automatically entitled to one.

Any Late Write request must first be approved according to the Late Write policy.

Once you have used an approved Late Write, another Late Write will not be approved during the course.
