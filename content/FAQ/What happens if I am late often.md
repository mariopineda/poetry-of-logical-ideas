title: What happens if I am late often?
created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z

---

Repeated lateness means you regularly miss the beginning of class, including the QOD, announcements, instruction, examples, and classwork.

Over time, this makes it much harder to keep up with the course and increases the likelihood that you will fall behind.

Being on time is part of taking responsibility for your own learning.
