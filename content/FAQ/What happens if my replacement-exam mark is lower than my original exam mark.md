title: What happens if my replacement-exam mark is lower than my original exam mark?
created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z
modified: 2026-08-27T15:34:28.887Z
---

The **higher mark is kept**.

If your replacement-exam mark is higher, it replaces the original mark.

If your replacement-exam mark is lower, your original mark remains unchanged.

There is therefore no mark penalty for attempting an eligible replacement exam.
