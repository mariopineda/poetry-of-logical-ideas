## What can I have with me during an exam?

You may have:

* the appropriate calculator;
* writing tools;
* the formula sheet provided by your teacher;
* a small snack; and
* water.

You may **not** have a phone, smartwatch, smart glasses, headphones, earbuds, unauthorized notes, your own formula sheet, or any other electronic device available to you during the exam.

## What happens before I enter the exam room?

The classroom will normally open approximately **five minutes before the exam**. Students should line up outside the classroom.

If you have a cell phone, it must be **powered down**. You must show the powered-down phone to your teacher as you enter and then place it in your bag.

Bags and jackets must be placed at the back of the classroom.

If you do not have a cell phone with you, that is fine.

## Can I choose where I sit during an exam?

No. Seating is assigned during exams, and your assigned seat may change from one exam to another.

You are expected to sit in the seat assigned to you.

## What happens if I arrive late for an exam?

You may begin the exam up to **30 minutes after the scheduled starting time**.

You do **not** receive extra time at the end. The exam still ends at the originally scheduled time.

Normal attendance rules also apply. If you were not present when attendance was taken, you are responsible for submitting the Late Report within the first 20 minutes of class.

If you arrive more than **30 minutes after the exam begins**, you will not be permitted to write the exam.

## Can I ask questions during an exam?

Yes, but there are very few questions your teacher can answer during an exam.

Do not expect hints, help solving a question, explanations of the mathematics, or confirmation that your answer is correct.

## Can I leave the room while I am writing an exam?

No. You cannot leave the classroom while you are actively writing the exam.

If you need to use the washroom, you must first **finish and hand in your exam**. Only one student at a time may leave for the washroom, and that student must return before another student may leave.

You may not access your bag, phone, or other personal belongings during the exam.

## Can I leave as soon as I finish?

No student may leave during the **first 30 minutes** of an exam.

If you finish before 30 minutes, you may hand in your exam, but you must remain quietly in your seat.

After 30 minutes, you may leave once you have handed in your exam. Only **one student at a time** should be up, collecting belongings, and leaving the classroom.

Until you actually leave the classroom, **exam conditions continue to apply**.

## Can I use my phone after I hand in my exam?

No. You may not access your phone or bag while you are still in the exam room.

## Can I talk after I finish my exam?

No. Other students are still writing. Remain quiet until you leave the classroom.

## What happens when exam time ends?

When the scheduled exam time ends, you must hand in your exam.

There is **no extra time beyond the scheduled end of the exam**.

## Can I use my own formula sheet?

No. You may only use the formula sheet provided by your teacher during the exam.

## Can I write on the formula sheet?

Yes. You may write anything you want on the formula sheet provided during the exam.

The formula sheet will be collected when the exam is finished.

## Can I take any exam materials with me?

No. **Everything is collected** at the end of the exam.

This includes the exam, formula sheet, scratch paper, and any other written materials used during the exam.
