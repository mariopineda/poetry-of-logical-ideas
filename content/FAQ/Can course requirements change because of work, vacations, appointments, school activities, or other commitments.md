This is a full-time, in-person course. Work schedules, vacations, appointments, and other personal commitments do not create special exceptions, extensions, or different course requirements. You are responsible for managing those commitments around the course.

A school-related activity may sometimes be treated differently for attendance if I receive confirmation from the appropriate staff member or assistant principal. It does not automatically excuse a missed exam or remove the normal missed-exam requirements.

If you are considering dropping or withdrawing from the course, speak with your assistant principal.
