This is a full-time, in-person course. Work schedules, vacations, appointments, and other personal commitments do not create special exceptions, extensions, or different course requirements. You are responsible for managing those commitments around the course.

School-related activities or meetings may be handled differently for attendance purposes. You are still responsible for making sure your attendance is correctly recorded through SchoolZone and for following any instructions provided by the school.

A school-related activity does not automatically excuse a missed exam or remove the normal missed-exam requirements.
