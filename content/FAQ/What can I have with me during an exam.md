title: What can I have with me during an exam?
created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z
modified: 2026-08-27T16:19:40.384Z
---

You may have:

- an appropriate calculator;
- pencils, pens, and other writing tools;
- the formula sheet provided by the teacher;
- a small snack; and
- water.

You may **not** have or access a phone, smartwatch, smart glasses, headphones or earbuds, your own notes or formula sheet, or any other unauthorized electronic device or material.
