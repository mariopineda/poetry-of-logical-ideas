A late write is a formal opportunity for a student who missed all or part of a scheduled unit exam to request a new complete exam for that unit. A late write is not available simply because you received a low mark.

If you miss an exam or an exam part, email me after it has ended and within 24 hours of its scheduled end time. Your email must include your name, your class, and the exam or exam part you missed.

An email sent before the exam does not replace this requirement. Confirming your absence in SchoolZone is also a separate process and does not replace the missed-exam email.

Exams are written at the scheduled time with the class. Knowing ahead of time that you will be absent does not allow you to write the exam early or choose another writing time.
