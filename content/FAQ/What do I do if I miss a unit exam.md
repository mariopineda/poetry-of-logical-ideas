---
title: "What do I do if I miss a unit exam?"
category: "Late Writes"
order: 1
publish: true
---

Email me after the scheduled exam has ended and within 24 hours of the scheduled exam end time. Your email must include your name, your class, and the exam you missed.

A late write is for a missed unit exam. It is not a way to rewrite an exam simply because you are unhappy with your mark.
