Yes, if a classroom calculator is available.

Calculators may be borrowed **only during the first 10 minutes of class**, while the QOD is in progress.

If you need to borrow one, arrange the loan immediately when class begins.

To borrow a calculator:

1. Power down your phone.
2. Give your powered-down phone to Dr. Pineda as collateral.
3. Receive the classroom calculator.
4. Keep the calculator for the remainder of the class.
5. Return the calculator at the end of class.
6. Your phone is returned when the calculator is returned.

If you do not arrange a calculator loan during the first 10 minutes of class, a calculator will **not** be issued later in the class.
