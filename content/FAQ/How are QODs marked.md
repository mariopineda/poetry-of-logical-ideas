---
title: "How are QODs marked?"
category: "QOD & Classwork"
order: 3
publish: true
---

Only selected QODs are marked, so you should complete every QOD as though it will be selected. A missed QOD receives zero.

A genuine, legible attempt normally begins around 50%. Marks then depend on the quality and correctness of the work. Major arithmetic errors may result in deductions of about 10% or more. Work showing a significant lack of understanding may lose 25% or more. Illegible work also loses marks because your reasoning must be readable. If none of the work can be read, the maximum is normally 50% for the attempt.
