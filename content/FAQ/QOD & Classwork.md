## When am I considered late?

Attendance is taken immediately at the beginning of class. If you are not physically in the classroom when your name is called, you are not present for attendance. Even if you arrive only a few seconds later, you are late.

Leaving your backpack, jacket, or other belongings in the classroom does **not** mean you are present.

## What do I do if I am late?

Submit the **Late Report** in Google Classroom. The Late Report must be submitted:

* while you are **physically inside the classroom**; and
* within the **first 20 minutes of class**.

You cannot submit the Late Report before you arrive at class. If you arrive within the first 20 minutes but do not submit the Late Report, you remain marked absent.

## What if I arrive more than 20 minutes late?

You are considered **absent**, not late. The Late Report cannot be used after the first 20 minutes of class.

## What happens if I am late often?

Being late means missing important parts of the course. You may miss the Question of the Day (QOD), important announcements, instruction, examples, and classwork.

Repeated lateness will make it much harder to succeed in the course.

## What do I do if I am absent?

Submit the **Absence Report** in Google Classroom on the **same day you are absent**. The date the form is submitted is the date of the absence.

You cannot submit an Absence Report before the absence or use it later for a previous absence. If you submit the Absence Report correctly, your absence will be recorded as **excused**. If you do not submit it that day, your absence remains **unexcused**.

## Can I tell you ahead of time that I will be absent?

Yes. However, telling me ahead of time does **not** replace the Absence Report. You must still submit the Absence Report on the day you are absent.

## What happens if I have many absences?

The more classes you miss, the less likely you are to be successful in the course. If you are rarely in class, you are missing the instruction, practice, and opportunities for help that the course provides.

A large number of absences may also result in you being **removed from the course by school administration**.

## What am I responsible for if I miss class?

You are responsible for finding out what you missed and getting caught up **before you return to class**. Use the online class schedule, Google Classroom, the online course materials, and your classmates.

Regular class time is not for catching up on work from previous absences.

## What if I know I will miss a class?

You may work ahead if you want to. You may also tell me ahead of time that you will be away, but the normal attendance procedures still apply. You must still submit the Absence Report on the day you are absent.

## What if I have to leave class early?

If you need to leave early, speak to me during class, preferably **before you leave**. You are responsible for anything you miss.

Leaving class early regularly means missing instructional time and will make it harder to keep up with the course.
