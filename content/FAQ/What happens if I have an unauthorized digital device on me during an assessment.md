If an unauthorized digital device is on your person at any time during an assessment, your attempt is automatically invalidated. You do not have to be using the device for this rule to apply.

Unauthorized digital devices include cell phones, smartwatches, smart glasses, earbuds, headphones, and other unauthorized electronic devices.
