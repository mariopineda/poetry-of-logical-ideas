Calculator sign-out is handled only during the **first 10 minutes of class**.

If you arrive late or do not arrange a calculator loan during that period, you cannot borrow one later in the class.

A borrowed calculator must remain with you until the end of class. You may **not return it early simply to get your phone back**.

The calculator is returned at the end of class, and your phone is returned at that time.

If a borrowed calculator is lost, damaged, or not returned, your phone will not be returned at that time. The situation will be referred to the department head to resolve.

The teacher and school are not responsible for loss or damage to a personal device left as collateral.
