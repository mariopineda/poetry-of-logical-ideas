## What is a replacement exam?

A replacement exam gives an eligible student the opportunity to replace the mark for **one specific unit exam**.

You may use a replacement exam to replace:

* an exam you wrote and want to improve; or
* an exam you missed and received a zero on.

## What happens if my replacement-exam mark is lower than my original exam mark?

The **higher of the two marks is kept**.

A replacement exam cannot lower your existing mark for that unit.

## Can one replacement exam replace several unit exams?

No. Each replacement exam replaces the mark for **one specific unit only**.

You must select which unit you want to replace before writing the replacement exam.

## Who is eligible to write a replacement exam?

To be eligible, you must:

* have a **QOD average of at least 50%**;
* select the unit you want to replace during the official selection period; and
* be present to write the replacement exam.

If you do not meet all of these requirements, you cannot write the replacement exam.

## How do I select a replacement exam?

About two weeks before the replacement exam, there will be a short selection period listed on the online class schedule.

During that period, you must speak to me **in person during class** and tell me which unit you want to replace.

You cannot make your selection by email or message.

## Can I change my replacement-exam selection?

Yes, but only while the official selection period is still open.

Once the selection deadline has passed, your choice is final.

The only possible exception is the final unit exam if its mark was not available before the selection deadline. If that situation occurs, you will be given instructions about how to select that unit.

## What if I forget to select a replacement exam?

You do not get to write one.

If you do not make your selection during the official selection period, you are **not eligible to write the replacement exam**.

## What if I select a replacement exam but then miss it?

You lose that opportunity.

There are **no late writes or make-up dates for replacement exams**. You must be present at the scheduled replacement exam to write it.
