A **QOD (Question of the Day)** is a short question or problem given at the beginning of class.

QODs are usually posted in **Google Classroom** and are designed to check your understanding of recent course material.

You have **10 minutes from the scheduled start of class** to complete and submit the QOD.

Your work must be **handwritten on paper**, photographed, and uploaded to Google Classroom before the submission closes.
