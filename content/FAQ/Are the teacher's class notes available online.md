No. My handwritten classroom notes are **not posted online**.

There are shorter online course notes available, but they were not written by me and are not identical to what happens in class.

During class, I may add explanations, different examples, additional steps, or other information that is not included in the online notes. If you miss class, use your classmates as one source for finding out what you missed.
