Students are expected to behave like mature, responsible, respectful adults.

When I or another student is speaking, give that person your attention. Do not interrupt. Raise your hand or wait for an appropriate time to speak. Side conversations during instruction are disruptive and disrespectful.

You may use the washroom during regular class without asking permission. Use this responsibly. You are responsible for anything you miss while you are gone. Different rules apply during assessments.

Food and drinks are allowed within reason. Food should not have a strong smell, be very greasy or messy, or involve noisy packaging. Clean up after yourself. Waiting in a food line is not an excuse for being late.
