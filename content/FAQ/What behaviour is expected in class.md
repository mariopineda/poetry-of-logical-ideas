---
title: "What behaviour is expected in class?"
category: "Classroom Expectations"
order: 2
publish: true
---

Students are expected to behave like mature, responsible, respectful adults. When I or another student is speaking, give that person your attention. Do not interrupt, and avoid side conversations during instruction.

You may use the washroom during regular class without asking permission, but use this responsibly and remember that you are responsible for anything you miss.

Food and drinks are allowed within reason. Food should not have a strong smell, be excessively greasy or messy, or involve disruptive packaging. Clean up after yourself. Waiting in a food line is not an excuse for being late.
