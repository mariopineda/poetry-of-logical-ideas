title: What behaviour is expected in class?
created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z
modified: 2026-08-27T15:34:28.926Z
---

Everyone is expected to behave as a **mature, responsible, and respectful adult**.

When the teacher or another student is speaking, give that person your attention.

Do not interrupt. If you want to contribute, raise your hand or wait for an appropriate opportunity to speak.

Side conversations while someone else is speaking are disrespectful and disruptive to the learning environment.
