Yes.

You are responsible for bringing the appropriate scientific or graphing calculator required for your course.

Your calculator should be with you and ready to use at the **beginning of class**, including during the Question of the Day.

A phone is **never permitted to be used as a calculator in this course**.

A limited number of classroom calculators may be available through the calculator borrowing procedure.
