## Can I get extra help from my teacher outside of class?

No. Extra-help sessions are not provided outside regular class time.

There is substantial work time built into regular classes. Use that time to ask questions and get the help you need.

Do not wait until the end of class when everyone is leaving to ask for help.

## Is tutoring available?

Yes. Drop-in math tutors are available on the **fourth floor**.

If you need additional help, you are encouraged to use this resource.

## What should I do if I am struggling in the course?

Talk to me **during class**.

Do not wait until you have fallen far behind. The sooner you ask for help, the easier it is to identify the problem and decide what to do next.

You may also email me, but speaking to me during class is normally the best first step.

## How do I contact you outside of class?

You can contact me by **email**.

Email is not instant messaging. I normally check my email approximately **once per school day during school hours**.

You should normally expect it to take at least **one school day** to receive a response. Messages sent over weekends, holidays, or school breaks will take longer.

## What if my question is already answered somewhere?

If the answer is already available in the FAQ, online class schedule, Google Classroom, or course outline, I may simply send you a link to the appropriate information.

Please check these resources before emailing a question.

## Should I ask my classmates for help?

Yes. Get to know the students you sit near.

Your classmates are an excellent resource if you:

* miss notes;
* miss a class;
* want to know what happened during class; or
* want to discuss classwork.

If you miss a class, asking a classmate for the notes should be one of your first steps.

## Are the teacher's class notes available online?

No. You are expected to take your own notes during class.

A shorter set of course notes is available online, but these are **not identical** to the notes, examples, and explanations given during class.

Your teacher may expand on topics, use different examples, explain ideas in different ways, or provide information that does not appear in the online notes.
