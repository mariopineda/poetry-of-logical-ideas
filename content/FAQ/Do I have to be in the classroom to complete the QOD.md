title: Do I have to be in the classroom to complete the QOD?
created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z
modified: 2026-08-27T15:34:29.094Z
---

No.

You may complete and submit the QOD from anywhere, as long as you submit it within the **10-minute QOD window**.

For example, if you are on the bus or still at home, you may complete the QOD there and submit it through Google Classroom before the deadline.

You are responsible for having a **functioning phone or other device that can take a picture and upload it to Google Classroom**.

Once the 10-minute submission window closes, the QOD is missed.
