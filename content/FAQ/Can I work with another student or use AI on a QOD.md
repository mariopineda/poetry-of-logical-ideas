---
title: "Can I work with another student or use AI on a QOD?"
category: "QOD & Classwork"
order: 6
publish: true
---

Collaboration is allowed when both students genuinely contribute, understand the work, and could explain the solution themselves. Simply copying another student's work is cheating.

AI may be used to help you learn or understand a concept. Copying an AI-generated solution and submitting it as your own QOD is cheating.
