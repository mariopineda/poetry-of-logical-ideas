## What do I do if I miss an exam?

You must email me **after the missed exam and within 24 hours**.

Your email must include:

* your name;
* your class; and
* the exam you missed.

This is the **first eligibility requirement for a late write**.

If you email me **more than 24 hours after the missed exam**, you are **not eligible for a late write**. In that case, the only way to replace the missing mark is later through the replacement-exam process, provided you meet the replacement-exam requirements.

## Do I need to explain why I missed the exam?

No. You do **not** need to explain or justify why you missed the exam.

## Does emailing me within 24 hours mean I automatically get a late write?

No. Emailing me within 24 hours only makes you **eligible to request a late write**. It does not guarantee one.

If your request is approved, there are **several additional requirements that you must complete** before you can write the exam.

The late-write process takes time, requires additional work from you, and may take **several weeks or longer** to complete.

A late write is **not a quick or easy way to make up a missed exam**.

## Can I write the missed exam when I return to class?

No. You cannot simply write a missed exam when you return to class.

You must follow either the **late-write process** or, if applicable, the **replacement-exam process**.

## What if I do not email within 24 hours?

You are **not eligible for a late write**.

Your missed exam remains a zero. Your only opportunity to replace that mark is through a **replacement exam**, provided you meet all replacement-exam eligibility requirements and correctly select that unit during the selection period.
