title: What am I responsible for if I miss class?
created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z
modified: 2026-08-27T16:19:40.369Z
---

If you miss class, you are responsible for finding out what you missed and catching up **before you return to class**.

Use the online class schedule, Google Classroom, course materials, and your classmates to determine what was covered.

Regular class time is for the current lesson and current work. It is **not** catch-up time for material you missed previously.

If you return without catching up, you may find it difficult to follow the lesson and complete the work.
