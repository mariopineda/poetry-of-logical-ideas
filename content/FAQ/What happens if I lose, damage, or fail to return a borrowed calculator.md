title: What happens if I lose, damage, or fail to return a borrowed calculator?
created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z

If a borrowed calculator is lost, damaged, or not returned, your phone will **not** be returned to you by the teacher.

The matter will be referred to the department head, who will determine how it will be resolved.
