title: Can I work with another student?
created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z
modified: 2026-08-27T16:19:40.431Z
---

Yes, during regular classwork and QODs, provided the collaboration is genuine.

Both students should contribute, understand the work, and be able to explain what they submitted.

Simply copying another student's work is cheating.

Collaboration is **not permitted during exams**.
