title: Can I eat or drink in class?
created: 2026-08-27T20:59:58.000Z
modified: 2026-08-27T15:34:29.058Z
---

Yes, within reason.

Food and drinks are allowed provided they do not create a distraction or mess.

Please avoid food with a strong smell, greasy or messy food, and loud or disruptive packaging. Clean up after yourself.

Waiting in a food line is **not** an excuse for being late to class.
